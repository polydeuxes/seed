# Bounded Goal Relation, Topology, and Horizon Connection Recovery 001

## Scope, method, and classification

This is one bounded, report-only constitutional recovery against merged `main`
after PR 1972. PRs 1971 and 1972 are immutable locator testimony, not authority.
The authorities used here are current runtime producers and consumers, their
focused tests, and the canonical Book. No historical or deleted goal-orientation
inventory is promoted into current standing.

The governing seam is always:

```text
external grammar
       ↕ Fidelity
constitutional grammar
```

Fidelity governs crossings; it is not another content family. At each material
crossing this report asks for subject/identity, assertion/content, standing,
source/provenance, responsibility, authority/warrant, scope/locality, and
occurrence/preservation, then separately asks Capability, Gap, and Demand. The
labels mean: **evidenced** (an active implementation and/or canonical boundary
directly supports the claim), **partially evidenced** (some required dimensions
exist), **compatibility-only** (shape permits it without constitutional
production), **compressed** (distinct responsibilities or standings are carried
without being separately established), **absent** (the examined current road has
no producer/field/consumer for it), and **Unknown** (evidence cannot decide the
claim). Artifact absence is not Gap automatically; Gap is not Demand; Demand is
not Question. Capability availability is not applicability, selection,
authorization, execution, observation/result, verification, or current standing.

revised, and later consumed while preserving history; it does not instantiate a

**Evidenced:** present horizon construction neither requires nor consumes any

## 5. Relation candidate -> evidence -> current-standing topology


This generic topology is **evidenced**. Its application to goal relations is
**absent**: no owner nominates a relation candidate, binds evidence to exact goal

Accordingly current evidence supports neither a mutable goal-relation artifact nor
immutable goal-relation occurrences plus current projection. The constitutional
orientation favors preserving occurrences and separately warranting current
projection, because expiry, staleness, contradiction, and supersession do not
erase history, but that is a constraint on any lawful road—not evidence that a
goal-relation road exists now. `relation supported later != relation established
earlier`; `changed standing != changed goal identity`.
