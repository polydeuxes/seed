## 5. `automatic correction authority` correction

PR 2195 recommended deleting `automatic correction authority` from the Fidelity negative-authority list. That disposition is corrected. The negative-authority boundary is necessary; the defective part is the modifier `automatic`, which may imply the false alternative that only automatic correction authority is prohibited while non-automatic correction authority can follow from a Fidelity finding.

That is not the constitutional boundary. A Fidelity finding does not establish correction authority by identity. The narrow lawful future wording repair is:

```text
automatic correction authority
→
correction authority
```

The complete negative-authority claim must remain: **a Fidelity finding must not become correction authority**. Correction would require its own responsible act, exact subject, purpose, evidence, warrant, authority, scope, occurrence, result, consumer boundary, and negative authority. A Fidelity finding supplies none of those by identity.

This report neither performs that amendment nor warrants a global purge of `automatic`. Broader uses of `automatic` may warrant a later bounded active-Book vocabulary audit where the word could conceal an absent responsible occurrence. That possibility does not make every ordinary English use defective.
