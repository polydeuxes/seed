# Operator-Ingress Candidate-Testimony Preservation Responsibility and Producer Coordinates Fidelity Recovery 001

## 0. Status, scope, method, and result

This is one bounded, report-only Fidelity recovery after PR 2123. It changes no
Book text, production code, tests, Events, projections, CLI, runtime wiring,
schema, identifier, or earlier report. Book statements are constitutional
testimony; current code, focused tests, imports, constructors, serializers,
projectors, calls, consumers, and history are implementation testimony.

The result is **mixed**:

* The Book **does warrant an independent, bounded preservation
  responsibility** when attributed candidate testimony actually reaches a
  responsible receiving/calling boundary and loss would erase material
  testimony, attribution, production evidence, or other standing that is not
  sufficiently retained. A later semantic consumer need not already be known.
  The remembering purpose can be preventing that loss within a declared
  preservation horizon. Non-rebuildability supplies pressure, not an automatic
  duty; receipt, materiality, sufficiency of other retained evidence, authority,
  purpose, and horizon remain local.
* The helper is a **lawful explicit preservation utility** for an explicit live
  call and a **disconnected implementation island** in the current repository.
  No non-test supply or call makes its preservation responsibility active.
  Disconnection does not make the boundary constitutionally invalid.
* Its returned object truthfully supports, at most, **live caller-held,
  process-local carriage**. The responsible helper return is observer-held
  evidence that the live preservation act occurred; the artifact carries no
  preservation-purpose, horizon, occurrence, or durable recording standing.
* The schema is faithful only where the supplier is also, by separately known
  standing, every relevant producer, or where producer/source coordinates are
  genuinely inapplicable. It cannot faithfully retain a known distinct
  candidate producer, proposed-meaning producer, candidate source role,
  producer provenance/authority, or verified-versus-attributed formation
  standing. Putting the producer's name in `attributed_supplier` would relocate
  supplier responsibility; putting the carrier's name there would erase the
  producer.
* Consequently the Book-warranted responsibility is real, but **production
  schema expansion is not yet specified**: no current applicable invocation
  presents a topology whose material coordinates are lost. The smallest next
  responsibility is another bounded recovery at the first actual or proposed
  supply boundary, or, if callers must be accepted before then, narrowing the
  helper's accepted contract to topologies whose role co-location and immediate
  horizon are explicitly established. Preserve the code unchanged now; do not
  delete, wire, or expand it on anticipated architecture.

These conclusions keep separate:

```text
Book-warranted preservation != active preservation invocation
preservation warranted != current carrier complete
carrier incomplete for a topology != present warrant to expand it
no active caller != constitutionally invalid explicit boundary
live return != durable recording
```

## 1. Constitutional recovery

The controlling Book distinctions are:

1. Addressable external material keeps source role or Unknown, provenance or
   Unknown, scope, uncertainty, and authority limits without becoming truth.
2. A candidate keeps **each applicable** producer, source-role,
   formation-occurrence, scope, authority, and provenance dimension where
   known, and explicit Unknown where unresolved. Transport and re-presentation
   do not relocate production or establish formation.
3. Attribution identifies who supplied or produced an assertion; it does not
   collapse those two acts, warrant a meaning relation, or prove the proposition.
4. A constructor proves constructibility. A responsible witnessed return may
   add observer-held occurrence standing, but that is not durable unless
   represented or recorded.
5. A recording boundary may preserve an attributed assertion within the
   recorder's horizon without performing later examination, extraction,
   establishment, or reliance. Remembering is preservation for later lawful
   recovery, not that later recovery itself.
6. Seed need not retain everything. Non-rebuildable material is preserved when
   discarding it would erase materially sufficient evidence or understanding
   not otherwise retained; non-rebuildability alone is not a duty.
7. Dimensions become required under the exact subject, responsibility, purpose,
   horizon, evidence, and consumer where applicable. An absent field is not
   automatically a schema defect.

Thus a known later semantic consumer is one possible remembering reason, not a
universal prerequisite. An authorized receiver can own a narrower purpose:
lossless attributed carriage or prevention of material evidence erasure until a
declared stopping boundary. Conversely, a merely constructible test object,
unused helper, or hypothetical future candidate establishes no receipt and no
applicability.

### Required topology tests

**Same-owner.** If separately established P forms C and P supplies testimony,
the current object can copy P as `attributed_supplier`, the candidate fields,
and an attributed formation ref. It is lossless for the supplied representation
only if P's production/source/meaning-production standing is already external
to the artifact or irrelevant to the immediate purpose. It does not itself
preserve the assertion `supplier P = producer P`; therefore “complete” is
purpose-local, not intrinsic.

**Distinct supplier.** If P forms C and carrier S supplies it, the object can
name S or P but not both in their correct roles. Naming S loses P, producer
provenance/authority/source role, and producer/formation relation. Naming P as
supplier relocates S's supply responsibility. `supplier_provenance` cannot
truthfully substitute for producer provenance without labeling the relation.
This is lossy compression or responsibility relocation.

**Unknown producer.** S can be named and `supplied_unknowns` can contain prose
that the producer is Unknown. That is attributed supplier testimony, not an
independently addressable producer coordinate. It avoids inventing a producer,
but structurally compresses what is Unknown and who asserted it.

**Seed-produced.** A Seed responsibility P may form C while S transports it.
The carrier has no place to preserve both. Calling S the producer or P the
supplier would relabel responsibility; boundary notes correctly forbid this.

**Operator testimony.** An operator may supply and originate a proposed
meaning. The carrier can name the operator as supplier and carry the text, but
cannot say the operator is the proposed-meaning producer, distinguish candidate
formation from meaning proposal, or preserve the exact formation occurrence.

Therefore the strongest faithful topology is a bounded immediate call in which
only supplier attribution and lossless carriage of the supplied fields matter,
or all role co-location is separately established and need not be recovered
from the artifact. Known distinct roles are not faithfully preserved.

## 4. Recovery C — independent preservation applicability

| Condition | Result | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| testimony received at a responsible boundary | possible preservation pressure | receipt makes a real subject available, but decision still needs purpose/materiality/authority/horizon |
| testimony merely constructible | mere availability | constructor or import establishes no supply occurrence |
| candidate fields assembled by a test | test-only availability | proves shape behavior, not live responsibility |
| candidate supplied during a live responsible call | preservation applicability for that call when the call owns remembering/lossless carriage | exact receipt plus authorized purpose creates bounded duty |
| candidate produced internally | mixed | production may create evidence worth retaining, but production != preservation decision |
| candidate transported from another source | possible preservation pressure | attribution/producer/source evidence may be uniquely vulnerable to discard |
| candidate cannot be reconstructed | possible preservation pressure | non-rebuildable != preservation-required; materiality and other retained evidence decide |
| candidate can be reconstructed | mixed | convenient representation may be discarded, but original attribution/occurrence may still be non-rebuildable |
| discard erases attribution or production evidence | preservation applicability when material and not otherwise sufficiently retained | evidence-loss prevention is an independent remembering purpose |
| discard erases only convenient representation | normally no responsibility | rebuildable convenience alone supplies no constitutional necessity |

Receiving attributed testimony does **not universally** create a preservation
duty. It creates a bounded duty where (a) receipt is real and within the
receiver's authority; (b) testimony, attribution, producer/source/occurrence
standing, or declared loss/limits are material to a responsible remembering or
lossless-transport purpose; (c) discard would erase them beyond the acceptable
horizon because sufficient evidence/standing is not otherwise retained; and
(d) scope, conflicts, Unknowns, privacy/authority limits, and a stopping horizon
permit retention. It stops at the declared horizon or when sufficient lawful
standing is otherwise retained. Later examination and semantic consumption are
separate.

```text
testimony supply occurrence != preservation decision
preservation decision != preservation act
preservation act != durable recording
durable recording != later semantic consumption
non-rebuildable != preservation-required
no known consumer != permission to erase material evidence
```

No such live receipt currently occurs in repository production. The helper's
contract can lawfully embody the decision when an authorized caller invokes it
for immediate lossless carriage, but invocation alone does not disclose why the
decision was applicable.

## 6. Recovery E — `formation_occurrence_ref`

Current standing is **mixed**: it is an optional, unchecked, supplier-attributed
string intended to reference candidate formation; it can serve as optional
provenance, but the repository resolves it nowhere. It is neither verified
producer-occurrence evidence nor a transport occurrence. In present code it is
an implementation placeholder carrying attributed reference text.

```text
non-null reference != formation occurrence verified
null reference != formation did not occur
supplier witnessed formation != supplier produced candidate
candidate producer identified != formation occurrence preserved
artifact construction != candidate formation
```

* **Known and evidenced:** preserve producer, occurrence identity, evidence and
  resolver/binding, provenance, authority, source role, scope, conflicts and
  limits applicable to the horizon. The current carrier preserves only the ref.
* **Supplied only as attributed testimony:** preserve that S asserted ref F,
  without upgrading F. The current carrier can do this if read narrowly.
* **Unknown:** preserve producer/formation Unknown under its responsible
  examiner or supplier and boundary. `None` plus the repository's field-local
  absence finding says only no ref is represented.
* **Formation known but no durable reference:** preserve the bounded testimony
  that formation occurred, its evidence/observer/provenance and the explicit
  lack of durable identity. Current free-form supplier Unknown/scope fields can
  carry prose but no independently addressable standing.

The candidate producer owns candidate-formation standing; a competent observer
may own testimony about it. The supplier owns supply standing. The preservation
owner copies their claims and limits; none may inherit another's authority by
field adjacency.

## 9. Recovery H — smallest lawful next action

**Choose `preserve unchanged` now, followed by `perform another recovery` at the
earliest concrete supply boundary.** Do not delete, expand, or wire the carrier.
The helper's explicit immediate preservation purpose is constitutionally
lawful, evidence-loss prevention can independently warrant it, and absence of a
caller does not invalidate it. But no production invocation currently loses a
known producer/source coordinate, so expansion would be future design rather
than smallest implementation-backed repair.

The next recovery must establish whether one concrete boundary will actually
receive testimony; whether supplier and producer are known, distinct, or
Unknown; which source role, producer/proposed-meaning provenance and authority,
formation standing, purpose, horizon, and occurrence must survive; what other
retention makes evidence rebuildable; and where retention stops. If a caller
must be exposed before those coordinates can be represented, narrow accepted
testimony topology to explicitly established same-role/immediate-return cases
rather than silently compressing distinct roles.

If that recovery finds an applicable distinct-role preservation duty, schema
repair is warranted for the missing constitutional information: independently
addressable testimony supplier and supply occurrence; candidate and
proposed-meaning producer responsibility; candidate source role; producer
provenance and authority distinct from supplier coordinates; formation claim
and its evidenced/attributed/Unknown standing; and preservation purpose,
horizon, and occurrence to the extent the selected crossing requires them. This
report deliberately proposes no field or class design and no Event.

Deletion is not warranted: the Book independently permits responsible
preservation before a semantic consumer, the helper accurately limits its
authority, and inactive capability is not constitutional falsehood. Wiring is
not warranted: there is no actual supplier. Production work is not sufficiently
specified because the earliest unresolved relation is **an actual testimony
supply occurrence to a named preservation-purpose owner, with its role topology
and horizon**.
