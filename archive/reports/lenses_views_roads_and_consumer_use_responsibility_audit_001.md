## Direct answers

1. **Independently established responsibilities.** The file directly establishes bounded lens formation and faithful representation; responsible consumer-local evaluation, admission, use, road validation, reliance, refusal, Unknown preservation, and revision; View formation, bounded assertion, constitutive disclosure, temporal handling, honest compactness, and consumer-local uptake. It describes emission as separately evidenced presentation but leaves its production authority external.
2. **Lens consumption and production.** A lens consumes existing supplied material and produces a bounded representation of what those sources expose under its declared method. It may expose, select, project, or compose that representation; it neither changes source standing nor manufactures absent provenance, authority, occurrence, or standing.
3. **Lens formation, selection, composition, and emission.** Formation, including representation-bounded selection/projection/composition, is one locally described lens responsibility. Emission is separate and separately evidenced. Subject-standing selection and the authority to produce or emit are not assigned here, so the file does not collapse all four into one responsibility.
4. **View and bounded assertion.** A View is a bounded representation formed from source material under a declared method for a consumer purpose. Its assertion is established by the warranted combination of constitutional subject, input standing, method, emitted assertion, consumer purpose, disclosure contract, and reliance authority—not by name, input kind, or shape.
5. **Constitutional road.** It is the assertion-preserving uptake subfamily in which a consumer accepts a producer assertion for a declared purpose and validates the identities and invariants necessary at that boundary.
6. **Road-standing producer.** The responsible consumer produces the consumer- and purpose-relative road standing through its acceptance and validation. Upstream production authority remains an external dependency; producer warrant does not itself produce the consumer's road standing.
7. **Preconditions for responsible use.** The consumer must possess prior standing for its bounded use. Upstream material and preserved evidence, warrant, identity, scope, authority, confidence, conflicts, Unknowns, temporal limits, applicability/admission constraints where relevant, and other exact requirements must satisfy the consumer's declared act and purpose.
8. **Consumer-local acts.** The file establishes evaluation at an applicability boundary; admission judgment; bounded use; acceptance, narrowing, refusal, and locally evidenced strengthening; validation; reliance; projection, request formation, testimony preservation, composition, explanation construction, establishment; Unknown preservation; and occurrence-backed revision. Coherence checks are constraints, not acts.
9. **Distinct standings.** Availability, applicability, admission, consumption, uptake, reliance, and revision/standing change remain distinct. The file expressly rejects a universal order. Applicability has applicable/inapplicable/Unknown/conflicting values; admission has admitted/unadmitted/Unknown/conflicting values. Consumption does not follow admission automatically, uptake is a relation family, reliance is consumer- and purpose-local, and revision requires a responsible occurrence.
10. **Relations and negative comparisons.** Established relations include lens-to-sources representation, consumer-to-upstream applicability/admission/use, assertion-preserving producer-assertion-to-consumer road, purpose-bounded reliance, and non-transfer among consumers. The `!=` list only denies equivalences; it does not positively establish “general selection standing,” “selection purpose,” “compressed transfer wording,” or any universal typed-value uptake relation.
11. **Representation clauses.** LVR-01–02, LVR-07's request-formation portion, LVR-15, LVR-21, LVR-24–29 address formation, content, disclosure, compactness, navigation, projection, or presentation of representations rather than automatically establishing represented-source or consumer standing.
12. **Implementation testimony.** The representative repository anchors, pipeline call-order example, and descriptions of the current `ObservationView` and `FactView` public shapes are implementation testimony. Their negative constitutional lessons are stated in the Book, but their current fields/classes are not timeless definitions.
13. **Vocabulary hooks or unsupported compounds.** “Selection purpose,” “general selection standing,” “compressed transfer wording,” and “typed values transported” receive no positive grammar from negative comparisons. “ObservationView,” “FactView,” and “current-facing Fact View” cannot acquire standing from names. “Constitutional uptake” is bounded as a relation family, not a universal stage. No replacement nouns are proposed.
14. **External production or selection dependencies.** Subject selection, selection standing, formation/constructor authority, producer-side production authority, and emission authority are explicitly preserved as external dependencies. None was resolved from an adjacent file.
15. **Possible later Responsibility heads.** Without restructuring now, the file could support heads for **Lens formation and bounded representation**, **Consumer-local use and road validation**, and **View formation, assertion, and disclosure**. Emission may not become a head from this file because its responsible producer and authority remain external. Applicability and admission are subordinate consumer-local branches, not automatically universal heads.

## Responsibility-head preparation

### Direct responsibilities, branches, relations, and results

- **Lens formation and bounded representation:** consumes supplied material; exposes/selects/projects/composes only a bounded representation; preserves source standing; warrants only faithful method-bounded exposure; may distinguish but not fill absence. Its result is a bounded representation.
- **Consumer-local use and road validation:** evaluates applicability, may judge admission, validates road identities/invariants, accepts/narrows/refuses material, preserves Unknown, relies under purpose, performs bounded use, and revises only by an evidenced occurrence. The results remain consumer-, subject-, purpose-, scope-, and limit-relative.
- **View formation, assertion, and disclosure:** forms a purpose-bound representation, asserts only contract-bounded standing, handles temporal evidence, discloses constitutive limits, and either preserves necessary distinctions or refuses stronger reliance. Its results range from navigation/testimony representations to standing-bearing assertions only when their full coordinates warrant them.
- Relations connect sources to a lens output, upstream assertions to a road consumer, material to consumer-local applicability/admission/use, and emitted assertions to purpose-bounded reliance. No relation transfers standing automatically.
- Consumer dependencies include prior act standing, exact material and evidence fit, applicable/admitted status where the act requires it, disclosed assertion limits, and consumer-local authority.

### Clauses that are not Responsibility heads

- The constitutional subject and core question are navigation.
- The `!=` list and counterexamples are distinctions and negative authority.
- Repository anchors and current-class descriptions are implementation illustrations.
- Coordinates such as purpose, scope, confidence, conflict, time, identity, applicability, and admission are not heads.
- Standings or result values—available, applicable, admitted, consumed, relied upon, Unknown, conflicting, revised—are not heads.
- “Producer,” “consumer,” and “participant” positions do not become heads without an assigned responsible act.
- A road relation is not a head apart from the consumer act that establishes its local standing.
- Negative vocabulary, class names, artifact shapes, and ordinary nouns do not create heads.
- Emission remains an unresolved external responsibility assignment in this boundary and is rejected as a locally complete head.

## Disposition

**F — unresolved external dependencies prevent disposition.** The consumer-side and representation boundaries are substantially explicit, but this file deliberately relies on out-of-scope selection, production/formation authority, and emission authority to determine whether particular representations or presentations are lawfully produced. Those dependencies prevent a complete responsibility assignment within this audit boundary; they are preserved rather than filled from adjacent material.

**Recommended follow-up:** None. The required dependencies are recorded for their independently governed audits; this report does not recommend a combined recovery.
