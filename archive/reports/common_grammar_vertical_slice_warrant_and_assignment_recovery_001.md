## Result and recovery boundary

This is one bounded, report-only recovery for the exact common-grammar scenario in
the request. Current Book text is authority; current production is implementation
evidence; tests, prior reports, deleted code, and surveys are testimony only. The
displayed scenario is therefore a set of claims to cross-examine, not a recovered
pipeline, universal ordering, fixed occurrence count, or requirement that every
claim have a separate artifact or occurrence.

**Disposition:** **the slice requires one or more explicit constitutional assignments
before implementation**.

The earliest production truth is a recorded ingress occurrence and its exact,
addressable operator material. The first missing occurrence is a communicative,
common-grammar-dependent interpretation examination by an identified responsible
consumer. The Book describes that consumer conditionally but does not assign its
exact owner, act, or purpose. Those coordinates are load-bearing: without them,
neither failure identity nor recurrence can be established. Farther downstream,
the exact Demand examiner, Demand establisher, family, established-Demand consumer,
translation owner and applicability location, standing-establishment owner,
Question-forming district, and examination consumer are likewise unresolved.

Accordingly, co-locating producers and consumers would not cure the absence of
warrant. One file is not one lawful occurrence; fewer artifacts are not less
cardboard; and vertical integration is not constitutional assignment. Every later
claim must trace either to an actual observed occurrence or to previously
established, applicable, warranted standing.

## Controlling constitutional findings

1. The common-grammar counterexample establishes a conditional relation: a
   responsible consumer **may** repeatedly fail or stop while attempting to
   examine the same preserved ingress because the common-grammar standing needed
   by that consumer for its declared act and purpose is not established.
   Recurrence is measurement evidence only. No number of attempts is fixed.
2. Such evidence **may become applicable** to a separately responsible Demand
   examination. The Book does not make applicability automatic or name the exact
   examiner. Examination and Demand establishment are distinct claims;
   establishment requires its own responsible occurrence and preservation of the
   required result, responsibility, scope, source evidence, materiality,
   applicability, Unknowns, and conflicts.
3. If established, this is a bounded relational Demand. Its exact family is
   expressly **Unknown**. Neither `inquiry` nor `clarification` follows from the
   desired downstream use or the missing grammar.
4. An established Demand is bounded required-result standing only. It does not
   establish movement, material applicability, translation authority, inquiry,
   a Question, a method, or examination.
5. For qualifying operator-originated inquiry, bounded translation is
   Book-established and must preserve identity, source, scope, evidence demand,
   authority limit, uncertainty, and lawful stop. Its exact owner and its input
   applicability/warrant location remain Unknown.
6. Translated material standing, bounded standing sufficient for Question
   formation, Seed-owned Question formation, and a bounded Question are distinct
   claims. The Book establishes Seed ownership generally, not an exact module,
   service, competency, object, district, or occurrence.
7. A bounded Question constrains evidence demand and stopping but does not select
   an examination, supply evidence, establish method applicability, or prove that
   examination occurred. Question applicability, method/lens applicability, and
   evidence warrant remain separate.
8. Refusal, Unknown, conflict, insufficient evidence, unavailable authority or
   competency, and a bounded stop can be lawful results when produced by a
   competent responsibility within scope. They are not failure to implement or a
   claim of global impossibility.

The evidentiary ladder remains non-collapsing:

```text
evidence reference present
!= referenced evidence resolved
!= evidence applicable to this claim
!= claim established
!= movement opened
!= act authorized
!= act occurred
```
