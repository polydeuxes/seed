# Bounded selection-testimony production recovery 001

## Mandate, scope, and disposition

This is one report-only constitutional recovery. It begins after PR 2188 and asks
only whether this crossing is warranted:

```text
captured response R
+ exact emitted presentation C
+ established token-to-alternative relation T-to-A
+ comparison finding R equals T
+ identification finding A corresponds to R

-> bounded selection testimony
```

It does not reopen formation, source recovery, meaning, applicability, admission,
BOGE, movement, or stopping. It preserves PR 2188's comparison/identification split
and corrects that report's unsupported purpose attribution concerning the
operator's material. The independently preserved
external fact is only that the operator produced response material R after C was
emitted.

**Principal disposition: A — identification is sufficient.**

The first current consumer, represented-source recovery, needs the identity of the
presented alternative corresponding to R and preserved representation lineage. It
does not independently demand testimony that a psychological or prior operator
selection occurred. The established T-to-A relation operates as a bounded
constitutive convention for *identification*: within C, R-equals-T plus T-to-A
warrants A-corresponds-to-R. It does not, without another warranted premise,
constitute operator receipt, comprehension, intention, assent, or an independently
performed selection act. The active phrase `selection testimony` therefore adds no
independently demanded proposition at this crossing. Its narrowest usable content is
the identification result itself: **presented alternative A corresponds to captured
response R within exact emitted presentation C**.

This result is established by a responsible Seed identification act, not supplied
as constitutional testimony by the external operator occurrence. Comparison is its
evidence, not its producer. Comparison and identification remain distinct acts and
may share one responsible occurrence. No current rule requires or forbids that same
occurrence also to preserve the identification result as testimony; because no
additional claim is needed, no separate testimony-production responsibility is
warranted. The exact result kind beyond the identification finding remains
**Unknown**.

## Fixed topology and the one unresolved crossing

The recovered local topology is:

```text
representation formation: exact bounded presentation C exists
exact-set participation: presented alternative A participates in C
presentation-coordinate relation: T corresponds to A within C
emission: C is emitted
external occurrence: operator produces response material R after emission
capture: Seed preserves R and occurrence lineage
comparison: R equals T, or no exact coordinate of C matches
identification: R equals T + T corresponds to A -> A corresponds to R
```

The T-to-A relation exists before capture and is consumed by identification, not
created by comparison. Comparison and identification are distinct acts. They may
share a responsible occurrence without becoming one act. If R equals T but T-to-A
is absent, unsupported, conflicting, inapplicable, or **Unknown**, A is not lawfully
identified from R. The report does not infer T-to-A from equality.

The only question is whether A-corresponds-to-R must be crossed into something more.
The first consumer independently declares that it recovers represented-source
identity “through preserved lineage.” The identification of A plus that lineage is
sufficient. It does not demand the proposition “the operator selected A.”

## Investigation 2 — the status of T-to-A

1. **Producer.** A responsible representation-coordinate establishment boundary
   establishes T-to-A. The active Book assigns representation formation and
   preservation but does not recover a more exact universal producer name.
2. **Purpose and scope.** It identifies A by T only within exact presentation C,
   its local selection purpose, participants, evidence, producer occurrence, and
   surviving **Unknowns**.
3. **Metadata.** It may be preserved as representation metadata in a realization,
   but a field or array is not standing. The constitutional relation must be
   warranted for identification.
4. **Warrant.** Yes, the premise fixed by this report is an *established* T-to-A
   relation. If support is absent, conflicting, inapplicable, or **Unknown**,
   identification fails even when R equals T.
5. **Emission.** Emission makes C and its exposed coordinates available toward an
   operator boundary. Active emission law expressly says emission does not establish
   delivery or receipt.
6. **Receipt/comprehension.** Neither is established or needed for the narrow Seed
   identification finding. Either would require separate evidence if a later claim
   required it.
7. **Exact equality.** R-equals-T permits Seed to identify A as the response-
   corresponding presented alternative. It does not by itself establish an operator
   selection act.
8. **Constitutive character.** T-to-A is constitutive for this bounded coordinate
   identification: given the exact applicable relation and equality, A is the
   alternative corresponding to R. This resembles active conventions that warrant a
   bounded relation under declared source, method, purpose, scope, provenance,
   conflicts, and **Unknowns**. It differs from a meaning convention: it fixes a
   coordinate identity inside C and does not warrant what A, its label, or source
   means. It also differs from an authority convention: it grants no permission.
9. **Applicability/admission.** No separate general applicability or admission act
   is demanded for using C's own established coordinate relation in C's declared
   identification purpose. Reuse by another consumer or purpose would require its
   own lawful uptake; this report does not erase later meaning-relation applicability
   and admission.
10. **Producer and consumer.** The exact universal producer name remains **Unknown**;
    the local consumer is the Seed identification act. The represented-source
    recovery act then consumes identified A and representation lineage, not T-to-A
    as a free-standing meaning premise.

Thus emission makes the convention usable at Seed's bounded identification
boundary when emission and response lineage are preserved. It does not prove that
the operator received or applied the convention. The phrase “constitutive
convention” is justified only for the response-coordinate identification, not for
operator intent, operator selection, candidate meaning, or downstream treatment.

## Investigation 4 — applying general testimony grammar

General testimony grammar separates attributed assertion, producer occurrence,
evidence, preservation, reliance, and established fact. A report or artifact does
not prove its act, and external testimony does not become Seed-established fact by
identity. Applied here:

* **Producer:** the Seed identification act produces the bounded identification
  finding. The exact producer of any stronger operator-selected-A testimony remains
  **Unknown**.
* **Subject:** the relation among captured R, identified A, and exact C—not the
  operator's mental state, A's source meaning, or an independently proven operator
  act.
* **Evidence:** emitted C and its lineage, captured R and occurrence lineage,
  established T-to-A, and the distinct R-equals-T comparison finding.
* **Claim:** A corresponds to R within C.
* **Attribution:** R remains operator-origin material; comparison and identification
  remain Seed-produced findings. Seed does not attribute its finding to the operator.
* **Scope:** one exact presentation, coordinate relation, captured response,
  interaction lineage, purpose, producer occurrence, conflicts, and **Unknowns**.
* **Occurrence evidence:** competent capture, comparison occurrence, and
  identification occurrence; artifact construction alone is insufficient.
* **Standing:** the identification result is a Seed-established bounded relation
  under its evidence. The proposition that the operator intended or selected A is
  not thereby established or preserved as attributed operator testimony.
* **Consumer:** represented-source recovery may consume identified A together with
  representation lineage.

## Investigation 5 — identification already produces enough

Yes. The first downstream step is represented-source identity recovery “through
preserved representation lineage.” Its required discriminant is which presented
alternative corresponds to R. Once A is identified, lineage recovers A's represented
source. The consumer does not independently declare a need for operator psychology,
a prior selection act, or even the adjective `operator-selected`.

Accordingly, at this crossing `selection testimony` is **redundant vocabulary** if
it says no more than identification and is an unsupported stronger claim if it says
that a prior operator selection act occurred. It is not evidence for identification,
because that would make the road cyclic. It is not source identity, meaning warrant,
applicability, or admission. No source-recovery rule asks for psychological selection.

* operator intent, understanding, assent, authorization, or an independently
  evidenced operator selection occurrence;
* candidate meaning, rendered-label meaning, represented-source meaning, or source
  identity without preserved lineage;

  permanent common grammar.

Each exclusion materially prevents the coordinate finding from being consumed as a
different constitutional subject. Source identity may be recovered next only through

* Not every selection grammar requires psychological intent; deterministic Seed

* `selected item` is not always the result of the same kind of internal choice, but
  the general chapter does not license relabeling identity resolution as selection.
* Operator-origin material and Seed-origin findings require different attribution.

No owner, act, or authority is transferred by analogy, and no universal bounded-
selection responsibility is introduced.
