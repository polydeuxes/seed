# Relation-Assertion Occupancy and Responsibility Investigation 001

Findings only. This report amends neither runtime nor the active Book.

## Determination

`the relation assertion` is an established coordinate name whose physiology is
not established.

Active law establishes that a relation is a bounded claim subject and that a
relation claim or Standing must preserve each applicable constitutional
dimension, or its explicit Unknown or unresolved standing, including `the
relation assertion`. It also keeps candidate relation, relation testimony,
evidence-supported relation Standing, and established relation Standing
distinct (`01.Standing.E`, lines 27-32).

Active law does not establish:

- what kind of content `the relation assertion` accepts;
- that a Representation may occupy it;
- that a source token may be selected as its occupant;
- which responsible occurrence places an occupant there; or
- what Standing follows merely from occupancy.

The exact clause boundary is between `01.Standing.E`, which establishes the
coordinate and its separations, and `06.Bounded Representations`, lines 12-28,
which establishes exact source-derived Representation formation without
connecting a formed Representation to the relation-assertion coordinate.

## 1. What active law establishes

For a general relation, `the relation assertion` stands alongside participants
and roles, evidence Standing, scope, producer, consumer and purpose, authority,
occurrence, conflicts, and limits. For a meaning relation it additionally
stands alongside source and attribution, provenance, known loss, and Unknown or
unresolved Standing.

This establishes:

1. the coordinate is real and already named;
2. it is not participant identity or participant role; and
3. it is not Evidence, Warrant, Standing, source, attribution, producer,
   occurrence, or scope.

The macro-dimensional `assertion / content` family locates the concern but does
not define the coordinate's occupants. Active law says that macro families are
not mandatory fields or a universal schema, and distinguishes a coordinate
from its answer or value (`01.Standing.F`, lines 51-65).

The coordinate name does not establish that its occupant is a verb, operator,
Predicate, relation kind, semantic proposition, canonical name, source token,
Representation, or normalized implementation value. Names, fields, schemas,
inventories, and recurring forms do not supply constitutional Standing or
kind-specific production authority (`01.Standing.B-C`, lines 18-22).

## 2. What may lawfully occupy it

### Present law

No positive occupant class is licensed.

Active law independently licenses exact source material, a bounded
Representation formed from exact source material, an attributed translated
Representation, an assertion, a claim proposal, a relation proposal,
attributed testimony, and evidence-supported or established relation Standing.
It does not state that any of these is the content of `the relation assertion`.

`01.External.A`, line 15, permits a bounded translation to state or form an
attributed Representation, term, assertion, claim proposal, or relation
proposal while preserving source boundaries and without establishing meaning,
truth, Evidence, Fact, or relation Warrant. The clause presents separate
possible translated results; it does not assign a Representation to the
relation-assertion coordinate.

`06.Bounded Representations`, lines 12-30, permits a responsible occurrence to
form a bounded Representation from exact source material without strengthening
the source's Standing. It does not connect that Representation to relation
grammar.

Therefore an exact source-derived Representation is a constitutionally
available thing that active law could permit as an occupant without
establishing semantic meaning, but active law does not yet positively license
that occupancy. This is not a prohibition. It is an unestablished relation
between two lawful forms.

### Stress test

No segmentation below may be inferred from syntax, infix position, adjacency,
typography, or ordinary English.

| Source material | Lawful now | Not established now |
|---|---|---|
| `A noun is a word` | Preserve the exact source; form an exact bounded Representation; form attributed translated material or a relation proposal through a responsible translation boundary. | That `A noun` and `a word` are participants; that `is` occupies relation assertion; its meaning; class membership; truth. |
| `X causes Y` | Preserve or represent the exact string; carry attributed source testimony; form a source-bounded relation proposal. | That `X` and `Y` denote participants; that `causes` occupies relation assertion; causal meaning; actual causation; truth. |
| `2 + 2 = 4` | Preserve or represent the exact expression. | Its parse; whether `=` occupies relation assertion; whether the surrounding expressions are participants; arithmetic meaning; equality; truth. |
| `F = ma` | Preserve or represent the exact source form. | Whether `=` occupies relation assertion; implicit multiplication; symbol denotation; physical-law meaning; empirical or mathematical Warrant. |
| `x < y` | Preserve or represent the exact source form. | Whether `<` occupies relation assertion; participant segmentation; ordering domain; denotation; comparison semantics; truth. |

The candidate placements `is`, `causes`, `=`, `=`, and `<` become lawful only
if the missing occupancy bridge is established and the responsible
source/translation boundary supplies that exact segmentation. The tokens do not
qualify because they appear between other material. `01.External.E`, line 28,
denies that adjacency, equality, a declared predicate result, or recurrence
establishes grammatical or semantic meaning beyond the exact measurement
assertion.

Even after the narrow bridge, common occupancy would establish no semantic
equivalence among the tokens, no universal binary relation grammar, no rule
that infix tokens occupy relation assertion, and no truth.

## 3. Responsible Act or occurrence

No currently named responsibility universally owns “populate the
relation-assertion coordinate.” The nearby responsibilities remain distinct.

### Preserving source material

Preservation retains source material and its limits. It may make exact text
recoverable without selecting participants, proposing a relation, or assigning
a Representation to a constitutional coordinate. An artifact may carry an
assertion made by another responsibility; its shape does not supply Warrant
(`01.Standing`, line 10).

### Forming a Representation

A responsible occurrence may form a bounded Representation from exact source
material for a declared purpose. Formation establishes only responsible
formation within that occurrence's purpose, Scope, Authority, and Evidence.
Whether Representation formation is an Act distinct from the exact Act that
forms it remains Unknown (`06.Bounded Representations`, lines 12-28).

Formation does not presently establish relation-assertion occupancy.

### Forming a relation proposal

A bounded external-source translation may form an attributed relation proposal
with its source boundary and limits. Translation does not establish
interpretation, meaning-relation Warrant, truth, Evidence, Fact, applicability,
admission, or reliance (`01.External.A`, line 15).

This produces proposal Standing, not a stated connection between one of its
Representations and `the relation assertion`.

### Carrying attributed testimony

Attributed testimony preserves that a source carried a claim, within
source-relative limits. Consumption as testimony does not establish the
testified content as Fact, prove its producer occurrence, or strengthen it by
repetition (`05.Testimony.A`, lines 23-24). Attribution identifies a supplier or
producer; it is neither meaning nor meaning-relation Warrant
(`01.Standing.E`, lines 30-32).

### Compare

A bounded comparison may consume preserved testimonies or findings and produce
relation Standing local to the comparison, such as agreement, disagreement,
contradiction, conflict, refinement, unmet requirements, or Unknown. Comparison
does not establish truth, Warrant, reliance, reconciliation, source
independence, or implementation permission (`05.Testimony.E`, lines 26-29).

Compare may produce its own bounded relation finding. It is not the universal
source relation-assertion population responsibility.

### Establishing relation Standing

A responsible occurrence warrants only the relation supported by
claim-appropriate Authority, Evidence, Scope, provenance, conflicts, loss, and
Unknowns. That Warrant establishes neither proposition truth nor applicability,
admission, act occurrence, or completion (`01.Standing.E`, lines 30-34).

Relation-Standing establishment must not retroactively absorb source
preservation, Representation formation, relation-proposal formation, attributed
testimony, or Compare.

### Smallest responsible owner after a bridge

The smallest owner would be the bounded occurrence already responsible for
forming a source-derived relation proposal, provided it also responsibly forms
or consumes the exact source-derived Representation and preserves source
identity, segmentation authority, purpose, Scope, provenance, known loss,
conflicts, and Unknowns.

One occurrence could perform both formations, but two claims would remain
independently recoverable:

1. the Representation was faithfully formed from exact source material; and
2. the Representation occupies the relation-assertion coordinate of this exact
   bounded relation proposal.

Act law requires the occurrence and result to be warranted by a responsible
validated production boundary; artifact construction is not enough
(`02.Acts`, lines 3-10).

## 4. Standing from occupancy alone

Occupancy alone establishes no Standing. Active law distinguishes coordinate,
coordinate value, Representation, artifact shape, candidate relation, relation
testimony, evidence-supported relation, and established relation.

Because current law does not license Representation occupancy, no positive
constitutional Standing can presently be assigned from the coordinate name. If
the narrow bridge were added:

| Proposed Standing | Follows from occupancy alone? | Additional boundary required |
|---|---|---|
| exact source carriage | No. It may follow from exact source identity and a faithful Representation-formation occurrence. | Source, provenance, faithful transformation, purpose, Scope, Authority, Evidence, and limits. |
| candidate relation | No. An occupant may be part of a candidate relation. | Responsible candidate or relation-proposal production with its formation coordinates. |
| attributed testimony | No. Attribution is independent. | Responsible testimony production or preservation with attribution. |
| evidence-supported relation | No. Occupancy is carriage, not support. | Claim-appropriate Evidence and a responsible relation-standing occurrence. |
| established relation | No. Representation, position, source syntax, and testimony establish none of these. | Kind-appropriate establishment with Authority, Evidence, Scope, provenance, conflicts, limits, Unknowns, and occurrence. |

Even exact equal content does not transfer Standing. `01.External.A`, line 15,
says equal proposition text does not confer equal Standing, equivalent relation
content does not make occurrences identical, and material carrying relation
content does not warrant the relation.

Under the bridge, occupancy would establish at most: this exact source-derived
Representation occupies this exact relation proposal's relation-assertion
coordinate, within the exact responsible occurrence and its preserved limits.
It would establish no semantic meaning or truth.

## 5. Implementation testimony

All implementation shapes are testimony only.

### `subject / predicate / value`

`05.Testimony`, lines 10-14, places `subject, predicate, value` in normalization
of an already interpreted Claim. Normalization makes that Claim comparable and
projectable but does not create a constitutional subject, prove the Claim, or
supply support.

The vocabulary testifies that implementations benefit from a compact middle
claim term. It does not establish that `predicate` equals `the relation
assertion`, that `value` is a participant, that every Claim triple is a bounded
relation, or that normalized predicate content may occupy relation assertion.
No active clause joins claim-triple grammar to relation grammar.

### Predicate machinery

`PredicateDefinition` and `PredicateCatalog` provide a developer-authored
canonical vocabulary with kind, value type, cardinality, allowed values,
mappings, validation, and lookup. `PredicateNormalizer` retains original fields
in metadata and emits a derived Observation using a canonical predicate and
possibly mapped value.

This testifies to the usefulness of stable labels and separating raw from
derived forms. It does not establish catalog semantic Warrant, identify a
canonical predicate with relation assertion, make normalization a
relation-establishment act, or require source normalization for occupancy.

`RelationshipDefinition` separately maps developer-authored relationship names
and kinds to fact predicates. This testifies to practical translation from
claim-shaped material into topology-shaped relations, not to constitutional
identity, meaning, or Warrant.

### Historical `relation_assertion`

The current tests list `relation_assertion` among deleted operator-ingress
payload coordinates (`tests/test_operator_ingress.py`, lines 58-86). Its former
presence testifies to representational usefulness. Its deletion testifies that
the payload was not a recovered production boundary. Neither event establishes
its occupant or owner.

> source material may preserve an exact source-derived Representation as the

This is the smallest amendment because it:

- does not introduce Predicate;

- does not define allowable values;
- says “may,” not “must”;
- does not require binary or textual relations;
- does not infer participants or segmentation from syntax;
- does not normalize source content;
- uses an already lawful bounded relation-proposal formation occurrence; and
- gives occupancy only source-faithful proposal-carriage Standing.

No broader amendment is warranted by this investigation.

## Closing findings

### what may occupy relation assertion

Active law establishes no positive occupant type. The narrow missing bridge
would permit an exact source-derived bounded Representation to occupy it as the
content of a bounded relation proposal when the responsible occurrence supplies
the exact source boundary, segmentation authority, purpose, Scope, Authority,
Evidence, provenance, conflicts, known loss, limits, and Unknowns.

`is`, `causes`, `=`, and `<` are possible exact occupants only under that
responsible boundary, not because of position, syntax, adjacency, or ordinary
meaning.

### who/what puts it there

No current universal owner is established. The smallest owner would be the
bounded occurrence that forms the exact source-derived relation proposal and
responsibly forms or consumes the exact Representation. Preservation,
Representation formation, relation-proposal formation, attributed testimony,
Compare, and relation-Standing establishment remain distinct.

### what Standing occupancy establishes

Occupancy alone establishes none. Under the narrow amendment, responsibly
produced occupancy would establish only exact source-faithful carriage in the
exact relation proposal, by the exact occurrence, within its preserved
boundaries. It would establish no semantics, truth, Evidence support, Warrant,
or established relation Standing.

### what remains Unknown

- permitted occupant types beyond exact source-derived Representation;
- whether non-source-derived Representations may occupy the coordinate;
- whether every relation proposal needs a represented occupant rather than
  Unknown or unresolved standing;
- any universal serialization;
- source-specific segmentation authority for the five stress tests;
- any shared constitutional kind among `+`, `=`, `<`, `is`, and `causes`;
- whether a current runtime producer preserves enough source and formation
  evidence to exercise the bridge;
- whether Representation formation is a distinct Act in each case; and
- every semantic meaning, denotation, truth, and Warrant in the tested strings.
