# Operator-ingress common-grammar clause disposition 001

## Mandate, vocabulary, and governing answer

This report is constitutional recovery testimony only. It changes no active Book
text. Its governing question concerns whether the active phrase
`communicative interpretation examination` names one independently warranted act
and responsibility. The answer is **no**: active wording names and structurally
describes a compound responsibility, but neither its act identity nor its owner was
independently warranted before assignment. The section carries distinct exact acts,
possible results, cross-cutting constraints, negative authorities, and possible
later responses under that compound wording. Some residue remains valid without
the assignment.

**Principal disposition: E — delete assignment and preserve bounded residue.**

The later Book change must remove the compound responsibility and act assignment,
including the unsupported ordinal `first`. It must preserve or place under exact
lawful owners the independently warranted applicability distinction, mechanical
comparison boundaries, attribution boundary, result-coordinate constraints, and
negative authorities. It must leave interpretation-relation and common-grammar-
relation producers, refusal ownership, and the relevant bounded-stop producer
**Unknown** unless another active clause independently assigns them.
