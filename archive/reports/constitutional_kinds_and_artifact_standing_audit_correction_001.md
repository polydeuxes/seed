# Constitutional Kinds and Artifact Standing Audit Correction 001

## Correction boundary and authority

This report corrects the findings and final disposition of
`constitutional_kinds_and_artifact_standing_audit_001.md`. It inspects that
report and
`book_of_seed/01-grammar-and-standing/constitutional-kinds-and-artifact-standing.md`
as its two subjects. Other active Book files were consulted only to test whether
an exact phrase already has independently established grammar. They are not
additional audit subjects.

Authority is applied in this order: active Book text is constitutional
authority; runtime and tests are implementation testimony only; historical
reports, including the PR 2202 report, are attributed testimony only; Git
history is provenance testimony only. Recurrence, adjacency, names, headings,
grammatical noun position, and plausibility are not authority. No conclusion in
an earlier report warrants itself.

This correction keeps constitutional subjects, participants, acts, relations,
standings, bounded results, coordinates, ordinary language, explanatory
shorthand, negative authority, unsupported constitutional-looking phrases, and
**Unknown** separate. A phrase can occupy more than one grammatical position,
but that does not turn every position into constitutional subject standing.

`Input-set sufficiency` and `act-permission standing` are therefore the risky
negative compounds: each carries contamination into a useful denial. The useful
distinctions do not authorize those compounds as positive constitutional
categories. `Act permission` is ordinary language needed to say that candidate
exclusion does not decide permission. `Readiness` and `non-performance` have
independent active Book grammar. `Act performance`, `successful execution`, and
`recommended performance` are necessary ordinary-English descriptions bounded
by established act/relation grammar; none is promoted to a new standing or
kind. No examined phrase requires an **Unknown** classification at this level;
positive standing for negatively bounded compounds remains **Unknown** unless
separately established.

## Correction 4 — redundancy and density in the six-denial sentence

| Denial | Distinct collapse blocked | Authority/value assessment | Preserve distinction if wording changes? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| applicability success is not act performance | applicability result becoming act occurrence | `applicability success` is not independently established and is ambiguous beside the exact `applicable` result; the next denial states the bounded case | Yes, but this exact denial is contaminated and materially duplicative |
| one input applicable is not act performance | one candidate's positive result deciding whole-act occurrence | authorized applicability and ordinary act language; more exact than the preceding denial | Yes |
| one input excluded is neither act prohibited nor act permitted | one candidate's exclusion deciding permission/prohibition for the whole act | authorized result plus necessary ordinary authority language | Yes |
| act performance is not output standing automatically established | occurrence/result production becoming warranted output standing | established act and standing boundaries; awkward density does not remove value | Yes |
| output standing is not downstream applicability | warranted output becoming suitable for a later exact act | established standings and act-local applicability | Yes |
| downstream applicability is not downstream admission | suitability becoming consumer admission | established consumer-local boundaries | Yes |

Only the first denial is materially duplicative within this sentence: it is a
less exact version of `one input applicable is not act performance`, and it uses
the unauthorized shorthand `applicability success`. The other five block
distinct plausible collapses. The last denial restates a distinction expressed
earlier, but it is not true-but-valueless: in this compressed same-occurrence
chain it prevents a separate downstream-boundary collapse. Likewise, repeated
act-performance/output-standing separation earns its place because the clause
expressly permits one occurrence to perform several responsibilities. Dense
prose alone is not a defect.

The distinctions that must survive any later wording change are: an
applicability result does not prove act occurrence; one input's inclusion or
exclusion does not decide whole-act permission, prohibition, or performance;
act occurrence does not establish output standing; output standing does not
establish later applicability; and later applicability does not establish
admission. This report does not draft replacement prose.

## Direct answers

1. **Which members have active-Book grammar?** `Lens`, `transport structure`, and
   `relationship mode`. The first has bounded method/output grammar, the second
   has bounded possible-transport grammar, and the third has negative authority
   distinguishing it from consumer act.
2. **Does `standing effect` remain unsupported?** Yes. It is the only member of
   the original cluster without independently recovered exact active grammar.
3. **Which Direct Answer 1 entries are subjects?** `Artifact` and `assertion` are
   subjects in their own grammar. A `relation` is expressly its own bounded claim
   subject while still being a relation. Concrete X or Y may be subjects, but the
   phrases `subject X` and `subject or proposition Y` name participant roles.
4. **Which are participants, relations, standings, coordinates, or results?** X,
   Y, and proposed input are participants; relation, relation claim, meaning,
   meaning relation, warrant, road, attribution, input-to-act relation,
   production/establishment boundary, and reachability are relations or
   conditions; standing, constitutional Fact standing, durable-kind standing,
   and applicability standing are standings; local coordinates are coordinates;
   applicability standing and bounded Unknown are also bounded results, and
   **Unknown** is a result/standing value. The detailed table controls where a
   phrase has overlapping roles.
5. **Which negative phrases use independently established grammar?** The
   relation-evidence/negation boundary; meaning/attribution/warrant boundaries;
   applicability/admission/consumption boundaries; act-local applicability;
   readiness; non-performance; output standing; downstream applicability and
   admission; representation/kind and construction/standing boundaries; and
   persistence/current-demand boundary. Necessary ordinary English supplies
   `act permission`, `act performance`, `successful execution`, and
   `recommended performance` without establishing new kinds.
6. **Which preserve value but use contamination?** `Input-set sufficiency`,
   `act-permission standing`, and `applicability success`. Their denials protect
   valid input-set, authority, and occurrence boundaries, but the compounds have
   no independent positive grammar.
7. **Which are redundant or true-but-valueless?** `Applicability success is not
   act performance` is materially duplicative of the more exact next denial and
   uses ambiguous unauthorized shorthand. No other examined denial is proven
   true-but-valueless; repeated consumer-boundary statements remain useful in
   their local chains.
8. **Which distinctions must remain?** Applicability is act-local; one input's
   result does not decide whole-act performance or permission; exclusion is not
   prohibition or permission; performance is not output standing; output
   standing is not downstream applicability; downstream applicability is not
   admission; relation-evidence failure is not a contrary relation; attribution
   is not meaning or its warrant; and successful production/execution is not
   standing by identity.
9. **Does the original final disposition remain supportable?** Yes, but not its
   four-item rationale. Disposition B remains supportable only after retracting
   `lens`, `transport structure`, and `relationship mode`, leaving the single
   `standing effect` defect. Negative-vocabulary concerns are narrower potential
   cleanup and are not proven indivisible from that defect.
10. **What is the single smallest warranted next action?** One narrow Book
    amendment limited to resolving the unsupported `standing effect` phrase.
    This correction supplies no replacement wording and does not combine that
    amendment with negative-vocabulary cleanup.

## Final disposition

**B — one narrow vocabulary defect remains after correction.**

The defect is `standing effect` alone. The original disposition letter survives,
but the original cluster finding and rationale do not. `Lens`, `transport
structure`, and `relationship mode` are expressly retracted from the unsupported
cluster. Category flattening is corrected in this report. The negative gate
finds one materially duplicative contaminated denial and two other contaminated
negative compounds, but their valuable distinctions can be preserved and they
are not shown to be one indivisible defect with `standing effect`.

**Recommended next PR: one narrow Book amendment.** It should address only
`standing effect`. No broader negative-vocabulary cleanup, runtime work,
registry, remaining-Books audit, or replacement prose is recommended here.
