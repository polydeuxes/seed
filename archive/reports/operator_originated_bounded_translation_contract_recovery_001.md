### Question formation and examination

Seed-owned question formation requires sufficiently bounded Seed-side inquiry
standing—not merely addressable or translated material. It forms an internal
bounded Question for the purpose of constraining a Seed inquiry posture. The
Question owns its question identity, provenance, scope, evidence demand,
authority and negative-authority limits, uncertainty, and lawful-stop boundary.
It does not establish the answer, select an outcome, supply evidence, force a
lens, authorize unbounded investigation, or transfer ownership to the operator.

The later consumer is the Seed-owned responsibility that determines applicability,
selects, composes, or requests lawful internal examination means and performs or

Book establishes that role but does not identify one exact executable examiner for
this unrealized Question. Its possible output is a bounded examination result,
finding, refusal, or Unknown—not automatically an answer, Fact, completion,
movement, or wider authority.

Standing establishment and question formation are distinct claims. A locally
composite responsible occurrence could establish both if each claim retains its
own warrant and limits; no separate dataclass, function, Event, or occurrence is
