# Seed-Established Goal Responsibility Recovery 001

## Scope, method, and controlling distinctions

This is one bounded, report-only Fidelity recovery of:

```text
constitutional responsibility
-> goal-bearing dimension
-> evidence-supported pressure
-> Seed-established bounded goal
```

It changes no implementation, test, fixture, schema, export, interface, event,
persistence, projection, canonical Book chapter, or earlier report. Repository
authority wins. **Seed-established bounded goal** is descriptive language in this
report, not a recovered constitutional kind, runtime artifact, or claim that Seed
has self-goal authority.

The recovery inspected tracked active implementation and tests with exact and
case-insensitive searches for `GoalDimension`, `GoalPressure`,
`GoalOrientationInventory`, `BoundedOperatorGoalEstablishment`,
`BoundedAdvancementHorizon`, `GoalAdvancementDemandFamily`, closed-choice
binding, operator interaction, common grammar, recurrence, and goal
establishment. It then read the producers, their boundary fields, their tests,
and the current canonical goal/Demand chapters. Reports were used as leads and
counterevidence, never as proof that their proposed owners are active.

The following inequalities control every conclusion:

```text
responsibility != dimension
dimension != pressure
pressure != bounded goal
bounded goal != active work
goal establishment != inquiry opening
goal establishment != operational selection
goal establishment != authorization
goal establishment != execution
```

No crossing below is inferred from adjacency, a constructor name, a
caller-supplied field, a fixture, an export, or inventory visibility.

### 2. Goal-bearing dimension

A goal-bearing dimension is only a stable responsibility neighborhood in which
a desired condition and a later bounded objective could vary independently. It
must preserve Null and reject collapse into mechanism, constraint, meta-target,
authority, or execution. This is audit-level vocabulary: active searches find
no `GoalDimension` owner or consumer.

Thus a responsibility can support several dimensions, and a dimension can draw
support from several responsibilities. Responsibility maturity alone does not
cross into dimension standing.

### 3. Goal pressure

Pressure is evidence-supported materiality relative to an exact dimension and
responsibility: a recurring incompatibility, inability, degradation, conflict,
missing sufficiency, or bounded concern. A lawful pressure finding would require
at least:

```text
current observations
+ declared responsibility and scoped reference condition
+ provenance and competent evidence
+ materiality/applicability finding
+ alternative explanations and conflicts
+ pressure-local responsible act
-> bounded pressure standing
```

No active `GoalPressure` producer or consumer was found. Recurrence alone can
support a recurrence finding; it cannot supply semantic explanation,
materiality, responsibility, required result, or goal authority.

### 4. Bounded goal standing

Current active bounded standing is narrower than a general goal theory. A
`BoundedOperatorGoalEstablishment` preserves intended outcome, known and
unresolved scope, Unknowns, conflicts, known loss, exact ingress reference, and
lineage. It establishes only when its exact operator-origin ingress is bound and
admitted. It is read-only goal standing, not active work.

The legacy `models.Goal` is a mutable/projected application record with summary,
status, facts, questions, and related entities. `state_patches.py` can turn a
caller-supplied `goals` payload into `create_goal` and a `goal.created` event.
Those structures do not recover intended-purpose admission, a responsibility,
pressure evidence, a Seed-origin warrant, or the modern bounded-establishment
lineage. They are compatibility/runtime state, not proof of constitutional
ownership.

### 5. Authority to establish goal standing

The active establishment owner has construction authority **conditional on
lawful operator ingress**. It does not have authority to originate the purpose.
For closed choice, the option set supplies the exact local meanings and the
operator token selects one. For interpreted material, upstream selection,
consumer-local applicability, and explicit admission evidence must agree for
the exact bounded-goal consumer and purpose. Applicable-but-unadmitted material,
unknown evidence, conflict, or identity mismatch is refused.

There is no active responsibility-to-pressure-to-goal establishment interface,
no Seed-origin ingress type, no warrant predicate for such ingress, and no
consumer demonstrating reliance on a Seed-established bounded goal.

### 6. Inquiry authority

Inquiry authority is separately bounded. `InquiryDemandProjection` consumes an
already established operator goal, its exact bounded horizon, and caller-
supplied inquiry testimony. Its projection and the assembled Demand set say
they do not open inquiry. Question admission, inquiry orientation, and inquiry
Demand standing therefore do not establish goal standing and do not authorize
movement.

A possible pre-goal common-grammar inquiry may be constitutionally intelligible,
but no active producer is recovered that may open it on Seed's own initiative.
At most, the specimen can motivate the smallest next inquiry stated at the end
of this report.

### 7. Operational/execution authority

Operational selection, authority Demand, authorization, and execution remain
separate. `BoundedAdvancementHorizon` preserves a caller-supplied present
movement boundary after an established operator goal; its booleans explicitly
refuse goal selection, focus establishment, inquiry opening, authority request,
realization selection, scheduling, authorization, execution, recording, event
writing, and cluster mutation. The Demand set and family projections preserve
equivalent negative authority. None can operationalize a Seed-established goal.
