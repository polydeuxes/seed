## Controlling constitutional clauses

`inquiry-frontiers.md` defines the subject as the bounded set of inquiry concerns
that may be considered **without** claiming movement or examination. Its bounded
resolution says the frontier assembles bounded, evidenced inquiry possibilities;
membership creates visibility for consideration rather than selection,
authorization, examination, or resolution. Establishment is consumer-local and
may establish bounded identity coherence and operative frontier standing from
warranted testimony without proving selected-Demand truth, testimony truth,
producer occurrence, inquiry opening, execution, or result knowledge.

`04.Frontier.A` makes identity conjunctive. The frontier must preserve:

1. the exact inquiry demand **or uncertainty** concerned;
2. source and provenance;
3. present movement boundary or consumer-local context;
4. included and excluded scope;
5. sufficiency and lawful stopping conditions;
6. Unknowns and conflicts; and
7. negative authority.

The disjunction is material. The lowercase phrase “inquiry demand” is not defined
there as Book III `Demand`, and the express alternative “or uncertainty” defeats
a universal canonical-Demand prerequisite. Where a canonical Demand is actually
the concern, its separately established required-result standing can contribute
to frontier identity; selected Demand alone cannot establish the frontier. Where
an uncertainty is the concern, the uncertainty must itself have established,
bounded identity—not merely occur as an unbound word or caller label. The Book
does not establish one mandatory artifact or origin for either concern.

`04.Frontier.B` makes support claim-relative. Preserved testimony contributes
positive support only when **the consumer** has warrant sufficient for the exact
claim, inquiry demand, frontier boundary, and reliance purpose. Thus the consumer
is the consumer-local frontier-establishing responsibility, not the original
source producer and not a universal admission service. The “exact claim” is the
particular frontier assertion the testimony is offered to support—for example a
coordinate of frontier identity or membership—not testimony truth generally and
not aggregate frontier completeness. Support is realization-independent and may
use different lawful mechanisms and different warrant for different claims.

The final paragraph then creates a distinct local relevance boundary. Visibility
and membership are insufficient. A bounded responsibility may treat material as
relevant for **further examination** only when explicit record material supports
that responsibility's exact local responsibility, subject binding, evidence or
provenance requirements, and authority boundary. The resulting standing is
permission to examine further inside that boundary. The grammar assigns this
relevance judgment to the bounded responsibility that proposes to examine; it
neither back-propagates translation applicability nor names one universal
examiner.

`questions-and-inquiry.md` preserves the neighboring relation. A bounded Question
has identity, provenance, scope, evidence demand, authority limits, uncertainty,
and lawful stopping conditions. It may constrain lens or method applicability.
But Question relations are local and do not establish a universal ordering, and
Question standing, frontier standing, evidence warrant, and method applicability
remain distinct. Operator-originated inquiry requires bounded translation before
internal inquiry. Nothing in the frontier chapter overrides that source boundary.

General standing, road, evidence, authority, refusal, and stopping law reinforce
this reading: artifact visibility does not establish consumer uptake; relations
need their own warranted standing; preservation is not reliance; authority does
not expand in transit; insufficient binding may yield Unknown/refusal/stop; and a
local stop is neither examination nor completion.

Positive support can support a membership or other exact frontier assertion, but
membership does not entail positive support. Likewise positive support does not
entail material relevance without the additional responsibility/subject/evidence/
authority binding stated in the relevance clause. When those exact relevance
requirements are themselves the claim supported by positive warrant, relevance
may be established in the same responsible occurrence; the claims remain
non-collapsing.

## Demand, uncertainty, and Question relation

“Inquiry demand” in `04.Frontier.A` is best recovered as a bounded local phrase,
not automatic identity with canonical Book III `Demand`. It can cover a
question-local evidence demand or another exact bounded requirement for inquiry;
where the evidence establishes a Book III Demand, that stronger standing remains
available but is not implied by spelling. This conclusion follows from the
lowercase local usage, the explicit “or uncertainty,” Book III's separately
specified establishment requirements, and the frontier clause's warning that a
selected demand alone is insufficient.

An established uncertainty can therefore be sufficient to supply the concern
coordinate, but never the whole frontier: source/provenance, local context,
scope, sufficiency/stop, Unknowns/conflicts, negative authority, and warrant still
must be established. “Uncertainty” in caller prose or an artifact field is not an
established uncertainty by identity.

A formed Question is one lawful neighboring source of an exact inquiry concern.
Its identity, scope, evidence demand, uncertainty, authority, and stopping
coordinates overlap frontier requirements, so it may provide warranted testimony
for a question-local frontier. It does not establish the frontier automatically.
Conversely, 04.Frontier.A permits a frontier around an independently established
Demand or uncertainty and never universally requires Question standing. Frontier
identity and Question identity remain distinct claims.

## Candidate topology tests

### A — frontier before translation: unsupported for operator ingress

The raw addressable artifact cannot establish the exact inquiry concern, meaning,
subject binding, consumer context, reliance purpose, evidence demand, or authority
boundary. Using frontier relevance to authorize bounded translation would also
replace “further examination” with a purpose the clause does not name. Therefore:

```text
OperatorIngressAddressableMaterial
-X-> inquiry-frontier establishment
-X-> translation applicability supplied by frontier permission
```

### B — frontier after translation: conditional, not a universal stage

Bounded translation may first provide the identified source-to-inquiry standing
needed for a particular operator-originated concern, and bounded standing
establishment may make that concern identifiable. Neither act automatically
establishes frontier identity or warrant. A local frontier can follow when all
coordinates are supported. The Book does not establish frontier as a mandatory
pre-question stage.

### C — frontier after question formation: supported local case

A formed bounded Question can supply a question-local inquiry concern, evidence
demand, uncertainty, authority, scope, and stopping constraints. A separate local
frontier act may then establish visibility and relevance/permission for choosing
or conducting further examination. This is the clearest operator-road placement,
but remains conditional because not every Question requires a new frontier and
Question standing is not frontier standing.

### D — local variability: established as constitutional permission, not a pipeline

The Book expressly permits the exact concern to be a demand **or uncertainty**,
and Question law denies a universal ordering. Thus frontiers may be established
from an established canonical Demand, an established local inquiry/evidence
demand, an established uncertainty, a formed Question, or another prior finding
that establishes such a bounded concern. These are locally variable inputs, not
four interchangeable labels and not proof of current occurrence. The chosen
disposition remains the more exact answer to the governing translation Unknown:
variability does not turn the frontier into translation applicability.

## Operator-originated material sufficiency test

`OperatorIngressAddressableMaterial` answers “which exact recorded attributed
material?” It does not answer:

* what the material communicatively means;
* which exact inquiry demand or uncertainty is established;
* which local consumer responsibility is considering it;
* what subject that responsibility would examine;
* what claim or reliance purpose the material would support;
* which evidence/provenance requirements apply to that examination; or
* whether the authority boundary permits that use.

Consequently it is not lawful direct frontier input sufficient for establishment.
For the normal operator road, bounded translation must first preserve the exact
source-to-inquiry coordinates, and bounded standing establishment must establish
an identifiable inquiry concern sufficient for the later responsibility. Whether
translation itself establishes its input applicability remains Unknown. Question
formation is a possible later source of a frontier concern, not a universal
frontier prerequisite.

## Examination consumer and authority boundary

The exact downstream consumer is local rather than a named universal examiner.
When a bounded Question governs, Book IV assigns Seed the responsibility for
question-local applicability, selection, and composition of lawful internal
means. That responsibility may consume frontier visibility and permission to
determine whether a method or lens is applicable, to select/compose/request a
lawful means, and then to conduct bounded examination. A different frontier
rooted in an established Demand or uncertainty may have another competent local
examining responsibility. Every consumer must preserve the concern, subject,
evidence/provenance, authority, scope, conflict, Unknown, and stopping limits.

Permission is applicability for **further examination in the named boundary** in
the ordinary constitutional sense that the material may participate in that
bounded posture. It is not universal method applicability: a method can still be
inapplicable, unsupported, conflicting, unavailable, or unnecessary. It is not
selection, scheduling, authorization beyond that use, access authority, execution,
or a requirement to act. The lawful consumer may instead refuse, defer, preserve
Unknown, use already-preserved evidence, or stop.
