# Developer first-contact active-law trace 001

## Scope, method, and answer

This report answers one question: which clauses of the active Book authorize or
describe the developer-supplied semantic bootstrap exercised by the #2298–#2310
runtime path, and what constitutional grammar remains when those assertions are
removed? It starts from `developer_semantic_injection_inventory_001.md`, checks
its classifications against active law and commit testimony, and makes no
inference from recurrence. The only historical recurrence finding retained is
that developers repeatedly supplied content when demonstrating a completed
path. Recurrence supplies neither constitutional Standing nor a requirement to
keep the content.

The bounded answer is:

* active law independently establishes the representation, emission, response
  occurrence, Compare, Identification, lineage-based source recovery,
  separately warranted meaning-relation, purpose-local Applicability,
  consumer-local Admission, BOGE, and lawful non-population topology;
* active law also names one developer-supplied first-contact candidate, its two
  literal propositions, potential-goal/local-stop roles, and developer meaning
  testimony as an admitted local example. Those particulars entered active law
  as part of the recovered first-contact road, without Evidence that establishes
  their content, role, or source Standing. They are campaign/bootstrap
  assertions, not recovered general grammar;
* active law does **not** prescribe the #2307 concrete `consumer_treatment`
  record, its developer attribution, treatment identity/kind, or fixed
  supported-Acts list. The implementation added that bridge after expressly
  finding it absent. Generic Consumer Authority, declared consumer purpose,
  Applicability, Admission, and preservation of relation identity and limits
  remain independently warranted;
* `CONSUMER_PURPOSE` is not wholly invented by #2307. Its general purpose—BOGE's
  bounded use of the exact warranted relation by which G expresses M—is stated
  independently in active law. The runtime's fixed sentence and guaranteed
  equality with a developer-authored treatment record are one implementation of
  that purpose, not law establishing that exact value or its Standing.

No clause authorizes developer authorship *by identity* to establish semantic
Standing. The clauses that mention developer testimony consistently bound it as
testimony and demand an applicable warrant. That protective law is not itself
contaminated merely because its example names the bootstrap.

## Clause-by-clause recovery and disposition

remains **Unknown**.

> `preserved operator ingress whose richer grammar is not presently shared`
> `-> developer-supplied potential-goal candidate and meaning, attributed and bounded:`
> `   establish richer shared grammar with the operator`
> `+ local alternative: establish no such goal and stop locally`
> `-> a responsible occurrence forms presented alternatives representing those`
> `   sources and presents them as a bounded closed-choice representation`

This text entered active law in #2223 to display the guarded first-contact road.

affirmative candidate's potential-goal classification, and both literal
meanings had no source Evidence beyond developer/recovery testimony (**B**).
“Attributed and bounded” prevents universalization but does not establish the
testimony's Standing. The exact candidate and meanings therefore remain

## What survives removal

treatment relation/Authority, and meaning testimony does not remove:

1. preservation of ingress and Presentation lineage;
2. stable identity and source-attribution machinery without a preloaded source;
3. formation and emission as distinct responsible occurrences;
4. exact-set, coordinate, binding, Compare, Identification, and no-match grammar;
5. representation-purpose/scope/provenance/loss/conflict/Unknown boundaries,
   without a pre-injected purpose;
6. represented-source recovery from an identified alternative through evidenced
   lineage;
7. the requirement for a separately warranted G→M relation;
8. BOGE-local purpose, Applicability, consumer-local Admission, and BOGE as
   distinct boundaries;
9. Consumer Authority as a required separately evidenced coordinate;
10. competent local stopping and the rule that unsupported coordinates remain
    Unknown or inapplicable.

The #2321 empirical result is accepted exactly as implementation testimony:

```text
no supplied alternatives
-> Presentation still forms/emits
-> ingress remains preserved with Presentation lineage
-> Compare refuses
-> no meaning relation
-> no goal Standing
-> projector remains coherent
```

It proves structural tolerance of dormancy, not an amendment warrant. It also
requires three different conclusions:

| Claim | Result |
|---|---|

| code sleeps without injected content | **Established** for the tested current path: it preserves and projects, then refuses before semantic consumption |
