# Present-Subject Responsibility Recovery 001

## 1. Question, authority, and method

This report asks one bounded question:

```text
Does active law establish an exact responsibility by which Seed
presently bears a bounded relation to an already-established subject
for one declared purpose?
```

The report does not begin with a constitutional name. `R1`, `R2`, `R3`,
subject `S`, purpose `P`, relation `Q`, result `X`, and standing `Y` are report
notation only. They name no constitutional kind.

The authority order is:

1. the active Book;
2. runtime and tests as implementation testimony;
3. reports as attributed testimony; and
4. git history as provenance testimony.

The active-law pass preceded every lower-authority pass. Recurrence, headings,
adjacency, capitalization, file names, and related-chapter lists supplied no
law. No lower-authority coordinate completes an absent active-law coordinate.
The repository begins this inquiry at `5ccc29c`, whose ancestry contains
`c60b885` (PR 2232).

An exact responsibility must identify at least its responsible owner or
boundary, exact subject, exact act or borne relation, declared purpose, exact
result or standing. Conditional branches are reported only where instantiated.
The five classifications used here have these meanings:

- **resolved**: active law instantiates the exact coordinate and supplies its
  value;
- **conflicting**: active law instantiates incompatible values;
- **responsibly Unknown**: a responsible finding positively establishes
  **Unknown** for the exact coordinate;
- **unresolved**: active law instantiates the coordinate but supplies no value;
- **uninstantiated**: active law supplies no exact coordinate.

This follows the Responsibility root: its headings expose coordinates without
assigning values; a conditional branch remains uninstantiated when its exact
road does not establish it; unresolved is not **Unknown** without a responsible
finding (`README.md:20-27,271-277`).

## 3. Threshold results from active law

### 3.1 R1: association and presentation fragment

The orientation chapter says that orientation “associates and presents goal
dimensions” and separately requires a warranted transition for movement
(`03-goals-and-advancement/orientation-and-movement.md:9-15`). That clause
positively establishes two relation verbs and goal dimensions as their generic
material. It does not identify:

- the responsible owner or boundary;
- an exact already-established subject `S`;
- one declared purpose `P`;
- a result `X`;
- standing `Y` for the association or presentation;
- a conditional occurrence coordinate;
- a concrete occurred instance;
- a consumer or Uptake relation; or
- exact Evidence, Warrant, Authority, scope, or locality for such a road.

R1 is therefore an **active-law fragment**, not an exact responsibility.
Its verbs must not be converted into a new noun. Its chapter title does not
supply owner, standing, or occurrence.

### 3.2 R2: selection responsibility outside the requested boundary

Active law does establish consideration selection as a selection family. It
chooses an already-established subject for bounded present consideration when
its responsible owner validates the Evidence required for that selection; its
result is selected-for-consideration and is not reusable Authorization
(`03-goals-and-advancement/selection-and-authorization.md:9-16`). General
selection law adds a bounded candidate set, a selection basis supported by
Evidence or exact comparison, owner-local identity and applicability validation,
and zero or more chosen candidates or lawful non-selection
(`02-acts-and-constraints/selection-artifacts-and-selection-acts.md:9-20`).

R2 does not answer the governing question because its exact act is selection,
one of the excluded establishments. Nor can an earlier, smaller present relation
be inferred from the selection's input, purpose wording, or result. The active
clause does not separately establish a pre-selection responsibility by which
Seed already bears a present relation to `S`.

R2 also remains generic rather than an exact subject road: active law supplies no
particular owner, candidate set, subject identity, selection purpose beyond the
family description, conditional occurrence, or actual occurred instance. Those
missing values matter to any claim about a present particular `S`, even though
the family-level selection grammar is real.

### 3.3 R3: consumer-local relevance and support fragments

Inquiry-frontier law says recorded or presented material is not relevant to a
local responsibility merely because it is preserved, presented, or a frontier
member. An exact consumer may take it up for an exact declared act and purpose
where required local Warrant exists; that relevance does not establish
selection, mutation, truth, or a later act occurrence
(`04-inquiry/inquiry-frontiers.md:20-27`). This establishes a conditional
consumer-local relevance grammar, not a particular present-subject
responsibility. The exact consumer, `S`, act, `P`, local Warrant, result, standing,
and occurrence are variables rather than recovered values.

Evidence support binding likewise establishes applicable represented support
for an exact declared consumer act only when specified identity, source,
context, subject, claim, authority boundary, temporal limits, uncertainty, and
provenance dimensions can be distinguished
(`05-evidence-and-knowledge/evidence-provenance-and-explanation.md:13-23`). It
does not establish a current Seed-to-`S` relation, factual truth, or universal
admission. Evidence becoming available also does not revise a consumer; revision
requires its own responsible local occurrence
(`01-grammar-and-standing/lenses-views-and-roads.md:9-20`).

R3 therefore comprises conditional fragments. It does not provide the exact
owner, subject, act, purpose, result, or standing required by the question.

report therefore neither treats BOGE as indivisible nor completes its full
decomposition.
