## 1. Scope and negative authority

This is one bounded, Book-first, report-only constitutional recovery on merged
`main` at `c77e9c9` (PR 1978). PRs 1977 and 1978 are immutable testimony: they
locate claims and implementation behavior but do not establish a reasoning mode,
an ATX grammar, or a competency. The only road examined is the corrected ATX
witness below. This report changes no code, test, fixture, schema, command,
persistence, event, projection, canonical chapter, existing report, or archived
testimony.

The words **abduction**, **deduction**, and **induction** remain external
investigation vocabulary. The Book does not name them as Constitutional Grammar.
This recovery characterizes the distinct constitutional responsibilities their
bounded forms would have to perform; it does not naturalize philosophical
vocabulary, implement recognition or candidate generation, design a generalized
grammar engine, or recover general Markdown, English, sections, titles,
hierarchy, links, front matter, or Prometheus.

The stopping boundary is:

```text
measurement/testimony
→ abductive candidate
→ deductive predictions
→ evidence acquisition
→ inductive scoped support
→ Fidelity findings
→ establishment / narrowing / contradiction / refusal / Unknown
```

No arrow grants standing merely because material crossed a process or city
boundary. At each crossing the recoverable constitutional payload is subject,
assertion, standing, producer, consumer, provenance, authority, scope,
occurrence, applicability, selection, preserved dimensions, loss, conflict,
Unknown, refusal, and lawful stop (the **crossing tuple**).

## 4. Central finding

Candidate formation, discriminating prediction, scoped generalization,
Fidelity comparison, and competency establishment are **five non-collapsible
constitutional responsibilities** when their respective occurrences are
warranted. Formation proposes a possible explanation; prediction derives
candidate-relative observable consequences; induction assesses recurring,
independent cases into a scope-limited generalization candidate; Fidelity
compares an expectation against an attributed witness; establishment alone
consumes sufficient admitted findings under authority and changes competency
standing.

They may compose, branch, refuse, and repeat, but are not one
“candidate-forming examination” act. A generated candidate is not probable,
selected, supported, compared, or established by construction. A satisfied
prediction is not establishment. Inductive support remains a candidate standing.
A Fidelity finding does not automatically move standing. Establishment is the
separate responsible transition.

## 7. Abductive candidate-formation characterization

In external investigation vocabulary, abduction performs:

```text
bounded inquiry pressure
+ available attributed measurements or testimony
→ possible explanatory candidate
```

The responsible producer must be an identified Seed candidate-formation
boundary for road A; B and C remain attributed ingress, not Seed abduction. Its
inputs retain measurement/testimony standing. Its output is a **possible
explanatory candidate**, never probability, selection, grammar truth, induction,
or competency.

A lawful candidate view preserves producer, input refs and standing, why it was
generated, exact scope, alternatives, conflicts, salience basis or Unknown,
authority limits, Unknowns, and refusal. Candidate examples include delimiter,
marker-count, one-through-six, local-fence, alternate Setext-like family,
decorative recurrence, and “no supported explanation yet.” Familiarity cannot
substitute for evidence. Refuse if the inputs cannot be attributed/reopened,
purpose is unbounded, the rationale is invented, or producer identity would be
erased.

Candidate generated != candidate probable; plausible != selected; familiar !=
evidenced; formation != induction completed.

## 8. Deductive prediction characterization

Deduction performs a candidate-relative derivation:

```text
candidate rule + declared conditions
→ predicted support, contradiction, or boundary cases
```

It requires a sufficiently definite candidate. The prediction producer must
preserve candidate dependency, exact predicted observation, scope, conditions,
counterfactual character, evidence needed, and what result would falsify or
narrow the candidate. The prediction remains an expectation until an
independent observation/comparison occurs.

Prediction != observation; satisfied prediction != established candidate;
contradiction != globally false candidate; test expectation != constitutional
deduction merely because both use expected values. A contradiction can expose
a boundary, an ambiguous condition, a version mismatch, or a defeated bounded
candidate. The responsible later consumer decides narrowing versus defeat.

## 9. Inductive generalization characterization

Induction performs:

```text
multiple attributed cases
+ independence analysis
+ bounded recurrence or behavioral agreement
→ scoped generalization candidate
```

Its strongest lawful assertion here is of the form: “within grammar/version G
and material scope S, forms satisfying R recurrently receive heading treatment.”
The output remains an inductively supported scoped candidate until separately
established.

The producer must expose case count, source independence and derivation,
selection method, positive/negative/boundary cases, cross-document recurrence,
cross-implementation behavior, counterexamples, scope narrowing, and confidence
or uncertainty. Independence requires evidence that items are not duplicate
projections, copied examples, a common upstream corpus, or several behaviors of
one implementation presented as corroboration. One repository corpus cannot
support a general grammar; it can support only a statement bounded to that
selected corpus and its known selection limits.

Many occurrences != independent support; repository recurrence != grammar
authority; one corpus != general grammar; parser agreement != correctness;
inductive support != establishment.

## 10. Fidelity comparison characterization

Fidelity is the responsible bounded comparison of constitutional expectation
against attributed evidence or implementation witness. On this road its seams
include exact line versus prefix measurement, prediction versus example,
candidate interpretation versus versioned specification testimony, claimed
applicability versus provenance/local region, and established competency versus
compiled regex behavior.

The comparer must identify expectation source, witness source, preserved
dimensions, support, contradiction, invention, loss, scope, authority, conflict,
Unknown, and lawful stop. It may find faithful within scope, unfaithful crossing,
mixed, conflict, or Unknown. It may run before candidate formation to audit
metrology, after prediction to compare evidence, during narrowing, and after
establishment to compare a realization.

Comparison result != standing change automatically; test pass != Fidelity
finding; regex agreement != specification fidelity; bounded Fidelity finding !=
universal certification.

## 11. Competency-establishment characterization

Establishment is the independent responsible act that consumes admitted,
applicable, sufficient findings and changes competency standing. Possible
outcomes are established within scope, partially established, candidate
retained, contradicted, refused, stale, superseded, or Unknown.

Its producer must preserve authority, sufficiency rule, scope/currentness,
conflict treatment, before/after standing, conditions of use/refusal, and
history. Admissible inputs may include candidate provenance, predictions and
their comparisons, attributed specification testimony, independent-case
analysis, inductive scoped support, Fidelity findings, applicability/currentness
evidence, conflicts, and Unknowns. No single input is sufficient by identity.

Immediately before establishment the strongest result is an admitted,
applicable, comparison-backed and inductively supported **candidate competency
within a proposed scope**, with unresolved limits visible. Immediately after a
lawful bounded establishment act it is an **established competency within the
declared grammar/version/material scope and currentness boundary**, with use and
refusal conditions and history. Supported candidate != established competency;
Fidelity finding != establishment; compiled realization != establishment;
availability != applicability != selection != invocation.

## 12. Reasoning-mode dependency graph

The smallest repository-supported dependency graph is partial, not a mandatory
philosophical sequence:

```text
attributed measurement/testimony ──► candidate formation
caller/external candidate ingress ──► candidate availability
definite candidate ─────────────────► prediction
cases + independence evidence ──────► scoped generalization candidate
expectation + attributed witness ───► Fidelity finding
applicable admitted sufficient findings + authority
                                   ──► establishment/refusal/Unknown

prediction ──► evidence request ──► new ingress ──► comparison
induction ───► new abductive pressure / narrowing
contradiction ─► narrow | retain conflict | defeat within scope
```

Abduction may precede recurrence/induction; one anomalous measurement or
external rule testimony can create inquiry pressure. Induction may later create
new abduction. Deduction alone has the hard dependency on a sufficiently
definite candidate. Fidelity comparison can occur at several crossings.
Establishment may consume several finding kinds, but not necessarily every
named external mode. Refusal is lawful at every boundary.

## 13. Iterative ingress/egress topology

The road is iterative:

```text
measurement
→ abduction
→ candidate egress
→ evidence request egress
→ new evidence/operator testimony ingress
→ deduction
→ prediction/evidence-request egress
→ evidence acquisition and new ingress
→ induction
→ Fidelity comparison/Unknown/conflict egress
→ narrowed candidate or new request
→ establishment / refusal / Unknown
→ competency-standing egress
```

Measurements, recurrence views, candidate views, prediction views, evidence
requests, Fidelity findings, Unknown/conflict views, and competency-standing
views may cross Seed egress. Later ingress may carry attributed examples,
specification/version testimony, parser/renderer results, provenance,
independence evidence, counterexamples, applicability/currentness evidence, and
operator-supplied candidate testimony. Crossing either boundary changes no
standing by itself. One establishment history may contain repeated ingress,
egress, prediction, acquisition, induction, comparison, narrowing, and refusal.
