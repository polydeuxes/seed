# Multi-input Compare relation recovery 001

## Scope

This report recovers which arrows around `05.Testimony.E` multi-input bounded
comparison exist in constitutional grammar at all. It amends no active law,
runtime, test, projection, or prior report. It authorizes no construction, and
it does not set out to prove any composition.

Active Book law is used first; runtime, tests, reports, and history are
subordinate testimony. Arrows are recovered separately. Where an arrow is not
recovered it is left disconnected rather than drawn for readability.

## 1. The governing clause

`05.Testimony.E — Bounded testimony comparison without source-local erasure`:

> A bounded comparison may consume multiple independently preserved testimonies
> or findings only while preserving each input's attribution, provenance,
> support basis, subject, scope, authority, confidence or uncertainty,
> Unknowns, standing, and forbidden inferences. It may produce bounded relation
> standing inside the comparison boundary, such as agreement, disagreement,
> contradiction, conflict, refinement, unmet requirements, or Unknown; the list
> does not require exactly one relation and does not define a universal enum or
> implementation schema.

Two things are stated and one is conspicuously not. Stated: the permitted input
kind, and the produced result kind with its bound. Not stated: any producer of
the inputs, any occasion for the comparison, or any consumer of its result.

## 2. Arrow-by-arrow recovery

### Q1 — What subjects may become candidates for multi-input Compare?

```text
independently preserved testimonies         A
independently preserved findings            A
```

`05.Testimony.E` names exactly these two kinds and no others. `independently
preserved` is a required property of the inputs, not a description of their
history: the clause conditions consumption on each input's attribution,
provenance, support basis, subject, scope, authority, confidence, Unknowns,
standing, and forbidden inferences being preserved.

```text
projections as inputs                       D
observations as inputs                      D
preserved ingress occurrences as inputs     D
relation standings as inputs                D
```

None of these is named. `05.Testimony:113` separately names *"prior
observations, comparisons, probes, measurements, or interactions"* as things
that may have warranted a new bounded standing — but that is Learning
establishment's dependency list, not Compare's input list, and the two clauses
do not cross-reference.

**Critically:** the plurality is bounded by `01.Standing.D`:

> Co-presence or multiplicity does not by itself establish membership,
> collection standing, relation, topology, ordering, selection, priority,
> focus, shared purpose, or higher-order identity.

So `multiple inputs available != one collective subject` is not merely a
distinction to preserve — it is active law. Several preserved testimonies do not
become a comparable set by being several.

### Q2 — What Responsibility determines their Applicability?

```text
the exact act owner, ordinarily                              A
```

`01.Standing.E.1` answers this directly and is the strongest recovery in this
report:

> Unless the Book explicitly assigns otherwise, the responsibility assigned to
> perform an exact constitutional act is responsible for ensuring that
> applicability is determined for every proposed input before that input
> participates in, is consumed by, or is relied upon in the exact act.

With four further bounds, each independently warranted:

```text
determination is local to the input's exact identity and the target act   A
the owner may delegate to an explicitly assigned responsible occurrence   A
delegation does not create a universal applicability service              A
applicability for one act is not applicability for another               A
upstream applicability is not downstream admission                        A
```

This is the answer to the question that the candidate-versus-grammar thread
never found: **input applicability has a recovered owner, and it is the act
owner.** For multi-input Compare specifically, the owner of the comparison act
owns the determination that each proposed input is applicable to it.

What remains unrecovered is who owns the comparison act itself — see §4.

### Q3 — What result does multi-input Compare produce?

```text
bounded relation standing inside the comparison boundary     A
  agreement / disagreement / contradiction / conflict /
  refinement / unmet requirements / Unknown

not exactly one relation                                     A
not a universal enum or implementation schema                A
```

All stated in the clause. The enumerated relations are examples, expressly not
a taxonomy.

```text
whether the result is itself a "finding" eligible as a later input    D
```

The clause permits `findings` as inputs and produces `bounded relation
standing`. It does not say relation standing is a finding, and `01.Standing.E`
holds relation standing as its own bounded claim subject. The identity is not
established either way.

### Q4 — Relations among finding, candidate relation, testimony, Evidence, Warrant, relation Standing

The positions are individually recovered and the arrows between them are not.

```text
comparison occurrence != comparison finding              A  (05.Recording:49,
                                                             05.Testimony:54)
comparison            != recording                       A  (05.Recording:48)
candidate relation    != relation testimony
                      != established relation standing   A  (01.Standing.E:28)
material carries or proposes meaning
                      != relation warranted              A  (01.External.G)
```

`01.Standing.E:28` also states what a relation claim requires:

> participants and roles, the relation assertion, evidence standing, scope,
> producer, consumer and purpose, authority, occurrence, conflicts, and limits

and:

> Evidence that does not warrant a bounded relation claim does not thereby
> warrant its negation or an opposing strengthened relation claim.

So five distinct positions, each with recovered non-equivalences to its
neighbours, and **no recovered arrow between any two of them.**

```text
comparison finding → candidate relation      D
candidate relation → relation testimony      D
relation testimony → Evidence                D
Evidence          → Warrant                  D
Warrant           → relation standing        D
```

The last is the closest to warranted and still is not: `01.Standing.E:28`
requires a relation claim to preserve evidence standing and authority, which
presupposes that *something* warrants it, without naming the occurrence that
does. `01.External.G` states any Seed-native warrant *"remains a separate
bounded relation-standing responsibility"* — naming the position, not filling
it.

### Q5 — Can a comparison finding participate in later candidate formation?

```text
D — not recovered
```

Nothing in `05.Testimony.E`, `05.Recording`, or `01.Standing.E` addresses what
becomes of a comparison result afterward. `05.Testimony:113` permits *prior
comparisons* among the things that may have warranted a new bounded standing,
which is adjacent but is Learning establishment's dependency, not candidate
formation.

### Q6 — Can new relation Standing make prior testimony newly applicable?

```text
D — not recovered, and one clause cuts against the loose reading
```

`01.Uptake.A` is directly on point:

> Evidence becoming available does not by itself change any consumer assertion,
> standing, confidence, reliance, or current result. A revision requires a
> responsible consumer-local occurrence that lawfully uses the material under
> the relevant purpose, scope, authority, applicability, admission constraints,
> conflicts, Unknowns, and temporal limits.

So newly established Standing does not by itself make anything applicable to
anything. Whether a responsible occurrence *may* determine that prior preserved
testimony is applicable to a new comparison is not prohibited — `01.Standing.E.1`
places that determination with the act owner — but no clause establishes the
backward reach as a recovered relation. The arrow is Unknown, not denied.

### Q7 — Does active law establish a recursive or cross-pollinating composition?

```text
B / D — no recovered composition
```

`01.Uptake.A` closes with the decisive sentence for this question:

> Availability, applicability, admission, consumption, Uptake, reliance, and
> standing change remain distinct, but the Book requires **neither a universal
> order among them nor a separately produced admission occurrence**.

Active law supplies distinct positions and explicitly declines to order them.
Any recursive topology drawn over them is supplied by the reader.

## 4. Is the implemented Compare a special instance?

representation against **one** presentation's recorded coordinates.


**Disposition: not established as an instance of `05.Testimony.E` at all,** let

recovered. Treating the implemented shape as the general one would be the
worked-road assumption the request warns against; treating it as a narrow case

### Correction: ownership is not the blocker

An earlier version of this section named the smallest unresolved arrow as *what
Responsibility owns a multi-input bounded comparison occurrence*, and concluded
that without it the applicability duty has no assignee and every downstream
arrow is unreachable. That is withdrawn.

`02.Acts` states:

> An act is a bounded responsibility at which something constitutionally
> occurs: a choice is made, material is admitted, standing is established, or
> an operation is performed.

So an exact Compare Act is itself a bounded responsibility. `01.Standing.E.1`
assigns the applicability duty to *"the responsibility assigned to perform the
exact constitutional act"* — occurrence-local by construction, requiring no
separately discovered universal owner. `compare_standing_continuation_recovery_001.md`
already recorded the same reading for bounded testimony comparison: the exact
owner is local to the instantiated comparison and is not named universally, and
that records only that it is not universally named.

The withdrawn claim treated *not globally named* as *not present*. That is the
mirror of the relocation error this corpus has recorded six times: rather than
naming an owner that was not recovered, it declared an occurrence-local owner
missing because no universal name was found, and then blocked everything
downstream on it.

What survives, narrowly:

```text
Producer, participant identity, Authority, and occurrence Evidence
for a multi-input bounded comparison remain unrecovered

that is not a blocker; Responsibility != Producer != Act != occurrence
```

No construction or active-law amendment is authorized by this report.
