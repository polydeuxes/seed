# Compare Standing continuation recovery 001

## Scope

This report tests one proposed orientation:

```text
Seed does not require a pre-existing owner of understanding.

Ownership is established occurrence by occurrence:
the exact Responsibility performing a Compare owns that
Compare occurrence, produces its bounded result, and may
establish Standing.

Newly established Standing then becomes available material
for a later Compare, and the process continues while further
comparison remains warranted.
```

Stated as the build target: construct the **cup** — the grammar that holds one exact Compare occurrence and its result — and the **faucet** — the lawful continuation by which established Standing becomes available to a later Compare. Do not attempt to pre-fill an architecture describing everything Seed will eventually understand.

This report amends no active Book law, runtime, or tests.

## Finding 2 — the continuation half does not typecheck

`05.Testimony.E` specifies both sides of a bounded comparison, and they do not match across iterations.

Consumed:

> A bounded comparison may consume **multiple independently preserved testimonies or findings** only while preserving each input's attribution, provenance, support basis, subject, scope, authority, confidence or uncertainty, Unknowns, standing, and forbidden inferences.

Produced:

> It may produce **bounded relation standing inside the comparison boundary**, such as agreement, disagreement, contradiction, conflict, refinement, unmet requirements, or Unknown.

So:

```text
input kinds     testimony, finding
output kind     bounded relation standing
```

**Bounded relation standing is not among the consumable input kinds.** Therefore this does not follow from the clause:

```text
Shape A relation standing
→ consumed directly by a later Shape A comparison
```

That is the exact scope of the mismatch. An earlier version of this report treated it as blocking the entire continuation, which overreaches: the proposed sequence need not be repeated Shape A comparison. It may be, for example:

```text
bounded Translation
→ attributed relation proposal
→ Shape B Compare
→ bounded candidate result
→ some exact production or representation boundary
→ new candidate or newly applicable relation grammar
→ Shape B Compare
```

or a later comparison may genuinely be Shape A once an earlier result has been represented as attributed testimony. Which of these applies is not recovered.

The supportable finding:

```text
direct Shape A relation-standing feedback does not typecheck
under 05.Testimony.E

the general continuation road remains unresolved because its
successive Compare shapes and intermediate production boundaries
have not been identified
```

This is a gap in the proposal, not a defect in `05.Testimony.E`. The clause governs one bounded comparison; it does not undertake to describe chains of them.

## Finding 4 — what this changes about persistence

If B holds, the persistence answer sharpens.

`orientation_attempt_observable_evidence_recovery_001.md` established that candidate formation and Compare may remain transient at first contact, because their results are consumed immediately and no later projected recognition depends on them. That remains correct for results nothing later consumes.

But under B, a result feeding a later Compare must cross into a consumable kind. That crossing is **structural** — required for the result to be consumable at all — rather than **archival**, required because history wants it.

**Structural conversion does not imply durable persistence.** Forming an attributed testimony representation for an immediate later Consumer requires no Event:

```text
relation-standing result
→ attributed testimony representation
→ immediate later Consumer
→ discard
```

Durable recording remains conditional on the archival reason: a later cross-tick Consumer, provenance claim, accountability relation, or reconstruction depending on survival. An earlier version of this report slid from "must become testimony" to "must be preserved"; those are different.

```text
archival reason      later reconstruction, provenance, accountability,
                     or cross-tick recognition depends on the occurrence

structural reason    the result's kind must change before any later
                     comparison may consume it
```

Those are different justifications and they bind differently. The archival reason is conditional on a later dependency arising. The structural reason applies at the moment the loop is attempted at all.

The proposal that "comparison results unused later may remain transient, and those used later should be preserved" reaches the right operational answer, but by the archival route. If B is the resolution, the structural route is the load-bearing one.

## Finding 5 — translator as entity, translation as act

The proposal treats competency and Translation together as external grammar imposed on Seed. That is right for one and wrong for the other.

```text
translator as entity     no constitutional object
                         "English competency", "Prometheus competency",
                         "general translator" — competency has no recovered
                         grammar as a kind

bounded translation      established. 01.External.A: a bounded translation
as act                   "may state or form only an attributed,
                         Seed-addressable translated representation, term,
                         assertion, claim proposal, or relation proposal"
```

Bounded translation is **one established Act** that may form an attributed relation proposal, and is the presently identified route under examination for forming the Shape B candidate from operator material.

That is not an exclusivity claim. `01.External.A`'s "only" bounds what a translation may form; it does not establish that no other Act may form a relation proposal. An earlier version of this report said "the only recovered act" and "the sole recovered route", inferring exclusivity from not having found an alternative.

A further distinction the earlier version collapsed:

```text
relation proposal != candidate equivalence automatically
```

A relation proposal becomes the exact candidate under Compare only once bounded by the PESC coordinates — Presentation, Equivalence, Scope, and Consumer-purpose. Forming the proposal is not by itself forming the candidate.

Discarding the translator entity remains correct. Discarding the act would remove the presently identified route.

## The Shape A cup

The **declaration** of a bounded testimony comparison, recovered as a bounded structure:

```text
owner              the bounded comparison boundary, local to this
                   instantiated comparison, not named universally

consumed           independently preserved testimonies or findings,
                   each preserving attribution, provenance, support basis,
                   subject, scope, authority, confidence or uncertainty,
                   Unknowns, standing, and forbidden inferences

act                Compare

occurrence         separately evidenced; comparison occurrence is not
                   recorded comparison

result             bounded relation standing inside the comparison
                   boundary — agreement, disagreement, contradiction,
                   conflict, refinement, unmet requirements, Unknown, or
                   another supported relation

limits             comparison alone does not create truth, Warrant,
                   reliance, source independence, Authority, or
                   reconciliation
```

The coordinates above are recovered as a general declaration. Several others are explicitly **not** universally established and remain local to each instance:

```text
Purpose                    "Not universally established by 05.Testimony.E;
                           the exact consumer-purpose remains local to the
                           instantiated comparison"

Comparison basis           "Not universally established by 05.Testimony.E;
                           must be recovered for the exact instantiated
                           comparison"

Comparison Authority       "Separate Authority for the comparison is implied
                           by responsibility topology but not globally named
                           in the clause"

Consumer                   "Not named universally"
```

So:

```text
Shape A declaration          recovered; implementable as a bounded structure
one exact Shape A occurrence requires local purpose, basis, Authority,
                             Evidence, inputs, and Consumer coordinates
```

```text
Shape A cup structure buildable
!= every exact Shape A comparison already complete
```

An earlier version said the cup was "recovered in full" and that "every coordinate above is recovered", which read the recovered declaration as a completed instance.

An earlier version rendered the limits line with bare `permission`. Active law at `05.Testimony.E` says *"implementation permission"*; dropping the qualifier converts qualified external vocabulary into a confirmed hook word. The candidate-versus-grammar recovery states the limit as Authority, and that rendering is used here.

## The faucet

Not buildable from recovered grammar, and the gap is not one universal kind-crossing.

```text
After one Shape B Compare produces a bounded result,
what exact Responsibility consumes that result,
and what does it produce that makes another
candidate-versus-grammar Compare applicable?
```

That question subsumes the kind question without presuming a single answer. Answering it requires identifying the successive Compare shapes and the intermediate production or representation boundaries between them — none of which is recovered.

Building the continuation before that is answered would require inventing the bridge, which is the pattern this campaign has corrected repeatedly.

## Supported findings

- The prior competency findings remain separate from Shape A ownership; a reusable competency is not the exact Compare owner.
- Occurrence-local ownership is recovered for Shape A; Shape B's exact owner remains unresolved, and the two are non-identical instantiated Responsibilities.
- `05.Testimony.E` consumes testimonies or findings and produces bounded relation standing.
- Those kinds do not match, so direct Shape A relation-standing feedback does not follow from the clause. That bounds Shape A self-chaining only.
- Bounded translation as an act is established and may form an attributed relation proposal; translator as an entity is not established.

## Unsupported findings

- That bounded relation standing is a finding.
- That a Shape A comparison result may be consumed directly by a later Shape A comparison.
- That all chained Compare is blocked — only direct Shape A feedback is shown not to typecheck.
- That the Shape B cup is buildable; its owner, Authority, occurrence, result production, Standing establishment, and result Consumer remain unresolved.
- That bounded Translation is the only Act able to form a relation proposal.
- That a relation proposal is a candidate equivalence without PESC bounding.
- That an exact Shape A occurrence is complete from the recovered declaration alone.
- That the continuation is impossible — it is unrecovered, not excluded.
- That structural kind-crossing implies durable persistence.
- That competency has no standing — it has no recovered grammar as a kind, which is a different claim.

## Unresolved coordinates

- The exact Shape B Compare owner, and whether the active interaction instantiates one.
- Which Responsibility consumes a Shape B bounded result, and what it produces that makes another candidate-versus-grammar Compare applicable.
- Which of A, B, or C resolves the Shape A self-chaining mismatch.
- If B, the responsible occurrence that crosses relation standing into attributed testimony, and its owner.
- Whether comparison results are findings in any general sense.
- Whether chained comparison requires its own clause distinct from `05.Testimony.E`.

## Disposition
