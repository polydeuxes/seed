# recurrence: consumer-first recovery 001

## 1. Executive

The proposed first crossing is a recurrence measurement over operator ingress,
built consumer-first. The consumer question was: what existing Responsibility
requires evidence that an exact pattern recurred across distinct ingress
occurrences?

**A consumer exists, it is named in active law, and the scenario is already
written down.** `03.Demands:49` carries a section headed *Bounded
common-grammar counterexample* describing this situation directly.

**But the recurring subject in law is not the proposed one.** Law counts a
consumer's own repeated stops over one preserved ingress. The proposal counts
a text pattern across several ingress occurrences. Those are different
subjects, and only the first has a warranted consumer.

**The cat is `pattern`, and it is fully absorbed.** `01.External:28`
enumerates six declared-measurement findings. Nothing `pattern` might name
falls outside them.

**A second gap sits above the first.** The measurement family that would
produce recurrence over representations has no production owner in active law.
A different measurement family does.

**The unmet requirement is a runtime absence, not a grammar absence.** Law's
counterexample needs repeated attempts over the same preserved material. The
console retries nothing. Zero `retry` forms in the ingress path.

## 2. The consumer already exists

`03.Demands:49`:

> A possible Demand may arise before an interpreted operator goal exists. For
> example, a responsible consumer may repeatedly fail or stop while attempting
> consumer-local interpretation of the same preserved operator ingress because
> the common-grammar standing required by that exact consumer for that exact
> interpretation act is not established. The exact operator material remains
> uninterpreted. Recurrence supplies measurement evidence only: it is not
> meaning, Demand, selection, authority, or execution, and it does not select
> English or a learning mechanism.

`03.Demands:51`:

> That evidence may become applicable to a separately responsible
> establishment of whether a bounded Demand exists. Demand establishment
> requires its own responsible occurrence and must preserve the required
> result, responsibility, scope, source evidence, materiality, applicability,
> material Unknowns, and conflicts. **Recurrence alone does not establish a
> Demand.**

So the chain is fully drawn already:

```text
consumer repeatedly fails or stops
attempting interpretation of the same preserved ingress
        ↓
recurrence — measurement evidence only
        ↓
may become applicable to Demand establishment
        ↓
Demand establishment — its own occurrence, eight preserved coordinates
```

The producer is governed too. `01.External.E` is headed *Measurement and
recurrence do not establish meaning*, and `01.External:28` states the
disclosure burden:

> A recurrence assertion must disclose the representation or projection
> measured, the rule by which equivalence or sameness was determined, and the
> bounded scope within which occurrences were counted.

Neither end needs new grammar.

## 3. Two different recurring subjects

```text
                      law's counterexample        the proposal
what recurs           the consumer's own          a text pattern
                      failed/stopped attempt
over what             one preserved ingress       several distinct ingresses
identified by         event identity              textual sameness
sameness rule         attempt over the same       unrecovered
                      preserved material
consumer              Demand establishment        none found
```

The difference is not presentational. Under law's version, the sameness rule is
already settled: the same preserved ingress is the same event. Under the
proposal, the sameness rule is the whole unrecovered question, and
`01.External:28` makes disclosing it mandatory before any recurrence assertion.

## 4. The cat: `pattern`

Active law, numbered chapters, contains two occurrences of `pattern`. Neither
gives it a subject. `05.Evidence:30` puts it in a denial list:

> Correlation, association, co-occurrence, sequence, proximity, adjacency,
> **pattern**, trend, repeated linkage, diagnostic adjacency, log adjacency,
> source-attributed causation, or model-inferred causation …

`05.Evidence:36` uses it once as ordinary prose about what evidence may make
reconstructable.

```text
1  does `pattern` name something?
     no subject — an enumerated item inside a denial

2  what kind of thing?
     one entry in a list of things that do not establish causation

3  does it own a Responsibility, or is it carried by another?
     neither; it is named only to be denied

4  what exact Act produces pattern-as-pattern, and what Standing
   does that production establish?
     none, and the selection problem is worse than the production one
```

Step 4 is where it fails hardest. Even granting a measurement that finds byte
equality across two preserved representations, **something must choose which
substring to measure.** Choosing `it keeps` rather than `it`, `keeps`, `t k`,
or the whole line is a selection. No occurrence warrants it.

That selection is where meaning re-enters. `it keeps` looks like the right
pattern because a reader who understands English already knows it is the
repeated complaint. Measuring it would produce a finding whose apparent
significance came entirely from the unwarranted choice of what to measure.

`01.External:28`'s required disclosure — *the rule by which equivalence or
sameness was determined* — cannot be satisfied by naming the pattern after the
fact.

### What becomes impossible to express if `pattern` disappears

Nothing. `01.External:28` enumerates the permitted findings:

> …bounded findings of **exact equality, count, recurrence, prefix occurrence,
> the result of a declared predicate, or adjacency** within its measurement
> boundary.

The candidate distinction worth testing was `pattern` as a structural shape
abstracted across non-identical material, against recurrence as repeats of one
identified subject. That distinction is real, and it is already covered:

```text
abc123
abc456
shared leading abc          -> prefix occurrence

"it keeps" in O2 and O3     -> exact equality, then recurrence

anything else bounded       -> the result of a declared predicate
```

Five findings are exact and enumerated, and the sixth is an open slot for any
declared predicate. No expressible measurement needs the word.

Active law contains zero occurrences of structural equivalence, structural
sameness, structural shape, or structural similarity. So the abstraction
`pattern` would name is not merely unnamed — the corpus has no vocabulary for
it at all, and `prefix occurrence` is as close as it comes.

`pattern` therefore lands in the second cat outcome: names nothing of its own,
maps onto coordinates that already exist.

## 5. `break` is a smaller cat, correctly handled

The proposal already declines to build `Break` as an act or standing, and that
is right. Distinct occurrence identity is available without it: each ingress
records its own event, and `_record` writes `attempt_ref`, `lineage`, and an
identity dimension.

One caution. Ordering across those occurrences is a separate claim from
distinctness. `05.Testimony:103` treats temporal standing as something a
producer names — "Expiry removes only the temporal standing named by its
producer or by the consuming projection rule."

No clause was found making a ledger's insertion order into ordering evidence.
That is a bounded absence within the chapters searched, not a finding that
insertion order fails to serve. Either way, an ordering claim would need its
own producer under `05.Testimony:103`, and none exists on the ingress path.

## 6. Nobody declares a declared measurement

`declared measurement` and `declared predicate` each occur exactly once in
active law, both in `01.External:28`. No clause states who may declare one,
what warrants the declaration, or what the declaration must preserve.

That absence is easy to overstate, because measurement *is* owned elsewhere.
`05.Testimony:33`:

> An operational measurement is bounded testimony about the observed behavior
> of a particular operation instance under declared conditions and measurement
> method. … **The measurement-production responsibility produces** that
> operation-instance testimony; recording is only one possible later
> preservation boundary.

These are two families, and only one has an owner:

```text
                    operational measurement    declared measurement
subject             an operation instance      a representation
findings            duration, resource         equality, count, recurrence,
                    behavior, phase            prefix, predicate, adjacency
production owner    named, 05.Testimony:33     none found
clauses             many                       one
```

The recurrence in `03.Demands:49` belongs to the second family. So the
consumer is warranted, the finding vocabulary is complete, the disclosure
burden is stated — and the act that would produce the finding has no named
owner.

This is the same shape as §4's selection problem, one level up. Choosing which
substring to measure is unwarranted; so is declaring the measurement that
would measure it.

## 7. The unmet requirement is retry, and it is absent

Law's counterexample requires a consumer that *repeatedly* fails or stops
attempting interpretation of **the same** preserved ingress.

`03.Prerequisite:52-57` anticipates the same shape on the ingress path:

```text
3. no exact coordinate of C matches R
   -> no presented alternative is identified
   -> meaning, intent, and requested treatment remain Unknown
   -> no potential-goal or local-stop presented alternative is identified from R
   -> zero or more bounded, separately evidenced retries may occur
   -> repeated obstruction may produce recurrence evidence
```

The route ends there. It produces recurrence evidence and names no consumer of
it, which is consistent with `03.Demands:51` — the evidence becomes *applicable
to* Demand establishment, and applicability is not establishment.

**The runtime performs no retries.** Zero matches for `retry`, `retries`,
`reattempt`, or `re-attempt` across `scripts/seed_local.py`,
`seed_runtime/operator_ingress.py`, and
`seed_runtime/operator_ingress_representation.py`. The console reads one line,
runs one attempt, and moves to the next line. The same preserved ingress is
never attempted twice.

So the occurrence law's counterexample counts cannot currently happen. That is
the unmet requirement, and it sits in the runtime.

## 8. What this recovery supports building

Nothing yet, on the proposal as stated. The text-pattern measurement has no
warranted consumer, no warranted subject selection, and no named declaring
owner.

The sameness rule itself is not the missing piece. `exact equality` is
enumerated at `01.External:28` and would serve. What is missing is warrant for
choosing *what* to compare, and warrant for declaring the measurement at all.

What it does support is a narrower question, already consumer-warranted at both
ends:

```text
does the console ever attempt interpretation of the same preserved
ingress more than once?
```

The answer is no, by inspection. Recording that is a witness, not a producer,
and it names the exact runtime absence standing between the ingress path and a
consumer that active law already describes.

Whether retry *should* exist is a construction question and is not settled
here. `03.Prerequisite:56` permits retries — "zero or more bounded, separately
evidenced retries may occur" — and permission is not a requirement.

## 9. What this does not establish

**That `pattern` must be banned.** §4 establishes that it names no subject and
that the six enumerated findings absorb every use tested. Ordinary-prose use in
reports is untouched.

**That declared measurement is unowned by design.** §6 records a bounded
absence across the numbered chapters. Whether the omission is deliberate, or an
owner exists under vocabulary not searched, was not recovered.

**That the proposed measurement is unlawful.** `01.External.E` permits a
declared measurement to find recurrence. The finding here is that no consumer
requires this particular one, and that its mandatory sameness disclosure is
unrecovered.

**That Demand establishment is reachable.** `03.Demands:51` states eight
coordinates it must preserve. None were audited. "Recurrence alone does not
establish a Demand" appears twice in the chapter and is not weakened here.

**That ledger order is ordering evidence.** §5 records this as a separate
claim requiring separate evidence; it was not recovered.

**That retry should be built.** §7 states the opposite: permission is not a
requirement, and the construction question is open.
