# Question under grammar-bounded orientation: the View hypothesis

## 1. Executive

The proposed reading was that pre-dimensional Seed built an entire Question
ontology to describe what later grammar would call View Applicability → View
Selection → View Composition → Presentation, and that `Question` may therefore
be a View or Presentation shape rather than a constitutional subject — the same
outcome as `closed-choice`.

**Three results.**

**The inflated ontology was never adopted.** The topology invented across
#1790–#1795 — `question-worthiness`, `candidate question`, `question binding`,
`question establishment` — appears in active law **zero times**, in any
chapter, in any form:

```text
question-worthiness      0
question worthiness      0
candidate question       0
question binding         0
question establishment   0
```

It lived in reports and never entered the Book. `BoundedConstitutionalQuestion`
does not exist in the runtime. There is no parallel ontology to dissolve,
because the corpus never took it up. What active law retains is small:
`question formation`, `question standing`, `question applicability`, and
`question relations`, one occurrence each.

**But the suspicion located a real defect.** **View grammar was written into
the question road, then scrubbed without being replaced**, leaving a node that
names nothing and has been carried ever since. §3 is that finding, and it is
the durable result of this investigation whichever way the identity question
settles.

**Neither `Question = View` nor `Question != View` is established.** An
earlier draft of this report claimed to refute the identity, using the
opposition *a View asserts what is held, a Question demands what is not*. That
argument is withdrawn: View law denies its own half of it. §4 records the
withdrawal and states the live question.

## 2. What the archaeology shows

`04.Question.E`'s chain, as written at **#1818** ("Project interactive question
ownership into Book"):

```text
...
Seed-owned question formation
↓
Seed-owned view applicability, selection, and composition     ← #1818
↓
bounded Seed question
```

At **#1862** ("Repair constitutional grammar contamination lineage"), whose
change list includes "Repair question topology and preserve Seed-owned
questioning", that line became:

```text
Seed-owned applicability, selection, and composition of lawful internal means
```

The word `view` was removed. Nothing took its place — "lawful internal means"
names no established kind. That node has stood unrecovered from #1862 to now.

So the proposed reading is right that View grammar was in the question road,
and right that it was removed during a contamination repair. What does not
follow is that Question is therefore View-shaped. A removed modifier leaves an
unrecovered node; it does not retroactively make the surrounding subject into
the removed word.

## 3. The unrecovered node, and an ambiguity that is not a conflict

A triple — applicability, selection, composition — appears in both chapters,
attached to different subjects and pointing different ways.

```text
#1818 / 04.Question.E   question formation
                        → view applicability, selection, composition
                        → bounded Seed question

current 04.Question.D   "A bounded question ... may constrain lens
                        applicability, selection, composition, or
                        request formation."
```

An earlier draft called this a contradiction. **Withdrawn.** Two reasons, both
of which this author should have applied unprompted.

First, the modifiers differ: `view` in one, `lens` in the other. Treating them
as the same triple was a conflation, not an observation.

Second, and more seriously, nothing requires these to be one relation. A View
exposing unresolved Standing could perfectly well become input to a later
consumer that it then constrains. Two occurrences with two consumers is the
ordinary reading, and asserting a contradiction from two orderings written in
different places is the chronology-versus-topology error this campaign has
recorded repeatedly — committed here by the author who recorded it.

What survives, and it is enough to matter: **the node names nothing.** At
#1862 the word `view` was removed and no established kind replaced it. "Lawful
internal means" has stood in that position since. Whether the triple belongs
upstream of the bounded question, downstream of it, in both places as separate
occurrences, or nowhere, is unrecovered.

This was reachable only by asking where the chapter's shape came from rather
than reading it as authority on its own kind. No reading of the current text
would surface it — #1862 deleted the word that made the question askable.

Two further notes on the same node. `04.Question.D` also states that "Question
applicability, lens applicability, and the evidence warrant required by the
question remain separate standings that must be preserved at the local
boundary." And `04.Question:49` records as a Preserved Unknown "which internal
question-forming responsibilities realize the constitutional act in every
district." So active law separates question applicability from lens
applicability, and files the realizing responsibility as unresolved — while the
node between them names nothing.

## 4. The refutation is withdrawn

An earlier draft argued that `Question = View` fails the different-
responsibility test, on this opposition:

```text
View      asserts something about material Seed already holds
Question  is constituted by NOT asserting; carries a demand for
          what is not held
```

**The second half is wrong, and the View chapter refutes the first half
itself.**

A View is not required to present an answer, or positive standing, or anything
Seed knows. `01.Lenses:65`: "**Not every View is a standing-bearing assertion
of its contents.** A View may be only a navigation artifact, inventory, or
provenance index when its contract asserts only source visibility, identity,
location, or faithful transformation." And `01.Lenses:17`: a lens output "**is
not automatically the answer to the question that motivated the lens**, not
proof that the evidence meets that question's exact requirements" — restated as
non-equivalences at `:25` and `:26`:

```text
lens output != answer
lens output != evidence meeting an exact consumer requirement
```

So the distinction used to refute the identity is one the View chapter already
denies. The operative correction:

```text
Seed does not hold the answer   !=   Seed has no material to View
```

A View over unresolved Standing can assert *this cause is Unknown, this
evidence is absent, this conflict remains* — assertions about Standing Seed
genuinely holds. Rendered outward, that can take interrogative form. Nothing in
`04.Question.A` rules this out: `Question standing != answer standing`
establishes only that, and a View of Unknown Standing establishes no answer
standing either.

**Consequence:** the identity is not refuted, and the `closed-choice` parallel
is live again. Neither direction is established:

```text
Question = View     not established
Question != View    not established
```

**The sharpened hypothesis**, worth stating because it makes the archaeology
cohere: `Question` may be a **View contract over unresolved Standing for an
inquiry consumer-purpose**, with `question formation` an older name for the
View formation and composition responsibility. On that reading #1818's original
chain is not confused at all, and `04.Question.D` describes a later consumer
rather than a contradiction.

**The one asymmetry still standing**, recorded as a candidate and not as an
argument: `04.Question.A` has a bounded question establish **evidence demand**
and **lawful stop conditions**. Neither phrase appears anywhere in the View and
lens chapter. A forward-looking constraint on what future acts may do is not
obviously the same responsibility as a bounded representation of present
material. But absence from one chapter is not a different responsibility, the
Book's silence is not a fact, and this was not tested. It is the first thing to
test next, not a finding.

## 5. What remains unrecovered

**The responsibility that performs question formation.** Active law names the
act — `04.Question:23`, "Seed owns the constitutional act that forms any
internal question" — and files the performer as a Preserved Unknown at `:49`.
This is unchanged by this report and is the live question.

**The node at `04.Question.E` between question formation and the bounded
question.** "Lawful internal means" is placeholder text. Whether anything
belongs there, and whether the triple is upstream, downstream, or both as
separate occurrences, is open per §3.

**Whether Question is a constitutional kind at all.** Per §4 this is now the
live question, in the form: is Question an independent constitutional kind, or
is `Question` the bounded View or Presentation of unresolved Standing for an
inquiry purpose?

**No live producer.** The runtime contains `question_surface_inventory.py` and
no question-forming module. `BoundedConstitutionalQuestion` is absent. What
`04.Question:47` calls the incomplete "executable dialogue loop" remains
incomplete, and active law explicitly holds that this does not weaken the
ownership boundary.

## 6. What this does not establish

That Question is a constitutional subject, or that it is not. This report
tested one hypothesis, produced an argument against it, and then withdrew that
argument. Neither direction is recovered. The cat test's four prior outcomes
also include "names established grammar without naming a subject," and Question
has not been checked against that reading either.

That #1862 was wrong to remove `view`. It may have been correct. What is
recorded is that the removal left an unrecovered node and made a directional
conflict invisible, not that the word should return.

That the #1790–#1795 reports are harmless. They are inert with respect to
active law, which is the only claim made here. Their vocabulary may still be
circulating in later reports, and that was not surveyed.

## 7. Method note

The instruction that produced the useful finding was to start at #1790–#1792
and not treat the current `04.Question` chapter as authority about its own
kind. That is the correct application of the Book being developer-compiled
testimony: a chapter's account of what kind of thing it describes is the
authors' vocabulary, not independent evidence.

Reading the current chapter alone would have found nothing. The conflict in §3
exists only in the diff between #1818 and #1862, because the repair deleted the
word that made it visible. Provenance reading is not optional for a
load-bearing clause — a point already recorded at #2339, and stronger here,
since this time the introducing commit and the scrubbing commit were both
needed.

## 8. Correction record

**Defect: the refutation in §4 used a distinction the cited chapter denies.**
The claim was that a View asserts what Seed holds while a Question is
constituted by not asserting. `01.Lenses:65` — "Not every View is a
standing-bearing assertion of its contents" — and `01.Lenses:17`/`:25` —
`lens output != answer` — were both available in the same file that supplied
the View definition used to build the argument.

`01.Lenses:65` was read during this investigation and quoted in working notes.
It was not applied. Having the refuting sentence in hand and building the
opposed argument anyway is worse than not finding it, and it is the second time
in this campaign that a report's own gathered material contradicted its
conclusion.

The operative correction, supplied by the operator: **a View can hold material
that is unknown.**

```text
Seed does not hold the answer   !=   Seed has no material to View
```

**Defect: a contradiction was asserted from two orderings written in different
places.** §3 originally called the #1818 and `04.Question.D` triples opposed.
They carry different modifiers — `view` and `lens` — and nothing requires them
to be one relation rather than two occurrences with two consumers. This is the
chronology-versus-topology error the campaign has recorded repeatedly,
committed by the author who recorded it, four reports after writing it down.

**Common shape with the #2341 defects.** Both are the substitution of a
plausible neighbouring reading for an unrecovered one, and both passed a
citation check because every quotation was accurate. What failed was the step
from clause to conclusion. The guard — write clause and conclusion as separate
lines and ask what carries you across — was applied in #2343 and not here.
