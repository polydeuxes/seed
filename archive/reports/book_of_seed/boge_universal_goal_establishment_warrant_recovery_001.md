# BOGE universal goal-establishment Warrant recovery 001

## Scope and governing answer

This report traces the clause introduced by PR #2026:

```text
BOGE is Seed's only goal-establishment apparatus
```

It distinguishes that universal claim from the narrower, evidenced claim that
bounded operator goal establishment (**BOGE**) is one bounded Responsibility for
establishing operator-origin goal standing. It changes no active law, runtime,
test, projection, or prior report and authorizes no construction.

**Disposition of the universal claim: B — a worked-road/campaign assumption was
promoted into active law.** The repository preserves antecedent testimony for a
bounded operator-origin establishment Responsibility and for two then-current
ingress examples converging on one implementation artifact shape. It preserves
no antecedent Evidence or Warrant that quantified over every lawful Seed goal,
every origin, or every possible establishment apparatus. The word `only` crossed
that unproved boundary.

**Disposition of the narrower claim: A — independently warranted active
grammar.** BOGE is a bounded operator-origin goal-establishment Responsibility.
That says what the recovered Responsibility does in its local district. It does
not close the constitutional set of all possible goal-establishment apparatuses.

```text
BOGE is one bounded goal-establishment Responsibility
!=
BOGE is Seed's only goal-establishment apparatus
```

## 2. Source testimony before #2026

### 2.1 The bounded audit testimony

`first_bounded_operator_goal_establishment_audit_001.md` asked for the minimum
evidence needed to establish **one operator-origin bounded goal**. Its scope was
not universal goal architecture. It examined two local ingress paths:

```text
closed-choice selection -> bounded goal
interpreted operator expression -> bounded goal
```

The audit found that no existing owner then established bounded operator goals,
identified a missing establishment boundary, and proposed that **both lawful
ingress paths** converge on **one artifact shape**. Its own qualifier binds
`both` to the two inspected operator-origin roads. “One artifact shape” is an
implementation/interface convergence judgment, not evidence that Seed can have
no other goal-establishment Responsibility.

The audit also explicitly separated the subject from constitutional meta-targets,
operating constraints, inquiry, authorization, execution, recording, and
satisfaction. Those exclusions make the local boundary more credible; they do
not make it exhaustive.

### 2.2 The implementation testimony

`bounded_operator_goal_establishment_slice_001.md` then recorded one read-only
`BoundedOperatorGoalEstablishment` artifact accepting the two currently supported
operator-origin evidence forms. Its claim is bounded twice: “lawful ingress
paths currently supported by Seed” and “bounded operator orientation.” It says
nothing about every constitutionally possible origin or establishment apparatus.

The later implementation recovery at the #2028 snapshot confirms the local
shape from the other direction. It distinguishes a communication probe from “a
goal apparatus, or BOGE,” and finds an existing BOGE adapter that could wrongly
turn every bound option into a goal. That is evidence for isolating and guarding
one BOGE consumer. It is counterevidence to treating mere implementation
availability as constitutional exhaustiveness.

### 2.3 Earlier Book, runtime, report, and history result

## 3. Evidence and Warrant at entry

For the local claim, the Evidence was concrete: exact operator-origin ingress
testimony, explicit bounded outcome/scope/sufficiency material, a separately
responsible establishment result, negative authority, and refusal on inadequate
standing. The local Warrant was the need to keep ingress evidence distinct from
the occurrence that establishes one bounded operator goal.

For the universal claim, the entry record contains:

```text
Evidence: none that ranges over all Seed goals or all establishment apparatuses
Warrant:  none recorded for exclusivity
Source:   a worked pair of operator-origin ingress roads converging on one shape
Move:     local convergence -> “Seed's only ... apparatus”
```

That is not merely an Unknown exact Standing. The source of the promotion and
the unsupported quantifier are recoverable. Classification **D** would be
appropriate if the clause's origin or its supporting record could not be
distinguished. Here they can: the bounded part is supported, and `only` is not.
Classification **C** would preserve an ambiguity that decomposition removes.
The universal proposition itself is **B**; its narrower local component is
separately **A**.

## 4. Operator-origin scope

Only operator-origin establishment was actually in scope. The antecedent audit
names “one operator-origin bounded goal”; its two paths begin in closed-choice
operator selection or interpreted operator expression. The slice describes
“bounded operator orientation.” The artifact and Responsibility names repeat
that same locality.

The negative claim that BOGE does not create a self-goal or instrumental goal
for communication can lawfully constrain this local road. It does not establish
the stronger proposition that Seed has no other constitutionally warranted goal
origin or establishment apparatus in any other district. This report neither
asserts nor constructs such an apparatus; outside the recovered operator-origin
scope, exact Standing remains **Unknown**.

## 7. Pre-#2062 upstream boundary

The dependent historical sentence was:

```text
preserved operator ingress
-> cannot yet reach BOGE through unresolved upstream relations
```

Its chronology does not give it constitutional priority. Its negative finding
about unresolved translation, interpretation, applicability, and admission
relations is supported as a local implementation/Standing boundary: preserved
raw ingress was not, by identity, admitted goal material. But the choice of
**BOGE as the necessary destination** was inherited from #2026's universal
premise. The sentence therefore decomposes:

```text
raw preserved ingress cannot cross unresolved relations into goal standing
  A — bounded relation/Standing constraint

every lawful goal-establishment route for that ingress must terminate at BOGE
  B — inherited #2026 assumption
```

Accordingly this report does not restore or classify the composite sentence as
warranted. The same result applies to the pre-#2062 closed-choice road. Its age
establishes chronology only. Its bounded representation, two local members, and
responsible formation occurrence may have their own testimony; their arrangement
as the constitutional route to the sole BOGE apparatus does not gain Warrant by
predating #2062.
