# Road Vocabulary Recovery 001

## Scope and authority

This report creates one attributed recovery record. It does not amend active Book law, modify runtime, modify tests, modify navigation, name a competency, or introduce an active constitutional term. It inventories and classifies every active-law occurrence of `road`/`roads` and recommends an amendment boundary for later, separate PRs. It does not itself remove `road` from any active file.

Authority was read in the requested order:

1. active Book constitutional law: every file under `01-grammar-and-standing`, `02-acts-and-constraints`, `03-goals-and-advancement`, `04-inquiry`, `05-evidence-and-knowledge`, `06-state-and-projection`, `08-authority-communication-and-stopping`, plus `README.md` and `concordance.md`;
2. `responsibility_rooted_book_topology_recovery_001.md` and the Responsibility root's own correction history as attributed testimony;
3. `candidate_versus_grammar_comparison_recovery_001.md` and its correction lineage, where the `road`/`recoveries` question first surfaced, as attributed testimony.

The active Book controls. Recurrence, headings, cross-reference titles, and shared English do not establish equivalence between distinct uses of `road`.

## Question under recovery

Two questions, asked separately rather than assumed together:

1. Can every active constitutional use of `road` be replaced by the exact already-established subject or relation it is standing in for?
2. Does any irreducible constitutional meaning remain that requires `Road` as a proper Book term?

## Method

Every occurrence of `road`/`Road`/`roads`/`Roads` in the directories listed above was located and read in its exact surrounding sentence, then classified by what it is standing in for, not by shared surface wording.

## Classification

### Category A — assertion-preserving Uptake label

The one active Book location that positively defines `road` as a constitutional term:

- `01-grammar-and-standing/lenses-views-and-roads.md` (title; lines 7; 12 — three uses; 23; 27; 52; 53).

Its own definition, quoted: *"A constitutional road is the assertion-preserving subfamily of uptake: the consumer accepts the producer's assertion for a declared purpose and validates the identities and invariants needed at that boundary."* That sentence is a complete definition expressed entirely in already-established vocabulary (Uptake, assertion-preserving, consumer, producer, purpose, identities, invariants, boundary). `road` supplies a name for the definition; it supplies none of the definition's content.

Consistent cross-references to this same chapter, by title only, with no independent redefinition:

- `01-grammar-and-standing/external-and-constitutional-grammar.md:60`
- `06-state-and-projection/projection-and-current-state.md:52`
- `01-grammar-and-standing/README.md:8`
- `03-goals-and-advancement/orientation-and-movement.md:32`
- `concordance.md:12, 14, 15, 32, 37`

Generic, non-redefining echoes of the same sense in a sibling chapter:

- `01-grammar-and-standing/constitutional-kinds-and-artifact-standing.md:10` ("road describes a warranted assertion-preserving relationship"), `:34` ("a particular road"), `:39` ("its exact local road"), `:41` ("the exact consumer road").

Finding: no irreducible content. Replaceable by "assertion-preserving Uptake relation" throughout, including the chapter title, without loss.

### Category B — Responsibility branch-configuration shorthand

`README.md`, six uses: lines 22, 24, 25, 166, 223, 243, 252. Each uses "exact road" (or, at line 22, "responsibility-shaped constitutional road") to mean the specific branch-configuration a given instantiated responsibility exposes — not the Category A relation.

Line 22 is a distinct, additional defect: it borrows the full Category A term ("constitutional road") for the Category B sense, producing a genuine collision inside the Responsibility root's own opening sentence. This predates and is independent of the current road-removal question; it is a misapplication of an established term, not a new coinage.

Every Category B occurrence reduces without loss to "the exact responsibility" or "that responsibility's instantiated branches." For example, line 24 ("the branches required by its exact road") states nothing beyond "the branches required by that responsibility."

Finding: no irreducible content. Fully replaceable.

### Category C — BOGE arrangement labels (`closed-choice road` / `admitted-interpretation road`)

Consistent, cross-chapter use naming two BOGE variants:

- `03-goals-and-advancement/construction-and-establishment.md:12` (four uses), `:16`
- `03-goals-and-advancement/operator-ingress-common-grammar-prerequisite.md:104`, `:146`
- `08-authority-communication-and-stopping/representation-emission-and-consumer-boundaries.md:12`, `:18`

These are not independent senses of `road`; all six locations describe the same two BOGE pathways (token/comparison/identification versus interpretation/warrant/selection), cross-referenced consistently across three chapters. The distinguishing content lives in the adjectives `closed-choice` and `admitted-interpretation` and their attached act chains, not in the word `road` itself — so `road` is not irreducible here either.

This category is left **unresolved**, not ready for amendment, pending one prior question this report does not answer:

- Are `closed-choice` and `admitted-interpretation` established grouping names for two BOGE-owned families, or merely descriptive headings for several independently owned responsibilities that happen to be presented together?

The word `road` is removable under either answer, but the correct replacement wording depends on which answer holds, and three chapters currently rely on these exact strings staying stable. A dedicated BOGE-grouping recovery should resolve this before any edit here.

### Category D — attributed or ordinary metaphor

Low-stakes, non-defining uses:

- `03-goals-and-advancement/demands-and-opened-movement.md:35` ("a staged operational-realization road") and `:74` ("execution road") — both appear only inside negative/rejected-candidate clauses naming things that are *not* established, not positive claims.
- `05-evidence-and-knowledge/testimony-and-established-fact.md:119` ("ObservationIngestor's current compressed road from Observation to evidence-linked Fact-shaped artifact") — explicitly framed as implementation testimony ("The Book recognizes that compression as implementation testimony, not as a universal constitutional rule" is the chapter's own governing sentence for this material), ordinary metaphor for a compressed sequence, not a constitutional claim.

Finding: consistent with attributed or ordinary external rendering; no independent constitutional weight to remove.

## Disposition

```text
Road has no irreducible constitutional content.

It is external rendering currently attached
to several different constitutional subjects:

Category A: assertion-preserving Uptake relation
Category B: the exact responsibility and its instantiated branches
Category C: two BOGE arrangements, exact grouping status unresolved

```

Category A and Category B are fully resolved: every occurrence has an exact, already-established replacement with no loss of meaning, and no amendment risk beyond routine cross-reference and concordance updates.

Category C is not resolved by this report. The word is not irreducible there either, but the correct replacement depends on a prior, separate finding about whether `closed-choice`/`admitted-interpretation` name established families.

Category D requires no urgent action; it is consistent with ordinary attributed rendering.

## Direct answers

1. Every Category A and Category B use can be replaced by the exact already-established subject or relation, with no loss of meaning.
2. Category C uses can also be replaced without loss of the underlying act chains, but the exact replacement wording requires one prior recovery: whether `closed-choice`/`admitted-interpretation` are established grouping names.
3. No irreducible constitutional meaning anywhere in active law requires `Road` as a proper term.
4. `README.md:22` additionally misapplies the Category A term to the Category B sense; this is a standing defect independent of whether `road` is later removed generally.
5. Category D uses are ordinary or attributed metaphor and do not require the same treatment as A/B/C.
