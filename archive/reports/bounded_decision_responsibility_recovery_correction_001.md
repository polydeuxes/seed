## 4. Decision as operator rendering

For the evidenced Seed cases inspected by the audit, the precise decomposition
is:

```text
bounded delegated Authorization
+
responsible Selection
+
exact candidate set and selection basis
→
selected result or lawful non-selection
```

Ordinary operator language may render that account as:

```text
Seed decided.
```

The rendering adds no Seed-native subject, owner, act, result, consumer, or
constitutional coordinate. It does not create another constitutional act
between Selection and the later responsible act. Decision adds no independently
evidenced subject, owner, consumed material, act, result, occurrence, consumer,
or standing.

This is not a universal synonym claim. When applied to the evidenced Seed cases
inspected by the audit, Decision vocabulary decomposes without loss into
Selection within bounded Authorization, or into another already established
exact act. Selection does not automatically become Decision.

## 6. Policy and Decision analogy

Policy is ordinary shorthand for a bounded representation of delegated
Authority. Decision is ordinary shorthand, in the evidenced Seed cases, for
Selection occurring within bounded delegated Authorization.

The analogy preserves the relevant asymmetry:

```text
Policy does not grant Authority.

Decision does not perform Selection.

The responsible selector performs Selection.

The responsible authority boundary establishes Authorization.

The shorthand merely renders those established boundaries.
```

Neither shorthand creates, grants, authorizes, or performs the established
constitutional relation it renders. Selection remains Selection without a
required Decision layer.
