# Constitutional Dimensions, Characterization, and Unknowns Recovery 001

## Scope and symptom testimony

This is one bounded, report-only Fidelity recovery from the current merged repository. Two outside model sessions independently recalled an “eight dimensions” concept and an “eight types of Unknowns” concept. That agreement is recorded only as **symptom testimony**: it neither proves either concept nor supplies a source, cardinality, relationship, or constitutional standing.

The governing questions are (1) what standing the repository warrants for eight-dimensional characterization, (2) whether it warrants a closed taxonomy of Unknown kinds, especially eight kinds, and (3) whether any exact relationship connects the two. This report changes no Book chapter, concordance, unresolved register, production code, tests, schema, Event, projection, CLI/API behavior, runtime wiring, historical report, or navigation surface.

## Authority and recovery method

The corpora were inspected independently rather than counted as votes:

1. **Active numbered Book chapters** supply current constitutional grammar.
2. **Book `README.md`, `concordance.md`, and `unresolved.md`** supply authority rules, navigation, and explicitly unresolved questions. The README says implementation and tests are evidence rather than automatic authority, historical reports are testimony, and the concordance is not an ontology.
3. **Historical recovery and characterization reports** supply bounded recovery testimony, not automatic current law.
4. **Production** supplies implementation witness.
5. **Tests** supply compatibility testimony.
6. **Git/PR history** supplies locally available provenance only.
7. **Outside recollection** supplies the symptom only.

The search used case-sensitive and insensitive variants of the requested dimension, characterization, and Unknown vocabulary; exact-phrase searches; implementation identifier searches; and `git log -S`, `git log --follow`, and file-content inspection. A match was classified by its subject, producer, purpose, scope, cardinality, requiredness, treatment of absence/Unknown/inapplicability, claimed authority, and consumer. Recurrence across unlike corpora was not aggregated.

## Active Book findings

The active Book does **not** name “eight-dimensional characterization,” “the eight constitutional dimensions,” “canonical eight-dimensional grammar,” or eight Unknown kinds.

It does use `dimension(s)` materially in several different senses:

* `questions-and-inquiry.md:26` says a bounded question *may require dimensions* and separates question applicability, lens applicability, and evidence warrant. Here dimensions are question-local requirements, named and counted only by the question; no fixed set or mandatory universal lens follows.
* `evidence-provenance-and-explanation.md:26` says missing required provenance dimensions cannot be manufactured and the answer remains Unknown. Here the subject is examined evidence under a provenance question; the examiner/lens boundary produces only a bounded absence finding. Cardinality is unstated and question-relative.

These are claim coordinates/implementation fields, not eight macro constitutional families. The producer is a normalization or fact-establishment boundary; consumers compare, support, project, contradict, or explain the claim.
* `testimony-and-established-fact.md:28` lists possible operational-measurement coordinates and expressly says no implementation must possess every dimension. The list is open, operation- and consumer-relative, and permits omission without universal defect.
* `external-and-constitutional-grammar.md:31` requires each **applicable** producer, source-role, formation-occurrence, scope, authority, and provenance dimension to remain explicit where known; an unresolved coordinate preserves Unknown standing. Six named concerns here are not declared a complete taxonomy.
* `lenses-views-and-roads.md:12` calls uptake vocabulary “dimensional rather than a list of sibling kinds.” This means several independently classifiable relations, constraints, acts, occurrences, and transport structures; it does not name eight peers.
* `lenses-views-and-roads.md:17,75,77` treats dimensions as source-preserved coordinates exposed or omitted by particular views. A lens cannot recover a missing coordinate; `ObservationView` and `FactView` expose implementation-local subsets.

This is cross-cutting constitutional analysis, not an eight-item field list.
* `constitutional-kinds-and-artifact-standing.md:28,30` requires a relation or meaning relation to preserve each **applicable** constitutional dimension or explicit Unknown/unresolved standing, then names more than eight possible coordinates. It expressly says no meaning relation needs every coordinate and permits Unknown or inapplicable coordinates.

The Book's Unknown grammar is correspondingly subject-relative. Active clauses preserve an honest epistemic answer as Unknown while warning that absence of a relation producer does not itself establish responsibly produced **Typed Unknown standing** (`constitutional-kinds-and-artifact-standing.md:28`). They preserve Unknown for unresolved dimensions (`external-and-constitutional-grammar.md:31`), absent provenance needed by a question (`evidence-provenance-and-explanation.md:26`), and distinct missing times or occurrences (`representation-emission-and-consumer-boundaries.md:47`). **Active-Book result:** dimensional reasoning is real and sometimes required at a local boundary, but applicability, subject, and question determine coordinates. The Book supports explicit Unknown/inapplicable coordinates and denies shape-to-standing promotion. It does not constitutionalize the historical approximate eight-family map.

## Candidate eight-family recovery

The supplied candidate list is independently verified as the exact list in Survey 011:

1. **subject / identity** — combines the characterized subject with identities of representations, occurrences, sources, consumers, and keys. The pair is a compression, not a stable alias: a subject can have several relevant identities.
2. **assertion / content** — groups what is asserted, selected, explained, recorded, or projected. Assertion standing remains separate; content can exist without fact standing.
3. **standing** — recurring and important, but explicitly cross-cutting. A subject, relation, assertion, evidence item, or occurrence may each have standing; it is not obviously a simple peer.
4. **source / provenance** — neighboring concepts, not aliases. Source identity, attributed source, represented lineage, evidentiary support, independent verification, and producer occurrence can diverge.
5. **responsibility** — asks who produces/checks/selects/records/projects/relies. It must remain distinct from source, implementation ownership, and authority.
6. **authority / warrant** — compressed neighbors. Authority concerns permission/jurisdiction; warrant concerns bounded support/reliance. They interact and can split.
7. **scope / locality** — scope restrictions and locality/vantage/consumer context frequently interact but have separate local coordinates.
8. **occurrence / preservation** — most visibly unstable pair. An act may occur without recording; a record may preserve testimony without proving occurrence. Survey 011 says the family may split.

The list characterizes exact subjects and relations in some historical reports, while Survey 011 used it to orient repository terrain broadly. It can also characterize occurrences and artifacts only by asking subject-relative questions about them. It is not a family registry and not a claim that every item is a peer scalar.

Counterexamples defeat closure or universal requiredness:

* A subject whose standing is Unknown still has a subject; Unknown is the value/posture at the standing question, not a new subject family or Unknown kind.
* A relation distributed across several artifacts needs participant roles, producer, consumer/purpose, evidence, conflicts, and limits; these may expand beyond eight local coordinates.
* An occurrence can have separately represented provenance, while preservation does not prove occurrence.
* A characterization lacking an authority coordinate is incomplete only if its exact question/consumer requires authority; omission otherwise may mean inapplicable or unexamined.
* Local provenance, time, movement, competency, measurement, or relation questions routinely require more than eight coordinates.
* A local subject may make one macro question inapplicable; the Book expressly permits that posture.
* `_dimensions(...)` has exactly eight keys, but its consumers project a local operator-ingress Event; no active Book consumer treats it as constitutional closure.

**Strongest warranted statement:** **approximately eight recurring macro-dimensional families**, usable as eight broad characterization questions. “Exactly eight constitutional dimensions,” “eight common report columns,” and “eight mandatory implementation fields” are not warranted.

## What “characterization” means

Recovered families are:

* **Bounded examination act:** a responsible author/examiner interprets evidence about an exact subject under declared scope, authority, and stopping conditions.
* **Bounded finding:** the act states recurring properties, distinctions, gaps, or current standing without changing the subject's standing merely by describing it.
* **Report artifact:** a Markdown report preserves the question, method, evidence, findings, Unknowns, and limits. Artifact production is distinct from constitutional establishment.
* **Subject-relative dimensional matrix:** some reports apply the eight macro questions to a concrete subject or relation, allowing distributed, implicit, Unknown, absent, or inapplicable answers.
* **Historical recovery method:** Survey 011 and later reports use the macro map for orientation and fidelity cross-examination.

The active Book contains “characterization” mostly as an ordinary act/result label, not a defined universal constitutional kind. Its controlling rule is negative: a characterization category or recurring report type does not close ontology or supply production authority. No production owner implements general constitutional characterization; no current consumer requires all subjects to carry a characterization artifact or fixed matrix.

The eight questions are not always used. Local characterizations add coordinates when their subject demands them; omission can mean inapplicable, unsupported, or merely unexamined and therefore must be explained locally rather than normalized. A dimension can remain Unknown. Characterization may establish only the bounded finding that its evidence and authoring authority warrant; it does not change the characterized subject's upstream truth, authority, occurrence, or implementation standing.

**Direct answer:** eight-dimensional characterization is **not recovered as a constitutional requirement imposed on every subject**. It is best warranted as a **bounded historical recovery discipline for making recurring important coordinates and gaps visible**. Active Book grammar independently requires applicable dimensions at some exact boundaries, but does not require this eight-family matrix.

## Unknown standing and candidate taxonomies

The repository uses Unknown in separable ways:

| Use | Classification subject | What it says—and does not say |
|---|---|---|
| Unknown as epistemic answer/standing | Exact question or claim | The answer is not warranted. It is not false, absent reality, or proof that Typed Unknown standing was produced. |
| Unknown as preserved value | A named field/coordinate/result | The producer retained unresolved state rather than guessing. The dimension whose value is Unknown is not an Unknown kind. |
| Absence of an answer | Question/examination | Existing material cannot answer; lawful stop or further inquiry may follow. |
| Missing evidence | Claim or derivation | Support is insufficient within the examined corpus. “Evidence Gap” is a useful local reason/type, not automatically a universal ontology member. |
| Unresolved authority | Proposed reliance/action/claim | No responsible authority boundary has supplied required permission or jurisdiction. |
| Unresolved provenance | Claim/evidence/occurrence | Required lineage/source/production evidence is missing or insufficient. |
| Conflict | Competing testimony or standing | Conflict is preserved separately and may cause an answer to remain Unknown; conflict is not identical to Unknown. |
| Non-applicability not yet determined | Candidate coordinate/method/question | Applicability itself is unresolved. Confirmed inapplicability and Unknown remain different postures. |
| String/enum/local record | Implementation surface | `"unknown"`, `None`, empty values, and `TypedUnknownRecord.unknown_type` are local representations; representation does not establish constitutional taxonomy. |

`typed_unknown_characterization.md` is the clearest historical attempt to categorize Unknown states. It names **ten**, not eight: Preserved Unknown, Evidence Gap, Implementation Unknown, Boundary Unknown, Relationship Unknown, Methodological Unknown, Neighborhood/Locality Unknown, Constitutional Unknown, Implementation Readiness Unknown, and Future Investigation (`:13-205`). Several overlap explicitly: Relationship Unknown has status “Evidence Gap / Relationship Unknown”; Neighborhood and Locality share one entry; and every entry retains further unknowns. The report calls them recovered distinct states but does not prove a closed, disjoint, exhaustive active-Book taxonomy.

Production is still more local. `TypedUnknownRecord` has free-string `unknown_type`, `area`, and `reason`; `preserve_typed_unknown(...)` validates against no enum or registry; `to_public_dict()` deliberately drops `unknown_type` and exposes only `{area, reason}`. Current reasoning-path production emits the one local label `Evidence Gap` when derivation evidence and all result/consumer material are empty. Other producers use other local strings. This is typed preservation, not taxonomy closure.

Therefore Unknown standing != its reason; a reason != the dimension whose value is Unknown; a local label != a constitutional kind. Multiple recurring causes do not become a closed enumeration.

## Implementation and test witness

### Exact eight-key payload

`seed_runtime/operator_ingress_common_grammar_prerequisite.py:_dimensions(...)` requires eight arguments and returns exactly:

`identity`, `content`, `standing`, `source_provenance`, `responsibility`, `authority_warrant`, `scope_locality`, and `occurrence_preservation`.

Its producer is the operator-ingress common-grammar implementation. `_record(...)` embeds the mapping in a local Event payload and explicitly sets `mutates_cluster=False`. State projection and operator-ingress views consume/preserve it. Values are mandatory at the helper call boundary, but may themselves carry phrases such as “meaning Unknown”; mandatory key presence is not resolved value standing. The structure closely mirrors Survey 011, making it a faithful local witness and an implementation hardening of approximate report grammar. It is not a universal schema: no active Book rule names it, and no constitutional consumer was recovered.

`tests/test_operator_ingress_common_grammar.py` asserts exact payload preservation and particular key values (including Unknown authority wording). These assertions preserve compatibility for this road. They do not call the list constitutional, prove universal completeness, or consume it across all subjects.

### Open local `dimensions` mappings

`seed_runtime/observations.py` and `seed_runtime/facts.py` define `dimensions: dict[str, str]` with empty defaults. Producers populate predicate-specific keys such as path, interface, address family, mount point, device, protocol, port, unit, user, or group. State-view and source tests verify deterministic preservation, ordering, and distinction. These structures allow zero, one, or many coordinates and are semantically local; they refute an inference that `dimensions` always means the macro eight.

### Typed Unknown record

`seed_runtime/typed_unknowns.py:TypedUnknownRecord` is an immutable implementation-local carrier. Its three fields are required, `unknown_type` is an unrestricted string, and the public compatibility projection removes that field. `reasoning_path_audit.py:_reasoning_path_typed_unknowns(...)` produces `Evidence Gap` only under one no-derivation-evidence condition. `tests/test_reasoning_path_audit.py:test_reasoning_path_typed_unknowns_owns_evidence_gap_preservation` proves that local behavior. Neither implementation nor test enumerates eight kinds or requires a closed set.

## Corrective-clarification ownership assessment

Clarification appears warranted because historical reports and one exact implementation payload make it easy to read an approximate orientation map as a closed constitutional grammar, while the “typed Unknown” phrase can be confused with either per-dimension Unknown values or a taxonomy. This report does not draft or apply a correction.

Candidate locations:

* **Active Book (`01-grammar-and-standing/constitutional-kinds-and-artifact-standing.md`)**: strongest candidate to own a durable non-collapse distinction if maintainers decide it is constitutional: macro family != required coordinate/field/kind; Unknown value/answer != Typed Unknown standing/kind. It already owns artifact-shape non-promotion, applicable relation dimensions, and the warning that an honest Unknown answer does not prove Typed Unknown production. It must not ratify exactly eight, require a universal matrix, or declare a closed Unknown taxonomy.
* **Survey 011 and harder-worded historical reports**: candidates for historical qualification pointing readers back to “approximately” and “orientation artifact.” They own their own testimony, not current grammar. Any qualification must not rewrite history as though the approximate conclusion never existed or claim current Book adoption.
* **Implementation/test language around `_dimensions`**: candidate for a local comment/name qualification only if maintainers find real confusion. This location owns the eight-key compatibility shape and must say only that it is local; it must not adjudicate constitutional ontology. No behavior/schema change is warranted by this recovery.
* **Concordance**: a navigation entry could become appropriate only after an active Book owner exists. Adding one now risks converting navigation visibility into apparent ontology; present ownership is not warranted.
* **`unresolved.md`**: could own the unresolved question whether the approximate macro map deserves active-Book grammar, including possible splits of standing and occurrence/preservation. It must not record the historical map as already ratified or imply an eight-Unknown taxonomy.

Assessment: an **active Book distinction appears missing**, and an **explicit denial of a closed Unknown taxonomy appears useful**, but the exact amendment owner remains **Unknown pending maintainer examination**. The best first examination owner is the active Grammar and Standing chapter because it already contains the relevant non-promotion and Typed Unknown production boundaries. If maintainers decline constitutional ownership, qualification should remain historical at Survey 011 and the reports that strengthened its wording. Concordance should follow, not lead.
