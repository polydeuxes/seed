# Historical initial-operator enum hypothesis recovery 001

## 1. Boundary and answer

This is one bounded, report-only recovery at commit `a572b88`, immediately
after PR 2024. It changes no canonical Book, implementation, tests, root
documentation, `docs/`, or prior report. Historical wording is quoted only to
identify repository testimony; it is not adopted as current vocabulary.

**Answer:** the repository contains several exact strings that have appeared as
closed-choice fixtures, clarification examples, placeholder shell choices,
candidate response families, and one proposed terminal refusal. None is an
authorized initial operator-response enum. The active Books support the
*possibility* of a local, bounded clarification or interpretation-treatment
question after a preserved operator occurrence, but they do not specify its
members. Therefore the smallest historically supported initial candidate set
is currently **no non-empty set**. No recovered option has enough standing to
become a constitutional candidate merely by this recovery.

This conclusion does not undo PR 2024's recovered owner. Seed-owned bounded
question formation can own an operator-specific response form when its exact
subject, consumer, answer domain, meanings, and limits are warranted.
Presentation and exact token binding can then carry those meanings. Ownership
of that future act is not authority for any historical member.

## 2. Authorities and distinctions applied

The canonical controls are:

* literal external material, including `.`, may become addressable while its
  representation, applicable grammar, meaning, source, and completeness remain
  Unknown (`book_of_seed/01-grammar-and-standing/external-and-constitutional-grammar.md`);
* Seed may form a bounded internal question from attributed material and
  uncertainty, but formation fixes inquiry posture rather than an answer
  (`book_of_seed/04-inquiry-and-examination/questions-and-inquiry.md`);
* selection needs a bounded candidate set and selection evidence; selection
  does not establish every downstream subject
  (`book_of_seed/02-acts-and-constraints/selection-artifacts-and-selection-acts.md`);
* representation and emission preserve source standing, not receipt, shared
  understanding, uptake, or establishment
  (`book_of_seed/08-authority-communication-and-stopping/representation-emission-and-consumer-boundaries.md`);
* repeated bounded failure to establish sufficient common grammar may expose
  pressure toward a bounded relational Demand, while recurrence remains
  measurement rather than meaning, selection, authority, or execution
  (`book_of_seed/03-goals-and-advancement/demands-and-opened-movement.md`,
  lines 49–65); and
* the Getting off Null review treats structured/enumerated first language as a
  possible communication form, not a universal first-question ontology
  (`book_of_seed/getting_off_null_constitutional_transition_review_001.md`,
  “Structured first language”).

Accordingly this report keeps these differences exact:

```text
response form presented != its grammar shared
interpretation treatment selected != interpretation selected
clarification selected != goal established
unsupported token != refusal
repeated failure evidence != Demand established
operator-specific bootstrap != global initialization
```

The `.` specimen supplies addressable literal material and question pressure,
not an initial menu meaning. “Getting off Null” names at most a local first
addressability movement. Neither supplies a universal startup state or an
answer-domain inventory.

## 4. Unsupported response, refusal, retry, and Demand

The binder's `unsupported` standing answers one question only: the captured
token is not in the exact presented set. It does not answer whether the operator
received or understood the form, intended new material, mistyped, declined,
refused, used another grammar, encountered an inaccessible rendering, or spoke
to another purpose. Refusal requires its own attributable act or responsible
Seed refusal result.

A bounded retry is historically hypothesized in
`interactive_shell_implementation_recovery_001.md` as an optional one-retry
extension. That report requires a new response and presentation identity,
supersession of the old presentation, and then STOP. It is report-only, not an
active policy. Retrying the same labels does not prove their grammar is shared.

Repeated non-selection could lawfully contribute evidence toward an Operations
Demand for common-grammar establishment only if a future occurrence owner and
comparison preserve:

1. a real operator ingress occurrence and attributed exact material;
2. a real, accessible presentation occurrence and immutable semantic set;
3. capture tied to that exact current presentation;
4. binding/non-binding result without interpreting the unsupported material;
5. interaction, predecessor/retry, time, currentness, and supersession;
6. the exact initial-communication responsibility and subject pair;
7. evidence that a shared representation is required for that relation;
8. counterevidence and viable alternatives, including typo, new request, stale
   prompt, capture fault, accessibility failure, and refusal; and
9. an exact consumer able to admit a relational common-grammar Demand and
   choose only a constrained next communication act or STOP.

Even then, repetition is evidence, not automatic Demand establishment. The
canonical Book calls the possible result a **bounded relational Demand** whose
exact family is Unknown. Historical reports variously say “Operations Demand”
while examining the operational road, but no canonical text establishes that
family name for this result. It does not select English, assign responsibility
to Seed alone, prove a competency Gap, authorize learning/acquisition, or open
BOGE.

## 5. Final adjudication

### 5.1 Smallest historically supported candidate set

There is **no non-empty initial operator-response candidate set with sufficient
standing**. The smallest supported set is therefore unavailable/empty at the
candidate-production boundary, followed by lawful local refusal or stop when a
warranted answer domain cannot be formed. This is not a one-member “stop” enum.

At a narrower, non-initial level, the repository supports the *shape* of a set
containing mutually exclusive, already-produced interpretation candidates plus
an unresolved/other path. Its members must be derived for one exact preserved
material occurrence; the historical car/cat example cannot supply them for `.`
or arbitrary operator input.

### 5.2 Exact members that remain excluded

The following must remain excluded from an initial constitutional enum:

* `1 — Show inventory`, `2 — Show shape audit`, `1 — Prove non-transition`,
  and `2 — Preserve unknown` (test-only);
* `Candidate A: literal vehicle event.`, `Candidate B: typo correction, "car"
  means "cat".`, `Candidate C: fictional/metaphorical example.`, `Candidate D:
  corrupted quotation from earlier material.`, and the incompletely specified
  `unresolved/other` path (sentence-specific report example);
* `restate or clarify current message`, `classify as bounded operator goal`,
  `classify as bounded inquiry/question`, `preserve without interpretation`,
  `stop without establishing meaning`, `establish a communication relation`,
  and `request external grammar assistance` (unowned report hypotheses);
* `[alpha] Alpha bounded result`, `[beta] Beta bounded result`,
  `bounded_result`, `retry`, and `refusal` (placeholders/result kinds);
* both proposed terminal/unsupported response specimens (responses, not
  options); and
* any member labeled `3`, because no exact historical initial meaning was
  recovered.

### 5.3 Evidence required before a candidate can enter the Book

Admission requires a constitutional decision or implementation-backed recovery
that names: the operator-specific responsibility; exact triggering standing;
preserved occurrence and attribution; exact subject, consumer, and purpose; why
a closed choice is lawful; mutually exclusive bounded meanings; stable member
identities and exact local tokens/labels; what each selection establishes;
option-specific consumer and applicability checks; BOGE eligibility (false by
default); presentation/capture occurrence and supersession rules; Unknowns,
conflicts, provenance, authority and accessibility limits; unsupported,
clarification, refusal, retry, and stop behavior; and evidence that canonical
reliance needs these members rather than merely accepting their shape.

Tests, configuration, report recurrence, UI labels, binder acceptance, and
consumer field shapes cannot provide that authority.

### 5.4 Later sequence

The repository supports the following only as a **constitutionally compatible
later sequence with missing producers and admission**, not as a currently
implemented or automatic road:

```text
preserved operator occurrence
-> bounded enum presentation
-> unsupported response
-> bounded retry
-> repeated obstruction evidence
-> possible Operations Demand for common-grammar establishment
```

Corrections are mandatory: the enum must already have warranted local meanings;
presentation must be evidenced and does not prove shared grammar; “unsupported”
means exact-set non-selection and not refusal; retry needs distinct occurrence
and supersession identity; repeated obstruction remains scoped evidence with
alternatives; and the last result is canonically only a **possible bounded
relational common-grammar Demand of Unknown exact family**, not automatically an
Operations Demand. It bypasses BOGE unless a later, independently admitted
operator goal is established.
