## 3. Defect in the original final compression

The original final disposition compressed current occurrences into:

```text
Refusal
→
bounded Stopping
+
preserved reason
+
bounded rendering
```

That formula is too broad. The audit's own inventory and ledger recover exact
local responsibilities for some Refusal vocabulary without recovering any
Stopping occurrence. A negative standing, locally owned act, or locally owned
result does not become Stopping merely because Refusal vocabulary describes
it.

The governing distinction is:

```text
Refusal vocabulary may describe a Stop.

Refusal vocabulary does not establish Stopping by identity.
```

More specifically:

```text
negative standing
!=
Stopping occurrence

constraint result
!=
Stopping occurrence

consumer-local non-reliance
!=
Stopping occurrence

lawful non-selection
!=
Stopping occurrence

non-permitted eligibility
!=
Stopping occurrence

preservation of attributed negative material
!=
Stopping occurrence
```

A later Stopping occurrence may consume one of those exact results where
applicable, but the earlier result remains owned by its own responsibility.

## 4. Complete Refusal-vocabulary decomposition

The complete lossless decomposition is:

```text
Refusal vocabulary
→
exact locally owned standing, act, or result
+
competent bounded Stopping only where an exact road actually stops
+
preservation of the exact reason and its producer where applicable
+
bounded representation and emission where an addressed consumer benefits
```

This decomposition does not assign every Refusal occurrence to Stopping, does
not turn every negative result into a Stop, and does not infer
non-continuation from a preventing condition alone. It preserves each exact
local owner before asking whether competent Stopping is separately evidenced.

## 5. Exact local owners

### Constraint vocabulary

```text
constraint “refusal”
→
exact prohibition, requirement, condition, narrowing, or deferral
owned by the applicable Constraint boundary
```

A constraint result may later warrant Stopping. It does not perform Stopping.

### Consumer reliance vocabulary

```text
consumer “refuses reliance”
→
the exact consumer does not rely on material beyond its applicable
standing and authority
```

This result remains with the exact consumer. It is not assigned to Stopping
unless the consumer separately establishes that an exact road stops.

### Selection vocabulary

```text
“refusal information”
→
the exact evidence, limits, or basis supporting lawful non-selection
```

Selection owns selected candidates or lawful non-selection. Stopping is not
established merely because Selection produces no selected candidate.

### Bounded-ask vocabulary

```text
bounded-ask “refusal”
→
non-permitted eligibility standing
+
exact reason
+
operator-facing error representation and emission
```

The live consumer reads the rendered message. No separate Refusal result or
Stopping occurrence is required by that road unless independently evidenced.

### Projection and representation vocabulary

```text
projection preserves “refusal”
→
projection preserves exact attributed upstream negative standing
```

Projection neither produces the upstream standing nor establishes Stopping by
representing it. Representation and emission likewise retain their own local
ownership.

## 6. Stopping boundary

Stopping exists only where a competent Stopping occurrence establishes that an
exact road ceases. The topology is:

```text
exact preventing condition
+
competent Stopping authority, warrant, and evidence
→
bounded Stopping occurrence
→
bounded Stop preserving the exact road and reason
```

Only this topology establishes Stopping. A preventing condition can warrant a
later Stop, but it does not automatically establish non-continuation. The
condition's producer remains the owner of that condition; the competent
Stopping boundary owns the separately evidenced Stopping occurrence.

## 8. Safeguards for the active-Book amendment

The amendment must not relocate every Refusal-labeled result into Stopping. It
must instead:

- return each occurrence to its exact established owner;
- retain Constraint results under Constraints;
- retain consumer-local non-reliance under the exact consumer;
- retain lawful non-selection under Selection;
- retain eligibility findings under the eligibility boundary;
- retain projection and representation under their own owners;
- use Stopping only where a competent Stopping occurrence is evidenced;
- preserve the exact reason and its producer; and
- preserve bounded attributed “refused” rendering where useful.

The independent Refusal chapter may still be removed because no distinct
Refusal responsibility survives. Its unique reason-preservation discipline may
be placed in Stopping only as discipline governing an established Stop—not as
authority for Stopping to own every negative standing.

    The earlier standing and the later Stopping occurrence retain their exact
