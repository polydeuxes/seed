# Relation Proposal, Candidate Relation, and Relation Claim Investigation 001

Findings only. This report amends neither runtime nor the active Book.

## Determination

Active law does **not** establish that `relation proposal`, `candidate relation`,
and `relation claim` are names for one form or stages of one universal road.
It also does not establish that all three are constitutionally distinct forms.
The smallest warranted result is asymmetrical:

```text
attributed external material
  -- established when a bounded translation occurs --> relation proposal

relation proposal
  -- Unknown --> candidate relation
  -- Unknown --> relation claim
  -- Unknown --> relation testimony

candidate relation
  -- explicitly distinct from --> relation testimony
  -- explicitly distinct from --> evidence-supported relation Standing
  -- explicitly distinct from --> established relation Standing

relation claim
  -- governed by --> the full applicable relation dimensions
```

`relation proposal` is a named, attributed, Seed-addressable output that a
bounded external-source translation may form. `candidate relation` is a named
candidacy rung separated from testimony and stronger relation Standing, and is
also subject to the general candidate-production preservation rule. `relation
claim` is the bounded claim subject to which the complete applicable relation
coordinate rule expressly attaches. No active clause identifies or includes
the first in either of the latter two.

Consequently, forming a relation proposal does **not yet establish** a relation
claim governed by `01.Standing.E`. The precise missing edge is:

```text
relation proposal --[identity with, or inclusion in]--> candidate relation or
                                                     bounded relation claim
```

The Book need not choose both joins. One narrow clause relating the already
established proposal form to the already established relation ladder would be
enough to resolve which downstream coordinate discipline applies. This report
does not propose its wording and does not amend it.

## 1. Independent recovery of each term

### 1.1 `relation proposal`

**Every active-law occurrence.** The exact phrase occurs once, in
`01.External.A` (`external-and-constitutional-grammar.md:15`). It is the last
member of the list of things a bounded translation may state or form:
attributed, Seed-addressable translated representation, term, assertion, claim
proposal, or relation proposal. The list does not declare its members equal.

| Question | Finding |
| --- | --- |
| Where does it appear? | Only `01.External.A:15`, inside the external-source translation boundary. |
| What produces it? | A bounded translation may state or form it. The source or provider does not become its Seed producer merely by supplying material, and existence of material does not prove that a translation occurrence happened. |
| What must it preserve? | Exact source boundary; source and provider or external context; scope; uncertainty; authority limits; provenance or provenance Unknown; known loss and conflicts where applicable; other applicable limits. Cross-seam consumption additionally carries forward source role or Unknown, source grammar boundary, translated claim and scope when present, negative authority and Standing, constraints, Stops, Fidelity limits, and other supplied Unknowns (`01.External.C:21-22`). |
| What Standing does it carry? | At most attributed, Seed-addressable **proposal** Standing warranted by that translation and its preserved limits. It is material carrying relation content, not relation Warrant. |
| What may it become? | Active law says translated material may later be proposed to or consumed by another exact responsibility through that responsibility's independently established boundary. An equivalent relation may be independently warranted by another responsible occurrence. It does not say that the proposal itself becomes that relation, a candidate relation, or testimony. |
| What is it explicitly not? | Not its external source material; not Evidence; not Fact, stronger Standing, applicability, admission, comparison, interpretation, a result of either act, meaning-relation Warrant, reliance, Authority, truth, cluster truth, repository authority, or adopted law by translation or identity. Equal relation content does not make occurrences identical. |

`01.External.G:33-34` supplies a nearby but narrower meaning witness: external
material may carry or propose a meaning assertion, while Seed-native warrant is
a separate relation-Standing responsibility. It does not rename a relation
proposal as a relation claim, candidate relation, or relation testimony.

### 1.2 `candidate relation`

**Every active-law occurrence.** The exact singular phrase occurs once, in
`01.Standing.E` (`constitutional-kinds-and-artifact-standing.md:28`):
"Candidate relation, relation testimony, and evidence-supported or established
relation standing remain distinct." The plural and hyphenated variants do not
add an active definition.

| Question | Finding |
| --- | --- |
| Where does it appear? | Only `01.Standing.E:28`, in the bounded-relation ladder separation. |
| What produces it? | No relation-specific producer or formation Act is named. `01.External.F:30-31` governs a candidate's production dimensions generally but does not identify a universal producer, and says Unknown formation occurrence does not mean no candidate exists. |
| What must it preserve? | Under the general candidate rule: every applicable producer, source role, formation occurrence, scope, Authority, and provenance coordinate, with Unknown or unresolved Standing explicit. The clause does not add participants, roles, relation assertion, Evidence Standing, consumer/purpose, conflicts, or limits to that list merely because the candidate is relational. Whether `01.Standing.E`'s full claim coordinates also govern it depends on the unestablished `candidate relation`/`relation claim` inclusion. |
| What Standing does it carry? | Candidate Standing only. Its production may be Seed-produced, operator-supplied candidate testimony, or external-source candidate or rule testimony, with different production Standing. Those examples are not a closed taxonomy. |
| What may it become? | No universal next rung is stated. It may be consumed only under a local consumer boundary. Consumption, comparison, transport, or re-presentation neither relocates candidate production nor supplies a missing formation occurrence. |
| What is it explicitly not? | It remains distinct from relation testimony, evidence-supported relation Standing, and established relation Standing. Candidate carriage or consumption is not candidate production. It is not stronger Standing merely because it exists, is compared, or is represented. |

The general word `candidate` is sufficient to apply `01.External.F`'s
preservation discipline to a candidate relation; that is not evidence that a
`relation proposal` is such a candidate. The rule governs a form after it has
candidate Standing. It does not confer that Standing on every proposal.

### 1.3 `relation claim`

**Every active-law occurrence.** The exact phrase occurs three times in the
single `01.Standing.E:28` paragraph: (1) a relation is its own bounded claim
subject; (2) a relation claim or Standing must preserve applicable dimensions;
and (3) inadequate Evidence does not warrant a bounded relation claim, its
negation, or a strengthened opposing relation claim. It recurs once in the
important-distinction summary at `01.Standing:85`, which repeats the third
rule rather than adding a form.

| Question | Finding |
| --- | --- |
| Where does it appear? | `01.Standing.E:28`, with its negative-warrant summary repeated at line 85. |
| What produces it? | No universal claim-formation Act is named. `01.Standing.E:32` says a **responsible occurrence** warrants only the bounded relation supported by claim-appropriate Authority, Evidence, Scope, provenance, conflicts, loss, and Unknowns. That is a warrant/Standing boundary, not a universal claim-proposal producer. |
| What must it preserve? | Every applicable dimension, or explicit Unknown/unresolved Standing: participants and roles; the relation assertion; Evidence Standing; Scope; Producer; Consumer and purpose; Authority; occurrence; conflicts; limits. Meaning relations add source and attribution, provenance, known loss, and applicable Unknown/inapplicable coordinates (`01.Standing.E:30`). |
| What Standing does it carry? | The clause establishes a bounded claim **subject**, not automatically evidence-supported or established Standing. Its exact Standing may remain unsupported, unresolved, or Unknown; constitutional Typed Unknown itself requires responsible production. |
| What may it become? | A responsible occurrence may independently warrant the bounded relation within claim-appropriate limits; later Evidence may revise relation Standing without mutating participant identity. Applicability, admission, and consumer use remain later local boundaries. |
| What is it explicitly not? | Not warranted merely by material carriage, equivalent text/content, co-presence, artifact shape, or Evidence that is insufficient for this bounded claim. Failure to warrant it is not its negation, unrelatedness, independence, non-conflict, non-dependency, or absence of dependency. Relation Warrant is not proposition truth, applicability, admission, goal Standing, act occurrence, or completion. |

The generic Claim chapter (`05.Testimony:10-18`) says a Claim is a semantic
proposition carried across artifacts and standings and is not a universal
durable artifact. That supports non-identity between proposition and carrier.
It does not establish that the externally translated `relation proposal` has
already crossed into the bounded `relation claim` subject.

## 3. The proposed road, edge by edge

```text
external source material
  -- established preservation rule --> attributed external material
  -- established only if the bounded translation occurs --> translated material
  -- established possible output of that occurrence --> relation proposal
  -- Unknown --> candidate relation
  -- Unknown --> relation testimony
  -- Unknown as a universal edge --> evidence-supported relation Standing
  -- Unknown --> established relation Standing
```

| Edge | Status | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| external source -> preserved attributed external material | **established** | `01.External.A:15` requires source material and source grammar to remain attributed external material/grammar. Preservation does not itself perform translation. |
| preserved external material -> bounded translation | **Unknown as occurrence; established as an available Act boundary** | The clause licenses what a bounded translation may form; source presence does not prove that the occurrence happened. |
| bounded translation -> relation proposal | **established, conditional on the occurrence** | It is an express allowed result of `01.External.A`. |
| relation proposal -> candidate relation | **Unknown** | No relating clause or responsible transition occurrence. |
| candidate relation -> relation testimony | **Unknown transition; identity contradicted** | `01.Standing.E` says the nodes remain distinct. It neither requires nor authorizes a universal conversion. |
| relation testimony -> evidence-supported relation Standing | **Unknown as a universal edge** | Testimony may be consumed premise-relatively (`05.Testimony.A`) and comparison may consume it (`05.Testimony.E`), but testimony is not support or Warrant by identity. Claim-appropriate Evidence and a responsible occurrence remain necessary. |
| evidence-supported relation Standing -> established relation Standing | **Unknown** | `01.Standing.E` keeps evidence-supported and established Standing distinct but names neither a transition Act nor sufficient establishment rule for every relation kind. |

There is one established bypass that disproves the proposed road as a mandatory
pipeline: bounded Compare may consume independently preserved testimonies or
findings and may produce bounded relation Standing inside its comparison
boundary (`05.Testimony.E:26-29`). It does not first produce a candidate
relation, does not establish truth or Warrant merely through comparison, and
does not establish stronger global Standing.

For meaning relations, another partial shape is established: external material
may carry/propose a meaning assertion, while a separate responsible occurrence
warrants only the bounded meaning relation supported by claim-appropriate
coordinates (`01.External.G:33-34`; `01.Standing.E:30-34`). The intermediate
candidate and testimony transitions remain unstated.

| Responsibility kept distinct | Established owner or occurrence | Result and limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| preserving source material | The external-source preservation/translation boundary must keep source and grammar attributed; an exact preservation occurrence is local. | Attributed external material, not translation, proposal, Evidence, or relation Standing. |
| forming a Representation | A responsible formation occurrence under declared purpose, Scope, Authority, and Evidence (`06.Representations:12-28`). | Compare | A bounded comparison occurrence preserving every input's limits (`05.Testimony.E:26-29`). | May produce bounded relation Standing inside that comparison boundary, but not truth, source independence, Warrant, reliance, or reconciliation merely from comparison. |

No established owner performs proposal-to-candidate, proposal-to-testimony,
candidate-to-testimony, or evidence-supported-to-established promotion. Naming

Shared coordinate names do not establish shared constitutional form. The
proposal's `scope`, Authority limits, occurrence, and conflicts belong to its
translation production. They do not silently satisfy a later relation claim's
Scope, Authority, producing occurrence, or conflicts. `01.External.A` expressly
preserves those later independently warranted coordinates separately.

**Answer to the key question:** no active law establishes that forming a
relation proposal already produces a relation claim governed by
`01.Standing.E`. The missing edge is an exact identity or inclusion between
`relation proposal` and `relation claim`, possibly mediated by the separately
named `candidate relation`. Until that edge exists, participants/roles and `the
relation assertion` cannot be imposed on a proposal merely because the proposal
has relation content.

## 6. Reconciliation of #2381 and #2382

#2381's narrow proposed bridge said that a responsible occurrence forming a
bounded relation proposal could place an exact source-derived Representation
into **that proposal's** `the relation assertion` coordinate. Its safety
conditions correctly denied meaning, truth, Evidence, and stronger Standing.
Its topology nevertheless presumed that a relation proposal already possesses
the coordinate named only for a relation claim or Standing.

#2382 correctly exposed the prior unresolved join:

```text
01.External.A:15                       01.Standing.E:28
relation proposal          -?->        relation claim / candidate relation
```

Therefore #2381's proposed occupancy bridge is downstream of, and assumes, an
unwarranted identity or inclusion. It is not warranted merely by making the
Representation exact or the occurrence responsible. Exact formation can prove
the Representation and proposal formations while leaving the constitutional
kind of the proposal unresolved.

This determination does **not** decide Representation occupancy. Resolving the
proposal/claim join is a prerequisite to asking whether the proposal has the
claim coordinate, but would still not identify the permitted occupant class.
The separate occupancy edge found Unknown in #2381 and #2382 remains Unknown.

Nearby predicate, relationship, Claim, Observation, Evidence, Fact, and

No witness located was both (a) produced from preserved Evidence for the

## 8. Disposition: smallest warranted topology

```text
external material
  -- bounded translation occurrence: established --> relation proposal

relation proposal
  -- identity/inclusion: Unknown --> candidate relation
  -- identity/inclusion: Unknown --> relation claim
  -- transition: Unknown --> relation testimony

candidate relation
  -- transition: Unknown; forms distinct --> relation testimony
  -- transition: Unknown; forms distinct --> evidence-supported relation Standing
  -- transition: Unknown; forms distinct --> established relation Standing

independently preserved testimonies/findings
  -- bounded Compare: established --> bounded relation Standing inside Compare

claim-appropriate Authority + Evidence + Scope + provenance + conflicts + loss
+ Unknowns
  -- responsible warrant occurrence: established --> only the supported bounded
                                                    relation Standing
```

- **What relation proposal is:** a named attributed, Seed-addressable result a
  bounded external-source translation may form, governed by translation
  provenance and negative-Standing limits.
- **What candidate relation is:** a named candidate rung with candidate
  production coordinates, explicitly distinct from testimony and stronger
  relation Standing, but without a relation-specific universal producer or
  stated full relation-claim schema.
- **What relation claim is:** a bounded claim subject governed by all applicable
  relation coordinates or their explicit Unknown/unresolved Standing; its
  existence or carriage is not its Warrant.
- **Established identities/inclusions:** none among the three.
- **Established non-identities:** candidate relation is not relation testimony,
  evidence-supported relation Standing, or established relation Standing.
  Translation-carried relation content is not independently warranted relation
  Standing and equivalent content does not identify occurrences.
- **Missing transition:** relation proposal to candidate relation or bounded
  relation claim. Candidate-to-testimony and the later universal ladder edges
  are also unstated and must not be invented to complete a road.
- **Owners of established transitions:** bounded translation forms a relation
  proposal; a responsible Representation-formation occurrence forms a
  Representation; an exact testimony consumer consumes attributed testimony;
  bounded Compare may produce its local relation Standing; a claim-appropriate
  responsible warrant occurrence establishes only its supported bounded
  relation.
- **What remains Unknown:** proposal/candidate/claim identity or inclusion; the
  proposal's possession of `the relation assertion`; the occupant of that
  coordinate; all universal promotion Acts; whether evidence-supported
  relation Standing necessarily becomes established Standing; and the missing
  pre-snapshot historical provenance.

If active law is amended later, the smallest warranted target is only the
missing relation between the already named `relation proposal` and the already
named `candidate relation` / bounded `relation claim`. Representation occupancy,
testimony production, Evidence support, Compare, Warrant, and established
Standing must remain separate questions.
