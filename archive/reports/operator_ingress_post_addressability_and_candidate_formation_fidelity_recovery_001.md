## Candidate formation and applicability

One `InterpretationCandidate` carries the candidate-local assertion:

```text
the bounded source material identified by source_span_refs
possibly identifies, represents, or expresses proposed_meaning
under candidate_ref
```

That is a proposed meaning relation, not its warrant. Its subject is the referenced
bounded source material (not operator intent and not the operator as a person). A
lawful formation occurrence would need a responsible producer, evidence or
constitutive convention sufficient for that assertion, applicable representation or
grammar competency, formation occurrence, source role or explicit Unknown,
provenance, scope, authority limits, conflicts, known loss and Unknowns. The Book
does not assign the exact producer or competency here. Formation explicitly does
not establish testimony supply, preservation, warrant, selection, intent, goal,
truth, or any downstream use.

The Book keeps open, without closing a taxonomy:

* **Seed-produced:** a responsible Seed competency, applicable grammar and exact
  material could perform **formation**, producing an attributed candidate. No such
  producer or applicability examiner is implemented.
* **Operator-supplied:** an operator testimony occurrence could **supply** an
  attributed possible-meaning assertion. Whether that same occurrence performs
  **formation**, and who owns that characterization, remain Unknown; current code
  only offers a test-only supplied-testimony carrier.
* **External testimony:** an external source may **supply**, and a carrier may
  **carry**, candidate-shaped testimony while producer, source role and formation
  occurrence are known or explicitly Unknown. Carriage and attribution are not
  formation.
* **No applicable formation:** an examiner could find grammar, competency,
  evidence, authority, binding or applicability insufficient/Unknown and form zero
  candidates. That is **examination**, not formation. It may support ordinary lawful
  stopping of this local responsibility only if the reason, examiner, evidence and
  scope are actually established; another examination, translation, testimony
  receipt, or other responsibility may still continue.

Exact addressable material alone is insufficient. With applicable common grammar,
known competent producer and sufficient material/evidence, zero, one or multiple
candidates are cardinality outcomes only if a responsible formation boundary
supports them. With grammar unavailable, applicability unexamined, competency
Unknown, insufficient material, or formation inapplicable, formation may not
proceed; the correct bounded result may be a represented reason or Unknown. The
Book supports an applicability examination as a possible bounded examination, but
does **not** establish a universal named “candidate-formation applicability
examination,” its owner, or that it must always be the immediately preceding act.
Common-grammar applicability examination, candidate-formation applicability
examination, testimony receipt, another Book-owned responsibility, and no presently
applicable semantic act therefore remain alternatives—not an inferred sequence.

## Formation occurrence and stopping

A candidate is actually formed when the responsible authorized production boundary
consumes its applicable subject, evidence/constitutive warrant and conditions and
returns the attributed assertion-bearing result. A test constructor, stable
`candidate_ref`, function argument, preservation result, or candidate warrant is not
that occurrence. The next boundary need not durably record an Event: the Book says a
witnessed responsible return may supply observer-held process-local occurrence
standing, and durability is required only when a specific horizon or consumer needs
recoverable occurrence evidence. No such next consumer/horizon is established here;
whether an attributed formation reference or durable occurrence is required remains
Unknown.

The active road may remain quiescent as implementation behavior, and constitutional
grammar does not compel unsupported movement. But none of the candidate-stopping
explanations was examined: no responsibility is shown to have begun and then
stopped; no condition preventing continuation, examiner, evidence, scope, or reason
is represented. The implementation may intentionally end at its addressability
boundary or merely omit the next responsibility; that distinction is Unknown.
Candidate formation might be inapplicable, prerequisites might be unavailable, no
semantic responsibility might presently apply, or another responsibility might
continue. A Python return proves none of them. Missing implementation is not
constitutional permission to stop, and one local responsibility stopping would not
stop every responsibility.
