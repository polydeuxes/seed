## Positive-grammar test for `relationship mode`

The audited chapter places `relationship mode` in an enumerated list and says
that the listed terms describe different dimensions of repository
responsibility. The only other exact active-Book occurrence is `relationship
mode != consumer act` in `Lenses, Views, and Constitutional Roads`, under
`Important distinctions`.

That evidence answers the required tests as follows:

| Test | Recovery |
| --- | --- |
| Is it positively defined? | No. |
| Is it the subject of an assigned act? | No. |
| Does it have identified participants? | No. |
| Does it have a producer or establishment boundary? | No. |
| Does it have a bounded result? | No. |
| Does it have standing independently of the negative comparison? | No. |

The comparison warrants only non-equivalence: whatever `relationship mode`
was intended to denote is not, merely by that wording, a `consumer act`. It
does not establish a mode, its participants, its production, its result, or its
standing. Those matters and the intended referent remain **Unknown**. PR 2203
therefore inverted a negative boundary when it treated the occurrence as
recovered grammar and removed the phrase from the unsupported cluster.

`Standing effect` likewise has no independent exact active-Book definition,
assigned act, participants, producer, establishment boundary, or bounded
result. The corrected unsupported cluster is therefore `relationship mode`
and `standing effect`. Because the two occur in the same enumerated
responsibility-dimension list and fail the same recovery test, they form one
bounded multi-phrase vocabulary defect; this report does not infer that they
denote the same thing.

## Recovery of `assertion`

The active clause `A relation is its own bounded claim subject` expressly gives
subject standing to a **relation**. No comparable clause makes `assertion` its
own bounded claim subject. The Book instead uses `assertion` as claim content:
artifact fields carry an assertion made by another responsibility; shape does
not supply its warrant; a stronger assertion needs its own bounded subject and
warrant; a relation preserves the relation assertion as one applicable
coordinate; material may carry or propose the assertion that X identifies,
represents, or expresses Y; and attribution identifies its supplier or
producer.

Thus `assertion` is independently used as **claim content**, including
relation-assertion and artifact-carried content. In relation grammar it is an
applicable coordinate of the relation claim. The artifact clause assigns the
making of the carried assertion to another responsibility, but leaves that
responsibility and occurrence **Unknown** in the general case. The Book does
not define assertion as a participant, bounded result, or constitutional
subject. Grammar as a noun, carriage by an artifact, support by warrant, and
supplier attribution do not supply subject standing. PR 2203's independent
subject classification is retracted.

## Distinction preservation

The following three-part separation does not authorize contaminated wording.

| Valuable distinction | Current wording | Constitutional vocabulary recovered |
| --- | --- | --- |
| An applicability result does not establish whether the assigned act occurs. | `applicability success is not act performance`; `one input applicable is not act performance` | The exact applicability results; the assigned exact act; whether that act is performed. |
| One input's applicability does not determine whether the assigned act occurs. | `one input applicable is not act performance` | Proposed input, exact input-to-act applicability, assigned exact act, performed or not performed. |
| Excluding one input does not establish authority for or against the assigned act. | `candidate exclusion is neither act permission nor act prohibition`; `one input excluded is neither act prohibited nor act permitted`; `act-permission standing` | Excluded input, assigned exact act, authority, performed or not performed, refusal, lawful stop. |
| A producing occurrence does not automatically establish output standing. | `act performance is not output standing automatically established`; `successful execution` does not automatically establish standing | Responsible producing act and occurrence, bounded result, evidence, authority, output standing. |
| Output standing does not establish later applicability. | `output standing is not downstream applicability` | Bounded output standing and applicability for the later consumer's exact act. |
| Later applicability does not establish admission. | `downstream applicability is not downstream admission` | Applicability for the later exact act and consumer-local admission. |
| Meaning-relation warrant does not establish a recommendation or completion. | It does not establish `recommended performance` or completion. | Meaning-relation warrant, responsible occurrence, bounded relation, completion. Recommendation remains only the distinction apparently protected here, not a newly established subject. |

All seven distinctions are valuable and should survive. `Act permission`,
`act-permission standing`, `act performance`, `recommended performance`,
`successful execution`, `input-set sufficiency`, and `applicability success`
carry unsupported compounds into those denials. `Act-permission standing` and
`applicability success` are additionally redundant in their immediate clauses.
The value belongs to the recovered distinctions, not to those expressions.
This report neither removes a distinction nor drafts replacement Book prose.

## Direct answers

1. **Does `relationship mode` have positive active-Book grammar?** No.
2. **What does its negative comparison actually warrant?** Only that the
   intended, still-**Unknown** referent is not a consumer act by identity. It
   warrants no positive mode, participants, producer, result, or standing.
3. **Does `standing effect` remain unsupported?** Yes.
4. **Is `assertion` independently established as a constitutional subject?**
   No. The subject-establishing clause belongs to relation.
5. **What narrower role does `assertion` have?** Claim content, including
   artifact-carried content and relation assertion as an applicable coordinate;
   its maker, warrant, and exact occurrence remain local or **Unknown**.
6. **Is `act-like operator` authorized active Book vocabulary?** No. It is
   unauthorized PR 2203 report coinage and is retracted.
7. **Which disputed negative phrases preserve valuable distinctions?** `Act
   permission`, `act-permission standing`, `act performance`, `recommended
   performance`, `successful execution`, `input-set sufficiency`, and
   `applicability success` all point at valuable distinctions. `Non-performance`
   and `readiness` also preserve valuable denials using independently bounded
   active grammar.
8. **Which carry contaminated vocabulary into the Book?** `Act permission`,
   `act-permission standing`, `act performance`, `recommended performance`,
   `successful execution`, `input-set sufficiency`, and `applicability success`.
9. **What vocabulary hooks could later work incorrectly promote?** A defined
   relationship-mode family, a standing-effect result, an assertion subject, an
   act-permission result or standing, a performance subject or standing, a
   recommendation-to-performance category, an execution-success result, a
   whole-input-set sufficiency result, or `success` as another applicability
   result. `Act-like operator` could likewise have introduced an operator
   category into later reports, but it is not active Book wording.
10. **Does disposition B remain supportable?** No. Two unsupported phrases form
    one bounded defect in the same enumerated responsibility-dimension list.
11. **What exact phrases constitute the highest-confidence defect?**
    `relationship mode` and `standing effect`.
12. **What is the single smallest warranted next action?** One narrow Book
    amendment limited to that two-phrase list defect. This report supplies no
    replacement wording and does not combine the amendment with the separate
    negative-vocabulary cleanup.

## Final disposition

**D — one bounded multi-phrase vocabulary defect remains.**

The highest-confidence active defect is exactly `relationship mode` and
`standing effect` in the same enumerated responsibility-dimension list. Neither
has positive active grammar; the negative comparison for the former proves only
non-equivalence to consumer act. Their intended meanings remain **Unknown**.
The negative compounds identified above are separate wording concerns whose
valuable distinctions remain recoverable; active authority does not prove them
indivisible from the enumerated-list defect.

**Recommended next PR: one narrow Book amendment** limited to the bounded
two-phrase defect. No broader report, runtime work, registry, replacement prose,
or whole-Book vocabulary audit is recommended.
