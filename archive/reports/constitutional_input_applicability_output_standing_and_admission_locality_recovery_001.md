# Constitutional input-applicability, output-standing, and admission locality recovery 001

## 1. Executive disposition

**D. an authorial constitutional choice is required to resolve recurring
Unknowns.**

The required disposition is:

> **the candidate rule is a warranted constitutional assignment needed to resolve
> recurring ownership Unknowns**

The Book repeatedly makes applicability purpose- and consumer-local, gives a
responsible producing boundary authority to assert only its bounded result, and
leaves later uptake or admission to the exact consumer. Those rules strongly
warrant one locality model. The Book does **not**, however, say generally that the
responsibility performing every exact act owns its input-applicability
determination by default. Translation and common-grammar interpretation preserve
that exact owner/location as Unknown. Applying the model there without an
amendment would convert cross-district coherence into law.

This is not an implementation-convenience decision. The evidence supports one
canonical clause because the ownership problem recurs across translation,
interpretation, Question formation, Demand examination, inquiry, and BOGE. Local
clauses may still assign another owner or require admission. No runtime work is
lawful before that constitutional assignment.

Throughout:

```text
available != applicable != admitted != consumed != selected
selected != sufficient != warranted != authorized != executed

input applicability != output standing
output standing != downstream applicability != downstream admission
same responsible occurrence != same constitutional claim
same implementation function != same responsibility
distinct claims != mandatory separate modules, artifacts, or occurrences
consumer-local ownership != universal applicability service
producer-local standing establishment != producer authority over downstream use
operational termination != lawful constitutional stop
```

## 2. Candidate principle

### Book-recovered text

The following propositions are already constitutional:

1. Standing depends on a kind-specific production or establishment boundary and
   its validated inputs; construction, shape, availability, and reachability do
   not establish standing (`01.Standing`, bounded resolution and A-C).
2. A relation has bounded, independently warranted standing. A warranted meaning
   relation establishes neither proposition truth nor applicability or admission.
   Applicability is consumer-local; admission is required only where the exact
   consumer boundary requires it (`01.Standing.E`).
3. Constitutional uptake is a consumer-side relation family. A responsible
   consumer tests upstream material and preserved limits against its exact act and
   purpose; there is no universal uptake stage or ordering
   (`lenses-views-and-roads.md`, bounded resolution).
4. An authorized producer validates the identity, provenance, state, and warrant
   required before asserting selection, establishment, admission, or occurrence.
   A returned shape is not that authority (`constructors-and-production-authority.md`).
5. Each act consumes the subject, warrant, and conditions appropriate to the
   occurrence and produces or preserves an attributed result. Consumer validation
   is not producer occurrence (`acts-and-act-artifacts.md`).
6. Distinct local claims may be produced in one occurrence; the Book does not
   require a universal artifact, stage, or separate occurrence merely because
   standings differ. Each stronger assertion still requires its own warrant.
7. A later consumer cannot inherit applicability, admission, reliance, authority,
   truth, or occurrence merely from upstream production or identical content.

### Cross-district inference

The strongest coherent inference is:

```text
For an exact act A, applicability of proposed input I is determined at an
A-local responsibility boundary, against A's exact subject, purpose, scope,
authority, participants, consumer context, and preserved limits.

The warranted producer of A establishes only the bounded result standing that
its act and occurrence separately support.

A later act B performs its own B-local applicability examination and, where B's
road requires admission, its own B-local admission before use or reliance.
```

This inference explains the established BOGE, frontier, selection, constraint,
Question, Demand, and uptake clauses without overriding local variation. It does
not identify which responsibility constitutes the A-local boundary.

### Authorial constitutional choice

The smallest missing choice is a **rebuttable default assignment**:

```text
Unless the Book explicitly assigns otherwise, the responsibility performing
exact act A owns determination of whether each proposed input is applicable to
A. That responsibility may determine applicability, perform A, and establish
A's bounded output standing in one occurrence, while preserving each as a
distinct claim. A later consumer owns its own applicability and any locally
required admission.
```

This choice must retain explicit delegation. An upstream responsible occurrence,
an admitted warranted relation, a constitutive convention, an exact binding, or a
prior preserved result may supply applicability standing when local law assigns
or accepts that road. Upstream applicability never establishes downstream
admission automatically.

### Implementation realization choice

After amendment, realization may use one function or several, transient or
recorded testimony, and one composite occurrence or adjacent occurrences. Those
are implementation choices only if each constitutional claim remains separately
recoverable. A universal applicability service, universal admission artifact, or
mandatory pipeline would not follow.

## 4. Input-applicability ownership

### Ordinary owner

The Book currently establishes **locality**, not the ordinary default owner. The
warranted assignment should make the exact act owner the ordinary owner because
that responsibility possesses the act-specific subject, purpose, scope,
authority, participant, and consumer requirements against which applicability is
defined. This is an authorial default, not recovered explicit prose.

### Delegation

The default must yield where the Book expressly assigns another responsibility or
where a local road warrants consumption of an upstream applicability result. An
upstream occurrence may establish applicability for an exact named downstream act
through an admitted warranted relation, constitutive convention, exact binding,
or preserved prior result. The downstream responsibility must still validate the
identity, scope, currentness, authority, provenance, conflicts, Unknowns, and
negative authority required by its road. Delegation is not a universal service;
upstream applicability is not downstream admission.

### Same occurrence

Applicability may occur inside the occurrence that performs A. Nothing in the
Book requires a separate prior stage, artifact, or module. The occurrence must
make the applicability claim independently recoverable, and an inapplicable,
conflicting, or Unknown result must prevent a fabricated A-result.

### Standing produced and limits

The applicability claim produces only `applicable`, `inapplicable`, `conflicting`,
or `Unknown` standing for the exact I-to-A relation and coordinates. It cannot
establish admission, consumption, selection, sufficiency, warrant, authorization,
execution, act success, output standing, Demand, movement, truth, or later use.

## 5. Output-standing ownership

The Book ordinarily ties an asserted result to its responsible validated
production or establishment boundary. The warranted producing act may therefore
establish the bounded standing of **its own** result when it separately validates
and preserves the claim. Produced representation is not established standing and
successful execution is not automatic establishment.

A separate standing-establishment responsibility is required only when local Book
law names one, when the producing boundary lacks authority/evidence to make the
standing assertion, or when a later act establishes a stronger or different
subject. `04.Question.E` names Seed-side interpretation and bounded standing
establishment before Question formation; it must not be erased by calling every
translation output already sufficient for Question formation. Conversely, the
Book does not universally require an extra establishment occurrence for every
result.

Recoverable evidence must include the exact subject and result assertion, input
standing, applicability basis where used, act and occurrence identity, producer,
purpose, authority, scope, participants, provenance, evidence/support, known
loss, conflicts, Unknowns, lawful stops, negative authority, and intended consumer
where applicable. One occurrence may carry several bases, but none may be inferred
from the others' success.

Output standing can exist without any downstream consumer, admission, reliance,
recording, projection, or execution. Its producer has no authority over later use
by identity.

## 6. Downstream admission ownership

The receiving consumer owns its local admission when its exact road requires
admission. It first determines whether established upstream standing is applicable
to its act. Applicability and admission may share one consumer occurrence if their
evidence, claims, results, failures, and limits remain distinct.

Admission is **not universal**. `01.Standing.E` explicitly conditions it on the
exact consumer boundary. A responsible consumer act may itself rely on admitted
material without another universal reliance occurrence. Where admission is
required, it must preserve the admitted relation or standing with subject,
participants, provenance, warrant, scope, authority, purpose, lineage, conflicts,
Unknowns, and negative authority. It may not detach proposition text from the
candidate and warranted relation whose standing is admitted.

```text
standing established for A != applicable to B
applicable to B != admitted to B
admitted relation != proposition content admitted by textual identity
```

## 7. Composite-occurrence rule

The Book permits, and the proposed clause should expressly preserve, one bounded
responsible occurrence that establishes adjacent claims:

```text
applicability = applicable
→ exact act A performed with its own evidence and warrant
→ A-result standing established with its own bounded claim
```

This is lawful only if every claim has an independently recoverable evidence
basis and result; failure at one does not masquerade as another; negative
authority is claim-specific; Unknowns and conflicts are typed to the exact claim;
and later consumers inherit neither applicability nor admission.

The lawful non-performance path is equally important:

```text
applicability = inapplicable / conflicting / Unknown
→ A is not performed (or is refused/stopped under its exact law)
→ no fabricated supported/unsupported A-result
→ applicability and refusal/stop testimony remain bounded and recoverable
```

Same occurrence permitted does not mean all claims succeed, authorize skipping
applicability, or turn operational return/quiescence into constitutional stop.
Operational return or quiescence is not a lawful-stop occurrence unless a
responsible stop act and its testimony are independently evidenced.

## 10. Exact amendment target

### Placement recovery

The canonical home should be **Book I, Constitutional Kinds and Artifact
Standing**, adjacent to `01.Standing.E` and dimensional characterization. That
chapter already governs kind-specific establishment, relation standing,
consumer-local applicability/admission, and exact local coordinates. It is broad
enough to govern translation, Question formation, Demand, inquiry, and BOGE.

`constructors-and-production-authority.md` should remain the authority against
mechanical construction and may reference the rule, but it is implementation-
boundary focused. `lenses-views-and-roads.md` already supplies consumer-local
uptake grammar and should retain its non-universal ordering. Evidence/provenance
and authority/scope chapters govern the warrant coordinates but should not own the
responsibility assignment. Burying the rule only in the common-grammar prerequisite
would wrongly make a general relation district-specific.

The supported shape is:

```text
one general clause in constitutional kinds and artifact standing
+ local references/examples only where future clarity requires them
```

### Smallest exact clause to add (target, not final prose)

Add one clause that assigns, unless explicitly reassigned:

1. act-local input-applicability ownership to the exact responsible act owner;
2. permission for one occurrence to determine applicability, perform the act, and
   establish its bounded result while retaining distinct claims and lawful failure;
3. producer-local ownership only of warranted output standing; and
4. later-consumer ownership of downstream applicability and any locally required
   admission.

Do **not** alter district wording that preserves: translation's bounded source
coordinates; Seed-side interpretation and standing establishment before Question
formation; BOGE's warranted-relation/applicability/admission chain; frontier
visibility/relevance/permission distinctions; Demand establishment versus
movement; or conditional rather than universal admission. This report does not
draft final Book prose or perform the amendment.
