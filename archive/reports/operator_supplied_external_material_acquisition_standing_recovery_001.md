# Operator-supplied external-material acquisition-standing recovery 001

## 1. Executive disposition

**C. operator purpose is required only for some acquisition or attribution
claims.**

The exact boundary is:

```text
operator supplied exact material M
+ operator purpose remains Unknown
+ independently warranted Seed acquisition act A has authority and scope to examine M
+ A-local applicability determines M applicable to A
-> M may participate in A

but

operator supplied M for acquisition
requires separate evidence binding that purpose to the operator
```

The Book does not require operator acquisition purpose as an input to every
common-grammar acquisition examination. It does require the exact act's purpose as
an applicability coordinate. Here that is **Seed's acquisition purpose**, not a
silently attributed operator purpose. Operator purpose is conditionally required
only when a claim or local road depends on why the operator submitted the material,
including the claim that the operator supplied it *for acquisition*. It is otherwise
optional evidence and may remain **Unknown**.

This permission is conditional, not a present occurrence. Current production
establishes exact preservation, operator-source attribution, and addressability; it
does not establish acquisition purpose, authority, applicability, resource identity,
candidate standing, or acquisition. The Book does not specifically establish
operator-pasted material as the input of the later acquisition occurrence. Its
general external-material and applicability law can govern such material once a
warranted acquisition road lawfully proposes it as input.

Governing safeguards:

```text
operator submission != operator authorship != endorsement != truth
operator purpose != Seed examination purpose
Unknown operator purpose != no lawful Seed purpose
available != authorized != applicable != sufficient
material != grammar != attributed external grammar != common grammar
structural finding != semantic interpretation
acquisition goal != movement != authority != occurrence
conditional responsibility != current producer demand
```

## 2. Current Book acquisition topology

Current Book authority establishes only this guarded topology:

```text

-> BOGE-local applicability
-> consumer-local admission
-> possible BOGE occurrence establishes bounded grammar-acquisition goal

-> may make attributed external-grammar resources
   or interpretation candidates available

   or interpretation concerning the same preserved ingress
```

The arrows after goal standing are conditional responsible boundaries, not an
implemented or compulsory pipeline. Goal establishment supplies neither movement
nor authority; movement supplies neither authority nor occurrence; and acquisition
does not establish translation, interpretation, applicability, admission, BOGE, or
permanent common grammar.

The general external-material clauses add a distinct permission:

```text
lawfully source-attributed external representation
-> may become constitutionally addressable for bounded examination,
   comparison, quotation, attachment, or translation assessment
-> an exact constitutional consumer may consume it only within scope,
   authority, provenance, uncertainty, Unknown, and refusal limits
```

Those clauses do not privilege a source class. Operator-supplied material can be
external material without being operator-authored material, an external-grammar
resource, or an acquisition input by identity. `01.External.F` expressly recognizes
operator-supplied candidate testimony as one possible attributed source role while
refusing to turn carriage into candidate production. It does not instantiate the
acquisition road.

PRs 2171 and 2172 now supply the controlling input rule:

```text
unless explicitly assigned otherwise,
exact act owner ensures applicability for every proposed input
before participation, consumption, or reliance

act owner may determine locally
or validate exact standing produced by an explicitly assigned occurrence
```

The required purpose coordinate is the target act's purpose. Nothing in that rule
requires the source's submission purpose to equal the consumer's examination
purpose.

## 5. Submission versus examination

Operator submission and Seed examination may be separate responsible occurrences
with different purposes:

```text
operator submission occurrence
-> establishes operator-source exact material M
-> operator purpose may remain Unknown

independently warranted acquisition occurrence A
-> has Seed purpose P, bounded authority, scope, and lawful reuse standing
-> A-local owner determines whether M is applicable to A
-> if applicable and all other conditions are sufficient, A examines M
```

Seed can therefore truthfully preserve all four claims without contradiction:

```text
M was supplied by the operator
operator purpose is Unknown
M is applicable to acquisition act A
Seed examined M for acquisition purpose P
```

Unknown operator purpose blocks only assertions whose truth depends on attributing
that purpose to the operator. It does not block every Seed-side examination. This
does not make operator purpose irrelevant: an exact local road, consent condition,
scope promise, privacy boundary, source-attribution claim, or operator-designated
treatment may make it a required input. In that case Unknown excludes M from that
road unless a lawful alternative exists.

Prior context can conditionally establish submission purpose when an independently
warranted bounded interaction preserves an exact request or treatment, the
operator's exact selection or acknowledgement, and a binding from that treatment
to the next exact ingress occurrence. Such a topology is **conditionally available,
not Book-established for current production, and not universally required**:

```text
bounded acquisition-material request/treatment
-> exact operator selection or acknowledgement
-> exact next-ingress binding under preserved scope and temporal limits
-> possible operator-purpose testimony for that occurrence
```

Merely preceding M with a request, being in the same console session, or receiving
M next is not the binding occurrence. Nor does this topology itself authorize Seed's
reuse, determine applicability, or perform acquisition.

## 6. “Make resources available” interpretation

The strongest supported classification is **producer-oriented**.

The sentence's grammatical and constitutional subject is “a later responsible
acquisition occurrence”; its described result is that attributed external-grammar
resources or interpretation candidates become available. The immediately following
clauses constrain later consumers: availability does not interpret, warrant, apply,
admit, or establish preserved ingress, and newly available material *may support*
later translation or interpretation under preserved limits.

Accordingly, the phrase permits a responsible acquisition occurrence, within its
separate warrant, movement, authority, and evidence, to produce or expose bounded
outputs such as:

```text
attributed external-grammar resources
or interpretation candidates
```

It does **not by itself** assign any of these mechanisms or inputs:

```text
fetching external resources
receiving operator-supplied resources
reusing preserved ingress
attributing already available material
structurally examining a corpus
translating external resources
forming candidates from operator material
publishing to a particular downstream consumer
```

Some may be lawful local means or component acts if separately assigned and
authorized. None is required by “make available.” The exact remaining Unknown is
the acquisition occurrence's input-acquisition and resource-attribution road,
including which lawfully available material it may examine and which responsible
boundary may establish that material as an attributed external-grammar resource or
candidate for an exact downstream consumer.

This is why the Book does not already establish “operator-pasted material” as its
input. General addressability permits that material to be proposed without
assimilation, but proposal, examination authority, applicability, resource
attribution, and output production remain distinct.

## 7. Acquisition-first-consumer disposition

```text
preserved ingress
-?-> acquisition-material applicability determination
-?-> acquisition examination
```

is **conditionally warranted and requires prior BOGE, movement, and authority**.

Acquisition may become the first semantic or acquisition-oriented consumer of
preserved ingress because no Book rule requires communicative interpretation to
consume every ingress first. External addressability expressly allows bounded
examination without assimilation, and the exact act owner may judge its proposed
input against its own purpose. But all of the following must precede participation:

1. a BOGE occurrence establishes the bounded grammar-acquisition goal;
2. ordinary advancement responsibilities warrant and select the acquisition
   movement;
3. a responsible authority boundary authorizes the exact acquisition act, purpose,
   material/reuse scope, locality, temporal boundary, and constraints;
4. preserved ingress is lawfully available under source, privacy, consent,
   retention, provenance, authority, and refusal limits;
5. M is proposed as an exact input to A;
6. A's owner, or an explicitly assigned producer for A, determines M's
   applicability; and
7. A has an independently sufficient lawful input set and evidence for whatever
   structural, attribution, candidate, or other bounded result it asserts.

Availability grants none of these. Material supplied during another interaction or
preserved before acquisition began may be examined only if the later authority and
scope cover that reuse without erasing the original occurrence and limits. The Book
does not currently provide a specific preserved-ingress reuse grant for acquisition;
that coordinate must be established inside the exact future acquisition authority
or by another explicit responsible boundary.

This is an **additional first-consumer road** and an **optional acquisition route**.
It is not a prerequisite to communicative interpretation, not a replacement for the
PR 2173 road, and not proof that acquisition will yield material applicable to that
road.

## 10. Acquisition subject and pre-interpretive standing

An acquisition act must name its exact subject; “grammar acquisition” does not
collapse these alternatives:

```text
external material itself
claims represented by the material
a proposed external grammar
mechanical or structural relations derived from material
attributed resource testimony
interpretation candidates
a common-grammar relation
```

The earliest possible acquisition-material examination can concern **the exact
external material M and bounded structural findings about its representation**. It
cannot by that identity claim a grammar, attributed resource, represented claim,
candidate meaning, or common-grammar relation.

Under an exact warranted mechanical responsibility, findings such as exact symbol
inventory, token recurrence under a declared token rule, candidate segmentation,
section or entry-shape recurrence, cross-reference recurrence, ordering patterns,
and co-occurrence may receive **bounded measurement or structural-candidate
standing**. Candidate morphological or syntactic patterns require a declared
nonsemantic structural rule and remain hypotheses; if their rule depends on meaning
or language-specific interpretation, they are not mechanical findings.

The owner is the exact responsible measurement or structural-examination act,
possibly composed within a warranted acquisition occurrence if each claim remains
recoverable. Corpus appearance—size, dictionary-like form, recurrence, alphabetic
ordering, definition-shaped entries, or synonym-like clusters—may support only a
bounded observation or hypothesis. It cannot establish operator acquisition
purpose, “grammar book” identity, truth, correspondence to the operator's grammar,
semantic meaning, or acquisition applicability. No statistical, ML, LLM, embedding,
external-model, universal-parser, or language-privileging authority follows.
