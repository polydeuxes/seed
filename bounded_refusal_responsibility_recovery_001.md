# Bounded Refusal Responsibility Recovery 001

## 1. Scope and authority

This is a report-only constitutional audit. It asks whether **Refusal** is an
independently established Seed responsibility or whether the vocabulary is
losslessly accounted for by an exact reason that prevents continuation, a
competent bounded Stopping occurrence, and preservation or rendering of that
reason. It changes no Book clause, implementation, test, prior report, or
generated artifact.

The authority order used here is:

1. the active Book as constitutional authority;
2. current runtime and tests as implementation testimony;
3. current reports as attributed testimony; and
4. history as provenance testimony and a locator only.

Operator or reviewer origin, intuition, recurrence, ordinary-language utility,
chapter existence, runtime names, test names, and historical importance carry
no constitutional preference. The audit applies the source-neutral
responsibility and deletion tests used by
`bounded_decision_responsibility_recovery_001.md`, without importing that
report's conclusion.

The mandatory chapters were read in full:

## 2. Question and competing hypotheses

The decisive question is whether any exact consumer requires a distinct
Refusal result and cannot instead consume the bounded Stop, stopped road,
preserved reason, responsible occurrence, evidence, and limits.

The four hypotheses were tested independently:

- **A — distinct responsibility:** an exact requested road and applicable
  preventing reason, under bounded Authorization and responsibility, lead to a
  Refusal act, distinct result, and exact consumer.
- **B — bounded Stop under a human description:** a supported reason prevents
  an exact road; competent Stopping establishes a bounded Stop preserving road
  and reason; a later representation may use “refused.”
- **C — condition consumed by Stopping:** an independently established Refusal
  standing precedes and is consumed by Stopping.
- **D — umbrella label:** the word groups results actually owned by Constraints,
  Applicability, Authority, Stopping, request formation, Selection, or inquiry
  boundaries.

No hypothesis receives weight from the chapter heading or grammatical position
of the word. A label, status, class, assertion, or rendered sentence is not an
exact consumer.

## 6. Candidate Refusal topology

Hypothesis A requires all of the following:

```text
exact requested act, reliance, treatment, or road
+ applicable preventing reason
+ bounded Authorization and responsibility
-> Refusal act
-> distinct Refusal result
-> exact consumer unable to use Stop plus reason
```

The first two inputs are recoverable in individual cases. Applicable authority
standing is also recoverable locally. The topology then fails:

- **subject:** occurrences concern different subjects: proposed acts, admitted
  material, a requested bounded-ask road, a presented local-stop alternative,
  or a reliance claim. No shared Refusal subject is established.
- **owner:** the Book says “Seed” generally or uses an unnamed competent act;
  current witnesses assign concrete ownership to Constraint, eligibility,
  Stopping, consumer Uptake, representation, or another local boundary.
- **act:** no act is recovered beyond establishing a local condition, declining
  consumer reliance, forming a request, selecting no candidate, or performing
  bounded Stopping.
- **result:** named statuses and messages preserve a local negative result and
  reason, but no distinct common Refusal result is established.
- **occurrence evidence:** the Book requires competent evidence, yet no common
  producer signature or occurrence evidence is supplied. The direct recorded
  witness is explicitly Stopping.
- **consumer:** none requires a distinct Refusal result.

Thus A does not survive. This is not a claim that negative reasons are
dispensable; it is a finding that their preservation belongs with established
reason producers, bounded Stopping, and representation boundaries.

## 7. Stopping topology

The positive topology recovered from the active Book is:

```text
exact present act, reliance, request, or road
+ an established applicable condition that prevents lawful continuation
+ competent Stopping responsibility, authority standing, warrant, and evidence
-> responsible Stopping occurrence
-> bounded Stop preserving:
   exact stopped road
   exact reason and its producer
   scope, authority, evidence, provenance, limits, conflicts, and Unknowns
-> bounded representation and emission where an external consumer is addressed
```

The reason producer and Stopping owner remain separate. A constraint
prohibition, failed precondition, negative authority, capability Unknown, or
contradiction may constrain a road without itself stopping it. Conversely,
Stopping is not established by a status, absence, quiescence, or reason alone.

Not every lawful Stop is aptly rendered as refusal. The Book evidences goal
satisfaction, bounded exhaustion, completion of scope, return to quiescence,
and the exact operator-ingress local-stop treatment. The latter expressly does
not establish general Refusal. The evidence does support the converse at issue:
every constitutionally supportable occurrence found under Refusal vocabulary is
accounted for by (a) an established local negative condition or unavailable
road, (b) bounded Stopping when non-continuation actually occurs, and (c)
preservation or rendering. No material occurrence resists that decomposition.

## 9. Deletion test

Conceptually deleting the Refusal word before substituting established grammar
produces these results:

| Test | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Does an evidenced owner disappear? | No. Constraint, Applicability, Authority, Selection, request formation, Stopping, representation, emission, and consumer Uptake retain their owners. |
| Does an act disappear? | No. The exact local act remains; no additional common act was recovered. |
| Does a result disappear? | No. Prohibition, failed precondition, deferral, request, non-selection, negative authority, local Unknown, bounded Stop, and representation remain. |
| Does a consumer lose required material? | No exact consumer does. |
| Does a warranted distinction become impossible to express? | No. Exact reason identity and reason-producer identity are more precise than the umbrella. |
| Does an occurrence become falsely attributed? | No, provided Stopping is asserted only where a competent occurrence is evidenced and local conditions are not called Stops by identity. |
| Does reason preservation become weaker? | It must not. The unique preservation discipline should move into Stopping in a later amendment. |
| Does operator communication lose an exact necessary rendering? | No. Ordinary attributed “refused” wording may remain where it truthfully renders the exact stopped requested road. |

Material occurrence replacements are lossless:

- chapter-level Refusal -> bounded Stopping occurrence + stopped road + reason;
- constraint “refusal” -> exact prohibition, narrowing, or deferral result;
- consumer “refuse” -> bounded non-reliance under the consumer's own authority;
- Selection “refusal information” -> lawful non-selection basis and limits;
- projection/refusal preservation -> preservation of the exact attributed
  negative standing;
- bounded-ask refusal -> non-permitted eligibility + reason + error rendering;
- operator-facing refusal -> representation and emission of Stop plus reason;
- old status vocabulary -> local failed establishment/non-admission plus reason.

The deletion succeeds only with two safeguards: never erase the reason or its
producer, and never infer a Stopping occurrence merely from a preventing
condition or absent act.

## 10. Contradiction counterexample

The required counterexample preserves both non-identities:

```text
contradiction found != Stopping occurred
contradiction found != Refusal occurred
```

A contradiction may be preserved while inquiry continues, evidence is sought,
scope narrows, both standings remain visible, reliance is deferred, a bounded
selection occurs under an established basis, or an exact value remains Unknown.
It is therefore neither a universal stopping condition nor Refusal by identity.

The exact supported case is narrower:

```text
contradiction removes a warrant required for exact road R
-> responsible consumer cannot lawfully continue R
-> competent Stopping occurrence establishes a bounded Stop of R
-> contradiction, its evidence, scope, and unresolved limits remain the reason
```

Refusal contributes no additional act or result. Calling the rendering a
refusal may communicate the bounded outcome when R was an addressed request,
but that does not add standing.

## 11. Constraint, precondition, Authority, and Unknown boundaries

These are distinct from Stopping occurrence:

- a **constraint prohibition** is produced by the applicable constraint
  boundary over an exact proposed act;
- a **failed precondition** is testimony about an exact required condition;
- **outside-authority standing** or negative authority is established and
  preserved at the competent authority boundary;
- a **capability Unknown** is an unresolved value at an established capability
  coordinate.

Any may warrant or require a later responsible consumer to stop an exact road.
None performs Stopping. Refusal is not recovered as another independently
produced condition between those results and Stopping. In the Stopping chapter,
its condition-like grammatical position is inherited vocabulary, not evidence
of a producer, act, occurrence, or consumer.

Unknown discipline is maintained. Local established coordinates may remain
Unknown—for example capability standing, response meaning, or exact authority
scope. The candidate Refusal owner, subject, act, result, and consumer are **not
recovered** or **unsupported**, not Unknown: no distinct responsibility was
first established for those coordinates.

## 12. Request and communication boundary

A refusal-shaped rendering is most intelligible where an exact request,
proposed act, reliance, or treatment is present. The full local distinction is:

```text
request occurrence
-> exact road addressed to a responsible boundary
-> applicable condition prevents continuation
-> competent Stopping occurrence establishes bounded Stop
-> representation preserves stopped road, exact reason, warrant, and provenance
-> emission presents that representation toward requester
-> separately evidenced receipt
-> separately evidenced requester Uptake
```

Stop, reason, representation, emission, receipt, and Uptake are not synonyms.
“Seed refused” or “request refused” may remain ordinary operator-facing
rendering if it is bounded and accurately attributed. It cannot prove the
Stopping occurrence, requester receipt, or Uptake. Nor does every Stop concern
a request: satisfied scope, bounded exhaustion, quiescence, and other local
roads may stop without requester-directed refusal language.

The operator-ingress local stop is decisive. Identification of the exact
presented local-stop alternative supports a separate competent Stopping
occurrence; it establishes no grammar-acquisition goal and may return Seed to
quiescence. The active clause expressly says it does not establish general
Refusal.

## 14. Direct answers

1. **Is Refusal an established Seed-native responsibility?** No. A distinct
   subject, owner, act, result, occurrence contract, and consumer were not
   recovered.
2. **Is Refusal distinct from bounded Stopping?** No distinct responsibility or
   result is established. The vocabulary sometimes names a condition or a
   rendering; actual non-continuation requires bounded Stopping.
3. **What exact subject does Refusal consume?** No common exact subject is
   recovered. Each occurrence concerns a locally owned proposed act, reliance,
   request, treatment, road, or representation.
4. **Who owns a Refusal occurrence?** No distinct owner is established. Exact
   reason producers and the competent Stopping owner retain their own acts.
5. **What exact act occurs that is not already Stopping?** None recovered.
   Condition establishment, non-selection, request formation, non-reliance, and
   rendering remain separately owned and are not a common Refusal act.
6. **What distinct result does Refusal produce?** No distinct result is
   established beyond local negative standing, bounded Stop, preserved reason,
   or representation.
7. **Which exact consumer requires that distinct result?** None found.
8. **Can that consumer instead consume the bounded Stop, stopped road,
   preserved reason, warrant, and provenance without loss?** Every claimed
   consumer inspected can; none requires more.
9. **Is Refusal an independently produced condition later consumed by
   Stopping?** No producer-to-result-to-consumer topology was recovered. The
   Stopping chapter's list does not establish one.
10. **Is Refusal an umbrella over constraint prohibition, failed precondition,
    deferment, request formation, Authority limits, Unknown, or other separately
    owned results?** Yes, in the central chapter and several neighboring uses it
    functions as umbrella or ordinary wording. Those local results must remain
    distinct.
11. **Does contradiction establish Refusal by identity?** No.
12. **Can contradiction warrant a bounded Stop?** Yes, when it removes a warrant
    required for one exact road and a competent Stopping occurrence is
    separately established.
13. **Does a bounded Stop require Refusal?** No.
14. **Does every lawful Stop concern an exact request?** No.
15. **Does the operator-ingress local stop establish a general Refusal?** No;
    the active Book expressly denies that inference.
16. **May “Seed refused” remain an operator-facing rendering even if no Refusal

    the exact Stop and reason.
17. **Does deleting Refusal vocabulary lose any evidenced constitutional

    occurrence evidence, limits, and bounded rendering remain preserved.
18. **Is an active Book amendment warranted?** Yes. A later bounded amendment
    should remove the unsupported independent responsibility while preserving
    its unique reason discipline in established grammar.
19. **Is runtime amendment warranted?** Not by this audit alone. Current names

## 15. Disposition

**Primary disposition: C — Refusal is not a distinct responsibility; current
occurrences decompose without loss into bounded Stopping, preserved reason, and
bounded rendering where applicable.**

Hypothesis A fails because no unique owner, act, result, or consumer is
recovered. Hypothesis B accounts for every supportable occurrence involving
actual non-continuation. Hypothesis C fails because the apparent prior condition
has no independently evidenced producer or consumer contract. Hypothesis D
accurately characterizes the remaining family-style uses, but it does not force
disposition D: after each use is assigned to its exact established owner, one
lossless constitutional disposition is available.

A later active Book amendment is warranted. It should:

- remove the independent Refusal chapter rather than retain a chapter solely
  for recurrence or familiar meaning;
- move its unique reason-preservation discipline into Stopping;
- remove or explicitly bound refusal vocabulary where it falsely suggests an
  independent kind; and
- preserve ordinary attributed or rendered uses where warranted.

This report does not draft that amendment and does not rename the active
chapter. Runtime amendment is not warranted in this report-only slice; it
should be assessed only after the constitutional amendment, with compatibility
and operator rendering kept separate from constitutional identity.

## 16. Recommended next slice

Perform one bounded active Book amendment, not another broad audit. Its scope
should be the active Refusal chapter, its direct navigation, the Stopping
chapter, and only those neighboring active clauses whose wording would otherwise
retain a false independent result. The amendment should preserve:

1. the exact reason and its producing responsibility;
2. the distinction between a preventing condition and Stopping occurrence;
3. the exact stopped act, reliance, request, treatment, or road;
4. competent Stopping authority, warrant, evidence, occurrence, and limits;
5. genuine local Unknowns and conflicts;
