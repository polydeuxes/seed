# Initial operator communication Demand-road Fidelity recovery 001

## 1. Boundary and governing answer

This is one bounded, report-only recovery at commit `166a5f3`, immediately after
PR 2020. It changes no canonical Book, root documentation, `docs/`, prior
report, production code, test, diagnostic, CLI, event, persistence, projected
State, or cluster state. The specimen is cross-examined against active code; it
is not installed as fixture testimony.

**Governing answer:** the repository can represent one exact presented closed
choice set, one caller-captured response, and an unsupported binding result. It
can separately preserve exact operator material and carry a warranted,
explicitly selected, applicable, consumer-locally admitted meaning into
`BoundedOperatorGoalEstablishment`. It does not, however, have an active initial
communication ingress, presenter, retry occurrence producer, interaction
sequence owner, or Demand producer/consumer after PR 2019 removed the inactive
four-family scaffold. Searches find the relevant constructors only in their
defining modules, exports, and tests.

Consequently the specimen can establish **at most one unsupported local token
binding per caller-constructed occurrence**, and, after handing that binding to
goal establishment, a **refused bounded operator goal**. It cannot establish
repetition, unresolved operator meaning, absence of common grammar, a direction
to learn English, any Demand family, a Gap, or incapability. No currently active
consumer relies on such Demand standing. The truthful movement is STOP before
goal establishment, horizon construction, clarification, inquiry, acquisition,
realization, or an evidence probe.

## 2. Exact active producer/consumer topology

### Implemented, externally callable islands

```text
caller constructs PresentedClosedChoiceSet
  + caller constructs OperatorSelectionTokenCapture
  -> bind_closed_choice_selection
       bound | unsupported | unknown | conflict
       (read only; no ledger/State/cluster write)
  -> establish_bounded_operator_goal_from_closed_choice
       established only for an exact bound option; otherwise refused
  -> establish_bounded_advancement_horizon
       refused when the goal was refused; otherwise preserves a
       caller-supplied present movement boundary
  -> STOP (no Demand producer or Demand consumer)
```

The free-text interpretation island is longer but equally caller-fed:

```text
caller constructs ExactOperatorMaterial + candidates + evidence
  -> produce_contextual_interpretation_warrant_set
  -> caller supplies candidate-bound selection evidence
  -> select_contextual_interpretation
  -> caller supplies purpose-local requirement evidence
  -> project_interpretation_applicability
  -> caller supplies consumer-local admission evidence
  -> admit_downstream_interpretation
  -> establish_bounded_operator_goal_from_admitted_interpretation
  -> optional caller-bounded horizon
  -> STOP (no Demand producer or Demand consumer)
```

This second road is not an automatic interpretation of `hello` or `learn
English`. A unique warranted candidate is expressly not automatically selected;
selection requires exact candidate-bound evidence. Applicability and admission
then require evidence local to the exact purpose and consumer. The only exact
implemented interpretation consumer at the end is
`consumer:bounded-operator-goal-establishment` for
`purpose:bounded-operator-goal-establishment`; it consumes an admitted meaning,
not failed communication or Demand standing.

### Missing crossings

There is no non-test producer that:

* receives an initial operator utterance and assigns an occurrence identity;
* preserves a prompt presentation and later response in one interaction;
* converts arbitrary free text into `OperatorSelectionTokenCapture`;
* relates a retry to the earlier attempt without collapsing the utterances;
* compares failures for the same current bounded goal and exact option set;
* establishes required-result standing from that comparison; or
* consumes that standing to constrain the next operator communication.

`CandidateRequestInspection` does retain `raw_text` and maps unmatched language
to a low-confidence unresolved candidate, but it is a standalone language
inspection helper. It does not preserve occurrence time, presentation identity,
retry lineage, testimony provenance, common-grammar standing, Demand, or a
communication act. `InquiryArtifactVisibility` is a static visibility view and
explicitly does not infer inquiry movement from prose.

The Event Ledger can append arbitrary events and projected State owns Evidence,
Facts, and their support, but no active projector recognizes initial operator
utterance, choice presentation, binding failure, retry, common grammar, or
Demand event kinds. The closed-choice and interpretation artifacts explicitly
write neither the ledger nor State. Thus generic storage capability is not an
active preservation road.

## 6. Asymmetric family cross-examination

### A. Clarification of one response

* **Required result/relation:** obtain a response bindable to the exact current
  option set, or establish the meaning of this response for that one prompt.
* **Subject/goal:** the T3 response and the still-unestablished bounded operator
  goal represented by T2.
* **Materiality evidence:** the attempted goal handoff refuses; this supports
  local obstruction, not yet a required clarification.
* **Why it might own:** if the option set remains applicable and one response is
  ambiguous or malformed, clarification is narrower than inquiry or realization.
* **Defeaters:** response is a new request, the prompt was not presented, wrong or
  stale set, capture loss, deliberate refusal, or an admissible meaning outside
  the enum.
* **Unknown:** current prompt, operator understanding, intended meaning, and
  whether clarification would advance anything.
* **Consumer:** none active. A future communication-turn owner could use it to
  present the same bounded choice once, without interpreting the non-enum text.
* **Prohibited:** do not assert intent, common-grammar absence, inquiry, learning,
  authority, or execution.

This is the strongest plausible *first-failure* family, but it is not currently
established because there is no producer of the required-result/materiality
standing or active consumer.

### B. Inquiry into unresolved meaning

* **Required result/relation:** determine which, if any, warranted meaning the
  exact operator material has for a bounded consumer.
* **Subject/goal:** the exact utterance occurrence, not the operator generally;
  the eventual bounded goal remains Unknown.
* **Materiality evidence:** failed binding may make meaning relevant, but supplies
  no candidate support and no inquiry necessity.
* **Why it might own:** competing supported interpretations, or a material
  unresolved candidate after clarification, would be epistemic obstruction.
* **Defeaters:** an exact later clarification selects one warranted candidate; the
  response is irrelevant/superseded; or the goal can lawfully STOP without it.
* **Unknown:** candidates, warrants, admissibility, question, method, and value of
  learning the meaning.
* **Consumer:** none consumes Demand. The interpretation chain can consume
  caller-supplied evidence, but it neither originates nor opens inquiry.
* **Prohibited:** do not manufacture candidates or treat an unmatched token as an
  admitted inquiry.

### C. Establishment of common grammar (relational Demand)

* **Required result/relation:** establish a bounded representation relation in
  which Seed's offered forms and the operator's responses can be mutually bound
  for this initial goal-establishment responsibility.
* **Subject/goal:** relation `Seed <-> operator` within initial bounded operator
  goal establishment; it is not a property of Seed alone.
* **Materiality evidence:** repeated, correctly related current failures plus
  evidence excluding narrower causes could support it. The active road preserves
  neither the relation nor the exclusions.
* **Why it might own:** the obstruction would be relational communication, not
  merely the meaning of one utterance.
* **Defeaters:** a later exact enum response, successful admitted interpretation,
  stale/wrong presentation, capture fault, refusal, or an isolated misunderstanding.
* **Unknown:** both parties' grammars, overlap, teachability, required breadth,
  duration, and whether common grammar is necessary.
* **Consumer:** none active. A future initial-communication controller would use
  it only to constrain communication to an evidence-backed grammar-establishment
  step or STOP.
* **Prohibited:** do not collapse this relation into competency, mechanism,
  acquisition, realization, or operator intent.

This is plausibly outside the four canonical goal-advancement families rather
than a fifth family automatically. Family classification remains Unknown.

### D. Acquisition/realization of a grammar mechanism

* **Required result/relation:** realize an exact mechanism capable of satisfying
  an already-established grammar relation.
* **Subject/goal:** a Seed instrumental goal subordinate to, and distinct from,
  an established relational requirement.
* **Materiality evidence:** none; no mechanism requirement, candidate, reachability,
  absence, authority, or applicability evidence exists.
* **Why it might own:** only after comparison shows an established relation cannot
  be satisfied by an existing applicable mechanism.
* **Defeaters:** clarification, an existing parser/presenter, operator selection,
  or lawful STOP satisfies the relation without acquisition.
* **Unknown:** mechanism, capability, authority, risk, and sufficiency.
* **Consumer:** none active; the prior operational-realization Demand scaffold was
  removed as inactive in PR 2019.
* **Prohibited:** no selection, installation, learning, execution, or probe.

### E. Operator-oriented goal establishment versus Seed instrumental goal

`learn English` cannot establish either. The operator-oriented goal road needs
an exact bound choice or a warranted, selected, applicable, admitted meaning.
A Seed instrumental goal additionally needs an established operator goal and a
lawful derivation showing grammar work is required for it. Failed communication
alone supplies neither crossing. The only active consumer here is
`BoundedOperatorGoalEstablishment`, and it consumes binding/admission—not Demand.

### F. Competency Demand and Gap

* **Competency required result:** an exact demonstrated ability under a bounded
  responsibility and conditions. No such result or conditions are established.
* **Gap relation:** separately compare required competency with evidenced current
  competency. Neither side exists here.
* **Materiality/ownership:** failed communication is compatible with causes not
  owned by Seed competency.
* **Defeaters:** successful binding or interpretation with the existing road,
  operator refusal, bad presentation, or capture loss.
* **Unknown:** competency identity, baseline, coverage, failure cause, and consumer.
* **Consumer:** no surviving competency-Demand or general Gap consumer reads these
  artifacts. Existing operational visibility gaps are different local artifacts.
* **Prohibited:** do not infer missing competency, incapability, acquisition need,
  or operational realization.

## 7. Current Demand standing and smallest missing crossing

**No Demand is currently establishable from active evidence.** The strongest
implemented standing after T3 or T5 is one independently unsupported binding and
a refused goal-establishment handoff. There is no exact Demand producer and no
active consumer that relies on Demand after the former family projections and
their reference/sufficiency/selection consumers were removed.

The smallest missing preservation is an immutable **communication-attempt
occurrence** crossing that joins, without semantic inference:

1. interaction and attempt identity;
2. predecessor/retry identity and occurrence time;
3. exact operator utterance testimony and ingress provenance;
4. exact presented-set fingerprint plus proof/preservation of presentation;
5. the exact binding result;
6. the bounded goal-establishment responsibility being attempted; and
7. current/superseded standing.

That producer alone still must not establish Demand. The next smallest missing
comparison must compare current occurrences under the same responsibility and
preserve alternatives/counterevidence. The final indispensable crossing is an
exact consumer: a bounded initial-communication owner that can rely on a
required-result standing to choose only a permitted communication step or STOP.
Without that consumer, adding a Demand projection would repeat the inactive
scaffold removed by PR 2019.

## 8. Smallest implementation-ready vertical slice, if warranted

Implementation is **not warranted yet** because the exact consuming decision is
absent. If maintainers first name that consumer and its lawful act, the smallest
vertical slice is one family-neutral relational road, not a generic Demand
system:

```text
real ingress/presentation occurrence producer
  -> immutable CommunicationAttemptOccurrence records
  -> same-interaction/currentness comparison
  -> one exact CommonGrammarEstablishmentDemand candidate
       required relation, subject pair, bounded responsibility, materiality,
       evidence refs, counterevidence, unknowns, constraints, authority,
       current occurrence set
  -> consumer-local admission to InitialOperatorCommunication
  -> exactly one constrained result:
       present a bounded grammar-establishment choice OR STOP
```

The comparison must leave standing `unknown` until evidence excludes narrower
clarification, new-request, stale-prompt, capture-fault, and refusal explanations.
It must not use a numeric retry threshold as its warrant. The consumer must
reject stale/superseded occurrences and must not learn, probe, mutate, or execute.
Only this vertical producer-to-reliance crossing would make the Demand real.

## 9. How this Demand would provide C for a later evidence probe

For a later probe, **C is constraint/context, not conclusion or authorization**.
An established relational Demand could provide:

* exact subject pair and initial-goal-establishment locality;
* the unresolved required relation (shared bindable grammar);
* current occurrence/evidence references and excluded stale attempts;
* counterhypotheses still requiring discrimination;
* allowed representation and authority boundaries; and
* a STOP rule and the exact evidence deficit the probe may address.

A later, separately authorized evidence-probe producer could use C to formulate
the smallest discriminating observation—for example, whether one explicitly
presented representation can be correctly echoed or selected. The probe result
would remain Evidence scoped to its run/occurrence. It would not inherit Demand
truth, establish operator intent, become a host/service Fact, mutate cluster
state, select a grammar mechanism, or satisfy the Demand automatically.

## 10. Explicit STOP conditions

STOP now, and in any future slice, when any of the following holds:

1. no witnessed presentation or operator-ingress occurrence exists;
2. response cannot be tied to the exact presented set and bounded responsibility;
3. attempts cannot be ordered without collapsing separate utterances;
4. evidence is stale, superseded, foreign to the interaction, Unknown, or conflicting;
5. `learn English` lacks a warranted selected and admitted interpretation;
6. a narrower clarification or new-request explanation remains materially viable;
7. common-grammar absence, materiality, or testimonial authority is missing;
8. no exact active consumer and constrained act has been named;
9. the proposed crossing would infer competency, Gap, incapability, mechanism,
   acquisition, inquiry, or operational realization from failed communication;
10. the next step would select, authorize, execute, record, persist, mutate, or
    probe beyond the separately established authority boundary; or
11. retry count is the only asserted warrant.
