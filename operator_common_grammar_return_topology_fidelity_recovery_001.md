## 1. Independent active-Book recovery

### 1.1 Preserved operator material and ordinary interpretation

The Book permits externally expressed material to become addressable only with
source role, provenance or its Unknown, source-grammar boundary, scope,
uncertainty, confidence limits, and authority limits preserved. Addressability
permits examination; it is not interpretation, adoption, or truth. Candidate
production must preserve producer, source role, formation occurrence, scope,
authority, provenance, and explicit unresolved dimensions where applicable.

The active interpretation-to-BOGE road is most exactly witnessed by the Book's
cross-seam consumption, selection, establishment, and consumer-boundary rules
together with the implementation owners. Its distinct standings are:

```text
source material available
!= interpreted

interpretation candidate
!= selected interpretation

selected interpretation
!= applicable to BOGE

applicable interpretation
!= admitted to BOGE

admitted interpretation
!= bounded goal established
```

Candidate formation is a responsible occurrence not implemented on the bare
road. The contextual warrant owner consumes candidates already supplied by its
caller; it does not form them. Candidate-local warrant production preserves
exact spans, corrections, retrospective and clarification evidence, conflicts,
Unknowns, and residual source material. A separate selection occurrence needs
candidate-bound selection evidence. A unique warranted candidate is still not
selected by identity.

Purpose-local applicability then compares the selected interpretation with one
supplied downstream purpose and its consumer-owned requirement evidence.
Consumer-local admission separately accepts or refuses that applicable
interpretation for the exact consumer and purpose. BOGE finally consumes the
admission's unchanged selected-meaning snapshot. Its code expressly refuses to
reinterpret source material, regenerate warrants, reselect meaning, recompute
applicability, or recompute admission. Thus BOGE is a downstream consumer of
admitted interpretation, not the examiner of raw operator prose.

No active clause makes continuation across every boundary mandatory. Each
producer can yield refusal, conflict, Unknown, or a bounded result; later use
requires its own responsible occurrence. Lawful stopping is established by a
responsible stopping occurrence at a competent boundary, not by silence,
negative selection, unsupported response, or local non-continuation alone.

### 1.2 Exact common-grammar dependency owner

The operator-ingress prerequisite chapter currently says the common grammar is
required by “the exact BOGE consumer to examine the exact preserved operator
ingress for that consumer's declared act.” Read alone, that clause assigns
initial examination to BOGE. Read against the active ordinary road and its
implementation witness, the assignment conflicts with BOGE's narrower act:
BOGE consumes an already admitted selected meaning and expressly does not
reinterpret source.

The active Book does not name a more exact owner for free-form interpretation
candidate formation. External-grammar clauses warrant addressable material,
bounded translation, candidate preservation, and consumer preservation of
limits. Examination clauses warrant a bounded examination responsibility and
separate method applicability, selection, request, invocation, and result.
They do not assign natural-language candidate formation or its common-grammar
dependency to one named responsibility.

Accordingly the strongest constitutional recovery is:

| Candidate dependency owner | Material consumed and act | Available dependency result | Unavailable dependency result | Standing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| representation examination | exact bytes plus encoding testimony; strict decoding | decoded text with decoder evidence | bounded decoder outcome | **against**: this act expressly stops before semantic interpretation |
| interpretation candidate formation | preserved addressable operator material; form source-bound candidate meaning | candidate interpretation(s), with source limits | that exact formation act cannot presently continue, if unavailability is evidenced | **best-fit but owner Unknown**: required by the relation, not assigned by an exact active clause or connected implementation owner |
| interpretation warrant | caller-supplied exact material and candidates; evaluate candidate-local evidence | candidate-local warrant standings | unresolved/unwarranted/conflicted candidate standing | **against as first contact**: candidates are already required inputs |
| interpretation selection | warrant set plus candidate-bound selection evidence; select or refuse | selected candidate or bounded non-selection outcome | no selection/refusal/Unknown as evidenced | **against**: does not interpret raw prose |
| applicability | selected meaning plus purpose requirements; compare local fit | applicable/inapplicable/Unknown/conflict | Unknown or inapplicable | **against**: requires a selected interpretation |
| admission | selection and applicability plus admission evidence; decide local uptake | admitted/unadmitted/Unknown/conflict | unadmitted/Unknown | **against**: requires selected and projected meaning |
| BOGE | admitted meaning snapshot; establish or refuse bounded goal | established or refused goal | refusal | **against**: explicit negative authority forbids reinterpretation |
| another exact responsibility | not specified | not specified | not specified | **Unknown** |

The exact dependency is therefore an applicable common grammar required by the
responsible occurrence that would examine preserved prose deeply enough to form
interpretation candidates. Evidence must bind grammar identity or source,
scope, material, purpose of examination, applicability/current availability,
limits, and the responsible occurrence. The Book does not specify the evidence
threshold or a named owner. Decoder success, `meaning Unknown`, a common-grammar
treatment label, and BOGE's existence do not establish either availability or
unavailability.

This corrects an assignment without silently amending the conflicting Book
sentence: the sentence remains active constitutional testimony requiring later
constitutional correction, while the conflict itself remains visible here.

### 1.3 Closed-choice initialization-era role

The active prerequisite chapter names the affirmative/negative enum a minimal
communication-probe representation and a bounded treatment-choice
representation, not an interpretation menu. The broader initialization-era review also
permits structured enum communication before prose when a responsible
occurrence preserves the request's subject, boundary, standing, evidence,
Unknowns, conflicts, authority distinction, scope, applicability, references,
and lawful next movement. A closed enum remains local and must not become a
universal ontology.

For this exact initialization-era, **bounded treatment-choice representation through a
minimal closed-choice communication grammar** is the most precise combined
characterization. “Minimal common grammar” describes the dependency-escaping
effect, but the Book does not establish that label as an independently acquired
common-language competency.

The acts remain separate:

```text
responsible formation from an evidenced local inability
→ presentation occurrence
→ operator response occurrence
→ exact token-to-option binding occurrence
→ treatment-selection occurrence
```

Formation lawfully requires evidence bound to the exact preserved material,
the responsible interpretation act, the required common grammar, its local
unavailability, the resulting inability to continue that act, and the two
bounded treatments with their authority limits. Current fixed formation has
only ingress identity/lineage and constants, not that evidence.

Exact binding establishes membership or nonmembership in the offered token
set. It does not interpret free-form operator prose. An affirmative selection
selects only the acquisition treatment; it does not perform acquisition,
establish applicability, choose every realization, or grant authority. A
negative selection selects only a local-stop treatment; a separate stopping
occurrence may establish stopping. An unsupported response leaves meaning,
intent, and requested treatment Unknown. Retry is optional, bounded, and
separately evidenced; recurrence is not automatic and does not itself establish
a Demand.

### 1.4 Acquisition, evidence, and competency

The prerequisite chapter warrants only a **possible** later relation:
affirmative treatment selection plus separately warranted acquisition movement
may support a responsible acquisition occurrence that makes attributed
external-grammar resources or interpretation candidates available. It does not
make that later movement mandatory and does not make a selection its authority.

The external-grammar and learning neighborhoods distinguish the following:

| Standing | Exact recovered limit |
| --- | --- |
| candidate grammar | attributed hypothesis with producer, source, scope, authority, provenance, conflicts, alternatives, and Unknowns; not selected or verified |
| grammar evidence | attributed manual, specification, example, comparison, or behavioral testimony; not understanding or truth |
| grammar-acquisition treatment | selected bounded treatment only; not an authorized or performed movement |
| acquisition occurrence | may make attributed material or candidates available; does not establish interpretation or competence |
| translator/interpreter realization | possible mechanism; existence is not verified behavior, applicability, selection, authority, or competence |
| successful bounded use | behavioral testimony for the exact conditions; not general competence |
| verification evidence | scoped observed-candidate evidence; not verified capability or establishment by identity |
| competency candidate | bounded candidate standing; not established or universally applicable competence |
| established competency | evidence-supported bounded standing for a declared transformation and conditions; not every material or purpose |
| available competency | later-consumable current standing; not automatic selection, invocation, or applicability |
| current realization | one implementation witness under current conditions; not constitutional knowledge by identity |

Manual or grammar text acquired is therefore not grammar understood. A
responsible attribution occurrence can recover declared syntax, vocabulary,
behavior, limitations, examples, and bounded examination expectations from a
manual. Behavioral examination can
compare a declared expectation with authorized bounded result testimony and
support, contradict, conflict with, or leave Unknown a scoped capability claim.
One success supports only its bounded fragment; one failure can implicate many
dependencies and does not prove absence.

Current implementation directly represents caller-supplied candidate external
grammar and local read-only verification evidence for observed capability
candidates. It does not connect those surfaces to the affirmative selection,
does not acquire English material, does not realize or verify an English
translator, and does not establish or make available common-English
competency. Common-English competency is represented **indirectly and
partially** by general external-grammar and bounded-capability distinctions;
there is no connected direct current standing for it.

### 1.5 Retention, re-examination, and return

The active prerequisite chapter explicitly warrants retaining the same ingress
occurrence and provenance while later material becomes available. Acquisition
must not replace it with a new operator occurrence. A responsible consumer
**may** later examine that same preserved ingress again under newly available
material and preserved limits. This is optional but warranted; no clause makes
it automatic, recurrence-dependent, or operator-directed.

```text
new competency available
!= original ingress automatically re-examined

re-examination
!= previous interpretation reused

same material identity
!= same examination occurrence

later interpretation
!= guaranteed BOGE admission
```

The currently warranted return is thus availability for a new examination
occurrence over preserved X. Candidate formation, warrant, selection,
applicability, admission, and BOGE each remain separate later acts. Which
responsible consumer chooses re-examination and what evidence triggers it are
Unknown.

### 1.6 Recurring competency growth

The Book supports a graph-like, self-similar constitutional form, not a rigid
hierarchy:

```text
[established bounded competency C1]
  └─ may make attributed material M2 reachable or examinable
       └─ responsible examination may produce evidence and candidates G2
            └─ separately warranted bounded behavioral comparison
                 └─ may support established bounded competency C2
                      ├─ may expand later reachability
                      ├─ may compose with C1 for a bounded purpose
                      ├─ may cross-examine earlier conclusions
                      ├─ may narrow earlier standing
                      └─ may replace or specialize a realization
```

Every recurrence preserves material identity, source/provenance, candidate
formation, warrant/evidence, scope, dependencies, applicability, authority,
selection, realization, behavioral result, conflicts, Unknowns, temporal
standing, and later-consumer limits where applicable. The form permits common
English to make a Bash manual examinable, English plus an authorized network
acquisition to make website documentation reachable, or Bash plus repository
access to make source and manuals reachable. These are search orientations,
not claims that those roads exist in current production.

The currently implemented connected road does **not** witness this recursive
form. Candidate grammar preservation, verification evidence, supplied adapters,
and capability-related projections are separate islands. No production call
connects a newly established language competency to newly reachable material,
another competency establishment, or re-examination of earlier ingress.

## Required conclusion

1. **Ordinary operator-ingress topology**

   Preserved source material must remain distinct from interpretation
   candidates, candidate warrant, selected interpretation, purpose-local
   applicability, consumer-local admission, and BOGE establishment. BOGE
   consumes admitted selected meaning and refuses responsibility for raw-source
   reinterpretation or meaning selection.

2. **Exact initialization-era dependency**

   Applicable common grammar is required by the responsible examination that
   would form interpretation candidates from preserved prose. The exact named
   constitutional owner and the evidence threshold remain Unknown. Assigning
   this dependency to BOGE conflicts with the ordinary road.

3. **Closed-choice constitutional role**

   The enum is a bounded treatment-choice representation expressed through a
   minimal closed-choice communication grammar. A responsible binding
   occurrence binds only exact treatment tokens; the representation is neither
   free-form interpretation nor a separate conversation
   system, terminal interaction, or direct BOGE route.

4. **Acquisition and competency relation**

   Affirmative selection makes only one bounded acquisition treatment selected.
   Separate applicability, warrant, authority, acquisition, evidence,
   verification, establishment, currentness, and later applicability remain
   required where relevant. Current production represents no connected
   common-English competency.

5. **Return path to preserved testimony**

   The Book warrants retaining X and permits, but does not compel, a later new
   examination of X under newly available attributed material or scoped
   competency. Current production retains X but provides no re-examination or
   ordinary-road return.

6. **Recursive competency-growth relation**

   Availability of a bounded competency may permit a responsible examiner to
   examine new material; responsible examination and verification may support
   another bounded competency, whose later responsible consumers may compose
   it with, revise, narrow, specialize, or extend earlier standing.
   This constitutional graph form is not a currently connected implementation
   road.

7. **PR 2058 correction**

   Its implementation observations remain valid, but its three BOGE-local
   assignment claims are misassigned. The initial examination and common-grammar
   dependency belong upstream of warrant, selection, applicability, admission,
   and BOGE; the exact owner remains Unknown.

8. **Smallest currently missing connected responsibility**

   The earliest missing occurrence is a responsible upstream examination of
   exact preserved X that can evidence its local applicable-common-grammar
   dependency and inability to continue. The full entrance-and-return center is
   not one responsibility: treatment formation, any acquisition, bounded
   verification/competency establishment, competency availability, and later
   re-examination are distinct responsibilities whose lawful production
   connections are also absent. They must not be forced into one owner.

9. **Remaining Unknowns**

   Unknown are the exact constitutional name of the candidate-forming examiner;
   the exact evidence threshold for common-grammar availability or
   unavailability; the responsible selector and trigger for later
   re-examination; whether grammar-resource acquisition yields candidates,
   realization, or competency in a given occurrence; the bounded behavioral
   threshold for common-English standing; and whether any external caller not
   present in this repository composes materially equivalent callable islands.

10. **Implementation posture**

   This recovery changes no law or behavior. It finds that the missing center is
   not a BOGE-local examiner, but several separately responsible and presently
   unconnected relations among preserved testimony, upstream interpretation,
   a local common-grammar dependency, bounded closed-choice treatment, possible
   acquisition, evidenced scoped competency, re-examination of the same
   testimony, and return to the ordinary interpretation road.

No implementation bridge, object, schema, Event, projection,
or sequencing recommendation is proposed.
