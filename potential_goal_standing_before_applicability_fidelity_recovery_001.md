# Potential-goal standing before applicability: fidelity recovery 001

## Scope and result

This report examines only the live operator-ingress potential-goal branch from its application-owned source declaration to the bounded operator goal establishment applicability boundary. It changes no runtime, Event, projection, schema, fixture, test, or Book surface.

**Result:** the selected source's identity, developer-attributed role and proposition are carried through a responsible representation occurrence, exact selection, source recovery, and a separately warranted `expresses` relation. The live road does **not** establish bounded potential-goal standing or source eligibility for this presentation. It copies both role and proposition from application constants; it asserts representation and exact-set participation in the representation occurrence; and it never consumes evidence of source standing or eligibility. The meaning convention is expressly limited to warranting the exact meaning relation and cannot repair either earlier crossing.

The Book treats bounded potential-goal standing and presentation eligibility as distinct upstream relations. It does not require dedicated artifacts or Events for them, but the current producer, evidence, and authority for either relation are absent. The earliest unsupported crossing is therefore source-role testimony to bounded potential-goal standing, before applicability.

## 5. Presented-alternative formation, selection, recovery, and meaning

The application owns formation. `common_grammar_choice_set` forms two options; `common_grammar_representation_lineages` maps each alternative to a source using `ALTERNATIVE_SOURCES` and copies role and proposition from `SOURCE_PROPOSITIONS`. It states a representation purpose, representation relation, exact-set participation, application attribution, producer reference, presentation/choice-set scope, provenance, rendering loss, and no meaning or operational authority. The `alternatives_represented` Event durably records both rows before the response.

This producer does not consume established bounded potential-goal standing or presentation eligibility. Its source material is constants. Thus:

```text
source exists
!= source has bounded potential-goal standing
!= source is eligible for this presentation
!= presented alternative is formed
!= presented alternative participates in the exact set
```

Exact operator selection proves only the token's membership in the exact fingerprinted choice set and identifies the selected presented alternative. Recovery then validates the response, presentation, binding, selection, durable representation row, fingerprint, relation strings, producer reference, option content, conflicts, and unknowns before recovering the represented source identity. Selection neither retroactively establishes source standing nor eligibility.

The meaning producer requires exact recovery, testimony, convention, source-role and proposition agreement, provenance, scope, and absence of carried conflicts or unknowns. Its authority and result are exactly `G expresses M`. The Book and implementation both refuse the inferences that the proposition is true, that `G` thereby has bounded potential-goal standing, or that the relation is thereby applicable.

## 6. Consumer boundary

The live applicability function accepts one exact recorded meaning-relation warrant and checks its carried conflicts. It has no parameter, payload check, or evidence slot for bounded potential-goal standing, eligibility, or exact-set participation. Its only positive condition instead demands an already-admitted interpretation; with no such evidence it returns `Unknown`. Tests prove that the `potential-goal candidate` role and ordinary relation fields supply no consumer evidence.

The Book says that applicability examines whether the warranted relation may support this consumer's bounded use of the proposition. It also displays bounded potential-goal standing, eligibility, formation, participation, selection, recovery, and meaning warrant as distinct crossings on this road. It does not presently specify whether source standing or presentation standing must be directly re-consumed at applicability, or whether their lawful establishment upstream is enough to make the exact warranted relation examinable. Therefore the exact presently established permission is **Unknown**. Production currently proceeds from the exact warrant alone; that fact is implementation behavior, not evidence that Reading C is constitutional.
