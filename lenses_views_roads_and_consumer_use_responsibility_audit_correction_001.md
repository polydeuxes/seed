3. **Formation, selection, composition, emission:** Exposing/selecting/projecting/composing describes how the bounded representation may be formed. Emission is separate. The file does not assign one owner across them.
4. **View assertion:** A View is a purpose- and method-bounded representation. A standing-bearing classification depends on the stated warranted coordinates, not on a report-created responsibility.
6. **Road standing:** The consumer is assigned acceptance and validation and the resulting road standing is consumer- and purpose-relative. This does not establish a producer-side road act.
8. **Consumer acts:** Only acts expressly described as consumer acts or directly assigned to a consumer are recovered. Descriptive transport structure supplies no carrying, routing, or addressing act.
10. **Relations and comparisons:** Uptake and road relations are positively bounded in the prose; the inequalities establish only their stated non-equivalences.
11. **Representation clauses:** They establish results and limits, not automatically an owner or Responsibility head.
13. **Vocabulary:** The six required phrases have the classifications in section 5; none acquires additional positive grammar from an inequality.
14. **Dependencies:** Selection, production, formation, emission, and fuller admission law remain Unknown/out of scope but do not force F.
15. **Heads:** None of the three prior proposed heads is fully supported as one owned responsibility.

Answers 5, 7, 9, and 12 remain materially unchanged: the road definition, exact consumer-use preconditions, distinct consumer-relative standings, and implementation-testimony boundary were substantially accurate.
