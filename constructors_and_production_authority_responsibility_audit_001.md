## Direct answers

1. **Constructor.** A constructor consumes values or representations and produces another representation. That definition establishes a mechanical/descriptive construction role only.
2. **Classification.** The constructor is not independently made a constitutional responsibility, authorized producer, participant kind, or establishment act. It is a descriptive implementation role/mechanism; only when a responsible owner is independently authorized and realizes the required validation can a producing occurrence carry constitutional consequence.
3. **Production responsibilities.** An authorized producer must validate the required identity, provenance, state, and warrant before asserting a bounded result. A responsible witnessed occurrence may produce observer-held occurrence standing, and that producer-side occurrence evidence must be represented or preserved if it is to persist beyond the live return.
4. **Authority to produce.** Authority is established by the warrant of the responsible producer boundary together with its validated identity, provenance, state, and result-specific warrant—not by function/method existence, export, constructibility, successful return mechanics, stable identity, artifact shape, or tests.
5. **Construction versus establishment.** Construction builds a representation and proves constructibility. Establishment is a distinct standing-bearing act whose grammar is not supplied here; an authorized realization may assert establishment only after the local production prerequisites and that external result-specific warrant are satisfied.
6. **Does construction establish standing?** No. Construction alone establishes neither occurrence nor constitutional standing. A witnessed responsible-owner return can add observer-held occurrence standing; a later consumer check can add only its local admissibility.
7. **Preserved production evidence.** The occurrence must preserve or separately represent the responsible owner's witnessed return as durable producer-to-result evidence. Before asserting a constitutional result, the realization must validate required identity, provenance, state, and warrant. The file does not specify a record schema.
8. **Result ownership.** The responsible authorized producer owns its production occurrence and attributed producer-to-result evidence. An observer owns only observer-held occurrence standing, and a later consumer owns its local admissibility check; the representation itself does not own the occurrence.
9. **Producer versus consumer-local ownership.** The producer owns validation, bounded result assertion, occurrence, and preservation of its producer-to-result evidence. The consumer retains its type/registration/identity-coherence checks and any admissibility. Admission, road, selection, reliance, and downstream use remain external; consumer success does not backfill producer occurrence.
10. **Implementation illustrations.** Python functions/methods as frequent current boundaries, `seed_runtime/__init__.py`, exported helpers, directly instantiated models, arbitrary dataclass returns, and directly constructed selection artifacts are current implementation examples, not timeless law.
11. **Unsupported kinds/hooks.** “Occurrence seal” and “canonical ingress” appear only negatively and receive no positive grammar. “Focus evidence,” “named producer boundary,” and “production authority” carry only the exact local claims stated; they do not create producer kinds or a universal producer service. Public constructor, helper, function, method, module export, dataclass, and artifact shape do not define constitutional kinds.
12. **Negative boundaries.** Mechanical construction, identical fields, stable identity, constructability, successful local checks, public reachability, exports, arbitrary returns, and direct instantiation cannot establish producer authority, producer occurrence, establishment, admission, selection, or standing. Live returns are not durable evidence unless recorded or represented.
13. **External dependencies.** Selection and exact-key occurrence, establishment, admission, consumer roads/check use, downstream reliance/use, and focus-evidence requirements are preserved as out of scope. None is imported from an adjacent chapter.
14. **Definitions/distinctions rather than responsibilities.** The subject and core question navigate; CPA-01 defines a constructor; CPA-02 and CPA-09–12 provide distinctions, negative boundaries, examples, and counterexamples. They do not become producer responsibilities.
15. **Possible later Responsibility heads.** **Authorized production and occurrence preservation** is supported, with validation, bounded assertion, witnessing, and preservation as branches. “Constructor” cannot be a head from this file alone. Consumer-local coherence/admissibility remains a dependent consumer responsibility, not a production branch.

## Responsibility-head preparation

### Direct responsibilities, branches, relations, and results

- **Authorized production and occurrence preservation:** an authorized producer realizes the boundary, validates identity/provenance/state/warrant, asserts only its warranted bounded result, and preserves or represents witnessed producer-to-result evidence when durable occurrence standing is required.
- Its subordinate acts are validation, result assertion, witnessed return, and occurrence-evidence preservation. Construction is a mechanical prerequisite/realization, not an independently standing-bearing branch.
- The producing relation connects responsible owner, realization, inputs, result, witness/observer, and durable evidence. The bounded result may assert selection, establishment, admission, or occurrence only when the separate result-specific warrant exists.
- Results locally recovered are a constructed representation, producer-attributed occurrence evidence, observer-held occurrence standing, and consumer-local admissibility. These standings remain distinct.
- Consumer dependencies include local type, registration, and identity-coherence checks. Their road, admission, reliance, and downstream-use law remains external.

### Rejected false heads

- Identity, provenance, state, warrant, focus evidence, stable result identity, and public reachability are coordinates or inputs, not responsibilities.
- Representation, returned artifact, occurrence standing, admissibility, and occurrence evidence are results or standings, not heads.
- Responsible owner, observer, arbitrary caller, and later consumer are participant roles, not heads without their assigned act.
- Constructor, Python function, method, helper, export, dataclass, and artifact shape are mechanisms or ordinary implementation nouns, not heads.
- Construction-to-consumer and producer-to-result relations do not become heads apart from assigned validation, assertion, checking, or preservation acts.
- “Occurrence seal” and “canonical ingress” are negative vocabulary hooks and are expressly rejected as heads.
- Selection, establishment, admission, consumer road, and downstream use are unresolved external dependencies, not production heads recovered here.

## Disposition

**F — unresolved external dependencies prevent disposition.** The file coherently denies authority and standing from mechanics and directly assigns an authorized producer's validation and occurrence-preservation responsibility. However, the authority for each asserted selection, establishment, admission, or occurrence result—and consumer-local admission or downstream use—belongs to external grammar. Those unresolved dependencies prevent a complete result-by-result production-authority disposition within this file.

**Recommended follow-up:** None. The report preserves each external dependency without proposing cross-file synthesis or replacement Book prose.
