# Competency Evidence Consumer-First Recovery 001

## 1. Boundary and governing answer

This is one bounded, report-only recovery on commit `60d7a2d`, after PR 2015.
It changes no canonical Book, root documentation, `docs/`, prior report,
production code, test, schema, CLI, diagnostic, event, persistence, projection,
execution, or cluster state. Current implementation and the active Book control;
prior reports are navigation and counterevidence only.

**Governing answer:** no active repository consumer currently asks the exact
question **“Can Seed perform bounded transformation T from accepted
representation I to required representation O, under constraints K, for this
consumer and current horizon?”** The repository contains active consumers of
package-derived candidate visibility, executable-file visibility, registered
operation association, caller-declared examination contracts, examination
method applicability, work selection, bound probe-request meaning, and
operational-realization Demand testimony. None consumes demonstrated
transformation evidence to decide applicability, selection, authorization, or
invocation.

The smallest bounded consumer question currently *evidenced as question-shaped
material* is the bound examination request's question:

> For this selected corpus member and version, what exact transformation from
> the named accepted input representation to the named output representation is
> requested under the selected work contract and examination constraints?

That active consumer is the immediate caller of
`bind_examination_probe_request`. It may rely on the returned artifact only to
preserve request meaning. It expressly may not conclude that the transformation
is realizable, that Seed or a provider can perform it, or that it is authorized
or invoked. Consequently it is **not yet a competency-evidence consumer**.

The smallest missing crossing is not a comparer. It is a responsible,
consumer-local decision that makes demonstrated transformation standing
material to an exact downstream choice or refusal:

```text
bounded question / Demand / responsibility
  -> exact transformation required for one subject and consumer
  -X-> consumer declares what demonstrated performance evidence it must rely on
  -?-> evidence production and preservation satisfying that declaration
  -?-> P/E/S comparison derived from the declaration
  -?-> consumer-local conclusion or refusal
  -> STOP before selection, authorization, or invocation unless separately owned
```

No measurement is warranted now. PR 2014 began at a byte-window instrument; PR
2015 correctly removed its generic-owner claim. This recovery goes further:
even transformation recurrence is not automatically relevant. A future
consumer might accept one authoritative conformance run, require repeated runs,
require distinct mechanisms, or reject behavioral evidence entirely in favor
of an already-warranted contract. P/E/S must follow that consumer choice.

## 2. Canonical competency and evidence grammar

The active Book does not define a universal `Competency` artifact or promotion
pipeline. It does provide the boundaries needed to judge a local road:

* A Demand may require a transformation or competency under exact
  responsibility and scope, but Demand is not Capability, candidate, selection,
  authorization, execution, or movement.
* A Gap is an evidence-supported incompatibility relative to a declared
  reference condition, responsibility, and scope. Unknown capability, one
  unsupported candidate, and an unresolved question do not establish a Gap.
* A bounded question constrains evidence demand and lawful stopping without
  supplying the evidence, forcing a lens, or establishing an answer.
* Examination applicability, selection, probe request, probe execution,
  finding, and established Fact are separate standings.
* Cross-examination may establish only bounded agreement, disagreement,
  conflict, insufficiency, or Unknown; repetition does not supply truth,
  warrant, source independence, or implementation permission.
* Selection does not authorize. Capability or mechanism fitness does not grant
  authority. Invocation and operation occurrence require their own responsible
  boundaries.
* Repeated observations do not establish trajectory, learning, capability
  revision, causal standing, or current applicability by count alone.
* Adaptive reliance is consumer-local: the consumer must expose the standing
  relied upon, its Demand or Gap applicability, the constrained decision, the
  warrant, and remaining uncertainty.

Therefore a truthful bounded transformation competency claim is not merely
“program X exists” or “equal outputs occurred.” At minimum it is a claim about
one identified transformation, input and output representations, bounded
subject/locality, mechanism or procedure standing where material, observed
performance evidence, comparison rule, constraints/dependencies, authority,
currentness, counterevidence, and the exact consumer for which applicability was
established. These dimensions may be distributed across artifacts and
occurrences. No single universal record is implied.

### 3.2 Active topology conclusion

There are active consumers adjacent to competency, but **no active consumer of
competency evidence**. The closest two are asymmetric:

1. operational-realization Demand projection can establish that an exact
   transformation is required and realization availability is unavailable, but
   it accepts that availability as explicit bounded testimony and does not ask
   whether Seed has demonstrated the transformation; and
2. examination request binding can name an exact input-to-output transformation
   for one artifact, but deliberately stops before operational realization.

Joining them would invent a road. The smallest missing crossing is a new or
recovered consumer-local responsibility that says why demonstrated performance
standing is required between those boundaries and exactly which decision it
constrains. It is not a generic “competency discovery” owner.

## 4. Evidence dimensions for the future exact question

If an active consumer later asks the exact performance question, the following
dimensions are candidates. Their necessity and strength remain consumer-local.

| Evidence dimension | What must be preserved if required | What it may support | What it cannot establish alone |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bounded transformation identity | stable identity of T; source and destination representation contracts; intended result relation; version | that all evidence addresses the same declared transformation | successful performance, applicability, authority |
| Accepted input representation | identified input artifact/occurrence, representation kind/version, producer/provenance, integrity binding, admitted scope | that the mechanism received an input acceptable under the claim | source truth, semantic fitness, all-input coverage |
| Produced output representation | identified output artifact/occurrence, representation kind/version, producer binding, completeness/loss | what one invocation produced | correctness or equivalence to required result |
| Mechanism/procedure | responsible mechanism identity/version, procedure/arguments/configuration, environment and dependency bindings | attribution of performance to a bounded realization | authorization, general competence, causal exclusivity |
| Observed invocation | fresh occurrence identity, input/output/mechanism binding, start/result or equivalent occurrence testimony, status/errors | that the responsible boundary observed one attempt/result | recurrence, durable history, success if outcome criteria are absent |
| Result comparison | expected result or invariant, comparer identity/method, operands, outcome, tolerances/Unknowns | conformance under the declared rule | semantic correctness beyond the rule, source independence |
| Recurrence across occurrences | separately identified invocations, membership/completeness of series, temporal/environment coordinates, failures as well as successes | repeatability within the bounded series | future reliability, same mechanism if mechanism coordinates differ |
| Constraints/dependencies | prerequisites, environment, resource/authority constraints, dependency identities/versions, exclusions | the conditions under which evidence is applicable | operation outside those conditions |
| Consumer-local applicability | consumer, subject, Demand/Gap/question, decision, accepted evidence strength, negative authority | lawful reliance for that exact consumer action/projection | transfer to another consumer |
| Authority | source/producer authority for each claim and separate authority for relying action | bounded use of evidence and, separately, action if granted | competence from permission or permission from competence |
| Currentness | described time, invocation time, establishment time if evidenced, expiry/horizon, later consumer time | temporal applicability to the consumer's decision | indefinite standing from a recent or repeated result |
| Failure/counterevidence/Unknown | failed attempts, mismatches, stale runs, unsupported inputs, missing series members, conflicts, loss | refusal, narrowing, confidence limitation, revised inquiry | automatic incapability or universal negation |

One authoritative conformance occurrence may be enough for a consumer whose
question is historical and occurrence-local. Recurrence is required only if the
consumer asks about repeatability, stability, ordinary behavior, or broader
applicability. Different artifacts may lawfully supply invocation, provenance,
comparison, authority, and currentness; their relations must be evidenced rather
than collapsed into one mega-record.

## 5. Consumer-derived P/E/S/C contracts

Because no active consumer asks the performance question, there is no current
operational P/E/S/C contract to implement. The contracts below are derived
conditionals showing what different evidenced question families would require;
they are not proposed schemas or instruments.

### 5.1 Closest active contract: request-meaning preservation

* **C:** the binder's immediate caller may conclude only that one selected
  examination request names transformation T for artifact A@H under constraints
  K. It may preserve or communicate that meaning. It may not conclude T is
  performable.
* **P:** the already-compiled request projection: selected work identity,
  artifact identity/hash, accepted input representation, requested output
  representation, contract convention, method and fidelity/attribution/claim
  constraints.
* **E:** exact identity and version agreement across selection, frontier,
  candidate-work, contract, and applicability references.
* **S:** one bounded question, selection, frontier, work-set member, artifact
  version, and applicability projection.

This P/E/S answers request coherence, not competency. Adding invocation counts
or content recurrence would be precise and irrelevant.

### 5.2 Conditional operational-realization consumer

If a future realization owner must decide whether T is technically admissible
for one bound request:

* **C:** conclude only `demonstrated_applicable`, `not_demonstrated`,
  `inapplicable`, `conflicting`, or `Unknown` for that request; constrain
  realization selection/refusal, not authorization or invocation.
* **P:** claim-preserving invocation/result/comparison artifacts for the same T,
  I, O, mechanism version, dependency envelope, and request-local subject.
* **E:** the result relation required by the request contract—not generic byte
  equality. It could be exact artifact identity, structural conformance,
  predicate satisfaction, tolerated observational equivalence, or another rule
  separately warranted by the consumer.
* **S:** exact inputs or admitted input class, artifact/version, mechanism and
  environment, occurrence set, temporal horizon, exclusions, failures, and
  consumer request.

### 5.3 Conditional repeatability inquiry

If a bounded inquiry asks whether T recurs reliably under conditions K:

* **C:** establish only bounded recurrence or counterevidence for the named
  inquiry; no general competency, causation, or future guarantee.
* **P:** separately identified invocation records plus their input, mechanism,
  output, outcome-comparison, condition, and time coordinates.
* **E:** sameness of transformation and applicability conditions plus the
  consumer's exact success comparison; equal outputs alone are insufficient.
* **S:** declared occurrence series, completeness rule, time interval, subject
  class, environment/dependencies, success/failure inclusion, and limits.

These conditionals reject a universal P. The useful representation is whatever
preserves the consumer-required claim operands. They also reject a universal E:
same bytes, same structure, same predicate result, and same externally observable
effect answer different questions.

## 8. Implementation adjudication and STOP

**No implementation slice is warranted.** The repository has neither an active
competency-evidence consumer nor a consumer-established P/E/S contract. The
smallest next crossing is evidence of consumer necessity, not code.

STOP this road immediately if any of the following is true:

1. no named active consumer asks whether a bounded transformation is
   demonstrated;
2. the question names no exact subject, transformation, accepted input,
   required output, constrained decision, or lawful reliance;
3. the consumer cannot say whether one occurrence, recurrence, mechanism
   identity, source independence, currentness, or counterevidence is material;
4. P is chosen because material is easy to tokenize, decode, window, parse, or
   count rather than because C requires that representation;
5. E is generic exact equality when the consumer's required result relation is
   absent or different;
6. S lacks occurrence identity, horizon, subject/locality, inclusion/exclusion,
   failure treatment, completeness, or finite bounds required by C;
7. purpose prose is being used as applicability, admission, authority, or
   uptake;
8. package presence, PATH executability, contract availability, registered
   operation association, successful construction, test passage, or repeated
   output is being promoted beyond its actual assertion;
9. the work would add a generic PESC owner, universal competency schema,
   promotion road, capability registry, semantic interpreter, learned grammar,
   codec knowledge, execution, CLI, diagnostic, event, persistence, or mutation;
10. the proposed result would select, authorize, invoke, establish Fact or
    Capability standing, mutate cluster truth, or generalize across consumers;
11. failure, counterevidence, conflict, missing provenance, stale evidence, or
    required Unknowns cannot remain visible; or
12. the implementation is justified as preparation for a hypothetical future
    consumer rather than the smallest local producer required by an active one.

If a consumer later appears, STOP again after its bounded measurement and
consumer-local conclusion. Do not cross into selection, authorization, or
invocation without their separately evidenced owners and warrants.

## 9. Legacy surface implications

This recovery neither removes nor preserves legacy surfaces by fiat. It narrows
the evidence needed for eventual deletion decisions:

* **`CapabilityCandidate`:** its active role is package-derived candidate
  visibility and input to executable-file inspection/single-capability display.
  It is not on a competency road. Eventual removal is favored if those exact
  visibility consumers are removed or replaced by truthful package/operation
  views. Do not migrate its candidate label into a competency schema.
* **`VerificationEvidence`:** the name overstates PATH executable-file
  observation. Its active consumer is visibility composition, not verification
  or transformation demonstration. Eventual removal or narrow renaming should
  preserve only any still-required candidate/path observation and its negative
  authority; it must not be retained as an easy seed for competency evidence.
* **Structure Probe:** external structural and surface projections are lawful
  compiled metrology with occurrence-bearing results for their local callers.
  They are not a generic competency producer. Remove only if their actual
  external-material consumers disappear; otherwise keep the local instrument
  without promoting it into P/E/S infrastructure.
* **Documentation Structure Probe:** it is a compiled, tested destination
  realization and active documentation-inspection consumer. Its recurrence
  output answers documentation questions under its parser, not competency
  questions. Eventual removal must be based on loss of that destination need,
  not on failure to discover grammar. It should not become the missing
  evidence-born competency adapter.

The deletion sequence, if later warranted, is consumer-first as well: enumerate
each remaining active consumer, preserve only its truthful assertion elsewhere
if needed, remove the now-consumerless producer/export/CLI/test surface, and
avoid converting residue into a new capability registry or promotion road.

## 10. Final road

```text
C: no active competency-evidence consumer found
  -> closest active bounded question preserves examination request meaning
  -> permitted reliance excludes realizability and competency
  -> required performance evidence therefore not yet demanded
  -> P: not lawfully selectable for competency
  -> E: not lawfully selectable for competency
  -> S: not lawfully selectable for competency
  -> measurement: not warranted
  -> STOP
```

The recovery outcome is deliberately negative but bounded. Seed has multiple
compiled transformations and evidence that some work in their tested local
destinations. That does not establish a current consumer need for a general or
local competency probe. The next lawful movement is to locate an active
consumer whose decision genuinely depends on demonstrated transformation
standing and recover its exact reliance contract. Until then, Unknown and
refusal are the truthful outcomes.
