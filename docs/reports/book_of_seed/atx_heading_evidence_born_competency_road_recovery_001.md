## 13. Applicability road

Applicability is its own consumer-local examination:

```text
candidate external grammar identity + version/dialect
+ selected artifact/local-region identity
+ source evidence that the grammar governs that material
+ rule scope/exceptions/options
+ conflict, time, authority and Unknowns
→ applicable | inapplicable | conflicting | Unknown
```

`.md` is path-selected compatibility/policy testimony only; it does **not**
establish Markdown identity, version, dialect, or local rule applicability.
Repository location likewise supplies selection/context evidence, not grammar.
Operator testimony and recurring syntax can support inquiry but do not alone
establish applicability. A specification can identify its own intended scope,
but independent evidence must connect this artifact and local region to it.
Repository authoring guidance, declared format metadata, provenance from a
known producer, toolchain/version configuration, and corroborating behavior may
together warrant a bounded finding.

Fenced regions require a separately evidenced opener/closer and rule saying
their contents use a different/local grammar. Escapes, malformed forms and
extensions remain rule- and region-relative. If applicability is Unknown,
selection and invocation refuse or return an explicit applicability Unknown;
they may not silently default from suffix. Competency available != applicable;
applicable != selected; selected != authorized; authorized != invoked.

## 18. Compiled-realization analysis

`_ATX_HEADING_RE = re.compile(r"^(#{1,6})(?:[ \t]+|$)(.*)$")` could realize a
rule accepting column-zero one-to-six marks followed by space/tab or EOL and
capturing the remainder. `_heading_outline()` converts mark count to `level`,
normalizes captured text with `.strip()`, and excludes line numbers supplied by
the separate compiled fence procedure.

It preserves start anchoring, literal mark count, 1-6 range, space/tab/EOL
delimiter, captured remainder, and source line number downstream. It erases the
marker lexeme, delimiter width/kind, leading/trailing whitespace of heading
text, exact line, rule/competency/version/evidence/applicability/selection,
alternatives, conflict and Unknowns. It encodes rejection of no-space, seven
marks, leading backslash, and leading indentation; fence exclusion is outside
the regex.

The regex proves only that this developer-baked compiled behavior exists and,
with tests, is compatibility-tested for inspected cases. It does **not** prove
Markdown applicability, specification fidelity, learning, establishment,
authority, title standing, semantic level/hierarchy, or author intent. No
reopenable constitutive-evidence or establishment-history link is present; Seed
cannot distinguish learned/regenerated realization from developer-baked grammar
or regenerate it from preserved competency standing. Efficient compiled
realization is not contamination necessarily, but here its constitutional birth
and replaceability are Unknown.

Views/projections participate as Seed egress by making warranted standing and
limits usable. Before establishment Seed can expose exact measurements,
recurrence, candidates, conflicts, Unknowns and evidence requests. Such views
remain useful without forcing the operator to decide grammar. A view,
projection, explanation, or emitted recurrence cannot establish competency;
only a separately warranted establishment occurrence can.

## 22. View/projection/explanation distinctions

* A **view** asserts only that a bounded representation was formed under its
  disclosed method for a consumer; availability/emission is not external
  evidence, receipt, reliance, or establishment.
* A **projection** transforms selected source standing under declared rules and
  loss; it is not an independent observation source or new standing.
* An **explanation** connects reasons, provenance, conflicts and limits; it does
  not create the evidence, comparison, Fidelity finding, or competency.
* **Testimony** reports a source/occurrence; a **candidate** hypothesizes a
  relation; neither becomes Fact through egress.
* Egress selection may orient further inquiry or request evidence. Operator
  response remains testimony unless separately examined.

## 28. Object-bias and vocabulary-bias audit

No dimension above implies a new `Competency`, `Unknown`, `Road`, or `Demand`
object. Constitutional responsibility may be discharged through existing
artifacts, linked records, invocation context, or reconstructable evidence if
the crossing tuple remains recoverable. Conversely, a class named
`CandidateExternalGrammarSet`, `DocumentationHeadingRecord`, View, Projection,
Capability, Gap, Demand, Fidelity, Evidence or Observation does not prove the
corresponding occurrence or standing.

“ATX,” “heading,” “Markdown,” “fence,” “level,” “title,” “section,” “grammar,”
“competency,” and “recurrence” remain external/presentation vocabulary until
their bounded roles are evidenced. Implementation symbol names locate compiled
testimony; Book authority and implementation evidence, not familiar naming,
control classification.
