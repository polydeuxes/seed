## 3. Correction 1 — Warrant restored as an independent coordinate

PR 2224's Correction 6 folded Warrant into Evidence, reasoning that Warrant was an "emergent conclusion that authority, evidence, and scope taken together are sufficient." That conclusion is withdrawn. Direct evidence against it:

- `concordance.md` gives `warrant` its own row (Book: Evidence and Knowledge; home chapter: `evidence-provenance-and-explanation.md`; aliases: "bounded support, mechanism-fitness warrant"; closely related concepts: "claim standing, evidence, authority"), listed separately from `evidence`'s own row (aliases: "support, provenance"; closely related: "fact, explanation"). The Book's own navigation index treats these as two different concepts with different related-concept sets, not one aggregate.
- `evidence-provenance-and-explanation.md:42` (05.Evidence.H, "Meaning assertion carriage is not warrant production"): "An artifact may preserve a meaning assertion, its attributed source, support or warrant references, scope, and limits. The artifact carries that assertion; **it does not perform the warranting act**. A separately responsible occurrence warrants a bounded meaning relation only within claim-appropriate authority, evidence, scope, provenance, conflicts, known loss, and preserved Unknowns." Warrant here is explicitly the *produced output* of an act ("performs the warranting act"), consuming authority/evidence/scope/provenance as inputs — it is not identical to any one of them, and the chapter names a distinct "warranting act" and its "separately responsible occurrence."
- `constitutional-kinds-and-artifact-standing.md:32`: "A responsible occurrence warrants only the bounded relation supported by claim-appropriate authority, evidence, scope, provenance, conflicts, loss, and preserved Unknowns." Same pattern: warranting is a verb naming a production act distinct from its inputs.
- `construction-and-establishment.md:16`: "The meaning-relation warrant remains independently supported by bounded developer-supplied or Seed-derived meaning testimony, applicable authority, provenance, scope, conflicts, known loss, and Unknowns." Here "the meaning-relation warrant" is a named noun-phrase result, produced and preserved as its own coordinate.

**Corrected rule:**
```text
evidence: the material supporting an assertion, relation, act, result, standing, or occurrence claim
provenance: the source and lineage of that material
authority: the warranting boundary under which the responsibility may bear and perform its exact act
warrant: the bounded support a responsible occurrence establishes for the exact assertion, relation, act, result, standing, or reliance claimed
warrant-producing boundary: conditional; independently owned wherever the warranting occurrence is a separate responsible party from the responsibility that consumes or relies on the warrant (evidence-provenance-and-explanation.md:42; construction-and-establishment.md:16, where BOGE establishment consumes a meaning-relation warrant produced by a distinct, separately responsible occurrence)
```

Warrant is not required for every responsibility unconditionally — it is required only where the responsibility relies upon, produces, establishes, or presents a substantive claim or relation (matching the pattern in every citation above: a lens's own "warranted by source identity and faithful transformation," `lenses-views-and-roads.md:10`, is the narrowest recovered form; a bare applicability determination over one input coordinate, by contrast, was not found to positively establish its own separate warrant — see §5, example 2).

## 4. Correction 2 — Authority re-audited; unrecovered instances marked Unknown, not filled

Each of the five examples was re-audited against the exact question: *what exact Authority warrants this responsible boundary to perform this exact act* — not what authority the reviewed input or produced standing itself carries, and not any of the explicitly rejected substitutes (consumer-local admission, locality, scope, an input's own carried authority, an authority gap consumed as a reason, ordinary responsibility ownership, read-only form, or absence of separate Authorization standing).

- **Exact input applicability**: `constitutional-kinds-and-artifact-standing.md:37` lists "authority" among the coordinates a determination reviews ("purpose, scope and locality, authority, participants and roles, consumer context") and among what it preserves about the input ("standing and warrant... negative authority"). On close reading, both occurrences describe the *authority-relevant standing of the input or reviewed act*, not what licenses the *determining occurrence itself* to perform the determination. This is exactly the rejected substitute "the authority carried by an input." No clause was found granting the determining occurrence its own separate authority beyond ordinary responsibility ownership ("the responsibility assigned to perform an exact constitutional act is responsible for ensuring that applicability is determined," :37 — itself a rejected substitute, "ordinary responsibility ownership"). **Authority: Unknown**, bounded to this exact responsibility.
- **Authorization establishment**: `authority-scope.md:44` (Authorization boundary correction 001) names "a responsible authority boundary" as the sole party that may establish Authorization, without citing any further external grant that authorizes that boundary. This is the one case where the Book treats the coordinate as a *foundational primitive rather than an unresolved value*: the chapter is Authority's own home clause, and its answer to "what authorizes the authority-establishing boundary" is the boundary's own named constitutional standing as that boundary — a positively established, self-terminating answer, not a gap. **Authority: positively established** (reflexively, as the Book's own authority-establishing primitive; `authority-scope.md:44`).
- **BOGE establishment**: `operator-ingress-common-grammar-prerequisite.md:139` states that authority for *later acquisition movement, downstream of BOGE*, "require[s] separate warrant and responsible occurrences" — this governs what comes after establishment, not what authorizes the BOGE establishment occurrence itself. No clause in `construction-and-establishment.md` or `operator-ingress-common-grammar-prerequisite.md` names what authorizes BOGE's own establishing act. The rejected phrase "BOGE-local authority," used in the prior correction, was invented, not cited. **Authority: Unknown**, bounded to this exact responsibility.
- **Lawful Stopping**: `stopping-and-completion.md:7` (Core question) asks "which exact evidenced conditions can warrant... a responsible stopping occurrence **at a competent boundary**" — "competent boundary" gestures at an authority-shaped requirement without resolving it. `:10` goes further and marks the adjacent question `[UNRESOLVED]` directly in the active text: "the evidence that warrants a bounded stopping occurrence or the stronger completion claim for an exact bounded goal remain unclear." "Authority gaps" (:10,37) are listed among *conditions a Stop may respond to as its reason*, not the authority licensing the stopping act itself — the explicitly rejected substitute. **Authority: Unknown**, and this is the one example where the Book itself marks the adjacent question `[UNRESOLVED]`, not merely silent.

Four of five examples resolve to Authority: Unknown. This is itself a finding, not a defect in the audit method: the active Book is thorough about describing what authority a *produced standing* carries (Authorization standing's eleven required coordinates; a frontier's own negative-authority limits) but was not found, across these five examples, to be equally thorough about what licenses the *performing occurrence itself* in ordinary (non-Authorization-establishing) responsibilities. Because the corrected topology in PR 2224 treated Authority as universally required, and this pass confirms the coordinate is real and appropriately raised in every example, but resolves to Unknown in four of five, the universal-required classification is retained (§6) with its value explicitly allowed to be Unknown — not downgraded to conditional, since "required, value may be Unknown" and "conditional, may be absent" are different claims, and this pass found the coordinate positively raised (never absent) in all five examples.

## 5. Correction 3 — three occurrences distinguished

PR 2224's "production or establishment occurrence" is split, per `acts-and-act-artifacts.md:10`, which lists "standing is established" as only one of four things that can happen at "an act occurrence" ("a choice is made, material is admitted, standing is established, or an operation is performed") — confirming act occurrence is the general category and production/standing-establishment occurrences are separable specific instances of it, not synonyms. `testimony-and-established-fact.md:14,18` supplies the clearest case where the two diverge: a Fact artifact's *production* (the artifact coming into existence) and its Fact *standing's establishment* ("A Fact artifact does not prove its own Fact standing... do not by themselves show that the establishing boundary occurred") require separately evidenced occurrences.

```text
act occurrence: the responsible occurrence at which the exact act happens, fails, or is found not to occur
production occurrence: the responsible occurrence that forms or produces the exact result
standing-establishment occurrence: the responsible occurrence that establishes the result's claimed standing
occurrence evidence: the evidence supporting each occurrence claim, individually
preservation testimony: a separately retained representation or testimony of an occurrence, independent of all three
```

Sameness across these three (and across producer/owner) must be positively established per road, never inferred from same function, same paragraph, same artifact construction, same returned object, same responsible owner, or same temporal adjacency — each of which the correcting prompt names explicitly and each of which this report avoids relying on in §7.

The corrected general road:
```text
owner / responsible boundary
→ subject or material addressed
→ exact act
→ act occurrence
→ producer / production boundary, where a result is produced
→ production occurrence
→ result
→ standing-establishment boundary, where standing is claimed
→ standing-establishment occurrence
→ result standing
→ preservation testimony, where applicable
→ consumer / Uptake relation, where applicable
```
with the lawful negative road preserved: responsibility established → exact act does not occur → responsible absence-of-occurrence finding → no produced result falsely inferred (`constitutional-kinds-and-artifact-standing.md:43`).
