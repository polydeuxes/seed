# External-occurrence preservation Responsibility orientation 001

## 1. Report boundary and governing question

This is a report-only, grammar-bounded orientation of Seed's earliest inward
boundary before interpretation. It asks:

> What Responsibility, if any, owns preservation of an exact external
> occurrence before interpretation?

The investigation begins with the smaller occurrence:

```text
something external becomes present at an exact boundary
```

It does not begin by treating `Observation`, Provider Language Translation,
External Artifact Intake, Structure Probe, `CandidateExternalGrammarSet`, or
`ObservationSource` as the constitutional owner. Those are possible artifacts,
implementation surfaces, historical labels, or later responsibilities; their
names do not settle the governing question.

This report changes no active Book clause, runtime, test, observer, diagnostic,
schema, event, projection, external-material surface, candidate carrier, egress
implementation, Learning surface, or BOGE surface. Historical reports are
testimony. The active numbered Book is the highest constitutional authority.

The controlling distinctions are:

```text
external occurrence != interpreted meaning
preservation != interpretation
interpretation != Observation production
Observation != current Observation card implementation
Seed grammar != developer-compiled provider classification

Producer != Responsibility != Act != occurrence
different Evidence != different Responsibility
different source relation != different Responsibility
different Authority != necessarily different Responsibility
same output shape != same Responsibility
same implementation function != same Responsibility
```

The report does not question whether Observation is lawful Seed grammar.
Active law positively says that an Observation is Seed-native testimony formed
after acquisition and interpretation. The question here is what precedes that
formation, what current code preserves, and where current code compresses the
prior responsibilities.

## 3. Active Book constraints

### 3.1 External material can be addressable without assimilation

`01.External.A` says provider vocabulary, external representations, and source
confidence enter only as attributed source grammar. External material is not
its translated representation, and translation is not interpretation,
Evidence, applicability, admission, comparison, or a result of either act.
`01.External.B` permits an external representation to become constitutionally
addressable for comparison, quotation, attachment, or translation assessment
without becoming Constitutional Grammar. Addressability must preserve source
role or its Unknown, provenance or its Unknown, external grammar boundary,
scope, uncertainty, confidence limits, and Authority limits.

These clauses support a pre-interpretation addressability boundary. They do not
name one universal acquisition or preservation Responsibility, one carrier
noun, one mandatory recording form, or one producer.

### 3.2 Observation follows acquisition and interpretation

The sensing amendment in `05.Testimony` controls this report's Observation
boundary:

```text
Sensing is production of bounded testimony about an environment, operation,
or condition through a declared acquisition and interpretation boundary.

External provider material is not an Observation.

An Observation is Seed-native testimony formed after acquisition and
interpretation.
```

Therefore:

* acquisition must already have occurred before Observation formation;
* an interpretation boundary must already have occurred before Observation
  formation;
* Observation production is a further production of bounded testimony, not
  acquisition or interpretation by identity;
* the Book does not require every implementation to materialize three classes
  or three events;
* current code may compress the responsibilities even though active law keeps
  their constitutional claims distinct.

The same chapter says observer-held occurrence evidence is not preserved
occurrence evidence, recorded testimony is not established Fact, and
Observation is not belief. Its constrained-movement correction says producing
Observation does not by identity admit testimony, revise standing, open
movement, compare, establish, or project current truth.

### 3.3 Operator-response law positively exposes capture before Compare

The active communication and goal-establishment chapters provide the strongest
positive local topology:

```text
C is formed and emitted
→ the operator may produce response material R
→ capture preserves R and its response-occurrence lineage
→ a responsible comparison act consumes captured R and C's exact response
  coordinates
→ a responsible identification act may consume the comparison finding,
  exact C, and an applicable T-to-A relation
```

Active law also states that the independently established external fact is
only that the operator produced R after C was emitted. Capture does not create
the T-to-A relation. Exact token equality is not communicative meaning.
Comparison and Identification are distinct even when one Seed occurrence
performs both. Identification does not establish intent, understanding,
selection, assent, authorization, source meaning, or treatment.

This is positive evidence for a local capture/preservation act-shaped
coordinate. It is not positive evidence that `Preservation` is the exact name
of a universal Responsibility, that all ingress begins with Presentation, or
that response capture and every provider acquisition share an owner.

### 3.4 Preservation yields no stronger standing automatically

Active law repeatedly separates construction, production, occurrence evidence,
recording, and standing. A constructor can build a representation without
proving the named producer occurrence. A warranted producer may establish only
the bounded standing of its own result. Representation and recording do not
strengthen source standing. The operator chapter specifically says later acts
may preserve response lineage but derive no standing merely from response or
Identification.

Thus pre-interpretation preservation can lawfully establish bounded occurrence
and recoverability testimony. It does not establish meaning, Observation
testimony, Evidence support, Fact standing, applicability, admission,
selection, goal, Authority, or future reliance.

## 4. Strongest current witness: post-2319 operator ingress

### 4.1 Exact current implementation spine

The historical names named in the request still survive, with responsibilities
distributed across these current surfaces:

```text
capture_stdin_material
  → CapturedOperatorMaterial
  → run_operator_ingress_attempt
  → _capture_representation
      → operator.ingress.raw_material_captured
      → examine_text_representation
      → operator.ingress.representation_examined
  → operator.ingress.ingress_occurred
  → OperatorIngressAddressableMaterial projection
  → run_operator_response_comparison_and_identification
      → operator.exchange.comparison_occurred
      → operator.exchange.identification_occurred
```

`capture_stdin_material` reads one `readline()` frame. For production text
stdin with `.buffer`, it reads `stdin.buffer.readline()` and calls the origin
`direct_boundary_observation`. A supplied binary stream is also direct boundary
observation. A programmatic text stream is explicitly different: the function
re-encodes already-decoded text, marks
`text_reencoding_after_prior_decoding`, and records that original transport
bytes and prior decoder behavior are unavailable.

`CapturedOperatorMaterial` holds:

* `exact_bytes`;
* EOF identity;
* exact recognized line delimiter (`0a`, `0d0a`, or none);
* capture-boundary description;
* byte-material origin;
* stream encoding testimony, if supplied;
* known loss.

Its invariant validation checks exact byte type, EOF coherence, and delimiter
coherence. This is the earliest current object at which received boundary
material is held without a claim about communicative meaning.

### 4.2 Earliest durable preservation point

The earliest in-memory preservation is the returned
`CapturedOperatorMaterial`. The earliest durable event-ledger preservation is
`operator.ingress.raw_material_captured`, produced inside
`_capture_representation`. That event stores the exact bytes as hexadecimal,
byte count, EOF, delimiter, capture boundary, origin, encoding testimony,
known loss, role, and lineage. Its implementation dimension says:

```text
responsibility = competent-raw-material-capture
authority = occurrence evidence only
standing = captured
occurrence = exact boundary bytes durably preserved as hexadecimal
mutates_cluster = false
```

These implementation strings are current behavior witnesses, not by themselves
constitutional names. They do, however, prove that the implementation can
durably preserve what crossed its available boundary before it claims meaning.

### 4.3 Representation handling is subsequent and non-semantic

`examine_text_representation` is called only after raw capture. It selects the
stream's encoding testimony or an implementation UTF-8 fallback and performs a
strict decoder invocation. Its result distinguishes:

* `decoded`;
* `decoder_unavailable`;
* `bytes_rejected`.

`RepresentationExamination` calls itself one bounded decoder invocation and
its evidence, not an encoding verdict. The examination event records mechanism,
selection basis, outcome, failure, raw-capture lineage, and the explicit
Unknown `true source-relative encoding Unknown`. Its current implementation
dimension calls the responsibility
`bounded-representation-evidence-production` and bounds Authority to decoder
outcome evidence.

Decoding is therefore visibly separate from byte capture. This report does not
silently equate decoding with interpretation. The implementation establishes
representation availability under a selected mechanism; it does not establish
communicative meaning, operator intent, or provider/source-relative encoding.

If decoding fails, a separate stopping event closes the interaction. The
original raw bytes remain recorded. Absence of a represented text does not undo
preservation. This is particularly strong evidence that interpretation or even
successful representation handling is not required for preservation.

### 4.4 Ingress occurrence and exact lineage

After successful decoding, `run_operator_ingress_attempt` records
`operator.ingress.ingress_occurred`. It stores:

* decoded text with its original captured delimiter in `raw_input` and
  `decoded_text`;
* an occurrence content dimension with one trailing `\n` and then `\r`
  removed for later comparison;
* the raw capture event id;
* the representation-examination event id;
* known loss and ordered lineage;
* where supplied, exact presentation formation and emission references.

The current event says `authority="occurrence-only; meaning Unknown"` and
describes strictly decoded text as preserving capture/examination lineage. The
projector changes the projected current standing for the ingress subject to
`preserved`; this is a current view label, not automatically the constitutional
name or all of its Standing.

Where a preceding C exists, the event records only that this material was
produced after that exact presentation emission. The function documentation
expressly refuses to classify it as a response or infer intent, selection, or
requested treatment. Thus the implementation spine is:

```text
C emitted
→ operator-side external occurrence
→ exact available boundary bytes captured
→ decoder occurrence examined and recorded
→ decoded ingress occurrence recorded with capture/examination lineage
→ C-produced-after lineage attached
```

No meaning has been established at this point.

### 4.5 Addressable material remains pre-semantic

For successfully decoded ingress, the projector can form an
`OperatorIngressAddressableMaterial` without writing another ledger event. It
contains an `ExactOperatorMaterial` with exact decoded text, one full source
span, and ordered raw-capture/examination/ingress provenance. Its explicit
Unknowns include communicative meaning, operator intent, operator goal,
Seed-question applicability, and next-consumer applicability. Its Authority
limits permit only addressability and exact-material carriage and state that
decoder success is representation availability, not interpretation or
competency.

This projection makes the preserved occurrence addressable. It does not make
the text an Observation, interpretation candidate, warrant, selection,
applicability, admission, goal, Demand, movement, authorization, or execution.

### 4.6 What later Compare actually consumes

`run_operator_response_comparison_and_identification` retrieves the recorded
presentation formation and emission, the recorded ingress occurrence, and the
raw-capture event. It refuses mismatched workspace/session/presentation,
missing formation or emission, missing produced-after references, and a capture
from a different attempt.

Its `compared_representation` is not a newly inferred meaning. It is the
ingress event's recorded `dimensions.content`: strictly decoded text with the
single trailing line delimiter removed by ingress. Compare performs no further
trim, normalization, case folding, or interpretation. It compares that exact
recorded representation to the response coordinates recorded in C's
alternatives and records match or no-coordinate-match.

The Compare lineage includes C formation, C emission, raw capture, and ingress.
The subsequent Identification separately consumes the comparison result,
exact C, and coordinate binding. This proves that later Compare consumes a
preserved occurrence representation and its lineage; it does not retroactively
define what was received. It also proves a lawful path on which meaning remains
Unknown before, during, and after exact coordinate comparison.

## 5. Observation boundary and current card compression

### 5.1 What makes something an Observation under active law

Observation is not made native by the Python class name. Under active law it is
Seed-native bounded testimony produced through a declared acquisition and
interpretation boundary. The exact production therefore must warrant the
testimony it forms, preserving applicable source, method, scope, temporal,
conflict, Authority, loss, and uncertainty limits. Observation production is
not external occurrence, acquisition, preservation, decoding, interpretation,
Evidence, Fact, or current Standing by identity.

### 5.2 What the current `Observation` card represents

The current `Observation` model contains id, source type, observed time,
subject, predicate, value, confidence, metadata, dimensions, and optional
expiry. Its constructor validates secret fields and confidence bounds. It does
not carry a required raw-source occurrence reference, acquisition occurrence,
exact source representation, decoder identity, interpretation act, interpreter
producer, interpretation warrant, known-loss field, conflicts field, or
explicit Unknowns field.

`ObservationSource.collect()` already returns `list[Observation]`.
`ObservationCollectionService` therefore begins after provider decoding and
provider-specific shaping. `ObservationIngestor` begins later still: it records
the supplied Observation and mechanically constructs Evidence and usually a
Fact-shaped artifact. It does not recover or validate the prior acquisition or
interpretation occurrence.

The card is a useful canonical implementation representation, but it compresses
the active-law path. The compression does not make Observation unlawful and
does not make the current card identical to Observation grammar. It means that
the card's fields and construction alone cannot prove the separate acquisition,
interpretation, or Observation-production warrants.

## 7. External-material structural path

The structural projection family supplies a useful adjacent non-semantic
witness:

```text
caller-attributed source/artifact identity
→ caller-supplied exact text and encoding
→ manifest/hash/count validation
→ exact lines and mechanically contiguous nonblank regions
```

What survives is source/artifact identity, expected and recomputed artifact
hash, encoding label, exact line text including terminators, character offsets,
blankness, stable line/region identities, input Unknown strings, and explicit
non-semantic boundary notes. It is read-only, does not write the ledger, and
does not mutate the cluster.

What remains compressed or caller-supplied is the external acquisition,
original byte occurrence, byte-to-text decoding, source attribution warrant,
manifest production, encoding correctness, and why the supplied text is the
external artifact. Re-encoding exact text for hash comparison validates a
caller-supplied representation relation; it does not recover the earlier
external occurrence.

This path proves addressability and mechanical projection before semantics:

```text
exact text != meaning
line != semantic unit
region != grammar
recurrence != interpretation
mechanical projection != Observation by identity
```

## 8. `CandidateExternalGrammarSet` as contrast witness

Current implementation names itself “Caller-supplied candidate external grammar
preservation.” `CandidateExternalGrammarInput` and its candidates arrive fully
supplied with ids, structural claims, scope, provenance strings, testimony
references, alternatives, and explicit Unknown strings. Assembly checks only
duplicate ids and returns a read-only set. It does not write the ledger, mutate
the cluster, generate a candidate, evaluate testimony, compare, select, verify,
promote, establish, interpret, or produce Observation.

The post-excision reconciliation correctly distinguishes:

1. a Seed-generated candidate, which requires a Seed candidate-formation
   occurrence and provenance;
2. an operator/caller-supplied candidate, which is attributed candidate
   testimony;
3. an external-source rule, which remains attributed external grammar.

Only the second producer identity is implemented in this class. No Seed
candidate producer occurrence is recovered here. Consequently
`CandidateExternalGrammarSet` does not participate in the native operator
capture → preserved ingress → Compare path. It is attributed injected candidate
testimony, not Seed learning and not Seed Observation. The constitutional
coordinates “candidate,” “external,” or “grammar” may be valid elsewhere, but
the class name cannot decide them.

## 9. Null testimony cross-examined against modern ingress

The Null audit found that bounded external representation could become present
to an existing local Responsibility before Observation, meaning, goal, or
inquiry existed. Its `.` experiment preserved a raw inquiry note and showed
that later read-only orientation did not retroactively give the period meaning.
It explicitly refused a universal Null manager, universal first event,
automatic observer selection, goal creation, or semantic interpretation.

That older witness lacked an exact production-grade byte-boundary occurrence
with durable raw/examination/ingress lineage. The post-2319 operator spine now
supplies that missing local implementation witness:

* direct byte-boundary capture where the stream exposes bytes;
* honest marking of prior-decoded text adapters;
* durable exact-byte preservation;
* distinct decoder examination including failure;
* occurrence-only ingress with meaning Unknown;
* exact C-produced-after lineage where present;
* later Compare over recorded content rather than retroactive receipt
  classification.

The modern path therefore confirms the older narrow shape in one operator
family. It does not make the Null vocabulary universal, prove the inquiry-note
and operator-response owners identical, or authorize a universal ingress
gateway/bootstrap lifecycle.
