# Multi-input Compare constitutional-relations recovery 001

## 1. Disposition and authority boundary

This report recovers only the constitutional relations already warranted around
`05.Testimony.E`. It does not amend active law, define a recursive candidate
engine, or authorize construction.

Authority was applied in this order:

1. the active numbered Book;
2. current runtime and tests as subordinate implementation testimony;
3. prior reports and history as subordinate locator or worked-road testimony.

The controlling clauses are `05.Testimony.E`, `05.Testimony:113`,
`05.Recording`'s comparison boundaries, `01.Standing.E` and
`01.Standing.E.1`, the general Uptake boundary, and `05.Evidence`. Active law
is sufficient to recover a small local topology, but not a recursive or
cross-pollinating one.

The classifications used below are:

- **A — independently warranted** by active law;
- **B — implementation/worked-road assumption** only;
- **C — locally warranted but not universal / requires decomposition**;
- **D — exact relation remains Unknown**.

## 2. Subjects and distinctions held fixed

`05.Testimony.E` supplies the exact input class: **multiple independently
preserved testimonies or findings**. This is a disjunction over independently
preserved input subjects; it is not a constitutional declaration that every
available artifact, claim, relation candidate, Evidence object, projection,
record, or established Standing is eligible. Each proposed input must still be
applicable to the exact Compare occurrence.

Multiplicity does not establish a collection or collective subject under
`01.Standing.D`. Thus several lawful inputs may participate in one occurrence
without becoming one collective constitutional subject. Independence here is
preservation of each input and its source-local coordinates; comparison does
not prove source independence or independent corroboration.

This recovery preserves all of the following non-identities:

```text
comparison occurrence != comparison finding != recorded comparison

candidate relation != relation testimony != established relation Standing

testimony carried != relation warranted

prior subject retained != applicable to current Compare

multiple inputs available != one collective subject

later Standing != proof of one universal traversal
```

The phrase **candidate relation** below means a proposed bounded relation claim,
not an assumed container for all Compare inputs and not an assumed output of
Compare. `01.Standing.E` expressly separates candidate relation, relation
testimony, and evidence-supported or established relation Standing.

## 4. Governing questions answered

### 4.1 Exact candidate subjects

The only universal candidate class recovered for `05.Testimony.E` is:

```text
independently preserved testimony
or
independently preserved finding
```

“Candidate” in this sentence means proposed input to the exact Compare, not the
constitutional subject `candidate relation`. A relation candidate, relation
testimony, comparison finding, Fact, or relation Standing can enter this class
only through its own local decomposition: it must be represented by a qualifying
independently preserved testimony/finding, proposed for the exact input role,
and found applicable. Active law does not make every prior subject a candidate.

### 4.2 Responsibility for Applicability

The Responsibility assigned to perform the exact Compare ordinarily owns
ensuring Applicability for each proposed input. An explicitly assigned
responsible occurrence may instead establish applicability for that exact
downstream act, which the owner validates and consumes. The owner is not a
global “Compare service,” and the delegate is not a universal applicability
service. Applicability belongs to the relation:

```text
this exact proposed input
-> this exact Compare act and occurrence boundary
```

not to the retained subject in isolation.

### 4.3 Exact result

Multi-input Compare may produce **bounded relation standing inside the
comparison boundary**. Supported examples are agreement, disagreement,
contradiction, conflict, refinement, unmet requirements, or Unknown; the active
clause permits more than one and does not establish an enum. The occurrence,
finding/result representation, and warranted relation Standing remain distinct.

The result does not by itself establish truth, reliance, source independence,
implementation permission, reconciliation, Fact standing, recording, or later
Applicability.

### 4.4 Finding, candidate relation, testimony, Evidence, Warrant, Standing

There is no single recovered chain joining all six nouns. The independently
lawful local relations are instead:

```text
responsible Compare occurrence
  -> may produce a bounded comparison finding

bounded comparison finding
  -> may carry one or more bounded relation assertions

claim-appropriate Evidence
  -> may support a separately responsible warranting occurrence

responsible warranting occurrence
  -> may establish only the bounded relation Standing it warrants

independently preserved finding
  -> may later be proposed as premise-relative testimony/input
```

The joins are conditional and local. In particular:

```text
relation assertion carried != relation warranted
comparison finding != candidate relation
relation testimony != Evidence
Evidence != Warrant
Warrant != Applicability
relation Standing != later use
```

### 4.5 Later candidate participation

A comparison finding can participate in later **proposed input formation** only
in the narrow sense already granted to independently preserved findings: it may
be proposed to a later exact Compare and then undergo a fresh applicability
determination. Active law does not establish that it becomes a `candidate
relation`, nor that preservation automatically proposes or schedules it.

### 4.6 Newly applicable old material

New relation Standing does not retroactively make old testimony or findings
applicable. A later exact Compare owner may make a fresh applicability
determination for a previously preserved proposed input, using whatever material
is itself lawful and applicable to that determination. That local possibility is
not an arrow from new relation Standing to old input Applicability.

### 4.7 Recursion and cross-pollination

Active law establishes only independently lawful local relations. It permits a
preserved finding from one occurrence to be proposed to another occurrence and
permits later evidence-supported revision of bounded Standing. It does not
establish automatic proposal, candidate generation, recursive invocation,
cross-pollination, back-propagation of Applicability, traversal order,
termination, convergence, or one universal semantic pipeline.

## 5. Bounded topology: A/C edges only

The following is the complete recovered topology. Bracketed conditions are
part of the edge; removing them would overstate the warrant.

```text
independently preserved testimony or finding
  --[proposed to exact Compare]-->
proposed exact input

proposed exact input
  --[exact Compare owner ensures, or validates exact delegated determination]-->
input-to-this-Compare applicability standing
  {applicable | inapplicable | conflicting | Unknown}

applicable input standings for multiple independently preserved inputs
  --[responsible Compare occurrence; any local admission requirements met]-->
bounded comparison finding
  --[claim-appropriate Evidence + Authority + Warrant + scope + provenance
      + conflicts/Unknowns + responsible output-standing boundary]-->
one or more bounded relation Standings inside that comparison boundary

bounded comparison finding
  --[independently preserved with attribution and all source-local limits]-->
preserved premise-relative finding/testimony
  --[separately proposed; fresh later applicability determination]-->
possible input participation in one later exact Compare

bounded relation Standing or preserved finding
  --[separate later consumer-local occurrence under exact requirements]-->
bounded later assertion or Standing
```

The topology contains no automatic cycle. Its final two arrows are repeatable
local possibilities, not an invocation rule and not proof that any later
occurrence happens.

## 7. Current operator Compare is a special local instance

The current runtime's operator response-versus-Presentation-coordinate road is
implementation testimony for a narrower comparison. It consumes captured
operator material and one exact Presentation's response coordinates, records a
comparison result, and then performs a distinct Identification boundary. Its
subjects, exact-coordinate matching method, lineage checks, event shapes, and
consumer purpose are local.

It is compatible with the broader discipline that a bounded Compare preserves
exact subjects and limits and produces no stronger result than its method
warrants. It is therefore **C: a special local instance of bounded comparison
discipline**, not evidence for the general input grammar, candidate topology,
or result enum of `05.Testimony.E`. Whether its response material and coordinate
set instantiate “multiple independently preserved testimonies or findings” is
**D**; implementation naming and mechanical comparison do not establish that
constitutional classification. Its Compare-to-Identification road must not be
generalized into Compare-to-candidate, Compare-to-relation-Standing, or a
universal continuation.

## 9. Smallest unresolved arrow

The smallest unresolved arrow exposed by the recovery is:

```text
independently preserved comparison finding
  -?->
formation as a proposed input to a later exact Compare
```

Active law warrants that such a finding **may be consumed if proposed and found
applicable**, but it does not identify the responsibility or occurrence that
forms that later proposal. Preservation is not proposal; availability is not
Applicability; and the gap must remain open.
