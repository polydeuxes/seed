# Examination Clause Disposition Recovery 001

## 1. Executive finding

This is a disposition recovery, not a Book amendment. The current `examination-methods-and-probes.md` shell does not state an independently necessary constitutional subject. Its title groups a deleted implementation theme; its subject is conditional architecture; its core question presupposes a probe-request subject; and its bounded resolution mixes durable distinctions with repository-status prose. Neither the pre-2144 implementation-shaped framing nor PR 2144's repository-status replacement belongs in stable constitutional grammar.

The three clauses do not share that disposition:

- **04.Examination.A is independently warranted but not independently housed.** It establishes consumer- and responsibility-local relevance for further examination: recorded or visible material is not relevant merely by preservation, visibility, or possible relation. Current frontier and evidence clauses supply most components, but no other single clause establishes the composite relevance judgment and its limited standing. Merge that distinction, in narrowed form, into `book_of_seed/04-inquiry-and-examination/inquiry-frontiers.md`, `04.Frontier.B — Positive frontier support requires claim-relative warrant`.
- **04.Examination.B is a scoped duplicate whose useful precision belongs in general stopping grammar.** Book VIII already makes evidence gaps, authority gaps, Unknowns, and refusal lawful stopping constraints and states that stopping is not failure. B usefully requires a distinguishable reason and distinguishes insufficient binding from a supported negative, but those are not examination-only law. Merge that precision into `book_of_seed/08-authority-communication-and-stopping/refusal-and-non-performance.md`, `Bounded resolution`; do not preserve B as an examination clause.
- **04.Examination.C is independently warranted and currently fills a real general-testimony gap.** Current Book V preserves one testimony's source-relative standing and forbids repetition from strengthening it, but does not fully govern comparison among independently preserved testimonies, the bounded comparison standings, or conflict without source-local erasure. Reports 010–012 survive as convergent testimony for those boundaries. The deleted representation-grammar witnesses remove one implementation-recurrence leg, not the distinction. Merge C into `book_of_seed/05-evidence-and-knowledge/testimony-and-established-fact.md`, `05.Testimony.A — Premise-relative testimony without fact standing`.

The later Book PR should excise the chapter after those two independently warranted distinctions are merged and B's useful precision is merged into Book VIII. No broad historical recovery is required.

## 7. Clause A analysis

### Exact current text and context

Under `Addressable boundaries for bounded examination witnesses`, immediately before B, A reads:

> **04.Examination.A — Bounded relevance before movement**  
> A bounded examination responsibility may treat recorded material as relevant only when explicit record material supports the local responsibility, subject binding, evidence or provenance requirements, and authority boundary required by that responsibility. The produced standing is permission to examine further inside that boundary. The forbidden inference is selection, execution, mutation, truth establishment, or required action.

### Findings

1. **Exact distinction.** Recorded/visible material and even a possible relation do not become relevant to a current inquiry responsibility by existence. A responsible, bounded relevance judgment requires local subject, support/provenance, and authority binding; it produces permission only for further examination, not movement's stronger standings.
2. **Already stated elsewhere?** Its components are, but not the whole composite. `04.Frontier.B` requires claim-relative warrant for positive frontier support; `05.Evidence.A` requires consumer-local evidence dimensions for applicable represented support; `05.Evidence.G` denies semantic support from visibility/membership; `04.Question.D` separates applicability from evidence warrant. No one other clause names the resulting bounded relevance posture over general recorded material and limits it to further examination.
3. **Frontier membership sufficient?** No. `04.Frontier.A/B` expressly make membership visibility and require separate claim-relative warrant. Membership can expose material without making it relevant.
4. **General selection sufficient?** No. Selection occurs after or separately from applicability/relevance and can lawfully select zero candidates; A expressly denies that relevance is selection.
5. **Relevance distinct from selection?** Yes. Relevance is consumer-local eligibility/support for further examination; selection is the responsible choice from a bounded candidate set.
6. **“Movement” defined?** It is general Book vocabulary for a lawful transition, but A's title uses it loosely. The body supplies the exact negative boundaries; the title's “movement” is explanatory compression, not a new constitutional kind.
7. **Probe dependency?** No. The clause mentions examination responsibility but no method, probe, request, or deleted class. Its distinction applies to recorded material entering a bounded inquiry.
8. **Independent clause required?** Independent grammar is required, but an independent examination clause is not. The distinction can be merged into an existing frontier clause.
9. **Merge without loss?** Yes, if `04.Frontier.B` absorbs the consumer/responsibility-local relevance judgment and its no-selection/no-truth/no-action limit. This report does not draft that wording.
10. **Strongest home.** `book_of_seed/04-inquiry-and-examination/inquiry-frontiers.md`, `04.Frontier.B — Positive frontier support requires claim-relative warrant`.
11. **Gap on bare removal?** Yes. Bare deletion would leave the ingredients scattered and lose the explicit limited standing “permission to examine further.” Removal after a faithful narrowed merge would not.
12. **Explanatory-only material.** “Movement” is compressive framing; the enumerated forbidden inferences partly repeat selection, acts, authority, and fact grammar. The irreducible core is the local relevance/support judgment and its limited further-examination standing.

**A verdict: merge in narrowed form.** It is independently warranted, but not an independent chapter warrant.

## 9. Clause C analysis

### Exact current text and context

Immediately after B and before `Important distinctions`, C reads:

> **04.Examination.C — Cross-examination without source-local erasure**  
> Cross-examination may compare independently preserved testimony or findings only while preserving each input's attribution, provenance, support basis, subject, scope, authority, confidence or uncertainty, Unknowns, standing, and forbidden inferences. The produced standing is bounded comparison standing: agreement, disagreement, contradiction, conflict, refinement, unmet requirements, or Unknown inside the comparison boundary. The forbidden inference is truth establishment, warrant, reliance, source independence, implementation permission, or reconciliation merely because material agrees, contradicts, repeats, or can be compared.

### Findings

1. **Exact distinction.** Comparison is a responsible bounded act over multiple independently preserved testimonies/findings. It must retain every input's source-local limits; it produces only comparison standing and cannot erase, strengthen, reconcile, or promote inputs by agreement, conflict, repetition, or comparability.
2. **One or several?** Several. `05.Testimony.A` governs consumption of one recorded claim/finding; C governs relations among multiple inputs.
3. **Agreement effect?** Agreement does not alter either input's source-local standing and does not establish source independence, truth, warrant, reliance, or reconciliation.
4. **Conflict effect?** Conflict does not automatically erase or weaken preserved source standing. It creates bounded conflict/contradiction standing for the comparison and may pressure later confidence or establishment judgments.
5. **Distinct act?** Yes as a general comparison act, though no special runtime class or universal pipeline is required. Its constitutional occurrence is the bounded production of a comparison relation while preserving inputs.
6. **Comparison elsewhere?** `05.Testimony.D` governs operational measurement-to-baseline comparison, not general testimony comparison. `05.Evidence.C` permits bounded comparison pressure. Neither fully covers general multi-testimony comparison.
7. **Reconciliation elsewhere?** `08.Authority.A` governs reliance and Book V governs establishment, but the current active Book has no complete general reconciliation clause. C correctly denies that comparison is reconciliation; it need not define reconciliation to preserve that boundary.
8. **Independent provenance preserved?** Current `05.Testimony.A` preserves attribution, source-relative limits, conflicts, and uncertainty; `05.Evidence.B` separates representation, applicability, verification, and source independence. That protects each input, but does not expressly require those protections to survive multi-input comparison.
9. **Reports independently support after deletion?** Yes as testimony, not authority. Report 010 demonstrates the same-source/counting and conflicting-provenance failure modes; 011 establishes independently varying source/provenance, standing, scope, authority, responsibility, and composition coordinates; 012 directly derives cross-examination, comparison/reconciliation, agreement/truth, contradiction/failure, and source-local preservation boundaries.
10. **Deleted-witness dependency.** The lost part is cross-realization recurrence from the representation-grammar applicability/recovery implementation and tests. The conceptual distinction and report synthesis do not depend on those modules' continued existence.
11. **Effect of deletion.** It weakens one recurrence argument only; it does not invalidate the distinction, which is still supported by current Book components and surviving reports.
12. **Book IV home?** No. C does not depend on questions, frontiers, methods, or probes; “cross-examination” is the historical label for general testimony comparison.
13. **Book V stronger?** Yes. Book V owns testimony standing, source/provenance, conflict, repetition, support, and fact establishment.
14. **Subject-language change?** A later merge must translate the local label “cross-examination” into Book V's general testimony-comparison subject and Book V numbering/context. It need not change the underlying distinction. No wording is drafted here.
15. **Gap on removal?** Yes if removed without merge: current Book lacks a complete rule for bounded comparison standing among multiple testimonies and conflict without erasure.
16. **Warrant.** C is independently warranted, but its current placement is not. Its list is broad and some forbidden inferences repeat general law; the multi-input preservation/comparison-standing core remains irreducible.

**C verdict: merge into current testimony grammar**, specifically `book_of_seed/05-evidence-and-knowledge/testimony-and-established-fact.md`, `05.Testimony.A — Premise-relative testimony without fact standing`.
