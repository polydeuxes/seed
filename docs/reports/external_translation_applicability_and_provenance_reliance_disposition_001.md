### 4.3 Direct translation findings

Translation **does** preserve an externally supplied assertion and form a
Seed-addressable representation. It **may** propose a Seed term, claim, or
relation in the source-attributed sense. It does **not by identity** warrant a
meaning relation, establish Evidence, determine applicability, admit material,
perform examination, or produce reliance. It supplies attributed testimony or
proposal that later, independently responsible acts may examine. A later act
could establish stronger standing only under its own Book assignment, inputs,
authority, evidence, scope, occurrence, and limits.

## 5. `applicable evidence` audit

The exact active-Book searches for `applicable evidence`, `evidence
applicability`, `applicability of evidence`, `evidence standing`, and `applicable
material` do not establish `applicable evidence` as a defined constitutional
kind. Material matches separate several usages:

- `01.External.A` contains the phrase under audit.
- `01.Standing.E` treats evidence standing as one coordinate of relation
  standing and distinguishes candidate relation, relation testimony, and
  evidence-supported or established relation standing.
- `01.Standing.E.1` assigns applicability for an exact proposed input-to-act
  relation ordinarily to the exact act owner, or to an explicitly assigned
  occurrence whose exact applicability standing that owner validates and
  consumes.
- `05.Evidence.A` states a narrower producer/consumer boundary: evidence-shaped
  material has standing for bounded examination only when the consumer can
  preserve specified evidence and provenance coordinates. Its result is
  “applicable represented support for further examination,” not universal
  Evidence admission, truth, or applicability to every act.
- `04.Frontier.B` permits material to be relevant for further examination only
  when explicit record material supports the exact local responsibility,
  subject, evidence/provenance requirements, and authority; its result is
  permission to examine further inside that boundary, not general standing.

The phrase in `01.External.A` is therefore not safely ordinary English. Read as
“potentially relevant or usable,” it would be understandable colloquially, but
its adjacency to the Book's exact four-result applicability grammar makes that
reading unsafe: “applicable” constitutionally concerns the exact input-to-act
relation, consumer, purpose, locality, authority, occurrence identity, and
preserved limits. Read constitutionally, translation lacks the exact consumer,
act, purpose, and applicability producer required to establish it. Read as a
distinct standing phrase, no definition or producer was found.

The best finding is **unsupported compression**. Translation can create
addressable, attributed translated material that a later responsibility may
propose as possible evidence. Evidence standing is established only at a
claim-appropriate evidence support/establishment boundary; for the narrower
`05.Evidence.A` result, the consumer must be able to distinguish evidence
identity from material existence and preserve the stated support coordinates.
The exact downstream act owner ordinarily determines evidence applicability for
its exact proposed use, unless the Book explicitly assigns another occurrence.

Accordingly:

- translation cannot by identity establish Evidence;
- translation cannot by identity establish that evidence is applicable;
- translation may produce translated testimony/material later examined as
  possible evidence;
- an evidence-support or claim-establishment responsibility establishes the
  exact Evidence/support standing its clause warrants; and
- the exact downstream act owner ordinarily determines or consumes
  applicability standing for that evidence-to-act relation.

## 8. The later-warrant route

Grammatically, “until” presents provenance-limited reliance as a reversible
condition: once some “separate bounded warrant” is established, the limitation
appears to lift. Because the sentence's grammatical subject remains the
provenance-limited external material, it risks saying either that warrant repairs
M, rehabilitates reliance on M, or grants permission to rely. Active law supports
none of those implications.

A coherent possible topology is narrower:

```text
M remains attributed and provenance-limited
↓
independent evidence E concerns exact X
↓
claim-appropriate responsible occurrence O may warrant X
↓
X preserves O, E, actual provenance (including Unknown),
authority, scope, conflicts, loss, limits, and Unknowns
↓
exact consumer determines X-to-act applicability
↓
consumer-local admission where that exact road requires it
↓
consumer may rely on X under its own authority and limits

M remains provenance-limited throughout
```

The active Book establishes the **constraints between these acts**: warrant is
claim-bounded; applicability is consumer/act-local; admission is separately
local where required; reliance does not strengthen standing; and absent
provenance cannot be reconstructed from the same material. It does not establish
this topology as a demanded current road for the paragraph's unidentified X.
The topology is therefore a lawful **possible** road, not evidence that its
producer, consumer, demand, or occurrence exists here.

The clause does not identify the claim/relation warranted, the warrant producer,
claim-appropriate evidence, later consumer, consumer act, applicability
occurrence, admission requirement, or reliance occurrence. A later warrant does
not supply missing provenance, repair M, or itself grant authority or permission.
Reliance can move to independently warranted X only if all independently owned
consumer boundaries are satisfied.
