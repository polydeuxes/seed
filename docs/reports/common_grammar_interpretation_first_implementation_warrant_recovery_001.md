## 1. Executive disposition

This is one bounded, report-only constitutional recovery on current merged
`main`, after PRs 2168, 2171, and 2172. Current Book text is authority; current
production is implementation evidence; tests are verification witnesses; prior
reports and deleted code are testimony only. Equality of text is not equality of
constitutional subject or standing.

**D. implementation remains blocked by absence of an exact communicative
consumer occurrence.**

PR 2168 assigns interpretation responsibility relationally, and PR 2172 now
places default responsibility for input-applicability determination at that exact
act owner. Neither amendment produces the condition that instantiates the
assignment: a current exact Seed consumer occurrence with a declared
communicative act and purpose requiring common-grammar standing. Current
production stops after exact source-addressable ingress projection. The Book's
operator-question road names later Seed-side interpretation and bounded standing
establishment, but production has no bounded translation occurrence, no exact
standing-establishment consumer occurrence, and no question-forming occurrence.
No other inspected surface supplies the missing consumer by identity.

Consequently no lawful attributed hypothesis source is presently connected to a
real examiner. This is not selected as the earlier blocker: hypothesis inputs are
consumer- and act-relative, and without the exact occurring act there is no exact
production demand or applicability relation for one. Once an independently
warranted exact consumer occurs, its lawful input may be developer- or
source-attributed testimony, bounded translation testimony, prior interpretation
testimony, a constitutive convention, or other claim-appropriate testimony; the
Book does not require `CandidateExternalGrammarSet` or a general candidate former.

## 5. Applicability consequence of PR 2172

PR 2172 resolves:

```text
the default owner for hypothesis applicability:
the responsibility assigned to the exact communicative interpretation act
ensures applicability is determined for each proposed input before participation,
consumption, or reliance
```

That owner may determine applicability locally in the same bounded occurrence,
or validate and consume exact applicability standing established for that act by
an explicitly assigned responsible upstream occurrence. Applicable input may
participate subject to all other conditions. Inapplicable, conflicting, or
Unknown input may not participate or support the result. Excluding one input does
not prohibit the whole act; if a required input has no lawful substitute, the act
is not performed.

PR 2172 does **not** resolve:

```text
hypothesis existence
hypothesis attribution
hypothesis substantive warrant
an exact communicative consumer occurrence
interpretation execution
interpretation-result warrant
common-grammar relation standing
downstream applicability, admission, or consumption
```

It therefore closes the prior applicability-owner assignment gap but does not
instantiate the relational examiner or create producer demand.

are not translation.

## 9. Exact blocking clause

**The Book and implementation establish one recorded, decoded, exact
source-addressable operator-ingress material occurrence, but do not establish an
exact production consumer occurrence responsible for a declared communicative
interpretation examination, concerning that exact preserved ingress and an exact
applicable attributed grammar or grammar-relation hypothesis, for the purpose of
determining whether the material may participate under the required bounded
common-grammar relation, producing supported or unsupported interpretation-relation,
conflict, Unknown, refusal, or lawful-stop standing for that same consumer or a
separately warranted downstream consumer.**

This is the single earliest missing occurrence after current real operator-ingress
material. The Book-assigned boundary from which a future vertical slice could
begin is the exact Seed consumer whose declared communicative act requires the
relation. The general relational assignment is established; its production
instantiation is not. On the strongest question road, bounded translation and
Seed-side standing establishment may be co-realized as separate claims, but their
absence does not permit inventing a consumer occurrence merely to implement the
examiner.

Examiner-terminal results may include a supported or unsupported interpretation
relation, conflict, Unknown, competent refusal, or lawful bounded stop when the
examiner independently warrants that exact result. A supported interpretation
relation is not common-grammar standing unless that bounded relation is separately
warranted. A later use, admission, Question, goal, Demand, or movement requires a
separately responsible consumer. Runtime return is not constitutional refusal;
quiescence is not lawful stop; unsupported interpretation is not act
non-performance; and an excluded hypothesis is not an unsupported interpretation.
