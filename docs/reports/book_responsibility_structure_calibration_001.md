# Book Responsibility Structure Calibration 001

## Mandate and epistemic boundary

This report is Pilot 1: a bounded calibration, not a Book amendment, a constitutional declaration, or a lint design.  It asks whether normative movement remains responsibility-owned and recoverable in four fixed specimens.  Its direction of reasoning is:

```text
active Book grammar
-> exact responsibility expectation
-> implementation witness
-> faithful within scope / crossing / missing witness / Unknown
```

Implementation absence does not author law.  A class, method, helper, branch, assignment, call stack, or passing test is testimony, not automatically a competency, act, or occurrence.  Material distinctions in subject, act, purpose, result, standing, consumer, authority, scope, or occurrence are the basis for decomposition.  Historical reports explain how wording arrived; only the active Book supplies current authority.

The report-local classifications below are analytical labels only.  They are not constitutional kinds and do not propose a universal field schema.  A coordinate may be inapplicable, supplied by an enclosing clause, explicitly inherited, intentionally unresolved, or **Unknown**.

**Segmentation rule.** A bounded constitutional clause comprises normative movement sharing one recoverable responsibility head, subject, act, and result boundary.  Adjacent sentences remain one clause when later sentences only bound or negate that same movement.  A sentence is split when it assigns materially different producers, acts, results, consumers, authority, scope, or occurrences.  Displays are segmented by constitutional edges rather than arrows or line breaks.  Lists of `X != Y` are treated as cross-cutting constraints unless a listed distinction itself assigns an act.  Thus punctuation is neither clause identity nor responsibility identity.
