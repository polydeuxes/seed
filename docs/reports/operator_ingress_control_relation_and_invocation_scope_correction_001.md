# Operator-ingress control-relation and invocation-scope correction 001

## 1. Scope and disposition

This is one bounded correction after merged PR 2181. The active Book remains
constitutional authority; reachable production is occurrence evidence; reports and
tests are testimony; and Git history is provenance testimony. PR 2181's historical
report is not amended.

PR 2181 used the formulations **“already-established bounded control relation”**
and **“current bounded control relation.”** Those formulations are retracted. No
constitutional kind, relation family, standing, or responsibility named `control
relation` was established. The phrase must not name the earlier empty position or
direct the next recovery.

This is not a vocabulary-excision campaign. `control`, `invoke`, and `invocation`
remain available where their exact established meanings warrant them. The correction
is local to the unsupported abstraction in this district and to active-Book residue.

- `trigger` was unsupported constitutional vocabulary;

These are absence and non-assignment findings. They do not establish a new kind with
a missing instance. In particular, they do not establish `control relation` as the

PR 2181's recommendation to search for a `control relation` is superseded. The
corrected inquiry is:

> Does any currently established responsibility consume the exact preserved
> operator-interaction material or standing, perform an exact bounded act for an
> established purpose, and produce a result that a bounded-presentation
> responsibility is independently warranted to consume?

**No such current responsibility or road is established** is a complete lawful
result of that inquiry. Recovery need not fill the position, assign it, or invent a
kind.

## 3. Interaction and occurrence recovery

`Operator interaction` is not assumed to be one established constitutional subject
merely because multiple capture attempts happen in one console or process. Recovery
must distinguish:

| Coordinate | What is actually recovered |
| --- | --- |
| process occurrence | Production starts, dispatches, loops, and exits within an operational process lifetime. |
| console occurrence | A console can be entered, can perform operational iteration, and can return. |
| capture occurrence | One capture attempt can produce bounded captured material or its exact local failure/ending evidence. |
| decoded ingress occurrence | A decoder can be called on captured material and can produce its decoder-local bounded result. |
| addressable material | Preserved material may be exactly referred to; addressability is not applicability, meaning, admission, or use. |
| interaction relation, if any | No single constitutional interaction relation connecting these occurrences to the unnamed responsibility is currently established. |

Operational co-location does not collapse these identities. Process-loop control is
implementation process control, not constitutional standing. A process, console,
capture, or decode occurrence does not by identity establish the missing relation,
an entrance to presentation, or a generic constitutional control layer.

Preserved distinctions are:

```text
implementation process control
!= constitutional control relation

responsibility-owned act
!= generic control mechanism

conditional topology
!= established entrance

unknown relation
!= missing kind awaiting a name
```

## 4. Why `control relation` is not merely a poor synonym

The phrase introduced unsupported structural assumptions, rather than merely using
an awkward label. It assumed that:

```text
control is a constitutional relation family

an existing control relation connects ingress to responsibility

constitutional movement requires a generic control layer

finding such a relation would resolve the empty position
```

None may be retained without an independently identified subject; participants and
roles; relation assertion; responsible producer; act; purpose; evidence; standing;
consumer; authority; scope; occurrence; limits; and **Unknowns**. Current testimony
does not supply that complete recovery.

The governing recovery method is therefore:

```text
do not name the empty position
and then search for an instance of that name

instead:
identify an actual responsibility
→ recover its exact act and purpose
→ recover its exact consumed standing
→ recover its bounded result
→ recover an independent consumer

if that road is absent:
preserve absence of a current road
without creating a constitutional kind
```

This method permits an established responsibility to be found if evidence supports
it, but it does not treat an unknown relation as a blank kind-name slot.

## 5. Bounded operational meaning of invocation

In its established operational sense, invocation means that a responsible actor
calls or undertakes an already-bounded command, operation, mechanism, source,
decoder, or similar realization. More exactly:

> Seed invokes a command or operation when Seed performs the bounded calling act
> under the exact required selection, warrant, authority, scope, and realization
> grammar.

This does not claim that every operational call requires a dedicated artifact or a
separately named invocation responsibility. The responsible calling act may occur
within an already assigned operational responsibility where the exact grammar
warrants it.

Invocation may enter a corridor involving external effect or mutation, depending on
the exact invoked act. Effect, mutation, and success do not follow from invocation
by identity. Likewise, execution does not establish recording, and recording does
not establish admission:

```text
selection
!= authorization

authorization
!= invocation

invocation
!= successful execution

execution
!= result recording
!= result admission
```

Nor does temporal precedence turn another act into invocation. Presentation
formation, representation emission, applicability determination, standing
examination, selection, authorization, and ordinary constitutional relation
establishment are not invocation merely because one may precede a later operation.
Each retains its own responsibility-local act and claim. This district's unsupported
`prerequisite invocation` use therefore does not invalidate legitimate operational
uses elsewhere in Seed.
