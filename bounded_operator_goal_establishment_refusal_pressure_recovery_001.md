#### Exact producers and input identity

| Boundary | Exact producer | Identity/provenance preserved | Boundary that remains |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Selection | `select_contextual_interpretation` | Warrant-set ID, exact carried operator material, candidate-bound evidence refs/kinds/text/provenance, selected and non-selected candidates | A unique warranted candidate is not automatic selection; downstream inability must not erase selected meaning |

| BOGE | `establish_bounded_operator_goal_from_admitted_interpretation` | Admission ID as ingress; upstream source, selection, applicability, and admission refs; selected-meaning snapshot; unresolved material, Unknowns, conflicts, known loss | It consumes carried standing and does not reinterpret, re-warrant, reselect, recompute applicability, or readmit |

The BOGE producer requires `artifact_type ==
"DownstreamInterpretationAdmission"`. It compares the admission's consumer and
purpose with the exact BOGE constants, and compares selection, projection, and
selected-candidate identities with the carried applicability projection.
Selective reading of carried objects preserves lineage; it is not an upstream
producer occurrence proof.

#### Success standing

Success requires all identity checks to agree, an admitted admission outcome,
an applicable projection, no preserved Unknowns or conflicts, and a nonempty
selected candidate with its carried object. The BOGE is `established`; its
reason is
`consumer_local_admitted_interpretation_supplies_bounded_operator_goal_standing`.
Its intended outcome comes from the selected proposed meaning, label, or ref;
known scope contains selected identity/label; source and admission lineage and
the selected snapshot survive.

#### Refusal standing and exact reasons

The BOGE producer lawfully returns a BOGE occurrence with state `refused`, an
empty intended outcome, and empty known scope under these ordered reasons:

| Condition | BOGE reason | Preserved material |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Consumer, purpose, selection, projection, or selected-candidate identity mismatch | `admission_identity_or_consumer_mismatch` | Mismatch explanations become conflicts; upstream refs and snapshot remain |
| Admission is not admitted or outcome is not `admitted` | `interpretation_not_admitted_to_bounded_goal_establishment` | Admission/applicability known refusals and unadmitted reasons become unresolved scope |
| Carried projection is not applicable | `admitted_interpretation_is_not_applicable` | Projection standing and lineage remain |
| Any accumulated upstream Unknown | `admitted_interpretation_has_unknown_upstream_lineage` | Candidate, applicability, and admission Unknowns |
| Any accumulated upstream conflict | `admitted_interpretation_has_conflicting_upstream_lineage` | Candidate, applicability, and admission conflicts |
| Missing selected ref or selected object | `admitted_interpretation_lacks_selected_meaning_identity` | Available lineage and unresolved material |

Because mismatch is tested first and Unknown before conflict, one reason does
not enumerate every defect. The structured `unknowns`, `conflicts`,
`unresolved_scope`, `known_loss`, and lineage must be inspected; reason-string
equality is not evidence of equivalent refusal causes.

#### Consumer and negative authority

The only implementation consumer found that accepts BOGE directly without
requiring it already to be established is
`establish_bounded_advancement_horizon`. It refuses a refused BOGE as
`goal_artifact_not_established` while carrying goal identity, ingress ref,
lineage, Unknowns, and conflicts. It does not compare refusals or recover
ingress. Goal-local Demand projections are later consumers of an established
goal and bounded horizon; they are not pre-goal consumers.

Every upstream road is read-only and denies some or all of goal binding,
inquiry movement, admission, authorization, execution, recording, state/event
writing, and cluster mutation until its exact local boundary. BOGE alone may
establish goal standing; it does not establish a horizon, open inquiry, select
work, authorize, execute, or record. A refused BOGE faithfully records that the
producer ran and goal establishment did not occur.

### Road B — closed choice


#### Exact producers and input identity

`bind_closed_choice_selection` is the binding producer. The presented input
contains a choice-set ref, exact prompt, token/ref/label/detail options,
presentation ref, provenance, Unknowns, and conflicts. Its deterministic
fingerprint covers the choice-set ref, prompt, options, presentation ref, and
convention. The capture contains capture ref, choice-set ref, exact captured

an error rather than producing a binding refusal.

The binding ID covers fingerprint, capture ref, token, binding state/reason,
bound option, unsupported material, Unknowns, conflicts, and convention. The
binding artifact preserves the presented prompt/options, fingerprint, capture
ref/token, and evidence classifications. It does **not** carry the source
provenance tuples from the choice set or capture into its own fields.

`establish_bounded_operator_goal_from_closed_choice` is the BOGE producer. It
requires the exact binding artifact type. Its ingress ref is the binding ID and
its lineage is the sorted set of binding ID, choice-set ref, exact fingerprint,
and capture ref.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |

The closed-choice BOGE reason deliberately compresses three distinct binding

The binding explicitly says it is not a goal transition and sets

authorization false. It is read-only, writes no event ledger, and does not
mutate the cluster. The BOGE producer's occurrence is the consumer uptake; a
binding alone—even a bound one—is not BOGE success.

#### Consumer and negative authority

The same horizon producer is the immediate downstream consumer and refuses a

### Comparability determination

One can compare two retained artifacts under an externally declared predicate,
for example exact equality of choice fingerprints and binding states. That is
only a bounded measurement. Current artifacts do not themselves establish:

* that occurrences belong to the same operator interaction or initial-goal
  episode;
* that the same responsible operator/source supplied them;
* that earlier evidence remains current;
* that materially equivalent communication was attempted;
* that an admitted-interpretation refusal and closed-choice refusal are members
  of one comparison class;
* that a count is constitutionally material; or
* that the common cause is grammar rather than content, provenance, identity,
  consumer contract, stale presentation, capture defect, or deliberate refusal.

Therefore current refusal artifacts provide **useful per-occurrence identity but
insufficient constitutional identity for recurrence across attempts**. Two or
three failures have no threshold standing. Matching
`closed_choice_selection_does_not_support_bounded_orientation` proves only that

## Pre-goal Demand analysis

Canonical Demand grammar says a Demand is a bounded required result, relation,
clarification, inquiry, authority, transformation, competency, or other
condition under an exact responsibility and scope. Where applicable it must
preserve required result, responsibility, scope, source/testimony, standing,
materiality, applicability, and material Unknowns/conflicts. It also says:

* a Demand may arise outside the current established-goal/horizon-local four
  family implementation;
* repeated bounded failure to establish sufficient common grammar may expose
  pressure before an interpreted operator goal exists;
* exact operator material remains uninterpreted;
* recurrence does not establish meaning, selection, authority, or execution;
* exact Demand family is Unknown;
* competency is only a possible satisfaction candidate, not the Demand; and
* the counterexample creates no producer, mechanism, authority, or execution
  road.

Accordingly, a relational Demand for sufficient lawful communication/ingress
for BOGE is **constitutionally possible, not established in the specimen or by
the current artifacts**. No producer owns it. Even if independently established,
it would establish required-result standing only; it would not establish a
Seed goal, select prose or enum, select English, acquire grammar, authorize a
question, open inquiry, or move.

The requirement is relational: sufficient common grammar between the parties
for this bounded operator interaction. It is not Seed competency by identity.
Its hypothetical satisfaction criterion would have to be local to lawful BOGE
ingress, not universal understanding. The exact responsibility, sufficiency
test, and stopping rule remain Unknown.

### What one occurrence proves

Assuming the supplied specimen corresponds to actual retained artifacts, one
occurrence proves only that:

1. the capture names the same choice-set ref accepted by the binder (otherwise
   the binder raises rather than returning unsupported);
2. the captured token is not a member of that exact presented option-token set,
   with no higher-priority conflict producing the shown unsupported binding;
3. the binding producer successfully produced `unsupported`, preserved the
   exact token as unsupported evidence, and selected no option;
4. the BOGE producer successfully consumed that binding and produced a lawful
   refused BOGE with empty intended outcome and known scope; and
5. operator goal establishment did not succeed by that occurrence.

It does not prove the operator saw, received, understood, accepted, or intended
to answer the presentation; operator identity or authority; initial-boot
session identity; a language deficit; a malformed capture mechanism; a Demand;
or the absence of a goal expressible by the other ingress road.

### What recurrence could prove

With an independently declared comparison predicate and preserved episode,
operator/source, fingerprint, capture, time/currentness, and scope identities,
multiple occurrences could prove a bounded count of repeated out-of-set captures
and repeated BOGE refusal branch outcomes. A competent materiality evaluation
could use that recurrence as evidence. Recurrence still would not explain why
the material was supplied, establish common-grammar insufficiency, pressure,
Demand, or select recovery.

Current artifacts lack the identities and consumer needed to make that series.
The word “repeatedly” in the specimen is caller testimony, not a standing
produced by either road.
