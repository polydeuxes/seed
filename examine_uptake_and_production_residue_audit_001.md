## 5. Responsibility decomposition

**Consumer test.** Inquiry is a consumer when it uses already-preserved evidence, findings, observations, relations, or recorded assertions. `testimony-and-established-fact.md:24` expressly says such material “may be consumed” for a bounded examination, and `lenses-views-and-roads.md:12` supplies the general consumer-side Uptake relation. Inquiry is not exempt from applicability, admission where locally required, authority, scope, conflicts, Unknowns, or consumer-purpose limits (`lenses-views-and-roads.md:12-20`).

**Existing versus new material.** `testimony-and-established-fact.md:123` makes the decisive separation: sensing produces testimony; acquisition and interpretation may produce Observation testimony; later examination, comparison, and establishment consume that Observation. Thus Uptake does not produce upstream testimony. Operational measurement production is separately named from recording and understanding establishment (`recording-and-knowledge-extraction.md:10`), and declared measurement owns its bounded measurement finding (`external-and-constitutional-grammar.md:28`).

**Exact acts and results.** Question clauses recover applicability, selection, composition, and request formation (`questions-and-inquiry.md:26`). The Uptake clause independently assigns projection, request formation, testimony preservation, composition, explanation construction, and establishment to consumer acts and keeps emission separate (`lenses-views-and-roads.md:12`). Trajectory, learning, and causal clauses assign comparison and establishment and name their results (`testimony-and-established-fact.md:111-133`). Fidelity assigns comparison and its qualitative result (`external-and-constitutional-grammar.md:25`). None attributes those acts or results to an `Examine` owner.

**Responsibility test.** The closest positive formulation—“A responsible examination or other kind-specific production occurrence” (`constitutional-kinds-and-artifact-standing.md:69`)—does not supply a distinct `Examine` owner, distinct act, consumed subject, and attributed result. Its “or other kind-specific” construction and local-coordinate limits leave the exact kind local. The positive clauses instead name consumers, applicability producers, measurement producers, sensing producers, comparison boundaries, and establishment boundaries.
