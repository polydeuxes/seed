# Lower-Layer Constitutional Movement Topology Fidelity Recovery 001

## Scope, question, and evidence discipline

This is one report-only orientation recovery. It asks:

> How does attributed material become available to a bounded responsible consumer, how may that consumer lawfully use it in a bounded occurrence, how may an attributed result be produced, and how may that result become available for another bounded crossing?

The recovery reads the active, numbered Book chapters first; reads the current implementation second; and only then overlays them. It changes no law or runtime surface. The implementation is testimony, not constitutional warrant. An import, call, common field, projection, formatting function, or displayed order is not treated as a road without a bounded producer assertion and a consumer occurrence.

The examined Book corpus is the current numbered chapters on grammar, acts, selection, constraints, inquiry, examination, evidence, recording, state, projection, communication, refusal, and stopping. The implementation corpus is the runtime districts named below and their direct CLI orchestration. Existing reports are corroborating historical testimony only. The bounded scope excludes Goal, Gap, Demand, and Capability except for the short orthogonal section near the end.

The following distinctions govern every diagram and table:

```text
available != applicable
applicable != admitted
admitted != consumed
consumed != constitutional Uptake automatically
constitutional Uptake != reliance
projection != standing
recording != upstream production
rendering != emission
emission != delivery
delivery != receipt
receipt != interpretation
```

## Part I — Book movement topology, recovered independently

### 1. Constitutional coordinates

The Book does **not** warrant a single compulsory pipeline. Its smallest recoverable relation is recursive and consumer-relative:

```text
responsible producer makes attributed material available
    |
    v
bounded consumer encounters that material for an exact purpose
    |
    +--> [optional] examines applicability
    |                 -> applicable | inapplicable | Unknown | conflict
    |
    +--> [optional where required] admits material
    |                 -> admitted | unadmitted | Unknown | conflict
    |
    v
responsible consumer-local use
    |  (constitutional Uptake only where the consumer adopts, narrows,
    |   preserves, projects, composes, establishes, or otherwise uses the
    |   upstream assertion under a warranted local relation)
    v
bounded consumer act
    |
    +--> attributed finding / testimony / representation / changed local standing
    +--> refusal / Unknown / conflict / lawful stop
    |
    v
result may become available at another bounded consumer boundary
    `--> recursion begins again; no delivery, receipt, interpretation,
         admission, Uptake, reliance, or revision is implied
```

This relation is warranted only in its modest form. Acts are occurrences owned by responsible boundaries, and artifacts are evidence of acts rather than the acts themselves ([acts and act artifacts](book_of_seed/02-acts-and-constraints/acts-and-act-artifacts.md)). Availability does not revise a consumer, while applicability, admission, consumption, Uptake, reliance, and standing change remain separate and need not all occur ([lenses, Views, and roads](book_of_seed/01-grammar-and-standing/lenses-views-and-roads.md)). Representation and emission are separate from every later consumer coordinate ([representation, emission, and consumer boundaries](book_of_seed/08-authority-communication-and-stopping/representation-emission-and-consumer-boundaries.md)). The recursion is therefore a minimum relation family, not a universal ordered process.

### 3. Preservation and visibility branches

Preservation is optional relative to constitutional occurrence. Recording preserves recoverability; it neither produces the upstream occurrence nor establishes the recorded standing. The Book explicitly permits ephemeral operational measurements when discarding them erases no material evidence or understanding ([recording and knowledge extraction](book_of_seed/05-evidence-and-knowledge/recording-and-knowledge-extraction.md)). An Event is therefore not required for every constitutional occurrence.

```text
attributed result
   +--> remains ephemeral
   |
   +--> preserved testimony or standing
           +--> [optional] Event recording in append-only history
           |        `--> replay under a declared method
           |                 `--> projected State / derived indexes
           |
           +--> [optional] Evidence linkage / Evidence Graph visibility
           |
           `--> [optional] later explanation or provenance navigation
```

Event recording, replay, projection, projected State, `FactSupport`, Evidence Graph visibility, and explanation do not define the constitutional relation by identity. Events report that something occurred; Facts assert bounded propositions; State is a rebuildable current projection rather than history ([events, facts, and state](book_of_seed/06-state-and-projection/events-facts-and-state.md)). Projection may expose material for a later determination but does not confer standing ([projection and current state](book_of_seed/06-state-and-projection/projection-and-current-state.md)). Evidence carries claim support and provenance, while explanation exposes why a claim is held without creating missing provenance ([evidence, provenance, and explanation](book_of_seed/05-evidence-and-knowledge/evidence-provenance-and-explanation.md)).

### 4. Inquiry and testimony branch

Questions preserve a concern and scope; examination methods describe how evidence may be produced. A probe is an artifact or request representation, not an invocation occurrence or responsible invoker, and a method description is not method performance. A responsible examination producer may acquire or examine material and produce attributed testimony. Testimony reports; establishment is the separate responsible boundary that may warrant bounded Fact standing. The claim, source, observation time and context, producer identity, method, confidence, scope, authority, provenance, conflicts, and Unknowns constrain later use ([questions and inquiry](book_of_seed/04-inquiry-and-examination/questions-and-inquiry.md); [examination methods and probes](book_of_seed/04-inquiry-and-examination/examination-methods-and-probes.md); [testimony and established fact](book_of_seed/05-evidence-and-knowledge/testimony-and-established-fact.md)).

```text
responsible consumer encounters a bounded concern or constraint
   -> a responsible representation-forming act may produce a probe or request
   -> a separately responsible invocation boundary may invoke an examination method
   -> a separately evidenced examination occurrence may produce attributed testimony or a finding
   -> optional preservation and renewed availability
```

Thus `probe artifact != invocation occurrence`, `probe representation != responsible invoker`, and `method description != method performance`. No agency, responsibility, selection, invocation, or movement ownership belongs to the probe artifact.

Whether a result is an Answer depends on a responsible Answer-forming act and its exact question, evidence, derivation, Unknown, conflict, and scope boundary. A lens output is not an Answer merely because the motivating question is known.

### 5. Representational egress branch

```text
bounded result with attributed standing
   -> presentation/View formation for a declared consumer purpose
   -> [optional] selection of representations
   -> [optional] composition preserving source assertions and limits
   -> rendering into an egress representation
   -> separately evidenced emission toward a candidate consumer
   -> delivery? -> receipt? -> interpretation? -> Uptake? -> reliance?
      each question remains independent and Unknown without evidence
```

Formation, presentation, composition, and rendering can remain entirely local and ephemeral. Invocation is distinct from request formation and emission; completed performance and returned material are distinct again. Returned material may be source-attributed testimony for a later inquiry, but it does not automatically return to, revise, or receive standing from the correct earlier consumer.

### 6. Book topology Unknowns

The Book leaves unresolved: the warrant by which an externally expressed distinction gains constitutional standing; a complete local calculus for deciding exactly which preserved coordinates a particular consumer requires; whether any particular crossing needs a separately produced admission artifact; the complete ordering among local refusal and stopping grounds; which Answer formations should be durable; and the exact consumer and re-entry relation for returned material in any particular road. These Unknowns prevent promotion of the recursive relation into a fixed ordered process.

### 8. Observation and State road

**Producer and artifact.** Source adapters collect provider or local material and construct canonical `Observation` objects. `ObservationIngestor.ingest_many` directly constructs one `Evidence` for every observation and, except for one narrow Prometheus OS suppression rule, one `Fact` carrying the same subject, predicate, value, dimensions, confidence, times, and evidence id ([observations.py](seed_runtime/observations.py)). This is implementation compression: no consumer-local applicability, admission, conflict examination, or Fact-establishment act appears between those constructions.

**Preservation.** The ingestor writes `observation.observed`, `evidence.observed`, and usually `fact.observed` or `fact.inferred` events as one batch. The responsible recording boundary validates or accepts the attributed record material within its local contract and invokes append behavior; `EventLedger` or `SQLiteEventLedger` is the append-only persistence witness and record-preservation implementation ([events.py](seed_runtime/events.py)). Recording witnesses these constructed artifacts; it does not reconstruct source collection or establish the proposition constitutionally. Thus `recording boundary != ledger object`, `responsible recording occurrence != storage mechanism`, and `record artifact != recording act`.

**Projection and lenses.** A projection occurrence invokes `StateProjector` over ordered ledger Events to rebuild `State`, then derives indexes including grouped `FactSupport`, conflicts, entity relations, and current selections ([state.py](seed_runtime/state.py)). A graph-formation occurrence invokes `build_evidence_graph` with projected `State`, not the ledger, to form read-only nodes, links, references, and fact-evidence views; this witnesses visibility formation, not a constitutional standing decision or recording ([evidence_graph.py](seed_runtime/evidence_graph.py)). Explanation formation invokes `ExplanationBuilder` with State/Fact material to expose support and conflicts. State Views, fact Views, graph summaries, JSON adapters, and human formatters are further lenses. A function or object reading fields does not automatically evidence responsible constitutional consumption; these field reads establish representation formation only. A CLI branch that calls a formatter and `print` witnesses stdout emission of that representation, not later receipt or interpretation.

**Actual consumer-local validation.** The strongest implementation validations on this road are schema construction, secret rejection, confidence range checks, the narrow suppression predicate, ledger batch validation, replay/event-kind dispatch, grouping, expiry/conflict rules, and surface-local lookup. They are real consumers for those exact implementation assertions. They do not evidence general applicability, admission, constitutional Uptake, Fact establishment, or reliance.

### 9. Operator-ingress road

`capture_stdin_material` reads the smallest available byte boundary, discloses text-stream re-encoding loss when applicable, and `examine_text_representation` invokes a selected strict decoder without claiming an encoding verdict ([operator_ingress_representation.py](seed_runtime/operator_ingress_representation.py)). The prerequisite runner records raw capture, examination, ingress, probe-production, presentation, response capture, binding, selection, and stopping occurrences in the ledger; it repeatedly projects a local attempt View through `StateProjector` ([operator_ingress_common_grammar_prerequisite.py](seed_runtime/operator_ingress_common_grammar_prerequisite.py)).

The choice enum is application-owned, fixed in `OPTIONS`, and described by a fixed prompt. It is developer-authored presentation material. No consumer-local constraint-evidence producer is called to derive those two options. A responsible representation-forming occurrence invokes `render_probe` to form text; the runner's `output_stream.write(...); flush()` call witnesses stdout emission. A separately recorded presentation Event provides local testimony that presentation occurred, but neither the probe artifact, formatting, nor Event existence proves external delivery or interpretation.

The response is captured and decoded, then `validate_capture_for_probe` validates current recorded presentation/capture identity, choice-set fingerprint, exact token, and single consumption before `bind_closed_choice_selection` produces a binding. The treatment selection records only selection. The current affirmative implementation road is exact: `preserved ingress X -> developer-authored treatment representation -> exact affirmative acquisition-treatment selection -> treatment-selection Event -> text states acquisition was not authorized or begun -> bounded attempt returns -> persistent console later captures another frame through fresh initial-ingress handling`. There is no evidenced acquisition consumer, competent stopping occurrence for the selected acquisition movement, continuation of the consumer blocked on ingress X, re-entry of ingress X, or preserved relation from the selected treatment to a later responsible consumer. The identity and exact lawful behavior of that missing later consumer remain Unknown. That Unknown does not erase the observable crossing: `selected treatment concerning preserved ingress X -> constrained movement abandoned without continuation or stopping witness -> later console input handled as fresh ingress`. Within the examined default-console scope, this is an **unfaithful boundary crossing**. If local stop is selected, the text explicitly says the bounded stop was not established; EOF and decode failure do have separately recorded local stopping occurrences.

### 10. Shared-explanation road

`project_shared_explanation_rendering` consumes exactly one already-produced source explanation and maps its stage-owned material into `SharedExplanationRenderingProjection`. Despite the class name, the projection is a shared representation; final human text is produced only by `format_shared_explanation_rendering` ([shared explanation rendering projection](seed_runtime/shared_explanation_rendering_projection.py)).

From caller-supplied bounded-inquiry and lineage evidence, the membership projection produces local belongs/does-not-belong/Unknown/conflict results. The set builder preserves cardinality and duplicate identity occurrences. Presentation admission consumes the set and explicit requested-presentation evidence; sequencing consumes admitted results plus local sequencing evidence; bounded composition consumes exactly one sequencing artifact and preserves it without selecting, reordering, resolving, or interpreting ([membership projection](seed_runtime/shared_explanation_membership_evidence_projection.py); [membership set](seed_runtime/shared_explanation_membership_evidence_set.py); [presentation admission](seed_runtime/shared_explanation_presentation_admission.py); [encounter sequencing](seed_runtime/shared_explanation_encounter_sequencing.py); [bounded composition](seed_runtime/shared_explanation_bounded_composition.py)).

Each stage is a genuine local consumer for its declared structural assertion and each returns an attributed, read-only result. Each also has its own dedicated formatter. The examined CLI can print those dedicated surfaces, but no general dialogue or emission boundary consumes the composed artifact. Thus the chain is a diagnostic/read-only composition road with meaningful local membership/admission/sequencing acts, not a witnessed normal-dialogue rendering-and-emission road.

### 11. Constitutional pipeline and bounded ask

`invoke_constitutional_pipeline` requires an already-established `BoundedConstitutionalQuestion`; projects that question and explicitly supplied capability material; selects registered Views by exact keys; converts selection to a composition request; and returns a composition ([constitutional_pipeline.py](seed_runtime/constitutional_pipeline.py)). It neither originates raw inquiry nor acquires evidence, writes Events, persists results, discovers capability, semantically interprets text, or mutates cluster state. Its formatter and JSON adapter expose a diagnostic result. This is a mixed object: connected and typed read-only composition across its own stages, but not a normal dialogue or evidence-to-Answer road.

Bounded ask is a compatibility dispatch road. The CLI supplies exact `QuestionFamily` text. Inventory lookup and eligibility enforce exact registration and static status; static maps select an existing surface; a dispatch request updates the CLI namespace; the ordinary CLI branch then executes that existing surface ([question_surface_inventory.py](seed_runtime/question_surface_inventory.py)). The lookup and compatibility checks are real local consumers, but exact keys and namespace mutation do not semantically interpret an operator question. The downstream surface, not bounded ask, owns result construction.

### 12. Candidate external grammar island

`assemble_candidate_external_grammar_set` consumes caller-supplied representation scope, candidates, structural claims, string testimony references, Unknowns, provenance, and authority notes. It validates required strings and duplicate candidate ids, freezes the material, and marks the result read-only with no ledger write or cluster mutation ([candidate_external_grammar.py](seed_runtime/candidate_external_grammar.py)). Its JSON and formatter consumers preserve and display the artifact.

No current call in the examined runtime makes this set input to representation examination, translation, testimony binding, candidate comparison or selection, grammar establishment, competency establishment, constitutional pipeline invocation, or reliance. Candidate preservation is therefore not grammar acquisition. This remains a disconnected preservation/diagnostic island.

### 13. Faithful crossings, mixed objects, and islands

**Faithful within examined scope:** append-only recording as recording; replay as projection; Evidence Graph construction as visibility; local exact-token validation and selection with negative authority; bounded composition's preservation of source identity and excluded/Unknown/conflicting material.

**Unfaithful boundary crossings:** the affirmative common-grammar acquisition-treatment road merits `unfaithful boundary crossing` within the examined default-console scope: a selected treatment concerning preserved ingress X is followed by abandonment without a continuation or competent stopping witness, and later console input is handled as fresh ingress. The missing later consumer's identity and exact lawful behavior remain `Unknown`; those Unknowns do not erase the observable crossing. The Observation road's direct Fact-shaped construction remains a serious compression, but its downstream surfaces and Book amendments explicitly narrow what projection and Views establish; its combined standing is `mixed`.

**Mixed objects:** `ObservationIngestor` joins normalization-adjacent intake, Evidence construction, Fact-shaped construction, and recording; `State` joins recorded material with derived indexes; the constitutional pipeline joins genuine typed stage consumption with diagnostic-only composition; class and surface names containing `Fact`, `View`, or `Rendering` can carry narrower implementation assertions than their constitutional readings.

**Disconnected islands:** candidate external grammar preservation; shared-explanation composition relative to general dialogue emission; constitutional pipeline relative to raw inquiry and evidence acquisition; bounded ask relative to semantic interpretation; affirmative common-grammar acquisition treatment relative to any acquisition consumer.
