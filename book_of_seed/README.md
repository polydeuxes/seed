# This Book

This Book preserves the durable constitutional grammar needed to read this Seed. It names bounded concepts and keeps important non-equivalences visible.

## Resolution

- A **Chapter** has one bounded subject: a concept, Responsibility, distinction, or relation.
- `[UNRESOLVED]` carries a distinction for which the current repository safely supports no constitutional resolution.
- this Seed must inspect the repository for current responsible occurrences, exact Acts, fields, topology, discrepancies, and residue.

The witness grammar and Markdown chapters are distinct Representations of the
same clause coordinates. Neither Representation is the Book by identity.

## Standing

Current Standing exposes each exact Responsibility carried at its boundary. Each Responsibility precedes and governs its exact Act. Standing != Responsibility.

```text
Standing
→ Responsibility
→ exact Act
→ Act occurrence
→ result
→ Standing
```

Later Standing has the exact result or relation established by the responsible occurrence as subject. Revision requires another responsible occurrence.

## Responsibility

1. Responsibility is the first branch read from current Standing.
2. Each exact Responsibility carries its required coordinates and precedes its Act.

### Required branches

#### Responsible boundary

Carries the exact responsible boundary. The subject, responsible occurrence, exact Act, and Standing boundary are separate coordinates.

#### Subject or material addressed

Carries the Responsibility's bounded subject.

#### Exact responsible act

Carries the exact constitutional Act governed by the Responsibility. The Responsibility precedes its Act occurrence.

#### Authority

Carries the exact Authority of the responsible boundary for the exact Act.

```text
Authority
!= separate Authorization standing
!= locality
!= admission
!= Authority carried by an input
```

#### Scope and locality

Preserve the Scope and Locality within which the Responsibility, exact Act, subject, and result apply. They do not establish Authority.

### Input branches

These branches are required where material participates in the exact Responsibility's Act.

#### Input source

Carries the exact input material source. Source by itself establishes no provenance.

#### Provenance

Carries the source, preservation, and occurrence history required by the exact use. Source and provenance remain distinct.

### Support-relation branches

#### Support relation

Where a Responsibility uses an Assertion as support, that exact support relation is another Assertion. Its participants, responsible Act, occurrence, Authority, Scope, conflicts, limits, Unknown, and Standing require exact coordinates under their responsible boundary.

Support-relation Standing establishes no supported Assertion Standing, Applicability, Admission, or later input-to-result support relation. Each requires its own responsible occurrence.

### Act-occurrence branches

These branches apply where the exact Act occurs or where another responsible occurrence establishes that the proposed Act did not occur.

#### Act occurrence

Carries the responsible occurrence at which the exact Act happens. An Act occurrence carrying failure remains an Act occurrence. Another responsible finding that the proposed Act did not occur establishes no Act occurrence.

```text
act
!= act occurrence
```

Another responsible occurrence may establish that a proposed exact Act did not occur. Its finding is a result under its own Responsibility, Authority, Scope, and support relations. It establishes no result for the proposed Act.

### Result branches

These branches apply where the exact Act occurrence establishes or preserves a result.

#### Occurrence-result relation

Carries the exact relation from the Act occurrence to the result under its responsible boundary, Authority, Scope, and limits.

```text
Act occurrence
!= Standing occurrence
```

#### Result

Carries the exact result established or preserved by the Act occurrence.

```text
result
!= result standing
```

### Standing branches

These branches apply where Standing is established for a result, relation, Assertion, or other bounded subject.

#### Standing responsible boundary

Carries the responsible boundary that establishes the Standing. The result's Act responsible boundary supplies no Standing responsible boundary.

#### Standing occurrence

Carries the exact occurrence that establishes the standing.

```text
Act occurrence
!= Standing occurrence
```

#### Standing

Carries the exact standing established for the exact result, relation, assertion, input-to-act relation, or other bounded subject.

Where the subject is a result, this is result standing.

Where the subject is not a result, Standing preserves its exact subject.

```text
result
!= established standing

subject existence
!= standing established

Standing occurrence
!= Act occurrence
```

### Preservation and Standing branches

Each branch occurs under its exact Responsibility and responsible boundary.

#### Preservation record

Carries any separately retained material preserving an Act, Standing occurrence, support relation, result, or other occurrence. The record establishes no preserved occurrence.

#### Separate Authorization standing

Carries any separately established Authorization input required by the exact Act. Authorization Standing != the Responsibility's Authority coordinate.

#### Applicability and admission standing

Carries any Act-local Applicability or Admission Standing required before material participates in that exact Act. Applicability, Admission, Participation, and input support are separate coordinates.

#### Lawful Stopping

Where a stopping occurrence carries a lawful Stop result, preserve the responsible boundary, stopping Act, stopping occurrence, and bounded Stop result as separate coordinates. No other Assertion or occurrence supplies that Stop.

The Book begins at Standing. Each exact Responsibility is a branch of current Standing and precedes its Act.

```text
responsible boundary != subject, Act, occurrence, or Standing boundary
occurrence != Assertion
```
