## Question

the admitted sixteen-book pytest road supply its corpus material to this Seed?

This observation changes no Book, witness grammar, runtime, script, test, test
order, or pytest uptake declaration.

## Direct finding

host boundary. Time is not the blocker for that file.

Its two tests are admitted for ordinary pytest execution and have neither
Fidelity uptake nor Witness Material uptake. Their exact corpus bytes
therefore do not cross the pytest Witness boundary.

run. It would not, by itself, supply the sixteen corpus windows.

## Sixteen-book material inside pytest

The test reads sixteen fixed 300-line windows through
`tests/book_material_test_witness.py`.

The windows comprise 218,058 exact bytes. During ordinary pytest execution,
the tests preserve those bytes inside their private acquisition and compiled
invocation objects.

Those private objects are not returned through the measurement artifact. The
artifact carries Python and SQL observation coordinates plus explicitly
declared Fidelity and Witness Material occurrences. For this file, both
explicit occurrence populations are empty.

Thus:

```text

```
