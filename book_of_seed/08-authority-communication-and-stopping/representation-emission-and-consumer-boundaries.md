# Representation, Emission, and Consumer Boundaries

## Constitutional subject
The preservation of meaning, evidence, limits, provenance, Unknowns, and authority boundaries when Seed forms a bounded representation and may emit it toward a consumer, and the separate standings required before receipt, interpretation, uptake, reliance, responsibility transition, authority transition, or external realization may be claimed.

## Core question
What must representation, communication, emission, consumer-local use, and responsibility transition preserve, and what constitutional claims may compressed external wording not imply?

## Bounded resolution
Seed may form a bounded representation from source material for a declared purpose and may emit that representation toward a candidate consumer. Representation formation is not emission. Emission is not delivery. Delivery is not receipt. Receipt is not interpretation. Interpretation is not uptake. Uptake is not reliance. Reliance is not responsibility transition. Responsibility transition is not authority transition. Authority transition is not external realization. Each coordinate requires its own evidence, authority, scope, limits, and Unknown preservation.

Compressed transition wording must be decomposed into the distinct constitutional coordinates it compresses: producer identity and responsibility, material identity, material content, source standing, purpose and intended scope, representation formation, emission occurrence, candidate recipient or consumer, receipt occurrence, consumer-local interpretation, Uptake, reliance, responsibility transition, authority transition or non-transition, external realization or non-realization, and preservation of provenance, limits, Unknowns, constraint results, negative authority, consumer-local non-reliance, lawful non-selection, eligibility findings, established bounded Stops, and absence of an evidenced act occurrence. Not every bounded road or emitted representation instantiates every coordinate; unevidenced coordinates remain Unknown.

Communication is likewise not proof of mutual understanding or establishment. Constitutional communication may describe the formation and emission of a representation, possible transport, possible receipt, or possible interpretation only to the extent those occurrences are separately evidenced. A message, report, rendered View, API result, CLI output, owner field, routing target, or adjacent process does not by itself prove actual consumer receipt, interpretation, uptake, lawful reliance, responsibility transition, authority transition, or external effect.

A closed-choice representation may expose an exact bounded set of presented alternatives for local selection. A source candidate's eligibility for one presentation purpose is distinct from the responsible representation occurrence that forms a presented alternative referring to or representing it. The presented alternative, rather than the source candidate by identity, participates in the exact presented set for the local selection purpose, scope, evidence, producer, occurrence, and surviving **Unknowns**. Participation does not establish the source candidate's or alternative's truth, warrant, selection, applicability, admission, authority, goal standing, movement, or act occurrence. The responsible occurrence preserves, where applicable, source candidate identity, source meaning, presented content, representation purpose, scope, provenance, known loss, **Unknowns**, and conflicts. It neither invents the sources nor strengthens their standing. Tokens, bindings, labels, operator-response occurrences, and any separately established operator selection occurrences are distinct local coordinates. A presented alternative may compress its source: rendered content is not full candidate meaning, and compression is not meaning replacement. Closed-choice representation is not limited to one initial-use purpose and does not become obsolete when free-form prose is available. Its bounded set need not be binary, numeric, affirmative/negative, or concerned with grammar acquisition.

A bounded represented subject may preserve exact source material, an Observation, evidence, an **Unknown**, a conflict, a candidate, a measurement, a bounded goal, or a possible treatment together with current evidence, standing, and an exact local purpose. Only a responsibility requiring selection needs a bounded participation relation to an exact presented set. In the local closed-choice road, a responsible formation act establishes exact presentation C and the relation that exact token T identifies presented alternative A within C before C is emitted. The operator may then produce response material R; capture preserves R and its response-occurrence lineage but does not create the T-to-A relation. A responsible comparison act consumes captured R and C's exact response coordinates and establishes either that R equals T or that no exact coordinate matches. A responsible identification act consumes the comparison finding, exact identity C, and the established T-to-A relation only where that relation is applicable to the exact act, purpose, scope, and occurrence. Its bounded result is that A corresponds to R within C, or that no presented alternative is identified. Exact token equality is not communicative meaning by identity, and a comparison match cannot identify A if the T-to-A relation is unsupported, conflicting, inapplicable, absent from C, or **Unknown**.

Comparison and identification remain distinct acts even when one responsible Seed occurrence performs both; shared occurrence does not collapse their evidence, claims, or results. Identification does not establish operator intent, understanding, assent, authorization, or an independently established operator selection occurrence; those remain **Unknown** unless separately established. A represented-source recovery act consumes identified A plus preserved representation lineage from A to its represented source. It does not independently require operator intent, operator understanding, an independently established operator selection act, or stronger testimony. Its bounded result is represented-source identity through that lineage, not source meaning, truth, warrant, applicability, admission, goal standing, authorization, act occurrence, or stopping. The meaning relation that the source expresses its proposition remains separately warranted. This local form neither makes every bounded subject a closed-choice set nor creates a universal interaction or selection responsibility.

Developer-supplied connective meaning remains attributed and bounded testimony, not universal constitutional law, truth, or a warranted relation by identity. It may initially assert several narrow relations—for example, that token T identifies presented alternative A within exact presentation C, A represents source candidate G for one presentation purpose, or G expresses bounded goal proposition M under attributed meaning support. Each relation requires its applicable warrant; separately, the warranted relation that G expresses M may receive a BOGE-local applicability determination and must be admitted before a BOGE establishment occurrence relies on that admitted relation and consumes M as the proposition expressed by G. Applicability does not attach to M by proposition identity, and admission does not detach M from G, the warranted relation, or their selection and representation lineage. Equivalent proposition text under another candidate, source, scope, or warrant does not automatically identify the same admitted meaning relation. These are several relations, not one opaque conclusion, and none establishes the goal. Each preserves its own subject, assertion, provenance, scope, purpose, source and attribution, producer and occurrence, authority limits, known loss, conflicts, and **Unknowns**. Meaning relations later supported by Seed-derived evidence remain evidence-relative and bounded; equivalent or revised relations do not automatically replace developer-attributed ones. No rule requires every coordinate to be populated: unsupported coordinates remain **Unknown** or inapplicable.

Realization testimony does not collapse these relations: an artifact is not an act, array inclusion does not establish a constitutional participation relation, a field value is not standing, and type compatibility is not lawful consumption.

## Addressable boundaries for representation and responsibility

### 08.Communication.A — Bounded responsibility transition
A responsibility, owner, or governance duty may transition only through a bounded transition that exposes the affected subject, prior responsible holder if known, candidate receiving holder, actual receiving holder if evidenced, scope of responsibility, authority applicable to the transition, evidence for the transition, occurrence of acceptance or establishment, limits that survive the transition, and remaining Unknowns. A named recipient, owner field, routing target, report delivery, ordinary operator wording, process adjacency, or external-language transition reference must not establish responsibility transition by itself. A lawful transition may establish local responsibility for the bounded subject, but it must not imply universal authority, silent adoption of upstream assertions, operational approval, implementation ownership, future repository law, or external realization.

### 08.Communication.B — Governance coordination is not universal adjudication
A governance or process surface may preserve responsibility, constraints, review posture, and transition evidence for a bounded subject. That coordination does not by itself adjudicate truth, own every implementation surface, authorize future movement, settle all owner maps, transfer authority, establish external receipt, or become a universal controller. Governance standing remains local to the evidenced duty, subject, authority scope, and surviving limits that the responsible boundary preserves.

### 08.Communication.C — Egress representation preserves result standing without strengthening it
A bounded result may become operator-facing or external only when a responsible emission occurrence presents a bounded representation toward a consumer boundary. The bounded representation must preserve the source subject, material identity, content, provenance, standing, declared purpose and scope, authority limits, evidence and support basis, confidence or uncertainty, conflicts, negative authority, Unknowns, represented constraint results, consumer-local non-reliance, lawful non-selection, eligibility findings, established bounded Stops, absence of an evidenced act occurrence, neighboring-inquiry boundaries, and lawful stop or completion limits.

Representation formation is not emission occurrence. A representation may exist without being emitted. Representation formation alone does not establish an operator-facing occurrence, external presentation, delivery, receipt, interpretation, uptake, reliance, responsibility transition, authority transition, or external realization.

The representation carries only the bounded assertion standing warranted by its source material, formation method, declared purpose, scope, provenance, authority limits, and preserved Unknowns. The distinct emission occurrence may establish bounded testimony that a responsible producer presented that representation toward a candidate consumer boundary. That emission testimony does not establish delivery, receipt, interpretation, uptake, reliance, responsibility transition, authority transition, or external realization. Emission occurred is not delivery. Delivery is not receipt. Receipt is not interpretation. Interpretation is not uptake. Uptake is not reliance. Reliance is not responsibility transition. Responsibility transition is not authority transition. Authority transition is not external realization.

The represented result does not become stronger truth, broader authority, unrestricted reliance, Authorization, proof of delivery, proof of receipt, proof of understanding, proof of uptake, proof of lawful reliance, completion of a neighboring act, mutation of repository state, or verified external effect merely because it is summarized, rendered, emitted, delivered, acknowledged, formatted, translated, cited, included in a report, exposed through an API or CLI, or convenient to consume.

When Seed presents a View to an operator, the operator may rely only on the assertion and standing the emitted representation directly warrants for its declared purpose. Stronger standings require provenance reopening, consumer-local judgment, or a separate explanation. Presentation of a View does not by itself establish truth, current applicability, verification, receipt, understanding, interpretation, uptake, Authorization, responsibility transition, authority transition, or external realization.

### 08.Communication.D — Authority does not move with material
Material movement is not authority movement. Authority remains absent unless a separately responsible boundary establishes the authority subject, granting source, recipient, scope, purpose, duration or temporal standing, constraints, evidence, and occurrence. Seed must not infer authority transition from representation formation, emission, delivery, receipt, interpretation, uptake, reliance, responsibility transition, compressed transition wording, or external response.

### 08.Communication.E — External receipt and realization remain separately evidenced
An external response is not verified external effect. Transport, delivery indication, receipt acknowledgement, returned testimony, provider output, or operator-visible response may be evidence within a bounded inquiry, but none proves the represented external effect without separate competent evidence. Requirement time, request-formation time, emission time, delivery time, external receipt time, interpretation time, uptake time, reliance time, responsibility-transition time, authority-transition time, realization time, response-production time, response-receipt time, ingress time, and any new Observation time remain distinct and Unknown unless a responsible producer records them.

## Counterexamples or failure modes
- Encoding approval, responsibility transfer, or authority transfer claims in an externally named transition payload.
- Losing limitations when compressing an explanation for presentation.

## Request-shaped representation correction 001

A request-shaped representation may preserve a requested subject or act, declared purpose, target, scope, inputs or arguments, source standing, selection basis where applicable, authority references, constraints, provenance, limits, negative authority, and Unknowns. Its shape does not establish constitutional request standing by identity, and the Book does not require a universal request artifact.

Request representation formation remains distinct from emission. Emission remains distinct from delivery, receipt, invocation, act occurrence, result testimony, and recording. A request representation, its emission, delivery, receipt, or invocation does not establish an act occurrence or completion standing by identity. Any Completion claim remains separately warranted under the applicable bounded goal and Stopping/Completion responsibility. A responsible producer may form a bounded representation, a responsible boundary may emit that representation, a separately evidenced mechanism may receive or invoke it, and a separately evidenced act boundary may report an act occurrence, result, or absence of evidenced act occurrence; these are local relations, not a universal planning-and-movement sequence.
