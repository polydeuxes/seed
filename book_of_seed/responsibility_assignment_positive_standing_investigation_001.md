# Responsibility-assignment positive Standing investigation 001

## Question

Given one already-recorded exact Responsibility assignment `A` preserving one
responsible boundary `B`, what exact physiology is required for positive
Standing whose subject is `A`?

This investigation changes no active Book clause, Witness Grammar coordinate,
runtime event, or test.  It constructs no scheduler and no collective
Responsibility subject.

The premise is deliberately narrow:

```text
A = one exact Responsibility assignment
B = the exact responsible boundary preserved by A
```

It does not assume that recording `A` established Standing for `A`.

## Direct answer

Active law requires this common coordinate skeleton:

```text
exact Standing subject                  A
Standing responsible boundary           B_S
Standing occurrence                     O_S
Standing-occurrence Evidence             E_S
Authority for establishing Standing(A)  Q_S
Scope / limits / Unknown                exact bounds retained for A
Standing                                exact positive Standing of A
```

The assignment's responsible boundary `B` and the Standing responsible
boundary `B_S` are distinct positions:

```text
B   = boundary under which A was assigned
B_S = boundary responsible for establishing Standing(A)

B != B_S by identity
```

They may eventually carry the same exact reference only through an occurrence
that establishes that relation.  No active assignment road currently does so.

The current runtime records none of `B_S`, `O_S`, or `E_S` for an assignment.
It therefore establishes no positive assignment Standing.

## 1. The exact Standing subject

The Book source is direct:

```text
Current Standing whose subject is the exact assignment
is required for this Seed to read that Responsibility.
```

The exact subject is the assignment Assertion `A` itself.  It is not:

```text
the subject assigned by A
the Responsibility named by A
the responsible boundary B
the event occurrence that recorded A
the later Act governed by A
```

For one assignment with content:

```text
responsible boundary B
is assigned Responsibility R
for exact subject S and required coordinates C
```

the Standing subject is that whole bounded assignment Assertion `A`.

This also decomposes the older connective phrase:

```text
B bears R
```

into the exact assignment Assertion rather than creating a separate Bear
relation.

### Runtime identity mismatch

Current assignment roads do not expose one common exact identity model for
`A`:

```text
recorded assignment event identity      present on every implemented road
assignment_identity                     present on many roads
assignment_subject_identity             present on many roads
assignment Assertion identity           not represented uniformly
```

`assignment_subject_identity` addresses `S`, the subject of the assignment;
it cannot address `A` by identity.  The recorded event identity addresses the
occurrence that recorded the assignment; active law keeps an Assertion and its
occurrence distinct.  Some roads allocate an `assignment_identity`; others,
including Candidate production, do not.

The question's premise supplies one exact `A` for constitutional reasoning.
The runtime nevertheless lacks one common exact assignment-Assertion
reference usable as a Standing subject across all assignment roads.

## 2. The responsible boundary

`B` is carried inside `A`.  It says where the Responsibility assignment was
bounded.  The Standing source requires a different exact coordinate:

```text
Standing responsible boundary B_S
```

Active law explicitly refuses these shortcuts:

```text
assignment responsible boundary B
!= Standing responsible boundary B_S

the Responsibility assigned by A
!= Standing responsible boundary B_S
```

`B_S` must be the responsible boundary of the occurrence that establishes
Standing for `A`.  Current assignment events contain no such coordinate and no
exact relation identifying `B` with `B_S`.

Disposition:

```text
assignment responsible boundary B      recorded where each road provides it
Standing responsible boundary B_S      unestablished for A
```

## 3. The Standing occurrence

Positive Standing requires one exact occurrence `O_S` that establishes
Standing for `A`.

The assignment-recording occurrence `O_A` does not fill this position by
identity:

```text
O_A records A
!=
O_S establishes Standing(A)
```

One physical occurrence could occupy both constitutional positions only where
its exact physiology independently instantiates both.  Current assignment
events record assignment content and assignment coordinates.  They do not
record:

```text
Standing subject A
Standing responsible boundary B_S
Standing occurrence O_S
Standing-occurrence Evidence E_S
Standing(A)
```

No runtime event kind records an assignment-Standing occurrence.  The only
runtime phrase calling an assignment event a "Responsibility assignment
Standing occurrence" is a reader docstring.  Its reader validates the
assignment event; it does not validate any Standing branches.

Disposition:

```text
assignment occurrence O_A              recorded
Standing occurrence O_S                unestablished
O_A = O_S                              not established
```

## 4. Evidence for the Standing occurrence

`E_S` must support this exact proposition:

```text
O_S occurred and established Standing(A)
```

It cannot be supplied by identity from:

```text
Evidence supporting assignment A
Evidence supporting A's later Act
Evidence of Yield from that Act to its result
the event that records A
the replay that later finds A
```

Those Evidence positions have different subjects and relations.

Many assignment roads preserve an `evidence_occurrence_reference`.  That
reference supports the clause-local assignment.  No current road records it as
Evidence of an assignment-Standing occurrence, and active law prohibits that
promotion by co-presence.

Disposition:

```text
assignment Evidence                     present on some roads
Standing-occurrence Evidence E_S        unestablished on every road
```

## 5. Authority

Positive Standing requires Authority for the responsible occurrence that
establishes Standing of `A`.

Assignment roads commonly carry active Book Authority bounded to their exact
assignment and later Act.  Several material dictionaries also contain a scalar
coordinate such as:

```text
authority.standing = bounded
```

That coordinate describes the status or bound of the Authority value.  It is
not Standing for the assignment Assertion and it does not authorize a missing
Standing occurrence by identity.

The required `Q_S` must bind at least:

```text
exact subject A
Standing responsible boundary B_S
Standing occurrence O_S
Scope
temporal and Locality boundary
limits
negative Authority
Unknown
```

Current runtime supplies no exact Authority binding to an assignment-Standing
occurrence because that occurrence is absent.

Disposition:

```text
Authority for assignment A              clause-local where recorded
Authority Q_S for Standing(A)           unestablished
```

## 6. Scope, limits, and Unknown

Standing of `A` cannot widen the assignment.

It must retain the exact bounds carried by `A`, including where applicable:

```text
assignment subject S
responsible boundary B
Responsibility R
Book clause
source and provenance
Locality
result boundary
Scope
Authority and negative Authority
limits
conflicts
Unknown
```

The Standing occurrence also needs its own exact bounds:

```text
Standing subject A
Standing responsible boundary B_S
Standing occurrence O_S
Standing-occurrence Evidence E_S
Authority Q_S
Scope of Standing(A)
temporal and Locality boundary
limits / conflicts / Unknown
```

Preservation may retain an Unknown coordinate.  It cannot resolve that
coordinate merely because positive Standing for the bounded assignment is
established.

The clause-local assignment coordinates differ.  Therefore the content
preserved by Standing cannot be one fixed runtime dictionary shared by every
road.

## 7. The required support relation

The constitutional Standing chapter requires an exact support relation from
the assignment's Evidence, Authority, Scope, provenance, and limits to the
assignment Assertion `A`.

The endpoints recorded beside `A` do not instantiate that relation:

```text
A
+ assignment Evidence
+ assignment Authority
+ assignment Scope and limits
!=
those coordinates support A
```

Thus one positive assignment Standing requires, at minimum:

```text
one exact support-relation Assertion S_A
whose supported subject is A

one responsible occurrence establishing S_A

positive Standing for S_A
```

The last coordinate retains the earlier support-Standing recursion.  Naming
`S_A` does not give it Standing.  Active law says absence of a prior Standing
population does not prohibit a responsible occurrence from supporting first
poor Standing, but no inspected assignment road records that first occurrence.

This is the earliest constitutional stop before the missing `B_S`, `O_S`, and
`E_S` can be claimed as established.

## 8. What makes the assignment readable by this Seed

Normalized without the unresolved `bears` connective, the read requirement is:

```text
current Standing has exact subject A

A asserts:
    exact responsible boundary B
    is assigned exact Responsibility R
    for exact subject and coordinates

Standing(A) carries:
    exact support relation S_A
    B_S
    O_S
    E_S
    Q_S
    Scope / limits / Unknown
```

Only then can this Seed read `R` from `A` for its later exact Act.

The following are insufficient:

```text
A was recorded
A is intact
A is available in one Locality
A appears in bounded replay
A is the latest event
a replay dictionary contains A: None
a later Representation was recorded after A
```

Those facts establish addressability, integrity, chronology, or availability.
They do not establish `Standing(A)`.

## 9. Current runtime crossing

`read_operator_locality_standing()` validates assignment events and inserts:

```python
responsibility_assignment_occurrences[event.identity] = None
```

Several later Act readers require that membership and call the supplied object
`responsibility_assignment_standing`.

Other helpers are named as carrying an assignment "into Standing" by mutating
the same replay dictionary and advancing its through-occurrence boundary.

The actual testimony is narrower:

```text
the exact assignment occurrence is intact
and available in bounded Locality replay
through this occurrence
```

The current crossing is therefore:

```text
assignment occurrence available in replay
↓ naming / dictionary membership
treated as current assignment Standing
↓
later Act reads the Responsibility
```

No intervening event supplies `S_A`, `B_S`, `O_S`, `E_S`, `Q_S`, or exact
Standing of `A`.

One existing test makes the gap especially visible.  It records an assignment
alone, reopens storage, reads the Locality replay, observes the assignment in
`responsibility_assignment_occurrences`, records a later Representation, then
permits the governed Act.  The later Representation proves only that another
occurrence was appended; it establishes no Standing for `A`.

## 10. Common skeleton and clause-local physiology

The answer is mixed.

### Common across every assignment road

The Book source requires the same constitutional positions:

```text
exact assignment Assertion A as Standing subject
exact support relation for A
Standing responsible boundary B_S
Standing occurrence O_S
Standing-occurrence Evidence E_S
Authority Q_S
Scope / limits / Unknown
exact positive Standing(A)
```

This is a common grammar skeleton, not a common event kind or fixed material
shape.

### Clause-local

Each assignment road supplies different exact content:

```text
Responsibility R
assignment subject S
responsible boundary B
Evidence for A
Authority for A
Scope and Locality
source / provenance
result boundary
limits / conflicts / Unknown
```

The exact Responsibility and occurrence that may establish `S_A` and
`Standing(A)` must preserve those local coordinates.  Active law does not
currently assign one generic Standing-establishment Act over all assignment
Assertions.

`01.Standing.D.1` names an Assertion Standing-coordinate preservation
Responsibility and says it begins with the Assertion.  That supplies useful
orientation toward a common Responsibility shape.  It does not presently
supply:

```text
one exact assignment of that Responsibility for A
one exact Act
one Standing occurrence O_S
one Evidence occurrence E_S
one Authority binding Q_S
```

Assignment events are also not represented uniformly as bounded Assertions.
Therefore current law supports:

```text
common required coordinates
+ clause-local exact physiology
```

It does not yet support either extreme:

```text
one generic recorder establishes Standing for every assignment

or

every assignment clause necessarily invents a different Standing grammar
```

## Disposition

```text
one exact assignment A                                      premise
assignment responsible boundary B                          recorded
assignment occurrence O_A                                  recorded
assignment Evidence / Authority / Scope / limits / Unknown clause-local

exact assignment Assertion reference shared across roads   absent
exact support relation S_A                                  absent
positive Standing for S_A                                  absent
Standing responsible boundary B_S                          absent
Standing occurrence O_S                                    absent
Standing-occurrence Evidence E_S                           absent
Authority Q_S for Standing(A)                              absent
exact positive Standing(A)                                 absent

assignment occurrence = Standing occurrence                no by identity
bounded replay membership = Standing(A)                    no
scalar standing coordinate = Standing(A)                   no
carrying A in replay = Standing(A)                          no

common Standing-coordinate skeleton                        established
one common Standing-establishment physiology               unestablished
clause-local assignment coordinates                        established where recorded
```

## Conclusion

Given one exact recorded assignment `A`, the next lawful coordinate is not a
later Act.  It is positive Standing whose exact subject is `A`.

The required shape is:

```text
exact assignment Assertion A
↓
exact support-relation Assertion S_A
↓
positive Standing(S_A)                         unresolved
↓
Standing responsible boundary B_S
↓
Standing occurrence O_S
↓
Standing-occurrence Evidence E_S
↓
Authority / Scope / limits / Unknown
↓
positive Standing(A)                           unresolved
↓
this Seed can read the Responsibility from A
```

The runtime currently stops after recording `A`, then crosses directly from
bounded replay membership to the later Act.  That is exact occurrence replay,
not assignment Standing.

The coordinate skeleton is common.  The exact subject content, Evidence,
Authority, Scope, limits, and responsible occurrence remain clause-local.  No
current testimony establishes one common Standing-producing road or a
Book-wide collective Responsibility subject.
