# Book and machine-grammar blind joint observation 001

## Question

Can the active Book and its active machine grammar, treated first as exact raw
bounded material, expose the joints already declared by Seed without supplying
those joints as discovery targets?

This report changes no Book chapter, witness grammar, runtime, or test. Three
disposable observers in `/tmp` performed the calculation. They recorded no
Seed occurrences.

## Blind local-joint pressure

After exact contractions were frozen, a second blind observer inspected only
relations addressable inside each contracted bound.

For every exact parent support population, it preserved:

- contracted child material whose occurrence coordinates were carried by
  every parent support;
- every binary split whose two children covered the exact parent;
- every three-part split whose three children covered the exact parent.

It did not pair results across a source or choose a split.

```text

supported binary splits               48,469
supported three-part splits          126,086
```

Distribution:

| population | binary splits | three-part splits |
|---|---:|---:|
| Book | 18,146 | 23,974 |
| machine grammar | 30,323 | 102,112 |

The blind joint artifact was frozen as:

```text

digest          911e32fba296ddd5ebdb64ae37186c64979f2b39549caf680fd66f3c24e2528f
```

This is the strongest falsifier in the experiment:

```text
exact finite parent boundary
↓

```

Local boundedness prevents a global Cartesian population. It does not by
itself discriminate a grammar joint. Most split surfaces are arbitrary byte
divisions of recurrent material.

The largest observed containment depths were 67 in the Book Compare source and
215 in the machine grammar. These are overlapping incremental substring chains,
not 67 or 215 recursive consumer rungs.

## Finding

Seed's Book and machine grammar cast a measurable structural shadow in their
raw bytes:

- grammar-bearing literals recur without being targeted;
- exact materials survive the representation boundary;
- changed separators preserve many internal structural surfaces;
- recurrent blocks make thousands of local joint positions addressable.

The declared grammar joints themselves do not emerge. Their names and source
bounds become addressable, while the responsible relation connecting their
subjects remains absent.

The experiment therefore narrows the vacancy without parsing or semantic
classification:

```text
the haystack contracts
the sockets become visible
the joint relation is still missing
```
