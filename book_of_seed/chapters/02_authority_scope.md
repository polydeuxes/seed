# Authority and Scope

Authority carries one exact source, Responsibility, subject, Act, occurrence,
result, Scope, and limits. Material carrying exact Authority coordinates establishes no
other Authority.

Scope bounds the subjects, relations, Acts, occurrences, results, Localities,
and limits addressed by one exact Responsibility. One Scope establishes no Scope
for another Responsibility.

Authority movement and Scope movement requires an exact responsible
occurrence. Carriage of exact Authority or Scope coordinates establishes no Authority or Scope at another
boundary.

### 08.Authority.A — Input support is bounded

Support for one exact input-to-Act position requires the subject, relation,
Act, responsible boundary, Authority, Scope, Locality, limits, and responsible
occurrence. Support establishes no Participation, result, or Standing
beyond exact bounds.
