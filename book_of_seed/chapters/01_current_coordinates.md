# Current Coordinates

### 01.Current.G — This Seed first current coordinates

This Seed first carries no current coordinates.

### 01.Current.A — Exact current coordinates

For one exact subject, its exact current coordinates through one exact
occurrence boundary are its exact established coordinates whose occurrences
are in one Locality through that boundary.

Current coordinates are a bounded reading that returns exactly those
established coordinates.

### 01.Current.A.1 — An Act result becomes current for its subject

The exact result of one Act occurrence is one current coordinate for every
exact subject carried with that Act before its occurrence, through a boundary
at or after the result occurrence in the same Locality.

### 01.Current.D — Each exact relation requires its own occurrence

Each exact relation requires its exact subjects and exact relation occurrence.

### 01.Current.D.1 — Assertion coordinates

An Assertion is exact content as one subject. It carries its source and exact
source occurrence references, Locality, conflicts, and
exact established coordinates.

### 01.Current.D.2 — Later Assertions

A later Assertion whose subject is current through one exact occurrence
boundary is one exact Applicability subject for one exact Act.

### 01.Current.E — Relation Assertion coordinates

One relation Assertion carries first subject, exact relation content, and
second subject. It requires the exact relation occurrence, Locality,
and conflicts.

### 01.Current.E.1 — Applicability

Applicability carries one exact subject-to-Act position. Its exact occurrence
carries the subject, Act, Locality, and
one of `applicable`, `inapplicable`, or `conflicting`.
