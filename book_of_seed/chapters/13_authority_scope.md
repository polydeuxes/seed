# Authority Scope

## Constitutional subject
The binding, Scope, and non-transferability of authority across this Seed's boundaries.

## Core distinction
What establishes Authority for a bounded result or Act, and what preserves its exact Scope in transit?

## Bounded resolution
Authority requires its exact source, responsible occurrence, addressed subject, Act, Scope, and limits. Material carrying Authority coordinates cannot create or enlarge that Authority.

Authority for an exact responsible boundary preserves its Act or movement, addressed subject, result boundary, Scope, temporal Standing, and Unknown only where each coordinate is established.

## Boundaries for bounded input support witnesses

### 08.Authority.A — Bounded input support
Standing of an Act on established content requires bounds carried by its exact role, addressed subject, result boundary, source or provenance, Authority, Scope, and Unknown. Input support establishes no Standing, truth, Authority, or Authorization beyond those bounds.

## Authorization boundary correction 001

A responsible Authority boundary is required to establish Authorization as bounded Standing. The boundary preserves the Authority subject, source of Authority, responsible boundary, authorized Act or movement, addressed subject, result boundary, Scope, temporal Standing, applicable Assertions, occurrence, and Unknown. Other findings establish no Authority.
