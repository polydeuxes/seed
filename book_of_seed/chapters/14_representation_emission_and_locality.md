# Representation, Emission, and Locality

## Constitutional subject
Bounded representation and movement between localities.

## Bounded resolution
One exact material-acquisition Responsibility preserves material supplied at one exact source boundary. Its exact Act occurrence yields one exact material result carrying its source role, source boundary, Locality, known loss, and Unknown. Material supplied by this operator and material supplied by this Witness have distinct source Responsibilities and occurrences. Shared material or result coordinates establish no shared Responsibility, Act, or source relation.

Each material-acquisition occurrence carries one exact result reference. Two material-acquisition occurrences yielding the same material each carry their own exact occurrence, result, and Yield references. The same material content establishes neither occurrence, result, nor Yield relation. Use of the material by another Act requires the exact result reference and Yield relation of the material-acquisition occurrence that yields it.

A material-acquisition occurrence establishes no Assertion about what the material represents, source relation, truth carried by this Seed, Authority, or later Standing.

A representation preserves its subject, content, source, provenance, Standing, Authority, Scope, Locality, conflicts, Unknown, and limits where established. For one intact source result with exact material, the exact rule is preserve exact material of source result. The Representation carries that exact material and source result occurrence and establishes no relation concerning its content.

An exact material Admission establishes the relation from Representation rule preserve exact material of source result to destination boundary rule write exact material as applicable only through its exact responsible occurrence. The relation preserves each rule. A write function establishes neither the relation nor effects beyond the boundary.

For one exact Representation candidate, emission Act, and destination operator Locality, current Standing exposes the bounded Representation emission Responsibility to determine Admission. An exact-material Admission requires one exact destination operator boundary and its exact boundary rule for output. After Admission and before Participation, that Responsibility determines Act-local applicable Standing carrying the exact Representation, Representation rule, destination operator boundary, destination boundary rule, destination operator Locality, emission Act occurrence, and result boundary. Failure of a required coordinate establishes no Applicability determination. Each other Applicability Standing requires a separate responsible occurrence.

A representation Act occurrence yields and carries its exact bounded Representation through separately established relations. Carried Locality coordinates establish no source Participation without the exact Participation relation and occurrence. A representation Act occurrence and an emission Act occurrence are separate. A candidate for emission preserves its exact Representation Act occurrence and source coordinates. The exact Representation emission Responsibility determines Admission of that candidate to the exact destination operator Locality before Participation. Admission preserves the exact operator boundary, Scope, Authority, provenance, limits, conflicts, known loss, and Unknown. Admission establishes neither Participation nor emission success. The exact admitted Representation participates in the emission Act occurrence under its exact role. That occurrence carries the exact emitted content and yields the declared boundary result and no other result. For an exact-material boundary write, the result is that the declared boundary accepted the exact material and reported its exact count. The material carried by the attempt occurrence and emission Act occurrence has two separate Locality relations. Every other relation or effect requires its own responsible occurrence.

Recording an emission attempt before the addressed boundary is invoked establishes no boundary acceptance. Current Standing exposes one bounded Responsibility to preserve an exact boundary-failure occurrence reported by that invocation. A reported partial write or error before acceptance carries the reported result, preserved Unknown, and no accepted result. Without an accepted boundary result, boundary acceptance carries Unknown. Where the declared boundary is the text-stream write, its acceptance establishes emission. That emission is recorded and preserved by this Seed before a later flush is invoked. A later flush failure is a separate boundary-failure occurrence carrying the accepted emission occurrence; it cannot erase that emission and establishes no effect at another boundary.

Present Applicability of current-facing material requires its exact representation rule, Standing boundary through its exact occurrence, availability, conflict treatment, and Unknown.

Acquisition by an Assertion of another exact Locality relation requires a responsible movement occurrence. No other boundary establishes that Locality relation. Movement preserves the Assertion's carried coordinates while recording its source occurrence, earlier Locality relation, newly related subject, Scope, Unknown, and limits. Movement cannot copy or strengthen the Assertion, revise its Standing, or establish Applicability.

An exact Act determines Applicability before an Assertion participates under a locality relation. The locality relation requires no Act and establishes no use.

Material movement cannot move Authority.

## Required distinctions
- representation != source material
- representation Act occurrence != emission
- emission attempt != emission
- emission failure != absence of occurrence
- boundary failure after accepted emission != emission nonoccurrence
- emission != Applicability
- candidate != Admission
- Admission != Participation
- Admission to one operator Locality != Admission to another operator Locality
- Applicability != input support
- material movement != Authority movement
- same material content != same material-acquisition occurrence, result, or Yield relation
- Assertion identity != Assertion occurrence
- locality subjects != locality relation
