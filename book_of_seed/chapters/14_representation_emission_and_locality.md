# Representation, Emission, and Locality

## Constitutional subject
Bounded representation and movement between localities.

## Bounded resolution
One exact Ingest Act preserves material supplied at one source boundary. Its occurrence yields the exact material and carries its source role, source boundary, Locality, known loss, and Unknown. Operator material and material supplied at another source boundary use the same Ingest Act. Different source coordinates do not revise the Act.

Each Ingest occurrence has one distinct result identity. Two Ingest occurrences that yield equal material remain distinct occurrences; each has a distinct result identity and distinct Yield Evidence. Equal material content does not identify either occurrence, result, or Yield relation. A downstream Act may use the material through the exact result identity and Yield Evidence of the Ingest occurrence that yields it.

An Ingest occurrence does not establish what the material represents, a source relation, Seed truth, Authority, or later Standing.

A representation preserves its subject, content, source, provenance, Standing, Evidence, Authority, Scope, locality, conflicts, Unknown, and limits where established. Missing coordinates remain absent or Unknown; representation words do not instantiate them.

A representation Act occurrence yields and carries its exact bounded Representation through separately evidenced relations. Carried Locality coordinates identify its bounded source; they do not establish source Participation without the exact Participation relation and occurrence witness. A representation Act occurrence and an emission Act occurrence are distinct. A candidate for emission preserves its exact Representation Act occurrence and source coordinates. The exact Representation emission Responsibility determines Admission of that candidate to the exact destination operator Locality before Participation. Admission preserves the exact operator boundary, Evidence, Scope, Authority, provenance, limits, conflicts, known loss, and Unknown. Candidate identity does not establish Admission; Admission does not establish Participation or emission success. The exact admitted Representation participates in the emission Act occurrence under its exact role. That occurrence carries the exact emitted content and yields the declared boundary result and no other result. For a text-stream write, the result is that the declared boundary accepted the exact text and reported its exact count. The same text carried by the attempt occurrence and by the emission Act occurrence has two distinct Locality relations. Applicability, an input-to-result support relation, Responsibility transition, Authority transition, and effects beyond the boundary require their own Evidence.

An emission attempt may be recorded before the addressed boundary is invoked. The attempt does not establish boundary acceptance. A reported partial write or error is a failed occurrence carrying the reported result, preserved Unknown, and no accepted result. An attempt without Evidence establishing boundary acceptance leaves boundary acceptance Unknown. Where the declared boundary is the text-stream write, its acceptance establishes emission; a later flush failure does not erase that occurrence and establishes no downstream effect.

Present Applicability of current-facing material requires its exact representation rule, as-of boundary, availability, conflict treatment, and Unknown.

An Assertion may acquire another exact locality relation through a responsible movement occurrence. No other boundary establishes that locality relation. Movement preserves the Assertion's identity and carried coordinates while recording its source occurrence, earlier locality relation, newly related subject, Evidence, Scope, Unknown, and limits. Movement does not copy or strengthen the Assertion, revise its Standing, or establish Applicability.

An exact Act determines Applicability before an Assertion participates under a locality relation. The locality relation does not require an Act and does not establish use.

Material movement does not move Authority.

## Required distinctions
- representation != source material
- representation Act occurrence != emission
- emission attempt != emission
- failed emission != absence of occurrence
- absent Evidence establishing emission != boundary rejection
- emission != Applicability
- candidate != Admission
- Admission != Participation
- Admission to one operator Locality != Admission to another operator Locality
- Applicability != input support
- material movement != Authority movement
- equal material content != same Ingest occurrence, result identity, or Yield Evidence
- Assertion identity != Assertion occurrence
- locality subjects != locality relation
- operator material != Seed truth
