# Movement

### 03.Movement.A — Exact movement

Before one movement Act occurrence, its exact subject, source coordinates,
destination coordinates, exact Act, and Locality are exact.

One exact Yield relation is from the movement Act occurrence as first subject
to one result as second subject. The result preserves the exact subject and one
exact source-to-destination relation. Source and destination coordinates
establish no earlier or later occurrence order.
