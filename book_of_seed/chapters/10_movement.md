# Movement

### 03.Movement.A — Exact movement

The exact coordinates of a movement subject-to-Act binding are its exact
subject, source coordinates, destination coordinates, exact Act, and Locality.

The result preserves the exact subject and one exact source-to-destination
relation. The first coordinates of that relation are the exact source
coordinates. The second coordinates of that relation are the exact destination
coordinates.
