# Assertion Source Coordinates

## Constitutional subject

The source coordinates carried by an Assertion and the separate Standing of any asserted source relation.

## Bounded resolution

Supplied or recorded material preserves its source role, provenance, and source occurrence only under its exact responsible occurrence and boundaries. Those coordinates establish no source relation.

The relation “source S supplied or asserted Assertion A” is itself an Assertion. It requires its own Responsibility, Act, occurrence, Authority, Scope, conflicts, Unknown, limits, and Standing.

Standing carries its subject reference separately from its source-relation reference. Establishing either one establishes no other.

### 05.Source.A — Later participation preserves source coordinates

Participation of a recorded Assertion or finding requires the exact Act's Applicability boundary. Participation preserves every carried source coordinate and limit; it establishes neither the asserted content nor its source relation.

## Temporal coordinates

Temporal coordinates distinguish the time an Assertion describes, its source occurrence, its recording occurrence, and a later Act occurrence. One temporal coordinate establishes no others.

## Important distinctions

- source relation Standing != asserted-content Standing
- recording != Standing
- preserved Assertion != current Applicability
