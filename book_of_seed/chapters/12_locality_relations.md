# Locality Relations

## Constitutional subject

The exact locality relation between two exact bounded subjects.

## Bounded resolution

### 06.Locality.A — Locality

Locality is an exact relation between two exact bounded subjects established by a responsible occurrence. The relation preserves both subjects, its occurrence, Authority, Scope, limits, and Unknown. The subjects, co-presence, and chronology establish no Locality relation.

One subject participates in several Locality relations only through their separate responsible occurrences. A Locality relation cannot enclose its subjects, establish Authority or Participation, or carry Standing for either subject.

A responsible movement occurrence preserves an Assertion while establishing a new Locality relation only under its exact Responsibility, Act, Authority, Scope, and limits. Movement preserves the Assertion reference, its earlier Locality relations, Scope, Standing, Unknown, and limits. It establishes the new Locality relation and no other relation.

Chronology alone establishes no locality, causation, or response. An Act occurrence preserves the exact locality relation of each participating subject separately from any locality relation resulting from that occurrence.

### 06.Locality.B — Standing Locality continuation Responsibility

Within the Scope of one intact addressed occurrence, current Standing exposes one Responsibility bounded to preservation of the exact prior Locality Standing boundary addressed by that occurrence as available at one new Locality. The Responsibility preserves the responsible boundary, source Standing boundary, addressed occurrence, destination Locality, Authority, Scope, limits, and Unknown.

The Responsibility precedes the continuation Act, Act occurrence, Locality relation, Yield, and result. Current Standing must carry that Responsibility before the continuation Act occurrence.

Active Book Authority is bounded to this direct occurrence and result boundary. The Responsibility establishes no Applicability, Participation, revision, other Locality relation, or continuation from another continuation.

### 06.Locality.C — Recorded Standing boundary Locality Responsibility

When current Locality Standing carries one intact recorded Standing boundary reference or one direct relation preserving that reference, current Standing exposes one Responsibility bounded to establish a direct Locality relation from that recorded result to one new Locality. The Responsibility preserves its subject, recorded result reference, responsible boundary, exact Act, Act occurrence, relation occurrence, destination Locality, Authority, Scope, limits, Unknown, and Standing.

The result carries exact Responsibility, Act, Act-occurrence, Locality-relation, Yield, and result references. The relation preserves the exact recorded result reference and destination. It cannot copy the addressed Standing, carry another relation, revise the recorded boundary, establish Applicability or Participation, or perform Compare.

### 06.Locality.D — Operator invocation Locality Responsibility

When current operator Locality Standing carries one exact operator material occurrence beginning an invocation, current Standing exposes one Responsibility bounded to establish one new Locality and one direct Locality relation from the operator Locality to it. The Responsibility preserves its subject, operator material occurrence, source Locality, new Locality, responsible boundary, exact Act, Act occurrence, relation occurrence, Authority, Scope, limits, Unknown, and Standing.

The exact Act occurs only when current Standing at the new Locality carries the exact Responsibility under its Authority, Scope, and limits.

The operator material occurrence carries operator Authority for that exact invocation only. The result carries exact Responsibility, Act, Act-occurrence, Locality-relation, Yield, and result references. Material supplied by the invocation in the new Locality requires exact provenance to the operator material occurrence and relation result. The relation cannot copy operator Standing, make supplied material applicable to the operator Locality, or establish Participation. Each other operator material occurrence requires another Responsibility and another new Locality.

## Important distinctions

- locality subjects != locality relation
- locality != occurrence
- movement != copying or revision
- availability != Applicability
- Responsibility != continuation Act or Locality relation
- recorded Standing boundary reference != its Locality relation
- one direct recorded boundary relation != another direct relation
- operator material occurrence != supplied material occurrence
- operator Authority for one invocation != supplied material Applicability
