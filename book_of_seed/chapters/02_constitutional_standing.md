# Constitutional Standing

## Constitutional subject
Standing for an exact subject.

## Core distinction
What exact responsible occurrence establishes Standing for its subject?

## Bounded resolution
Preserved material establishes no support relation to an Assertion it carries. Standing carried by this Seed requires an exact responsible occurrence establishing that relation under its Authority, Scope, and limits.


## Preserved material

### 01.Standing.A — Preserved material is not promotion
A result or preserved material establishes no Acceptance, Admission, support relation, recording, or Authorization. Each requires its exact responsible occurrence and boundaries.

### 01.Standing.D — Multiplicity establishes no collective
Co-presence or multiplicity establishes no relation, collective Standing, shared Act or result boundary, or higher-order identity. Each stronger Assertion requires its own bounded subject and established support relation.

### 01.Standing.D.1 — An Assertion preserves its Standing coordinates
A bounded **Assertion** is the exact asserted content as its own subject under its provenance, Scope, Authority, conflicts, limits, **Unknown**, and current Standing. The Responsibility preserving those applicable Standing coordinates carries the bounded Assertion as its subject.

The Assertion cannot perform the Act occurrence that supplied it, supply itself, or become that occurrence. Authority supporting an Assertion establishes no Standing for that Assertion. Result Standing requires its exact subject, responsible boundary, occurrence, and Standing. The Assertion's Standing Responsibility begins with the result; it cannot relocate the Act's Responsibility or occurrence-result relation into the result. Act occurrence != Assertion responsible boundary, result-carrying Act != Assertion Responsibility, and recorded occurrence != result-carrying occurrence.

An Assertion having current Standing requires no other Act to persist. Revision or movement requires an applicable responsible occurrence with its own Act, Scope, Authority, and limits. An **Unknown** supplies no required result and persistence establishes no Stop.

### 01.Standing.D.2 — A later Assertion meets current Standing, not every preserved Assertion
Where an Assertion and established Standing have the same subject, the Assertion's applicability is considered with respect to that current Standing. Preservation of earlier Assertions with the same subject makes no one of them a separate applicability boundary and requires no comparison of the later Assertion against each of them because preservation carries each exact reference and its coordinates. Applicability concerns that exact Assertion-to-Standing position for the exact Act; it establishes no agreement, relation, truth, corroboration, or revision.

Where an applicable Assertion with a bounded subject participates in a Compare, a separately established Compare result subject carries the comparison, the coordinate distinction, or the result shape under its exact result coordinates. That result subject cannot replace, supersede, or discharge the subject for which applicability was determined. Where a responsible occurrence establishes movement in Standing for that subject, that movement and no other movement is carried; the movement preserves previously established coordinates, **Unknown**, and the exact subject coordinate when no new Standing for them was established. A subject that ceases to be carried has not been superseded by the result; it has been abandoned, and nothing further can be established about it. This clause establishes neither what carries current Standing into a Compare nor that a Compare result is itself a finding participating in another Act. For those crossings, Standing: unestablished under this clause.

`01.Standing.D.1` bounds an Act to current Standing under its responsible boundary, Authority, Scope, and limits. Standing for a subject requires no count of prior Assertions.

### 01.Standing.E — Relations have bounded standing
A relation is its own bounded Assertion subject. Its Standing preserves each applicable coordinate or Unknown Standing, including participants and roles, the relation Assertion, Scope, exact Act, Authority, occurrence, conflicts, and limits.

One bounded relation Assertion carries X, one exact relation, and Y. It preserves each applicable coordinate, including participants and roles, the relation Assertion, source occurrence where established, Scope, exact Act and result boundary, provenance, Authority, known loss, conflicts, limits, and Unknown Standing.

Material carrying the Assertion of one exact relation from X to Y does not support that relation. A responsible occurrence establishes the relation bounded by its Authority, Scope, provenance, conflicts, loss, and preserved **Unknown**, and establishes no other relation.

An Act-local Applicability determination for an established relation requires its own exact occurrence. Where the exact Act requires Admission, the relation must be admitted before participation. Applicable is not admitted; Admission is not participation; participation establishes no input-to-result support relation. Each relation carries exact Responsibility and Act coordinates.

### 01.Standing.E.1 — The exact Act responsible boundary ordinarily determines input Applicability
Input is an Act-local role. That role requires an exact bounded subject and its exact relation to one Act while preserving its content, locality, provenance, Authority, Scope, limits, Unknown, and Standing. Input role != input subject, locality, Applicability, Admission, Participation, input-to-result support, Act occurrence, or result Standing.

A subject occupies one exact Role relative to an Act only through an exact Participation occurrence. Participation is the occurrence-bound relation that an exact subject occupied an exact role in an exact Act occurrence. Subject, role, Act, applicable Standing, and Act occurrence do not establish participation by co-presence.

Unless the Book assigns otherwise, the Responsibility assigned to an exact Act ensures Applicability is determined for every input before participation. Applicability is determined for that Act's exact subject, content, result boundary, Scope, locality, Authority, participants and roles, and preserved limits. The determination preserves every applicable input and Act coordinate.

For determining Applicability, the responsible boundary need not duplicate every Applicability determination personally. Applicability is determined by the same bounded occurrence or by an assigned responsible occurrence carrying Applicability Standing for that exact Act. The latter Standing must have the exact input-to-Act relation and coordinates as its subject. Usefulness, agreement, or availability without that exact relation and coordinates establishes no Applicability. A Book assignment therefore is a revision of the default exact Act Responsibility assignment; it cannot create one Applicability occurrence shared across Acts. Applicability for one Act is not Applicability for another, and Applicability for one Act is not Admission for another Act. The Responsibility assigned to a later Act determines its Applicability unless another exact responsible occurrence is assigned; the exact Act requires admission where applicable.

An Applicability occurrence carries one of **applicable**, **inapplicable**, **conflicting**, or **Unknown** only for its exact input-to-Act relation and preserved coordinates. It carries none of Admission, participation, truth, a support relation for the input's Assertion, Authorization, addressed-Act occurrence, result Standing, or movement. An excluded input does not participate in that Act, and no result asserts support from that excluded input.

Exclusion of one proposed input establishes no Act occurrence. The Responsibility governing the exact Act carries that determination under its relations, Authority, Scope, and limits. Candidate exclusion establishes no Authorization or Stop, and an Act result establishes no support from an excluded input. A lawful Stop requires its own Responsibility, Act, occurrence, Authority, Scope, and result.

Availability, the same content, or exclusion of another input establishes no Participation for an alternative input. Before participation, the Responsibility assigned to the Act determines or validates Applicability for that exact input-to-Act relation and every coordinate the Act requires. Whether one input occupies another's exact role carries **Unknown** unless a responsible occurrence establishes that relation for the exact Act, result boundary, and Scope.

One bounded responsible occurrence determines Applicability for inputs, excludes **inapplicable**, **conflicting**, or **Unknown** inputs, performs the exact Act or establishes that the proposed Act did not occur, and carries bounded output Standing only under its exact Responsibility, Authority, Scope, and limits. Each Assertion carries exact coordinates. Applicability success is not Act occurrence; Act occurrence supplies no output Standing; and output Standing is neither Applicability nor Admission for another Act.

Result Standing requires its exact subject, responsible boundary, occurrence, and Standing. The result Act boundary and occurrence establish no Standing boundary or occurrence. Authority supporting the result establishes no result Standing. Yield, result existence, and Locality availability establish no result Standing. Lawfully supported Standing persists without later Participation, but persistence supplies neither another Act, Responsibility, result boundary, nor occurrence. Applicability supplies neither a result, another Assertion, nor Authority.

## Important distinctions
- represented provenance != verified result occurrence
- representation != constitutional standing
- representation shape != constitutional kind
- Representation validity != lawful provenance
- public visibility != constitutional status
- preserved assertion != support for the assertion
- dedicated representation != durable constitutional kind
- concordance entry != constitutional legitimacy
- Standing established for a new subject != Standing re-established for a prior subject
- act-local standing != inherited responsible occurrence standing
- behavior != constitutional standing
- multiplicity != collective standing
- Assertion responsible boundary != responsible occurrence or result-establishing Act
- Assertion Standing persists != continuation or constitutional Stop established by identity
- new or conflicting material available != Assertion revised by identity
- represented-relation Assertion carried != relation established
- represented relation != proposition truth
- represented relation != source label
- source label != established source relation
- relation established != Act-local Applicability
- applicable != admitted
- Admission != participation
- participation != an established input-to-result support relation
