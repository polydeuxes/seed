# Constitutional Standing

## Constitutional subject
The difference between representing a constitutional subject and possessing the standing of that subject.

## Core distinction
Which kinds are constitutionally recognized, and what establishes a representation's Standing as one of them?

## Bounded resolution
Where a preserved representation or record carries an Assertion made by another Responsibility, its shape preserves only its exact identity, provenance, result, uncertainty, and boundaries. That shape establishes no support relation from its Evidence, Authority, Scope, provenance, and limits to the Assertion. The relation requires its own Assertion and Standing.

Preserved material establishes no support relation to an Assertion it carries. Standing carried by this Seed requires the exact support relation from its Evidence, Authority, Scope, and preserved limits to the Assertion.

Current Standing whose subject is the exact Assertion that its responsible boundary bears a Responsibility is required for this Seed to read that Responsibility. The assignment Assertion retains its own Evidence, Authority, Scope, limits, occurrence, and Standing. Responsibility != Standing; Standing whose subject is an assignment cannot create, perform, or become the Responsibility.


## Preserved material

### 01.Standing.A — Preserved material is not promotion
An exact identity and Locality of a result, preserved record, representation, index entry, or other material establish none of acceptance, admission, support, representation as truth, writing to the event ledger, or Authorization for mutation. Exact identity, preservation, and Locality do not establish acceptance, input support, admission, representation, recording, or action; each requires a separate family-local boundary.

### 01.Standing.B — Names do not establish Standing
A kind label, Representation, characterization category, function name, concordance entry, or recurring report type preserves a bounded distinction only for the exact subject and Act established by its occurrence. A shared name establishes neither another subject nor relation and supplies no Authority for the exact Act without preserved Evidence, provenance, Scope, Unknown, and the applicable occurrence boundary.

### 01.Standing.C — Behavior cannot confer standing
Compiled behavior, direct instantiation, and exact represented results witness only what occurs at their exact read boundary, emission boundary, or other bounded seam. They do not establish the Standing suggested by a name, output words, or behavior. Such Standing still requires its own subject, responsible occurrence, Evidence, Scope, Authority, and limits.

### 01.Standing.D — Multiplicity establishes no collective
Co-presence or multiplicity establishes no relation, collective Standing, shared Act or result boundary, or higher-order identity. Each stronger Assertion requires its own bounded subject and established support relation.

### 01.Standing.D.1 — An Assertion preserves its Standing coordinates
A bounded **Assertion** is the exact asserted content as its own subject under its carried Evidence, provenance, Scope, Authority, conflicts, limits, **Unknown**, and current Standing. An Assertion bears the Responsibility for preserving those applicable Standing coordinates. That Responsibility carries the bounded Assertion as its local subject.

The Assertion cannot thereby perform the Act occurrence that supplied it, supply itself, or become that occurrence. Evidence and Authority supporting an Assertion establish no bounded Standing for that Assertion by identity. Result Standing requires its exact subject, Standing responsible boundary, Standing occurrence, Standing-occurrence Evidence, and Standing. The Assertion's Standing-coordinate Responsibility begins with the result; it cannot relocate the Act's Responsibility or occurrence-result Evidence into the result. Act occurrence != Assertion responsible boundary, result-carrying Act != Assertion Responsibility, and recorded occurrence != result-carrying occurrence.

An Assertion having current Standing requires no other Act because it exists. New, different, or conflicting material cannot revise its Standing by identity. An **Unknown** cannot create a required result to eliminate it. Further movement requires an applicable responsible occurrence with its own Act, Evidence, Scope, Authority, and limits. In the absence of such an occurrence, preserving the Assertion and its current Standing is lawful persistence, not an inferred constitutional Stop.

### 01.Standing.D.2 — A later Assertion meets current Standing, not every preserved Assertion
Where an Assertion and established Standing have the same subject, the Assertion's applicability is considered with respect to that current Standing. Preservation of earlier Assertions with the same subject makes no one of them a separate applicability boundary and requires no comparison of the later Assertion against each of them because preservation carries each exact reference and its coordinates. Applicability concerns that exact Assertion-to-Standing position for the exact Act; it establishes no agreement, relation, truth, corroboration, or revision.

Where an applicable Assertion with a bounded subject participates in a Compare, a separately established Compare result subject carries the comparison, the coordinate distinction, or the result shape under its exact result coordinates. That result subject cannot replace, supersede, or discharge the subject for which applicability was determined. Where a responsible occurrence establishes movement in Standing for that subject, that movement and no other movement is carried; the movement preserves previously established coordinates, **Unknown**, and the exact subject coordinate when no new Standing for them was established. A subject that ceases to be carried has not been superseded by the result; it has been abandoned, and nothing further can be established about it. This clause establishes neither what carries current Standing into a Compare nor that a Compare result is itself a finding participating in another Act. For those crossings, Standing: unestablished under this clause.

`01.Standing.D.1` bounds an Act to the Standing its Evidence and Authority support. Poor Standing supported by a single Assertion is Standing; absence of prior Standing cannot prevent a responsible occurrence from supporting Standing, and Standing for a subject requires no count of prior Assertions.

### 01.Standing.E — Relations have bounded standing
A relation is its own bounded Assertion subject. Its Standing preserves each applicable coordinate, or Unknown or unresolved Standing, including participants and roles, the relation Assertion, Evidence, Scope, exact Act, Authority, occurrence, conflicts, and limits. Evidence supporting no relation supports neither its negation nor an opposing strengthened relation. A bounded finding preserves absence of applicable relation Evidence only within its exact boundary. Candidate relation, source-labelled relation material, and established relation Standing carry separate exact coordinates.

One bounded relation Assertion carries X, one exact relation, and Y. It preserves each applicable coordinate, including participants and roles, the relation Assertion, source identity, source occurrence where evidenced, Evidence, Scope, exact Act and result boundary, provenance, Authority, known loss, conflicts, limits, and Unknown or unresolved Standing.

Material carrying the Assertion of one exact relation from X to Y does not thereby support that relation. A responsible occurrence establishes the relation bounded by its Authority, Evidence, Scope, provenance, conflicts, loss, and preserved **Unknown**, and establishes no other relation. A source label carries an asserted source role; it establishes neither a source occurrence nor the represented relation.

An Act-local Applicability determination for an established relation requires its own exact occurrence. Where the exact Act requires Admission, the relation must be admitted before participation. Applicable is not admitted; Admission is not participation; participation establishes no input-to-result support relation. Each relation carries exact Responsibility and Act coordinates.

### 01.Standing.E.1 — The exact Act responsible boundary ordinarily determines input Applicability
Input is an Act-local role, not a constitutional kind. Bearing that role requires an exact bounded subject and its exact relation to one Act while retaining its own identity, content, locality, provenance, Evidence, Authority, Scope, limits, Unknown, and Standing. Another subject's locality by identity makes no represented subject the input. Input role != input subject, locality, Applicability, Admission, participation, input-to-result support, Act occurrence, or result Standing.

A subject occupies one exact Role relative to an Act only through an exact Participation occurrence. Participation is the occurrence-bound relation that an exact subject occupied an exact role in an exact Act occurrence. Subject, role, Act, applicable Standing, and Act occurrence do not establish participation by co-presence.

Unless the Book assigns otherwise, the Responsibility assigned to an exact Act ensures Applicability is determined for every input before participation. Applicability is determined for that Act's exact subject, content, result boundary, Scope, locality, Authority, participants and roles, and preserved limits. The determination preserves every applicable input and Act coordinate.

For determining Applicability, the responsible boundary need not duplicate every Applicability determination personally. Applicability is determined by the same bounded occurrence or by an assigned responsible occurrence carrying Applicability Standing for that exact Act. The latter Standing must have the exact input-to-Act relation and coordinates as its subject. Usefulness, agreement, or availability without that exact relation and coordinates establishes no Applicability. A Book assignment therefore is a revision of the default exact Act Responsibility assignment; it cannot create one Applicability occurrence shared across Acts. Applicability for one Act is not Applicability for another, and Applicability for one Act is not Admission for another Act. The Responsibility assigned to a later Act determines its Applicability unless another exact responsible occurrence is assigned; the exact Act requires admission where applicable.

An Applicability occurrence carries one of **applicable**, **inapplicable**, **conflicting**, or **Unknown** only for its exact input-to-Act relation and preserved coordinates. It carries none of Admission, participation, truth, a support relation for the input's Assertion, Authorization, addressed-Act occurrence, result Standing, or movement. An excluded input does not participate in that Act, and no result asserts support from that excluded input.

Exclusion of one proposed input by itself establishes neither Act occurrence nor absence of Act occurrence. The responsibility assigned to perform the exact act carries that determination under the relations, evidence, authority, scope, and other boundaries assigned to that responsibility for the exact occurrence. One rejected candidate is not all candidates rejected; candidate exclusion establishes neither Authorization, act prohibition, nor a lawful Stop; act occurrence without an excluded input cannot mean that input was applicable; and an act result is not a result supported by an excluded input. A lawful Stop requires its own competent act, authority, evidence, and material carrying source coordinates; absence of act occurrence is not by itself a lawful Stop, and a return or quiescence is not by itself a lawful Stop.

Availability, the same content, or exclusion of another input establishes no Participation for an alternative input. Before participation, the Responsibility assigned to the Act determines or validates Applicability for that exact input-to-Act relation and every coordinate the Act requires. Whether one input occupies another's exact role carries **Unknown** unless a responsible occurrence establishes that relation for the exact Act, result boundary, and Scope.

One bounded responsible occurrence determines Applicability for inputs, excludes **inapplicable**, **conflicting**, or **Unknown** inputs, performs the exact Act or carries an absence-of-Act-occurrence finding, and carries bounded output Standing only where its exact assignment, Evidence, Authority, Scope, and limits establish every coordinate. Each Assertion carries exact coordinates. Same occurrence is not same Assertion; Applicability success is not Act occurrence; Act occurrence supplies no output Standing; and output Standing is neither Applicability nor Admission for another Act.

Result Standing requires its exact subject, Standing responsible boundary, Standing occurrence, Standing-occurrence Evidence, and Standing. The result Act responsible boundary establishes no Standing responsible boundary by identity. The result Act occurrence establishes no Standing occurrence by identity. Evidence and Authority supporting the result establish no result Standing by identity. Yield, result existence, and Locality availability establish no result Standing by identity. Lawfully supported Standing persists without later participation, but persistence supplies neither another Act, Responsibility, result boundary, nor occurrence. Applicability supplies neither a result, another Assertion, nor Authority.

### 01.Standing.F — Dimensional characterization
Exact orientation by this Seed commonly carries constitutional subjects and relations through approximately eight recurring macro-dimensional families:

1. subject / identity
2. assertion / content
3. standing
4. source / provenance
5. responsibility
6. authority / support
7. scope / locality
8. occurrence / preservation

These families are broad constitutional distinctions, not mandatory fields or one fixed representation required across exact subjects. Dimension != field, value, constitutional kind, fixed subject-family set, or key. The paired labels are bounded compressions of neighboring coordinates, not declarations that every pair is one indivisible scalar: standing is recurrent and cross-cutting; occurrence and preservation are related but separate; source and provenance are neighboring but not equivalent; and authority and support are neighboring but not equivalent. Approximately eight macro-dimensional families != exactly eight constitutional fields; this grouping is neither mathematically nor constitutionally final.

Macro-dimensional families unfold into local coordinates under the exact subject, distinction, Act, Responsibility, result boundary, Scope, and Evidence boundary. No fixed coordinate count, final set, completeness requirement across exact subjects, or mandatory Representation applies. A coordinate carries applicable, inapplicable, known, **Unknown**, conflicting, or unresolved only within that boundary.

Acts and relations are distinct from dimensions. They operate over or connect dimension-bearing subjects only under their exact constitutional grammar. Dimension-bearing material != the responsible act over that material. Their presence in dimensional characterization establishes no relation carrying them as additional dimensions, a mandatory layer, or one exhaustive set of kinds.

**Unknown** Standing is not one fixed set of kinds. A coordinate, relation, distinction, or Standing carries **Unknown** only when its required Evidence, Authority, provenance, occurrence, Applicability, or other local relation is not established or available. **Unknown** carries its exact subject, distinction, Act, result boundary, Scope, Evidence, Authority, and responsible occurrence as bounds. Inapplicable != **Unknown**.
