# Representation and Emission

One material-acquisition Responsibility bounds one exact source boundary and
one acquisition Act. One exact Yield relation carries the Act occurrence as
first subject and one exact material result as second subject. The result preserves its source role, source boundary,
provenance, Authority, Scope,
Locality, known loss, limits, and Unknown.

Material supplied by this operator and material supplied by this Witness have
separate source Responsibility branches and occurrences. Shared material establishes
no shared occurrence, result, Yield, source relation, or Standing.

A Representation Act preserves exact material from one exact source result.
One exact Yield relation carries its occurrence as first subject and one exact
Representation result as second subject. The result carries that material and its source coordinates. It
establishes no relation for the material content.

An emission Responsibility carries one exact Representation result, one
destination operator boundary, one emission Act, Authority, Scope, Locality,
limits, conflicts, known loss, and Unknown.

Required Admission carries one exact occurrence establishing the
Representation-to-destination position for that emission Act. Admission
establishes no Participation or emission.

The admitted Representation requires one exact Participation relation to the
emission Act occurrence. That occurrence and the exact destination boundary
result are the first and second subjects of one Yield relation. The occurrence carries the emitted material.

For an exact-material boundary write, the result preserves the exact material
accepted by the boundary and its reported count. Attempt, partial write,
failure, accepted write, and emission are separate occurrences and results.
An accepted write and a later failure are separate; the later failure
establishes no erasure of the accepted write. Effects beyond the addressed
boundary requires a separate responsible occurrence.
