# Stopping and Completion

One stopping Responsibility carries its exact subject, stopping Act,
Authority, Scope, Locality, limits, and required support relations.

A support Assertion requires one exact Participation relation to the stopping
Act occurrence. The occurrence and one exact Stop result are the first and
second subjects of one Yield relation.

Completion is a separate Assertion with its own exact subject,
Responsibility, Act occurrence, Yield, Authority, Scope, Locality, limits, and
Standing.
