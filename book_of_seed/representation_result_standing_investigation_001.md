Measurement, Compare, recording, supplied material, and the current bounded

answer circularly.

## 1. The producer is not the general Representation boundary

The active Book keeps this distinction:

```text
representation
!=
Act that established it
```

It also permits a recording boundary to create an exact representation of
Events carrying source coordinates, findings, Assertions, or established
Standing within its Scope.

Recording and the current bounded Representation Act are different Acts.
Therefore:

```text
result was established by the bounded Representation Act
```

cannot be a necessary condition for every Representation result.

The converse also fails. A result of the bounded Representation Act does not
obtain every claimed Standing merely from the function name, event kind, or
result label. The exact occurrence-to-result relation, Evidence, Authority,
Scope, source coordinates, limits, and supported Standing remain required.

Disposition: **producer identity is neither a universal necessary condition
nor a sufficient condition for Representation Standing**.

## 2. Result shape is not the general Representation boundary

Active constitutional Standing keeps these distinctions:

```text
representation shape
!=
constitutional kind

dedicated representation
!=
durable constitutional kind
```

A preserved representation or record may have a shape carrying source,
provenance, result, uncertainty, and boundaries. That shape does not create
the support relation from Evidence, Authority, Scope, provenance, and limits
to the Assertion whose Standing is claimed.

Therefore no structural checklist can lawfully say:

```text
has subject
+ source
+ Evidence
+ Authority
+ Scope
+ limits
+ Unknown
therefore Representation
```

Those coordinates are required to preserve the exact claim. Their co-presence
does not establish the claim.

This applies equally to:

- the current `operator.representation.recorded` material;
- Candidate Standing results;
- Measurement results;
- Compare results;
- recorded references;
- explanation material;
- supplied representation-shaped material.

Disposition: **coordinate shape is preservation testimony, not
Representation Standing**.

## 3. What the result represents cannot be the prerequisite

Active Representation material permits:

```text
exact Representation exists
what its material represents: Unknown
represented relation: not established
```

The exact-material Representation rule preserves the same exact material and
source-result occurrence while establishing neither what the material
represents nor a represented relation.

`01.Source.F` separately requires a responsible occurrence for the represented
relation. Locality, labels, reference resolution, shape, and repetition cannot
establish it.

Therefore Representation Standing cannot universally require an already
established semantic relation between the result and what humans say it
represents. That would make the Representation depend on a relation the Book
explicitly permits to remain Unknown.

The narrower distinction is:

```text
Standing that this exact result is a Representation
!=
Standing for what that Representation represents
```

The second may remain Unknown even if the first is established.

Disposition: **represented-relation Standing is not a universal prerequisite
for Representation Standing**.

## 4. Active uses do not recover one universal result rule

Active material uses `representation` across several boundaries.

### 4.1 Supplied representation

A supplied representation may enter as material with exact source
coordinates. Entry supplies neither Evidence, applicable input, adopted law,
truth, native grammar, nor Seed Standing by identity.

This is source testimony that the supplied material is addressed as a
representation. It is not sufficient for this Seed to carry Representation
Standing for it.

### 4.2 Bounded Representation Act result

The current Representation road records:

```text
bounded Representation Act occurrence
    -> Evidence of Yield relation
    -> bounded Representation result
```

It preserves one Locality Standing boundary and optionally one same-Locality
source occurrence. It is the strongest current positive result witness.

But the runtime directly authors its Representation kind and records Authority
as `unestablished`. It records no exact Representation Responsibility
assignment occurrence. `01.Source.A` describes what the responsible
Representation occurrence preserves without completing that assignment
physiology.

Thus even the strongest current witness does not yield a producer-independent
definition.

### 4.3 Recording-created representation

A recording boundary may create an exact representation of Events carrying
source coordinates and Standing. The same clause keeps recording, Measurement,
Standing-bearing occurrence, and emission as distinct Acts.

This proves that another Act family may establish a representation. It does
not say that every recording result is one or supply a universal relation for
all exact results.

### 4.4 Explanation and stopping material

Active material calls an Explanation a representation exposing already
established Evidence, provenance, conflicts, and reasons. A stopping Act may
also respond with a Representation carrying material and an unmet-requirement
Assertion.

These uses preserve the separation between exact producer and represented
result. They do not share one exact machine-readable result rule with the
operator Representation road.

Disposition: **active uses recover several family-local Representation
positions, not one universal result criterion**.

## 5. Generic Standing requirements do not recover the exact relation

The general Standing rule supplies the narrowest recoverable requirement.

For this Seed to carry Standing for an exact Assertion, the exact support
relation must connect the Assertion with its Evidence, Authority, Scope, and
preserved limits. The Assertion and relation retain their exact subject,
responsible occurrence, provenance, conflicts, Unknown, and result boundary.

Applied here, the subject is not every coordinate inside the result. The first
version named the bounded claim as:

```text
Representation Standing of this exact result
```

That phrase is useful orientation, but active Book and machine grammar do not
name `Representation Standing` as one exact coordinate. It compresses at
least:

```text
exact result reference
+ exact Representation-bearing relation position
+ exact Assertion carrying that relation
+ exact Standing for that Assertion
```

The active material does not yet establish the second coordinate.

The required coordinates are at least:

```text
exact result reference
exact Representation-standing subject
exact responsible occurrence
occurrence-to-result Evidence
support relation
Authority
Scope
source and provenance where established
limits
conflicts
Unknown
Standing boundary
```

This list does not define what Representation means or which relation connects
the result to that word. It defines what must bound a lawful Standing claim
once an exact family establishes the subject and relation.

The missing coordinate is therefore neither a Python field named
`representation` nor an Assertion whose connective is silently filled with
`is`. It is the exact relation position, supported Assertion, and Standing for
the result.

Disposition: **the generic Standing physiology is recovered; the exact
result-to-Representation relation is not**.

### 5.1 `is` cannot fill the vacancy

The active Book uses `is` widely for materially different work, including:

- named distinctions such as `Responsibility is not Standing`;
- statements about exact subjects;
- ordinary English connective material;
- negative boundaries;
- descriptions of what one relation or result carries.

`is` is admitted to Book material, but Rosetta currently supplies no
decomposition that gives all of those uses one exact relation. The repository
therefore cannot use the sentence:

```text
this result is a Representation
```

as though `is` already established identity, membership, classification,
predication, equality, or another exact relation. Those proposed
interpretations are reader testimony.

The safer unresolved shape is:

```text
exact result
    -- exact relation: Unknown -->
Representation-oriented subject: unresolved
```

Even that drawing is orientation. A responsible occurrence must establish
the exact participants, roles, relation Assertion, Evidence, Authority,
Scope, limits, Unknown, and Standing before the relation can be carried as
Standing.

Disposition: **`is` is the vacancy carrier, not its solution**.

## 8. Standing is not a Representation field by identity

Those maps make exact coordinates recoverable. They do not create one current

Active material also keeps:

```text

!=

```

Therefore the recurrence does not currently contain an automatic step:

```text

```

Such a result could be established by an exact future Responsibility. It is
not established by co-presence, append order, replay, or the fact that all
coordinates are addressable.

Disposition: **no recursively updated aggregate Representation field is
currently established**.

## 9. Heartbeat disposition

The strongest current recurrence is:

```text
Standing_n
    -> exact Responsibility
    -> exact Act
    -> exact occurrence
    -> exact Yield
    -> exact result
    -> Standing_n+1
```

This is generic physiology shared by several Act families.

The operator and curator's stronger topology remains coherent as a
hypothesis:

```text
Standing_n
    -> many exact Responsibilities
    -> many exact Acts
    -> exact results
    -> result-level Representation Standing where supported
    -> Standing_n+1
```

It does not require the producing Acts to be Representation Acts. It does
require exact result-level Representation Standing rather than a type name or
shape inference.

Disposition: **`heartbeat` is useful Rosetta language for the recurrence;
the Representation layer inside each beat remains partially Unknown**.

## 11. Answer

No active universal rule currently answers:

```text
What exact Standing makes any result a Representation?
```

The repository recovers family-local positive positions and generic Standing
requirements. It does not recover the connective in this earlier proposed
shape:

```text
exact result
+ exact relation to a Representation-oriented subject
+ exact Assertion carrying that relation
+ exact responsible occurrence
+ Evidence / support relation / Authority / Scope / limits / Unknown
    -> bounded Standing for that exact Assertion
```

The exact relation remains Unknown. Calling it `is`, Identity, membership,
classification, predication, or equality would supply the result of the
investigation in advance.

The exact current dispositions are:

```text
Candidate-production Responsibility
!= existing Representation Responsibility        established

Candidate-production Act
!= existing Representation Act                   established

Candidate Standing
= Representation                                 Unknown

Candidate Standing
!= Representation                                Unknown

Measurement result
= Representation                                 Unknown

Compare result
= Representation                                 Unknown

all current Standing through C
= one recursively updated Representation          not established
```

The relation between a result and what it represents is a separate boundary.
The relation by which a result bears whatever humans call Representation is
also unresolved; neither can be inferred from the other.

## 12. Smallest next recovery

Do not add a wrapper Representation around Candidate Standing merely to make
the type relation true. Do not rename Candidate Standing as Representation.
Do not assert that Candidate Standing is excluded from Representation.

The smallest next recovery is family-local:

```text
For one exact Candidate result,
what exact relation position, responsible occurrence,
and support relation, if any,
connect that result with the currently unresolved
Representation-oriented subject?
```

That recovery must preserve:

- Candidate production Responsibility and Representation Standing as
  distinct coordinates;
- Candidate Act and existing Representation Act as distinct Acts;
- Candidate completeness debt and exact B/C boundaries;
- source Assertions in their original Localities;
- represented relation Unknown;
- no automatic Representation Standing for Measurement, Compare, or every
  result by analogy;
- no aggregate Standing Representation by co-presence.

It must not prefill the relation with `is`, identity, membership,
classification, predication, equality, or a Python kind check.

If no exact occurrence assigns or supports that result-level subject, the
answer remains Unknown. The usefulness of the heartbeat hypothesis cannot
supply the missing Standing.
