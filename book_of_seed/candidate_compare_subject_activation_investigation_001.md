# Candidate Compare subject activation investigation 001

## Question

Given one complete Candidate population already recorded by this Seed, what
exact current Standing exposes those Candidates as subjects of one active
Compare Responsibility?

The required road is:

```text
current Standing
        ↓
exact Compare Responsibility
        ↓
complete required Candidate subject population
        ↓
Applicability for each subject-to-Compare position
        ↓
Admission only where that exact boundary requires it
        ↓
Participation(role)
        ↓
Compare Act occurrence
        ↓
Yield
        ↓
result
```

This investigation does not add a Compare Responsibility, address a Candidate
subset, or infer a relation from co-participation. It changes no active Book
chapter, machine witness, Rosetta material, runtime, script, or test.

## 1. The complete Candidate population is one result population

`01.Source.E.1` declares two Candidate Acts:

```text
one source Assertion at a time

and

each distinct ordered source Assertion pair
```

The ordered-pair Act exhausts the complete source Assertion population through
one exact boundary. Its result records exactly:

```text
N × (N - 1)
```

ordered Candidates. Each Candidate preserves its first and second source
Assertion references, their coordinates, its Act-local roles, and its carried
relation content. No developer-supplied partner address bounds the population.

The calculator Witness already proves that one result can carry both orders of
the interesting pair:

```text
path finding, calculator-result position Assertion

calculator-result position Assertion, path finding
```

Their co-presence establishes no relation between them. The Candidate relation
content remains `Unknown`.

## 2. Recording the population does not place its subjects in current Standing

The exact Candidate result carries `candidate_assertions`. Bounded Locality
replay validates that result and records only its result occurrence address in:

```text
candidate_result_occurrences
```

Active `01.Source.E` is explicit:

```text
The Candidate result establishes no Standing.
```

Therefore the current testimony establishes:

```text
complete Candidate result recorded
        +
Candidate result available in bounded Locality replay
```

It does not establish:

```text
current Standing carries each Candidate Assertion
```

The public readers reinforce that boundary. Reading every Candidate and its
two lower Assertions requires the caller to supply:

```text
candidate_standing_result_event_identity
```

Reading one Candidate additionally requires its Candidate Assertion address.
There is no Responsibility-local reader that starts from exact current
Standing and returns every Candidate subject applicable to Compare.

The runtime name `Candidate Standing` does not supply the missing Standing.

## 3. One later Candidate Act can consume the population, but Compare cannot

`source_assertion_references_for_candidate_standing` reads every exact source
Assertion through one supplied append boundary. Candidate Assertions carried
by an earlier Candidate result enter that source population through their exact
result coordinate:

```text
candidate_assertions/<position>
```

Consequently an earlier Candidate Assertion can receive Applicability and
Participation as a subject of a later Candidate-production Act. That is real
recursive Candidate production.

It establishes only:

```text
Candidate Assertion
        ↓
later Candidate-production subject position
        ↓
later Candidate Act occurrence
```

It establishes no Candidate-to-Compare subject position.

## 4. Active `04.Compare.A` has different exact subjects

`04.Compare.A` requires:

```text
one earlier exact byte-pair position Measurement result
one later exact byte-pair position Measurement result
```

The later result must extend the earlier result's exact acquisition-result
sequence once. The Compare rule addresses the two complete Measurement finding
populations.

A Candidate may preserve references to Assertions carried by such results.
That does not make the Candidate either exact Measurement result. Substituting
the lower references would make the Measurement results act again and would
discard the Candidate's own boundary, roles, and carried coordinates.

Thus `04.Compare.A` does not expose a Candidate subject position.

## 5. Active `04.Compare.B` also has different exact subjects

`04.Compare.B` requires:

```text
one exact ordered relation-path Assertion
one exact recorded pair-finding Compare result
```

Its bounded reader forms the complete Cartesian population of those two exact
result populations already present in its current Standing. It excludes a pair
whose durable Responsibility record already exists. It does not read:

```text
Candidate result
Candidate Assertion
Candidate source roles
Candidate relation content
```

The calculator Candidate carries a path-finding Assertion and a calculator
result Assertion. The second subject is not the exact recorded pair-finding
Compare result required by `04.Compare.B`.

Even where a Candidate's lower sources happen to include subjects used by
`04.Compare.B`, using those lower sources would again consume the sources, not
the Candidate.

Thus `04.Compare.B` does not expose a Candidate subject position.

## 6. The Candidate can be the subject without moving its sources

The refusal above does not prevent a Candidate from acting. It prevents the
Candidate's source Assertions from being substituted for the Candidate.

One Candidate already carries:

```text
Candidate C
├── first source Assertion reference
├── second source Assertion reference
├── first and second Act-local source roles
└── relation content: Unknown
```

A Candidate-Compare road can therefore preserve this distinction:

```text
Candidate C
        ↓
Participation(candidate role)
        ↓
Candidate-Compare Act occurrence
```

The first and second source references remain coordinates carried by `C`.
They need not participate again merely because the Compare Act reads those
coordinates under its exact rule.

This preserves the Candidate as a constitutional subject rather than reducing
it to a convenient pair generator.

## 7. Existing grammar can express the road, but no clause declares it

The active grammar already supplies every general distinction needed to state
the physiology:

```text
current Standing carrying one complete Candidate result CR
        ↓
one exact Candidate-Compare Responsibility
        ↓
every Candidate C carried by CR
        ↓
Applicability of C to the Candidate-Compare Act
        ↓
Admission only where that exact boundary requires it
        ↓
C --Participation(candidate role)--> Compare occurrence
        ↓
Yield
        ↓
result
```

No new universal relation, Candidate grammar, or Compare primitive is needed.

But general Compare anatomy does not declare this exact Responsibility.
Neither `01.Source.E`, `01.Source.E.1`, `04.Compare.A`, nor `04.Compare.B`
supplies:

```text
subject: exact Candidate Assertion
exact Act: Compare Candidate carried coordinates under one exact rule
completeness boundary: every Candidate carried by CR
```

The runtime's `CANDIDATE_ASSERTION_RESPONSIBILITY` preserves one Candidate's
coordinates. It does not govern a Compare Act.

Therefore active grammar can describe the road, but one exact Book clause is
missing before runtime work is warranted.

## 8. Applicability cannot repair a missing Responsibility subject

Applicability addresses one already-exact subject-to-Act position. It does not
discover a Responsibility or create that position.

For the Candidate population, current grammar supplies neither:

```text
exact Compare Responsibility carrying Candidate subjects
```

nor:

```text
current Standing carrying those Candidates under that Responsibility
```

Therefore no exact Candidate-to-Compare Applicability result can yet be
recorded. Without that result, no Candidate Participation relation to a
Compare occurrence can be established.

The active `04.Compare.A` and `04.Compare.B` boundaries require no Admission
coordinate. A future Candidate-Compare clause must state whether its own exact
boundary requires Admission. None should be fabricated from the other Compare
roads.

## 9. Participation would not establish the desired translation relation

Even after one Candidate lawfully participates in one Candidate-Compare
occurrence, Participation establishes no relation between its carried source
Assertions.

For arithmetic material, the desired eventual findings may externally read:

```text
2       ↔ two
+       ↔ plus
=       ↔ is
```

The current Candidate population is useful exact material for that work. It
does not yet establish any of those relations. A Candidate-Compare Act must
apply one exact rule to each exact Candidate subject, Yield one result, and
preserve only the distinctions established under that rule and boundary.

## 10. Smallest missing slice

No runtime-only implementation slice is warranted yet.

The earliest missing constitutional coordinate is one exact Book clause:

```text
Candidate-Compare Responsibility
subject: each exact Candidate Assertion carried by one complete Candidate result
```

Immediately beside it remains:

```text

under that Responsibility
```

No active Compare Responsibility supplies those coordinates. Adding a reader,
loop, runtime event-name union, or caller-provided Candidate addresses would
declare the missing Responsibility in Python.

The active Book has established that no existing Compare Responsibility has
Candidate subjects. Candidate-to-Compare motion stops until one exact clause
declares that Responsibility, its rule, its complete Candidate population, and
its required coordinates.
