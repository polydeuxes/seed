## Delivery, Receipt, and Acknowledgement boundary

This report does not recover constitutional houses named Delivery, Receipt, or Acknowledgement.

Active law supports these bounded distinctions:

- Seed emission does not establish claimed delivery.
- Delivery indication does not establish claimed receipt.
- Receipt acknowledgement does not establish the represented external effect.
- An external claim may become bounded testimony only through exact Evidence, attribution, and responsible production.

Active law does not establish that external delivery or receipt is impossible. It also does not establish that such an occurrence happened merely because the words occur beside Seed-side formation, emission, or representation grammar.

Acknowledgement was audited only where it currently occurs: `receipt acknowledgement` in `08.Communication.E`. It is not promoted here into a standalone subject.

## Unknown discipline

The active communication paragraph correctly states that naming a coordinate does not establish Applicability, that an exact responsible Act requires it, or Unknown standing. It also states that absence of Evidence for an Act occurrence remains only absence of evidenced Act occurrence unless a responsible negative finding establishes nonoccurrence, and that only a positively instantiated coordinate remaining unresolved by responsible finding must preserve Unknown or unresolved standing.

Therefore:

- lexical presentation is not positive coordinate instantiation;
- uninstantiated coordinate is not Unknown;
- positively instantiated coordinate remaining unresolved becomes Unknown only where responsibly established;
- for external delivery, receipt, realization, or time claims, absence of Evidence is not positive occurrence, nonoccurrence, or Unknown by identity.

Potential violation to amend later if the residue finding is sustained: the sentence `Each coordinate requires its own evidence, authority, scope, limits, and Unknown preservation` can be misread as assigning every named item the same coordinate status and an automatic Unknown-preservation duty. The next paragraph narrows that reading, but the peer-chain sentence remains residue-prone.

## Peer-chain answer

The peer-chain presentation is useful as a set of forbidden inferences, but it over-presents unlike matters as peers. Seed-side act/relation items such as representation formation, emission occurrence, bounded responsibility transition, authority-transition boundary, Uptake relation, and bounded lawful reliance are not the same constitutional kind as external delivery, claimed receipt, consumer-local interpretation, or external realization claims. A != B does not establish positive topology for both A and B.

The chain also does not establish an ordered universal sequence. It is presented in an inherited order, and that order is useful for reading the former Handoff compression, but active law elsewhere denies that every communication, representation, request, emission, receipt, invocation, act occurrence, result testimony, and recording relation forms a universal planning-and-movement sequence.

These non-equivalences remain constitutionally useful as forbidden-inference boundaries:

- representation formation != emission;
- emission != delivery;
- delivery != receipt;

- responsibility transition != authority transition;

Their usefulness does not establish positive constitutional houses on both sides of each inequality.
