## 2. Why current exhaustiveness does not generalize automatically

Current runtime reconstructs exact source Assertions through one frozen source
boundary. It accepts only independently addressed Assertions carried by exact
Measurement, Compare, or prior Candidate results
(`seed_runtime/candidate_results_from_exact_result_assertions.py:272-349`).

The Responsibility then derives its complete required subjects by one of two
fixed rules:

```text
one exact source Assertion reference

or

two distinct exact source Assertion references in both source orders
```

(`seed_runtime/candidate_results_from_exact_result_assertions.py:382-426`)

Every required subject has a deterministic address derived from:

```text
exact Responsibility reference
exact source boundary
source position in that bounded work
Act-local source position
exact source Assertion references
```

The runtime refuses an extra Candidate whose required subject is not carried by
that exact Responsibility
(`seed_runtime/candidate_results_from_exact_result_assertions.py:1192-1257`).

Therefore:

```text
Candidate exhaustiveness
= every subject required by one exact Candidate rule

Candidate exhaustiveness
!= permission to create a broader Candidate rule
```

The exact rule is load-bearing. Without it, “all possibilities” has no bounded
meaning.

## 5. The six possible Candidates

The opaque observation in `15206207` produced one exact reusable material
result carrying three distinct coordinate-finding references. The first and
third findings carry equal byte material, but they remain distinct exact source
coordinates.

Let those references be described only for this report as:

```text

source coordinate Z
```

Let the three exact active grammar requirements be:

```text

```

An observer can mechanically enumerate six one-to-one correspondences. That
enumeration requires no preferred answer:

```text
each source coordinate occurs once in each Candidate
each grammar requirement occurs once in each Candidate
no Candidate is omitted
no Candidate is repeated
```

For each possibility, an exact Candidate could in principle carry:

```text

```

These proposed cross-references would be Candidate content only. Candidate
formation would not establish the relations they propose.

The six Candidate descriptions are observer consequences of a proposed exact
rule. They are not active Seed results. No active Responsibility carries that
rule.

## 6. Does active Candidate/Compare support the proposed loop?

### 6.1 Candidate formation

No.

Current E.1 does not admit the reusable exact-material result because it carries
no independently addressed Assertion. It has no three-source subject and no
native grammar coordinate input. E.1 cannot be widened by analogy without
amending active law.

Current E can preserve one Candidate after one exists. It is not the missing
producer.

### 6.2 Recursive Candidate production

One later continuation is active, but it does not discriminate possibilities.
A Candidate result carries an independently addressed Candidate Assertion. At
a later frozen source boundary, that Assertion may become a source Assertion
for another E.1 Candidate Act.

Thus:

```text
Candidate
-> later Candidate production
```

is active.

But:

```text
Candidate recursion
!= comparison
!= evidence pressure
!= narrowing
```

Without another exact rule, recursion merely creates more unary and source-pair
Candidates.

### 6.3 Candidate Compare

Current `04.Compare.C` is also too narrow. Its exact subject is one Candidate
already carrying first and second source Assertion references. Its rule
compares those two source Assertion coordinates. It has no coordinates for:

```text
three proposed source-to-grammar correspondences
the native Standing.E requirements
later evidence bearing on one proposed correspondence
```

The historical candidate-versus-grammar Compare owner remained Unknown, and no
active owner has replaced it.

Therefore the proposed learning loop presently contains at least two separate
vacancies:

```text
vacancy one
    produce every neutral correspondence Candidate required by exact rule G

vacancy two
    compare later exact evidence against those Candidate coordinates
```

Candidate formation alone does not make the gauntlet live.
