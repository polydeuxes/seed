# Checkpoint Recording binding occurrence subtraction report 001

## Question

Does through-boundary Recording need a separate subject-to-Act binding event,
or can its exact binding coordinates be established in the Recording Act
occurrence itself?

This is one isolated cleanup control. The checkpoint command is supplied by
operator mechanics outside Seed; its addressed boundary does not arise from
an internally established Seed result. The subtraction therefore makes no
positive claim about internally produced binding physiology. It changes no
Applicability road and makes no claim about Compare, Measurement, Source,
Locality, or other binding families.

## Former shape

The checkpoint road recorded:

```text
exact addressed command and through-occurrence boundary
        ↓
separate Recording binding event
        ↓
Recording Act occurrence referencing that event
        ↓
Recording result occurrence
```

The binding event held:

```text
Book clause
subject reference
exact Act identity
future Act occurrence identity
future result identity
Locality through its event
```

The Act copied those lifecycle identities and referenced the binding event.
No other physiology consumed the binding-only floor.

## Subtraction

The separate binding event and kind are removed. The Recording Act occurrence
now records directly:

```text
Book clause
exact addressed boundary subject
exact Act identity
Act occurrence identity
future result identity
Locality through its event
```

The Act reader reopens the exact addressed boundary, verifies its Locality and
integrity, reconstructs the exact Act material, and proves:

```text
addressed boundary occurrence
        ↓ exact same-Locality occurrence order
Recording Act occurrence
```

The result directly addresses the Act occurrence and preserves the same exact
source reference.


The direct console road appends one fewer occurrence per checkpoint.

## What disappeared

```text
checkpoint binding event identity
checkpoint binding-only stoppable floor
checkpoint binding event in current coordinates
binding-event reference copied into Act and result
```

No later exact result, replayed coordinate, refusal, or independent operation
distinguished that floor. It existed because the runtime appended the wrapper
and then taught the Act reader to depend on it.

## Constitutional reading

Active law states the exact coordinates of the Recording binding and states
that an Act occurrence has the exact coordinates of its binding. It does not
state that the binding itself has another occurrence.

The direct Act shape therefore remains within active law:

```text
Recording Act occurrence
    exact subject
    exact Recording Act
    Locality

result occurrence
    exact Recording Act occurrence
    exact addressed boundary reference
```

No Binding Act, Encounter, Contact, Selection, Candidate, or intermediate
recording occurrence is introduced.

## Finding

For checkpoint Recording:

```text
binding coordinates           established
separate binding occurrence   not needed
Recording Act occurrence      established
Recording result occurrence   established
```

The earlier census treated downstream use of binding-event identity as
evidence that the event added occurrence order. This subtraction shows that
the dependency was circular on this road. The exact addressed boundary already
supplies the prior occurrence; the Recording Act occurrence supplies the next
one.

It does not show that an internally produced exact subject can reach an Act
without a separate binding occurrence. The operator supplied the checkpoint
command and thereby fixed both the addressed boundary and the requested
Recording operation before the former binding event was appended. Counting
that authored request as Seed testimony would put the laboratory on the scale.

## Disposition

Accept the checkpoint binding-event subtraction as cleanup of an externally
authored command road, not as an internal constitutional control.

Do not generalize it globally. The first positive control must begin with an
exact subject established by earlier Seed physiology, then pressure a family
with no Applicability while preserving its exact subject and Act coordinates
directly in the Act occurrence. Only after independent internal controls
should the more complex Compare binding-before-Applicability floor be touched.
