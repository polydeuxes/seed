## Direct finding

No current exact coordinate lets Seed establish Standing concerning another
bounded system as an exact subject.

The active Standing grammar does not restrict an exact Standing subject to a
subject inside this Seed.  Seed could therefore carry warranted Standing
*concerning* an external subject if one Seed-side responsible occurrence
established every required coordinate from exact bounded Evidence.

That must not be confused with possessing the external system's actual current
condition or organization:

```text
external system E
└── actual condition / organization at boundary B and occurrence t

boundary testimony about E at B,t
                    ↓
this Seed
└── Standing concerning E warranted by that testimony
```

The target may change after `t` while Seed still lawfully preserves what the
bounded observation warranted at `t`.  Currentness, provenance, loss, Scope,
and `Unknown` prevent the observation from pretending to be the target.

This is not evidence that active grammar needs two constitutional Standing
species or a generic `bearer` field.  Seed may never possess the external
system's own current condition.  The narrower question is what exact testimony
crosses the boundary and what Standing concerning E that testimony warrants.

The real ingress road does not establish E as an exact subject or an exact
testimony relation from the supplied result to E.  It therefore cannot begin
that Seed-side Standing physiology.  An external Responsibility, Act, or Yield
would be additional external testimony where independently available; this
investigation must not require every outside system to implement Seed's
grammar.

The external Standing is not established and then discarded.  The intended

The provider resolves richer external mechanics in the middle, but the

### 1.3 Exact relations and relation Standing

### 1.4 Authority, Scope, Locality, provenance, and support

These coordinates are not synonyms for Standing:

- Authority carries one exact source, Responsibility, subject, Act,
  occurrence, result, Scope, and limits.
- Scope bounds the subjects, relations, Acts, occurrences, results,
  Localities, and limits addressed by one Responsibility.
- Locality is one exact relation with its own subjects and responsible
  occurrence.
- Provenance is the exact occurrence-reference population preserved with
  material.
- Support for one input-to-Act position requires its own subject, relation,
  Act, responsible boundary, Authority, Scope, Locality, limits, and
  responsible occurrence.

Thus:

```text
same source       != same Standing
same Locality     != same Standing
same provenance   != same Standing
all evidence concerning X != Standing for X
```

    "result_occurrence_identity": acquisition_event_identity,

This is not demonstrated coordinate loss.  The durable event remains intact,

The same disposition applies to maps whose values are `None`, including
Responsibility assignments and recorded Standing-boundary references.  Their

the addressed physiology was erased.

Act Evidence names an exact Responsibility assignment, current Standing keeps
that assignment reference beside the result.  Only a result with no established
ownership retains `None`.  The invocation-Locality assignment still mistakes
the richer value for non-membership.
