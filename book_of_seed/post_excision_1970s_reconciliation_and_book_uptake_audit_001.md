## 10. Structure Probe reconciliation

The Structure Probe district currently supplies hash-bound exact text,
one-based ordered line coordinates, exact line text, mechanical `blank_v1`
predicate results, nonblank contiguous regions, lengths, terminator flags,
adjacency and exact equality inputs. Prefix extraction, equality grouping and
counting are **derivable** examinations over retained text, not current named
surface-feature fields. Markdown grammar, headings, documentation ontology, and
English architectural relations are not established by these measures.

The durable recurrence statement is:

> A recurrence finding is relative to a declared projection **P**, equivalence
> relation **E**, and bounded scope **S**. Its lawful consumer must additionally
> declare purpose **C** when deciding whether aggregation or representational
> loss preserves the distinctions needed for reliance.

Thus P/E/S constitute measurement; C is always relevant to **consumption** and
to any purpose-relative choice of P/E/S or acceptable loss, but current evidence
does not make C a mandatory coordinate of every exact count. The compact
`R under P using E within S for C` remains a useful report-local checklist, not
yet one canonical formula. `blank_v1` measures the implementation's declared
predicate; it does not establish semantic blankness. The earlier crossing
`external material → exact_text` precedes `exact_text → line projection`, but
its decoding/selection producer and provenance remain compressed at the request
boundary.

## 12. Operator-ingress and Seed-egress reconciliation

The apparent four-axis picture collapses lawfully. The active Book defines a
View as a purpose-bounded representation, projection as a scoped view under
declared rules, and emission as a distinct presentation occurrence. The
communication chapter separates formation, emission, delivery, receipt,
interpretation, uptake, reliance, responsibility/authority transition, and
external realization. Therefore views, projections, explanations, requests,
testimony, observations, candidates, Unknowns, and conflicts are possible Seed
egress forms, not constitutional axes parallel to ingress/egress.

Representation formation does not prove emission. Emission does not prove
receipt or interpretation. Receipt does not prove uptake or reliance. None of
these proves standing change. “Handoff” is compressed ordinary language, not a
primitive.

## 16. Fidelity versus evidentiary-comparison reconciliation

Fidelity is distinct. Clause `01.External.D` makes it a bounded comparison of a
constitutional subject/expectation with an implementation witness under a
declared seam, preserving invariants and detecting erasure, invention, mutation,
authority relocation, conflicts and Unknowns. Generic evidentiary comparison
instead asks what attributed evidence supports, contradicts, conflicts with, or
leaves insufficient for a candidate. Either may inform later standing; neither
automatically establishes it. “Fidelity as generic evidence comparison” is a
compression and must not enter the Book.
