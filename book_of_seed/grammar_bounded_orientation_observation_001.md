## First comparison

For every new or suspicious constitutional-looking word or compound, the first pass asks:

1. What grammatical role does the phrase occupy?
2. Which established Seed coordinate already governs that role?
3. What exact constitutional difference does the proposed word add?
4. Does the proposed phrase survive comparison against the full established grammar, not only the local sentence?
5. What unsupported residue remains?
6. Does the proposed replacement itself survive the same comparison?

The recursive rule is:

```text
Mapping an unsupported phrase to an established coordinate
does not authorize a new compound built from that coordinate.

The proposed replacement must itself be compared
against established grammar.
```

For example, `operation occurrence` may initially map toward `act occurrence`. That mapping does not authorize `act-occurrence measurement`, `act-occurrence baseline`, `act-occurrence behavior`, or `act-occurrence record`. Each proposed compound must be tested again.

The stronger questions are compositional:

- Is the measurement testimony concerning an exact act occurrence?
- Is the baseline standing supported by testimony about a bounded set of act occurrences?
- Does the record preserve attributed testimony that an act occurred?
- Is the phrase naming a new constitutional kind, or can the relation be expressed through already-established coordinates?

Direct compositional description is preferred over creating a new hyphenated or adjective-modified family.

## Failure evidence

A proposed phrase may remain grammatically readable, appear locally precise, survive deletion analysis, preserve the apparent meaning of a sentence, pass owner / act / result / consumer review incompletely, and still unlawfully occupy a coordinate already owned by established grammar.

A replacement can therefore be contamination even when the sentence still makes sense, the old distinction appears preserved, no obvious information was removed, the replacement sounds narrower, or the replacement uses an established word as one component.

The missing first question is:

```text
Which already-established word or coordinate
is this proposed word trying to play as?
```

## Recurring contamination classes

### 1. Redundant specialization

A new modifier adds domain flavor to established grammar without establishing a distinct responsibility.

Example diagnosed here:

```text
operational act
→ Act
```

The modifier must be tested against owner, subject, act, Authority, result, standing, consumer, and Uptake. It does not create a distinct species merely because the subject concerns an external mechanism or measurable occurrence.

### 2. Renaming

A new phrase repeats an established coordinate without adding a lawful distinction.

Examples diagnosed here:

```text
consideration selection
→ Selection

selected-for-consideration standing
→ Selection standing
```

The phrase may look specific while contributing no independently established purpose, act, result, standing, or consumer.

### 3. Coordinate compression

One noun or label can make several separately owned branches appear to belong to one settled responsibility.

Examples requiring direct comparison include:

```text
Recording
operational understanding establishment
operational testimony and standing
```

For Recording, two possibilities remain live:

```text
A. several responsibilities have been compressed into one noun;

B. the chapter already separates several responsibilities,
   but some remain incompletely owned or instantiated.
```

Direct reading shows that the active chapter discusses or distinguishes preservation decision, formation or preservation of retrievable material, record production, record-existence standing, preservation of attributed assertion standing, retention or discard, diagnostic-scoped admission, retrievability, consumer availability, consumer receipt, consumer reliance, and later extraction. This observation does not claim that every listed item is an established responsibility. It classifies only the bounded support described below.

### 4. Relation smuggling

A noun can make an unestablished producer-to-consumer road appear complete.

Example diagnosed here:

```text
handoff
```

The phrase must not be preserved merely to keep a sentence flowing where producer, production occurrence, result, consumer, consumer-local act, purpose, applicability, admission, or Uptake are absent.

### 5. Subject matter promoted into grammar

A word may lawfully identify what testimony concerns without becoming a Seed-native constitutional kind.

Example diagnosed here:

```text
operation
```

Ordinary subject matter may be described as testimony concerning an external operation. That does not establish `operational act`, `operation occurrence`, `operation-occurrence record`, `operation status`, `Operation standing`, a new act kind, a new occurrence kind, a new result kind, a new standing family, or a new responsibility.

### 6. Replacement contamination

An unsupported phrase can be removed and replaced with a new unsupported phrase occupying the same grammatical role.

PR 2238 evidence:

```text
execution
→ operation occurrence

execution record
→ operation-occurrence record

execution status
→ operation status
```

The replacement preserved local readability while repeating the original defect.

### 7. Recursive replacement contamination

A replacement may map toward an established coordinate and then create another unsupported compound around that coordinate.

PR 2239 testimony included these proposed replacements:

```text
operational measurement
→ act-occurrence measurement

operational baseline
→ act-occurrence baseline

operational behavior
→ act-occurrence behavior

operation-occurrence record
→ act-occurrence record
```

This report does not adopt those compounds as constitutional vocabulary. Each must be tested again. The lawful expression may instead be compositional: measurement testimony concerning an exact act occurrence; baseline standing supported by bounded testimony about relevant act occurrences; testimony concerning the behavior of an exact act occurrence; or a record preserving attributed testimony that an act occurred. Those are candidate descriptive forms only, not amendment drafting.
