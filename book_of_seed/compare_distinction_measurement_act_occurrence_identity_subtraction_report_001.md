# Compare-Distinction Measurement Act-occurrence identity subtraction report 001

## Question

Must a Compare-Distinction Measurement Act mint a family identity for its
future occurrence before the Act occurs?

## Prior shape

The Act material carried:

```text
act_occurrence_identity = prospective identity A*
```

The result copied `A*` while also addressing the actual Ledger Act occurrence
`A.identity` through `act_occurrence_event_identity`.

No independently prior occurrence addressed `A*`. Its apparent importance came
from the lifecycle writing the identity and later requiring its own copy.

## Falsifier

Remove `A*` from the Act and result while preserving:

```text
exact Compare-result subject
exact Measurement Act occurrence A
exact Measurement result occurrence R
R addresses A.identity
A before R
one result per A
complete Distinction findings
current-coordinate replay
restart
equal finding content in separate occurrences
```

## Result

The actual Act occurrence establishes its address when it occurs. The result
addresses that exact occurrence directly. Act and result remain separate exact
occurrences, and equal material does not collapse their occurrence identities.

## Disposition

```text
prospective family Act-occurrence identity   withdrawn
copied family Act-occurrence identity        withdrawn
actual Act occurrence identity               retained
actual result occurrence identity            retained
Act-before-result order                      retained
one result per Act                           retained
```

The separately minted exact Act identity remains under independent pressure.
No binding occurrence, Applicability, Yield, result, or relation is added.
