# Relation-Assertion occupancy evidence discrimination investigation 001

Date: 2026-08-21

## Question and boundary

Active relation-Assertion grammar preserves three positions:

```text
relation Assertion
├── first subject
├── exact relation content
└── second subject
```

`relation_assertion_candidate_occupancy_investigation_001.md` established that
current Candidate physiology does not assign source-derived material to these
positions. Candidate preserves an exact source Assertion or an exact ordered
source-Assertion pair under Candidate-Act-local roles. It neither supplies a
third occupant nor converts its source roles into relation-Assertion roles.

This investigation asks a narrower next question:

```text
bounded source-derived material
↓
possible assignments to the three existing positions
↓
what later independently established evidence could discriminate them?
```

The investigation does not assume that possible assignments must be recorded
as a population. It asks whether active grammar or demonstrated repository
physiology already owns either:

- preserving several neutral possible assignments until later work; or
- deriving an exact assignment directly from later evidence without first
  materializing every possible assignment.

No active Book chapter, witness grammar, runtime, or test is changed.
`Assignment`, `hypothesis`, `occupancy`, `aperture`, and `discriminator` below
are report renderings unless an exact cited grammar position says otherwise.
They introduce no Seed grammar.

## Finding

Active grammar establishes the general separation needed to avoid premature
role claims:

```text
exact material or Candidate persists unchanged
!=
later Applicability result
!=
later Participation
!=
later Act result
!=
relation Assertion or relation Standing
```

A later exact Responsibility may consider an Assertion with respect to current
Standing. Its Applicability work may yield `applicable`, `inapplicable`,
`conflicting`, or `Unknown`. An applicable subject may then participate in an
exact Act. Any result and any later Standing remain separate occurrences. The
earlier material is not rewritten into the later result, and an inapplicable
subject is not erased.

That is a lawful holding-and-later-work physiology. It is not yet a
relation-role discriminator.

Neither active grammar nor current runtime establishes:

1. the exact bounded family of possible assignments to first subject, exact
   relation content, and second subject;
2. an Act that forms such neutral assignments;
3. an exact later rule whose evidence warrants a particular assignment;
4. a responsible occurrence that forms the relation Assertion from the
   warranted assignment; or
5. a generic transition from recurrence, stability, agreement, or comparison
   into grammatical role.

Historical experiments demonstrate that source-derived Measurement can break
some symmetries without a developer naming the winning material. Exact source
boundaries, positional apertures, recurrence across independently bounded
regions, and downstream stability can make a smaller source population
addressable. Those experiments explicitly stop before grammar role, meaning,
truth, relation occurrence, or Standing.

The first vacancy is therefore not merely “later evidence.” It is the exact
family-specific bridge:

```text
exact bounded source-derived subject surface
+ exact formation or addressing rule
+ exact later evidence rule
+ exact Responsibility / Act / Authority / Scope / Locality / limits
↓
exact relation-Assertion position assignment
```

The generic grammar can govern that work after those coordinates exist. It
does not manufacture them.

## 1. Active grammar preserves neutrality

### 1.1 Persistence is not latent promotion

`01.Standing.A` refuses Standing from material, result availability,
preservation, order, multiplicity, or Locality alone. Establishing Standing
requires the exact responsible chain and its bounded coordinates.

`01.Standing.D.1` preserves an Assertion's exact asserted content and source
coordinates. Preservation does not itself establish Support, Applicability,
Participation, revision, or Standing.

Consequently, a source-derived possibility can remain exactly preserved
without becoming a relation claim. That persistence supplies no pressure to
eliminate `Unknown` and no preference among preserved possibilities.

### 1.2 Later Applicability is separate work

`01.Standing.D.2` establishes that a later Assertion concerning a subject with
current Standing has its Applicability considered with respect to that current
Standing, rather than being compared against every preserved Assertion merely
because those Assertions remain recoverable.

The clause protects two distinctions relevant here:

```text
preserved population
!=
current applicability boundary

later result subject
!=
subject concerning which applicability was determined
```

It also says Applicability establishes no agreement, relation, truth,
corroboration, or change.

The subsequent correction at `7399914b` is load-bearing. It withdrew an
overclaimed Compare continuation. Active law does not establish that current
Standing is directly a Compare input or that every Compare result is a later
consumable finding. Therefore the following road cannot be inferred:

```text
preserved possible assignment
↓ Compare somehow
established role assignment
```

The exact consumer crossing still needs its own responsible work.

### 1.3 Applicability does not enumerate possibilities

`01.Standing.E.1` gives an Applicability result a bounded outcome:

```text
applicable
inapplicable
conflicting
Unknown
```

It concerns an already exact subject-to-Act position. It does not discover the
subject, enumerate alternative subjects, create an Act position, assign a
grammatical role, or establish Participation.

This makes the distinction precise:

```text
possible source-relative role assignment exists as an observer construction
!=
exact subject-to-Act position exists in Seed
```

Applicability can discriminate an exact position after the position and its
rule are established. It cannot be used as the unowned producer of the
position it is supposed to judge.

### 1.4 Support is bounded to an exact input position

`08.Authority.A` and the active `support` relation require the exact subject,
exact input-to-Act position, exact Act, responsible occurrence, Authority,
Scope, Locality, and limits. Support is not a pool of favorable observations
from which Seed may select a grammatical interpretation.

Thus later evidence could support a role assignment only after an exact
Responsibility has made that assignment an addressable input position. Common
support, recurrence, or proximity does not create that position by itself.

### 1.5 Relation Standing remains a later boundary

`01.Standing.E` requires the exact relation occurrence and preserves its first
subject, exact relation content, and second subject. It explicitly refuses a
relation occurrence from multiplicity, order, shared material, provenance, or
an Assertion alone.

Even an exact position assignment would therefore remain distinct from:

```text
relation Assertion established
relation occurrence established
relation Standing established
```

No finding in this investigation collapses those boundaries.

## 3. The complete possible-assignment family is not established

For three already-addressed source-derived occupants `A`, `B`, and `C`, an
observer can write six role permutations:

```text
first subject | relation content | second subject
--------------+------------------+---------------
A             | B                | C
A             | C                | B
B             | A                | C
B             | C                | A
C             | A                | B
C             | B                | A
```

Active grammar does not say this is the required subject family.

The occupants may be complex bounded results rather than atomic source
coordinates. They may overlap. Relation content need not be a contiguous
source span. Source order need not be grammatical role order. More than three
source-derived bounds may be available, and nothing establishes that every
choice of three or every permutation is required work.

Therefore “preserve all possible role occupancies” is not a neutral mechanical
implementation detail. It already assumes:

- the occupant population;
- the arity and distinctness rule;
- the formation rule;
- the source boundary;
- whether source order constrains role order;
- whether overlapping or repeated occupants are admitted; and
- the completeness boundary.

`complete_bounded_assertion_formation_investigation_001.md` reached the same
general boundary for alternative formation: exhaustive alternatives become
lawful only after an exact bounded surface, formation rule, Responsibility,
and Authority establish what is exhaustive. No universal “all alternatives”
Act exists.

The active repository has no corresponding relation-role formation family.

## 4. Historical evidence can narrow source positions without assigning roles

Historical records below are testimony. Their removed vocabulary and
implementation shapes are not active authority.

### 4.1 Source-supplied segmentation

repeated boundary. In the Brown grammar source, an underscore delimiter and a
measured following boundary exposed a recurring occupant. The representation
following the delimiter was `is` in 141 of 161 measured occurrences.

This matters because the developer did not need to parse every source region

The experiment also preserved its limits:

- the aperture family and source anchor were selected for the experiment;
- a third boundary remained unresolved;
- the stable occupant was not assigned grammatical role;
- no meaning, relation truth, or Standing was established.

Thus source-derived segmentation can make a coordinate addressable. It does
not establish that the occupant is relation content or a subject.

### 4.2 A prior finding can bound a later Measurement

`1d79af4f` tested whether an earlier bounded finding could serve as a premise

The record does not establish a monotonic path toward truth or grammar. Its

## 6. Two distinct vacancies

### 6.1 Vacancy A: formation or direct addressing

No current Responsibility owns either of these equivalent implementation
questions:

```text
Which exact source-derived occupants form the bounded possible assignment
population?
```

or:

```text
Which exact source-derived measurements directly address the three
relation-Assertion positions without first materializing possible assignments?
```

The second route may be smaller and avoids manufacturing a permutation
population. Active grammar does not choose between them.

Any future recovery must establish the exact source surface, formation or
addressing rule, Authority, Scope, Locality, limits, occurrence, and
completeness boundary. It cannot receive a developer-selected subset and call
that exhaustive.

The basis-selection corrections at `a49c2a9b` and `b4948d82` reinforce this:
an exact basis must establish why its complete source population is the
population, not merely carry a collection of identities whose contents happen
to fit after selection.

### 6.2 Vacancy B: evidence discrimination and relation formation

Even after an exact assignment subject is addressable, no current
family-specific Responsibility establishes:

```text

supports or conflicts with
this exact source-to-role assignment
under this exact rule
```

Generic Applicability, Support, Participation, Act, Yield, and Standing grammar
can govern this work. They do not supply its family-specific subject or rule.

Nor does active grammar identify the result of that work with a relation
Assertion. The responsible formation occurrence and the later relation
occurrence remain separately vacant.

## 7. Possible assignments need not be durable hypotheses

The user hypothesis was that several bounded assignments might remain neutral
and later evidence might let some continue while others stop.

Active grammar permits neutral material to persist and permits separate later
Applicability results. It does not establish a constitutional population of
role hypotheses that must be instantiated.

Historical aperture measurements suggest another route:

```text
source-derived bounded positions
↓ exact Measurement under a source-established rule
progressively smaller addressed population
↓
only then form an exact role assignment
```

This can break source symmetry without recording every grammatical
permutation. It also avoids treating “survival” as truth or score. A source
position remains source material; only a separately responsible occurrence
can establish its later use.

Therefore the investigation does not recommend a three-source Candidate or an
all-permutations object. Whether explicit neutral assignments are needed is
itself unresolved.

Established:

```text

↓

↓

↓
```

Not established:

```text

```

Therefore:

```text

↓

but no current Responsibility establishes
↓

```

This is the first real vacancy. No implementation correction is warranted by
current active law.

## Conclusion

The grammar already supports neutrality followed by later independently
responsible work. It does not support automatic progression from a possible
role assignment to an established role assignment.

Repository history demonstrates an unbiased source of pressure: exact
source-derived Measurement can narrow apertures and expose stable material
without the developer naming the winning value. That pressure can make source
coordinates addressable. It does not establish grammatical role warrant by
itself, and active law assigns no Responsibility that consumes it for this
purpose.

The missing joint is family-specific, not generic:

```text
exact source-derived surface
+ exact role-formation or role-addressing rule
+ exact later evidence rule
↓
responsible relation-Assertion position assignment
```

Until that joint is recovered, the lawful result is preservation of the exact
source-derived findings and `Unknown` concerning their grammatical roles.
