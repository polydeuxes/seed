# Testimony and Assertion Standing

## Constitutional subject

The boundary between attributed testimony and Standing concerning an exact Assertion.

## Bounded resolution

An Assertion carries exact asserted content. Testimony attributes an Assertion to its source and preserves the source, Scope, provenance, Authority, conflicts, Unknowns, and surviving limits that the responsible occurrence establishes. Recording testimony preserves that attribution; it does not establish the testified content beyond its source-relative Standing.

Normalization may represent asserted content through declared subject, predicate, value, dimensions, time, source, and provenance coordinates. It does not create a higher constitutional subject, establish the Assertion, supply Evidence, or strengthen Standing.

Standing concerning an Assertion is established only by the responsible occurrence whose Evidence, Warrant, Authority, Scope, provenance, conflicts, Unknowns, and limits warrant it. One source-relative Observation may warrant Standing no stronger than the preserved testimony. Broader, current-facing, authority-heavy, or verified Standing requires the corresponding stronger boundary. Repetition is not independent corroboration.

Contradiction, staleness, expiry, supersession, or loss of current selection does not rewrite the preserved Assertion or its provenance. A later responsible occurrence may establish different current applicability, confidence, conflict, selection, verification, or Standing.

Repository types named `Fact`, `FactSupport`, or similar are implementation representations. Their construction, schema validity, recording, replay, or visibility does not create a constitutional layer above Assertion.

## Addressable boundaries

### 05.Testimony.A — Premise-relative testimony

A recorded Assertion, diagnostic finding, or Evidence record may participate as attributed testimony or premise-relative input only through the exact Act's Applicability boundary. Participation preserves the input's attribution and carried limits; it does not establish the testified content, prove the source occurrence, or strengthen Standing merely through repetition or copied provenance references.

### 05.Testimony.B — Bounded testimony comparison

A comparison may consume independently preserved Assertions or findings while preserving every coordinate each input carries. No input gains a coordinate by comparison. The comparison may establish only its bounded relation Assertion, such as agreement, disagreement, conflict, refinement, or Unknown.

Premise and support are different relations. An input may stand in both only where each is separately warranted. Comparison alone establishes neither truth, reconciliation, independent corroboration, nor authority to rely.

### 05.Testimony.C — Implementation measurement testimony

An implementation measurement is bounded testimony about one observed Act instance under declared conditions and rule. Measurement production, recording, the measured Act occurrence, and the measured Act result remain distinct. Ambient runtime or resource observation is not act-instance measurement by identity.

An implementation baseline is retained bounded Standing concerning ordinary behavior under declared conditions. Establishing it requires applicable measurement testimony, Scope, temporal coordinates, comparison rule, conflicts, and Unknowns. Retained samples, summaries, averages, and thresholds do not establish that Standing by identity. Difference from one sample is not material deviation, and repetition alone does not establish a transition.

## Temporal standing

Temporal coordinates distinguish what time an Assertion describes, when its support was observed, when an occurrence happened, when material was recorded, and when a later Act considered it. One timestamp does not establish the others.

Expiry changes only the applicability or selection named by its responsible boundary. It does not delete the Assertion, make historical testimony false, or prove historical invalidity. Latest-by-rule is not current with warrant by identity.

## Important distinctions

- asserted content != Assertion identity
- testimony != established Standing beyond the attributed source
- normalization != establishment
- recording != establishment
- repetition != independent corroboration
- premise relation != support relation
- preserved Assertion != current applicability
- implementation representation named `Fact` != constitutional kind
- measurement production != measurement recording
- measurement occurrence != measured Act occurrence
- retained samples != baseline Standing
- timestamp != chronology, causation, or currentness by identity
