# Attributed Assertions and Standing

## Constitutional subject

The boundary between an Assertion attributed to a source and Standing established by Seed.

## Bounded resolution

External or recorded material may carry an Assertion attributed to an exact source occurrence. Attribution preserves the source, asserted content, provenance, Scope, Authority limits, conflicts, Unknowns, and surviving limits. It establishes only that the source asserted the content; it does not establish the content as Seed Standing.

Normalization may represent asserted content through declared coordinates. It does not create a higher subject, establish the Assertion, supply Evidence, or strengthen Standing.

Standing concerning an Assertion is established only by a responsible occurrence whose Evidence, Warrant, Authority, Scope, provenance, conflicts, Unknowns, and limits warrant it. Repetition of one source path is not independent corroboration.

Contradiction, staleness, expiry, supersession, or loss of selection does not rewrite the preserved Assertion or provenance. A later responsible occurrence may establish different Applicability, conflict, selection, verification, or Standing.

## Addressable boundaries

### 05.Attribution.A — Source-relative input

An attributed Assertion, diagnostic finding, or Evidence record may participate only through the exact Act's Applicability boundary. Participation preserves attribution and carried limits; it does not establish the asserted content or strengthen Standing.

### 05.Attribution.B — Bounded comparison

A Compare may consume independently preserved Assertions or findings while preserving every coordinate each input carries. No input gains a coordinate by comparison. The result is only its bounded relation Assertion: agreement, disagreement, conflict, refinement, or Unknown.

Premise and support are different relations. Compare alone establishes neither truth, reconciliation, independent corroboration, nor Authority to rely.

### 05.Attribution.C — Implementation measurement

An implementation measurement is an attributed Assertion about one observed Act instance under declared conditions and rule. Measurement production, recording, the measured Act occurrence, and its result remain distinct.

An implementation baseline is retained bounded Standing concerning ordinary behavior under declared conditions. It requires applicable measurements, Scope, temporal coordinates, comparison rule, conflicts, and Unknowns. Samples, summaries, averages, and thresholds do not establish that Standing by identity.

## Temporal Standing

Temporal coordinates distinguish what time an Assertion describes, when Evidence was observed, when an occurrence happened, when material was recorded, and when a later Act considered it. One timestamp does not establish the others.

Expiry changes only the Applicability or Selection named by its responsible boundary. It does not delete the Assertion or prove historical invalidity. Latest-by-rule is not current with Warrant by identity.

## Important distinctions

- asserted content != Assertion identity
- source attribution != Seed Standing
- normalization != establishment
- recording != establishment
- repetition != independent corroboration
- premise != support
- preserved Assertion != current Applicability
- measurement production != recording
- measurement occurrence != measured Act occurrence
- retained samples != baseline Standing
