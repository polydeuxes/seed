## Direct finding

Establishment is a recovered **kind-specific production possibility**, not an
active universal Act or Responsibility.

History permits two different lawful shapes:

```text
A. producing occurrence

exact responsible producing Act
-> Yield exact Assertion/result
-> that same warranted occurrence may establish only the bounded Standing
   of its own result

B. separately assigned establishment

exact produced or preserved Assertion
-> explicitly named establishment Responsibility and Act
-> Yield establishment result
-> bounded Standing
```

The active compact Book preserves the coordinates both shapes must satisfy,
but does not select either shape or name an owner for arbitrary recorded
testimony.

The historical statement that poor Standing may be established from one
Assertion removes two false prerequisites:

```text
prior Standing required       false
multiple Assertions required  false
```

It does not establish:

```text
Recording establishes Standing
every attributed Assertion is true
Authority alone manufactures a responsible occurrence
one universal Establishment Act owns all Assertion kinds
```

The bounded number experiment therefore does **not** need independent
manifestation evidence proving the granted successor edges. It does need one
exact responsible occurrence whose claim is no stronger than the granted
Authority and Scope warrant. No such active producing or separately assigned
establishment owner presently accepts the proposed prior-domain declaration.

The exact vacancy survives, but its neutral form is:

```text
No active exact Responsibility/Act produces or separately establishes
one bounded attributed prior-domain Assertion as a Standing-bearing result
under its supplied Authority, Scope, provenance, limits, and Unknown.
```

This does not yet warrant naming the missing Act `Establishment`.

## 1. What active Standing law requires

### 1.1 Standing describes a completed responsible result chain

Active `01.Standing.A` requires, for one exact subject:

```text
exact Responsibility
Act occurrence
Yield
Authority
Scope
limits
result for that subject
```

`01.Standing.A.1` says the branch's exact result is a coordinate of Standing
for the subject of that Responsibility. Neither clause names the Act that
produces the result.

The machine witness preserves that refusal. `01.Standing.A` contains
`requires`, a subject, and a result name; it has no `Responsibility` or
`exact_Act`. `01.Standing.A.1` carries the exact result but likewise names no
Act owner.

This omission is deliberate recovery, not an unfinished spelling exercise.
An earlier machine projection minted:

```text
Responsibility: establish_Standing_for_exact_subject
exact_Act: establish_Standing
```

Those names were removed because the Book stated the required coordinates but
did not assign such an Act. Reintroducing them from the verb `requires` would
repeat the same projection error.

### 1.2 Assertion and relation Standing remain bounded

Active `01.Standing.D.1` makes an Assertion exact content as one subject and
requires it to carry source, provenance, Authority, Scope, Locality, conflicts,
limits, Unknown, and Standing with exact established coordinates.

Active `01.Standing.E` requires a relation Assertion to carry:

```text
first subject
exact relation content
second subject
```

Its Standing further requires an exact relation occurrence, Authority, Scope,
Locality, limits, conflicts, and Unknown.

Thus a developer-supplied edge serialized as three fields is not relation
Standing. The exact responsible relation occurrence is still required. But
that occurrence need not be an independent empirical test of the edge when
the bounded claim is itself an authorized domain declaration. The required
Evidence and Authority must be appropriate to the exact **claim strength**.

### 1.3 Applicability cannot appoint Establishment

`01.Standing.D.2` and `01.Standing.E.1` make a later Assertion an
Applicability subject only for one already-exact Act. Applicability evaluates
one exact subject-to-Act position. It cannot decide whether the owner should be
a producing Act, a separate establishment Act, Compare, Measurement, or
anything else.

The ordering recovered in `a220dc43` therefore remains intact:

```text
owner / exact Act first
Applicability second
Participation third
```

This investigation changes only the characterization of the unresolved owner.

## 2. Recording is not necessarily upstream of establishment

Active `05.Recording.A` and `05.Recording.E` preserve an exact Assertion, its
source coordinates, one recording occurrence, its Yield, and one exact record.
They do not say that the recording occurrence produced the Assertion or
established its Standing.

Two chronologies are therefore distinct:

```text
producing-Act shape

authorized declaration occurrence
-> Yield exact bounded Assertion T with warranted result Standing
-> Recording preserves T and its source coordinates
```

```text
separate-establishment shape

some earlier occurrence produces T without the required Standing
-> Recording preserves T
-> explicitly assigned establishment Act consumes T
-> Yield bounded establishment result
-> Standing
```

The proposed sequence:

```text
recorded T
-> Establishment
```

is therefore one possible shape, not the constitutional default. If the
operator or experiment authority is performing a bounded domain declaration,
the declaration occurrence may be the producing occurrence. Recording would
then be downstream preservation, not the gate through which the declaration
becomes Standing.

The active runtime currently provides neither a generic declaration-producing
road nor an explicitly assigned generic establishment road for opaque
prior-domain relation testimony.

## 3. What `ac16af3` actually established

### 3.1 The no-threshold finding is exact

The then-active Book stated:

```text
Poor Standing established by a single Assertion is Standing;
absence of prior Standing does not prevent establishment,
and no count of prior Assertions is required before Standing concerning
a subject may exist.
```

The paragraph addressed a maturity-threshold defect. It established that a
subject may begin with weak, bounded Standing. It did not say that an
unproduced field or recorded string establishes itself.

This is directly relevant to the opaque number subjects:

```text
no prior Standing concerning sable
!=
Standing concerning sable is forbidden
```

One exact warranted Assertion may begin Standing. The claim must remain
bounded to its Evidence, Authority, Scope, provenance, limits, and Unknown.

### 3.2 The same history preserved two owners

The same chapter later stated that a warranted producing Act may establish
only the bounded Standing of its own result when its Evidence and Authority
warrant that exact Assertion. It separately preserved the controlling role of:

```text
an explicitly named separate standing-establishment responsibility
or a later stronger/different establishment
```

This language is incompatible with a universal implicit Establishment Act.
If a separate establishment owner were always present, there would be no need
to distinguish it from the producing Act.

### 3.3 Establishment remained kind-specific

The same historical grammar repeatedly used:

```text
kind-specific production or establishment boundary
```

and explicitly warned that an artifact's form or label does not supply the
warrant for its Assertion. Establishment was one possible kind-specific
production occurrence, not one global constitutional service.

The historical `Construction and Establishment` chapter is a concrete
example. Its subject was the transition from a constructed goal-shaped
representation to one bounded operator goal. It required admitted meaning,
consumer-local relations, exact goal purpose, and other goal-specific
coordinates. It was removed with the obsolete goal-establishment road. Its
existence testifies that establishment can be an exact Act; its specificity
refuses using it as the owner of opaque successor testimony.

## 4. Claim strength controls what one Assertion can establish

Historical testimony law allowed one Observation to support a bounded Fact
only when the Fact asserted no more than the source-relative observation could
support. Its example shape was:

```text
this source reported this predicate/value
at this preserved time, vantage, dimensions, confidence, and expiry limit
```

It required stronger Evidence for a broader, live-state, authority-heavy, or
unqualified claim.

That distinction applies to prior-domain testimony.

These are not the same Assertion:

```text
unbounded metaphysical claim:
    R universally and independently relates sable to fern

bounded attributed declaration:
    Authority A declares, for experiment Scope S and boundary B,
    that sable --R--> fern; outside S is Unknown
```

The experiment grants the second. Seed is not required to rediscover it from
outputs before using it as a bounded premise. Authority is doing legitimate
work: it identifies who may constitute the experimental domain and the exact
Scope in which the declaration is accepted.

This does not let Authority erase physiology. The declaration still requires
an exact responsible occurrence, Yield, result, provenance, limits, and the
relation coordinates required by active Standing. The developer placing the
edge in JSON is not that occurrence unless the fixture itself faithfully
represents an independently warranted declaration occurrence under active
grammar.

## 5. Support, testimony, and use remain separate

### 5.1 A support relation does not promote its subject

Historical Warrant decomposition replaced one umbrella with an exact relation
Assertion among Evidence, Authority, Scope, provenance, limits, and the
supported Assertion. That support relation required its own occurrence and
Standing.

The recovered refusal was important:

```text
support-relation Standing
!= supported Assertion Standing
!= Applicability
!= Admission
!= later reliance
```

Consequently, merely attaching Authority and Scope fields to T does not
establish T. Those coordinates must participate in the exact responsible
production or establishment occurrence.

### 5.2 Attributed testimony need not become universal Fact

Historical Testimony law allowed a recorded claim to be consumed as attributed
testimony or premise-relative input while refusing to establish the testified
content as an unbounded Fact.

This distinction is sufficient for the intended number experiment:

```text
given as bounded premise:
    exact origin and successor relations under A/S

discover:
    consequences of composing and comparing those exact relations
```

The later path Act need not claim that the premise is metaphysically universal.
It must preserve the premise's Authority, Scope, provenance, limits, and
Unknown in every derived result.

Historical assertion-preserving Uptake described how an exact consumer with
prior Standing for one declared purpose could accept upstream material and
produce consumer-local Standing without re-establishing universal upstream
Standing. That chapter was later moved to Rosetta. It is useful testimony for
the distinction, but it is not active authority for a new generic Uptake road.

The present runtime therefore has two adjacent vacancies, which must not be
compressed:

```text
1. no active producer or separate establishment owner gives the bounded
   prior-domain Assertion a Standing-bearing result

2. no active generic consumer assignment admits that attributed Assertion as
   premise-relative input to the later successor-path Act
```

The first prevents the proposed prior Standing from becoming live. The second
will remain even after the first is recovered unless the later Act declares
that input position itself.
