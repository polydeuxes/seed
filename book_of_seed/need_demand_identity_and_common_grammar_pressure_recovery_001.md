## Disposition

**Recovered disposition: Hypothesis B plus the locality qualification in Hypothesis C is strongest; Hypothesis A is unsupported.**


This is asymmetric. The repository positively evidences one implementation subject named Need that PR 1935 deliberately described as Demand-family variants. It does **not** evidence a `Need -> Demand` act, stronger later Demand standing, distinct Demand input, or separate Demand artifact. Conversely, neither PR 1935 nor the four-member `FAMILIES` tuple warrants the universal claim that every constitutional Demand belongs to those four goal/horizon-local variants.

## Hypothesis cross-examination

### Hypothesis A — distinct constitutional subjects

Candidate account: Need says what advancement may require; a later Demand states a stronger transformation requirement. This would require an identifiable producer consuming a bounded Need standing, performing a responsible establishment act under a warrant, and emitting a distinct Demand standing for a consumer.

No such producer, transition, promotion, projection, serialization, or consumer road was found. Later need-reference projection preserves native meaning; consideration selection selects an exact reference; sufficiency consumes the need set; inquiry-frontier components consume a selected inquiry need. None names or produces Demand, and later consumption is not strengthening. The active Book's different word choices are an **asserted distinction**, not an evidenced crossing. Hypothesis A is therefore **unsupported**, not disproved for all future constitutional grammar.

### Hypothesis B — synonymous constitutional subject

The implementation's family projections classify why a bounded result is required: clarification, inquiry, authority, or operational realization. Each can preserve `established`, `unsupported`, `unknown`, or `conflicting` standing, while refusing movement. PR 1935 explicitly called these Demand-family variants and treated `NeedFamily="inquiry"` as a label that becomes an exact Inquiry Demand only with goal, horizon, evidence, component, owner, and standing bindings. That is direct recovery evidence for `Need ≈ Demand` at this boundary.

Qualification is essential: `NeedFamily` is a type label, not an occurrence; a projection is a container with multiple standing buckets, including no established item; and `established` means established only under the local producer's premises and scope. Thus synonymy applies to the constitutional subject represented by an exact bounded item, not to every `Need*` class or label by identity.

### Hypothesis C — broader Demand kind, local Need-family implementation

Every current family projection requires an already established `BoundedOperatorGoalEstablishment` and `BoundedAdvancementHorizon`; the assembly rechecks those identities. `GoalAdvancementNeedSet` therefore assembles stage-owned projections for one established goal and one advancement horizon. It is not a pre-goal interaction-pressure collector and declares no universal taxonomy.

The first-contact witness can arise while Seed cannot interpret operator meaning and therefore before a bounded operator goal or advancement horizon exists. That timing and ownership mismatch is evidenced. It makes the witness ineligible for faithful assembly **at that time**, not constitutionally impossible forever: after a separately lawful goal/horizon establishment, some derived local Demand might enter the current machinery. No present road performs that crossing. Hypothesis C therefore coexists with B: Need/Demand vocabulary is synonymous locally, while the machinery is not universal.

### What recurrence contributes

One uninterpretable occurrence establishes at most occurrence-local inability under an applicable grammar comparison. The first unsupported enum token additionally establishes that the exact closed-choice binding failed for that occurrence; it does not establish refusal or learning pressure.

A second independently preserved, same-scope failure can support the stronger topology claim that the current operator-interaction mechanism repeatedly failed to establish sufficient common grammar for the present interaction responsibility. Recurrence is a measurement over identified occurrences under a declared comparison; it is not meaning. It neither interprets repeated material nor grants authority, selects English, or selects a mechanism.

To move from possible to established Demand, the repository supports requiring at least: a bounded faithful-interaction responsibility; operator/interaction and mechanism applicability; material consequence (faithful continuation blocked); evidence of current grammar standing; evidence that the available bounded clarification failed under its stopping rule; scope; and an explicit responsible establishment warrant. Recurrence strengthens failure evidence but is not alone sufficient. Authority is not required to establish the Demand's existence, but is separately required before acquisition/execution.

## Clarification Demand versus common-grammar establishment Demand

The best current disposition is **different bounded Demands at different responsibility and scope, with the second a broader competency Demand; exact family classification remains Unknown**. Clarification Demand resolves this bounded operator-meaning uncertainty. Common-grammar establishment would establish a relation or competency whose absence repeatedly generates a class of uncertainties. The latter may support later clarification, inquiry, or realization work but is not the same occurrence merely enlarged.

Current fit is mixed: inquiry can examine grammar standing; authority can represent missing permission; operational realization can represent a later required transformation with unavailable coverage; clarification can represent occurrence-local meaning uncertainty. None, alone, owns the pre-goal relation-level requirement. Family classification is not required to recognize **possible** pressure, and current evidence neither warrants forcing it into one of four nor inventing a fifth.

## Demand and Gap

For the witness, a defensible candidate topology is:

```text
responsibility: sustain faithful bounded operator interaction
reference condition: sufficient common grammar for that interaction
current standing: sufficient shared grammar not established
comparison result: repeated bounded clarification crossings failed
possible Gap: current grammar relation does not satisfy the reference condition
possible Demand: establish sufficient common grammar within that scope
```

It is not yet an implemented or automatically established topology. Demand and Gap can be established together from shared evidence, but their acts assert different things. A Demand can exist without an established Gap: a declared required result may be established before current-condition comparison, or the Demand may not require asserting present insufficiency. A Gap can exist without an established Demand: a comparison can establish failure against another declared condition while the responsible transformation remains Unknown. A Demand may supply the Gap's reference condition; a Gap may support Demand establishment by demonstrating material insufficiency. Neither direction is automatic, and Gap never supplies authority.

## Capability-demand audit

The phrase `capability demand` is not evidenced as an independent constitutional kind between Need and Capability. Canonical Book wording defines it as a bounded transformation/outcome requirement and as a constraint on candidate formation. Reports identify an exact demand relation carried along operational-realization candidate/reachability/selection/warrant artifacts rather than one universal object.

The active chapter currently over-separates when it presents this as a general chain in which a Need is followed by a distinct capability Demand without an evidenced producer. The more faithful reading is:

```text
Demand: required bounded result (including transformation or competency)
Gap: scoped current incompatibility relative to that Demand or another condition
Capability candidates: possible means of satisfying the Demand
```

Thus “capability demand” is Demand content concerning a required transformation and/or a road-local constraint on candidate formation, not a second constitutional layer. A Gap may support its establishment, but does not select the transformation.
