# Cross-experiment support-population observation 001

## Question

Can independently recovered exact support populations from two raw-material
experiments contract the source search further without supplying words,
phrases, grammatical roles, or expected values?

This report changes no Book chapter, witness grammar, runtime, or test. A
disposable observer in `/tmp` performed the calculation. Its results are
observer testimony, not Seed occurrences.


The observer received only the raw line populations. It received no target

## Observer operation

For each experiment, the observer:

1. addressed every exact contiguous literal surface recurring in at least two
   source lines;
2. preserved the exact source-line support population of each surface;
3. retained maximal consecutive surfaces carrying the same exact support
   population;
4. compared only support populations present in both experiments;
5. identified complementary support populations;
6. determined whether any three complementary axes exhaust every exact
   three-bit combination across the eight source lines.

Exact literal recurrence entails recurrence of its complete internal
same-content/difference result population. The observer did not durably record
that lifecycle. It rendered the later same-support contraction directly.

The matching line positions across the two experiments are external observer
coordinates. Seed has not established a relation between those experiment
positions.

## What this establishes

The observer calculation establishes that the two supplied raw-material
populations contain:

- six nontrivial support populations shared across both experiments;
- three exact complementary axes;
- a unique complete three-axis factorial among those shared populations;
- stable support divisions despite changed literal lengths;
- no established ordering or grammatical meaning among those axes.

It does not establish:

- a word;
- a phrase;
- a relation Assertion;
- first-subject, relation-content, or second-subject occupancy;
- a relation between corresponding line positions across experiments;
- that recurrence count is importance or Standing strength.
