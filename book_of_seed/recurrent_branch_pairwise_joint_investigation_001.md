## Finding

The first draft looked only at the eleven findings produced by the arithmetic
witness and asked which pairs among them should enter Compare. That question
was too narrow.

The active Book already carries a representation-neutral three-coordinate
joint:

```text
relation Assertion
├── first subject
├── exact relation content
└── second subject
```

`01.Standing.E` establishes that anatomy. `01.Source.F` separately permits
supplied material carrying a relation Assertion to preserve its exact relation
and source coordinates without thereby establishing a relation occurrence.

Exact relation content may therefore be source material such as `is`, `to
be`, `causes`, `=`, or another exact source form. Nothing in this anatomy
privileges arithmetic, English, a single byte, a single word, or infix
notation. Meaning, truth, and the relation occurrence remain separate work.

The current vacancy is not a complete pair population over all findings. It is
the live source-derived work that addresses the three exact relation-Assertion
positions from already-established bounded results:

```text
exact bounded results and source coordinates
↓
first-subject bound
exact-relation-content bound
second-subject bound
↓
preserved source-relative relation Assertion
```

No current runtime continuation performs that work for the results produced by
`54af52ae`.
