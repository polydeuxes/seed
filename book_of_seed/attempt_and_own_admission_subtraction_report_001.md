# `attempt` and `own` admission subtraction report 001

## Question

Do the active Emission uses of `attempt` and `own` express exact distinctions
beyond the coordinates named beside them?

## `attempt`

`07.Emission.C` previously stated:

```text
emission Act occurrence
attempt
accepted write
        ↓
one occurrence and one result
```

The clause gave `attempt` no occurrence identity, subject, Act, result, boundary,
Locality, or relation that could vary independently. It introduced a third name
and immediately identified that name with the emission Act occurrence and
accepted write.

The exact identity statement remains:

```text
emission Act occurrence
+ accepted write
= one occurrence and one result
```

This does not claim that `attempt` can never identify an exact coordinate. A
separately recorded execution with its own occurrence and result could supply
that distinction. Active Seed Emission has no such coordinate.

## `own`

The two active uses modified exact references:

```text
failure Act occurrence with its own result
material supplied ... has its own exact subject
```

The result is already addressed through the separate failure Act occurrence.
The exact subject and source-boundary Act coordinates are already coordinates
of the supplied material. Removing `own` changes neither address and creates no
ownership relation.

## Positive control

The machine grammar now pins the surviving Emission identity directly:

```text
one_occurrence:
    emission Act occurrence
    accepted write

separate_occurrence:
    failure
```

Thus the subtraction does not merge the accepted write with the separately
named failure occurrence.

## Disposition

```text
attempt                         narrated alias; withdrawn
own                             emphasis on exact references; withdrawn
emission Act occurrence         retained
accepted write occurrence       retained
one occurrence and one result   retained
separate failure occurrence     retained pending its own census
```

No runtime road, occurrence kind, Act, result, relation, or coordinate is
added.
