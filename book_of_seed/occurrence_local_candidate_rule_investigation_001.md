### 1.2 Candidate ownership may be occurrence-local

Responsibility, not whether a global `CandidateFormation` Responsibility

This does not mean an owner may be assumed. An exact local Responsibility still
needs an exact subject, rule, Authority, Scope, Locality, limits, required

## 9. Answers to the required disposition

### 1. Is `4bd4c556` wrong to require a native grammar subject?

Yes. The active relation dimensions govern the local rule; they are not a
second subject.

### 2. Does Candidate ownership need a universal named Responsibility?

No. Exact Candidate Responsibilities can be local to their bounded
occurrences. Active E.1 already demonstrates that ownership shape. The
proposed occurrence remains unavailable because G is absent, not because no
universal office exists.

### 3. What common Responsibility is recoverable beneath unary and pair cases?

No third named Responsibility. The common obligation is that one exact local
Candidate Responsibility must produce every Candidate required by its exact
bounded rule, with no omission or addition. Unary and source-pair rules are two
manifestations of that obligation.

### 4. Does current relation grammar sufficiently bound all assignments?

No. It bounds the coordinates an already exact relation Assertion must carry.
It does not make W applicable, establish one-to-one use, or require all six
assignments.

### 5. What is the exact missing rule?

G must establish that every complete one-to-one assignment of W's three exact
coordinate findings to the three applicable Standing.E dimensions is one
required Candidate subject, with none omitted or preferred. No active owner or
source evidence supplies G.

Therefore this investigation stops without Book amendment or implementation.
