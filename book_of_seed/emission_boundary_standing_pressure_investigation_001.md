The exact-material road supplies one mechanical and clause-level lower bound:

```text

↓

```

```text

↓

↓

↓

↓

↓

```

Current raw admission does not provide this road. A structured Representation
with no exact material reaches Candidate production, then exact-material
Admission refuses it. That refusal preserves the distinction:

```text

!= exact material exists

!= candidate is admitted to raw exact-material output

!= exact semantic emission boundary exists
```

Consequently, current emission is not a general proof of this Seed's Standing.
It is a strict projection of the coordinates its exact rule preserves.

The useful inverse question is:

```text
Which exact coordinates would this emission strengthen
if Seed emitted this result under this boundary rule?
```

Where the answer includes an unestablished relation, truth position, Authority,
Scope, or Standing branch, that semantic emission remains outside the current
boundary. Exact material preservation remains available only because it does
not claim those coordinates.
