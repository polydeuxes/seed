# Compare Standing continuation recovery 001

## Scope

This report tests one proposed orientation:

```text
Seed does not require a pre-existing owner of understanding.

Ownership is established occurrence by occurrence:
the exact Responsibility performing a Compare owns that
Compare occurrence, produces its bounded result, and may
establish Standing.

Newly established Standing then becomes available material
for a later Compare, and the process continues while further
comparison remains warranted.
```

Stated as the build target: construct the **cup** — grammar sufficient to hold one exact Compare occurrence and its result — and the **faucet** — the lawful continuation by which established Standing becomes available to a later Compare. Do not attempt to pre-fill an architecture describing everything Seed will eventually understand.

This report amends no active Book law, runtime, or tests.

## Finding 1 — the ownership half is already recovered

The search for a universal Compare owner was unnecessary. `candidate_versus_grammar_comparison_recovery_001.md` records, for Shape A:

> **Responsible owner or boundary** — The bounded comparison boundary that consumes the testimonies or findings. **The exact owner is local to the instantiated comparison and is not named universally.**

Occurrence-local ownership is therefore not a new proposal. It is the recovered position, and it has been available throughout the ownership investigation that produced `prometheus_translation_ownership_recovery_001.md`.

That investigation's negative results stand and are now explained rather than merely observed:

```text
durable competency owner          not recovered
one competency, many invocations  not observable
```

Those searched for something the recovery had already declined to name. A universal owner is absent because ownership is instance-local by construction, not because the evidence was insufficient.

```text
no universal owner recovered
!= ownership unresolved

ownership local to the instantiated comparison
= the recovered position
```

## Finding 2 — the continuation half does not typecheck

`05.Testimony.E` specifies both sides of a bounded comparison, and they do not match across iterations.

Consumed:

> A bounded comparison may consume **multiple independently preserved testimonies or findings** only while preserving each input's attribution, provenance, support basis, subject, scope, authority, confidence or uncertainty, Unknowns, standing, and forbidden inferences.

Produced:

> It may produce **bounded relation standing inside the comparison boundary**, such as agreement, disagreement, contradiction, conflict, refinement, unmet requirements, or Unknown.

So:

```text
input kinds     testimony, finding
output kind     bounded relation standing
```

**Bounded relation standing is not among the consumable input kinds.** The proposed continuation —

```text
S1 + E2 → Compare C2 → S2
```

— does not follow from the clause as written. The faucet's output kind and input kind differ, and nothing recovered here bridges them.

This is a gap in the proposal, not a defect in `05.Testimony.E`. The clause governs one bounded comparison; it does not undertake to describe chains of them.

## Finding 3 — three resolutions, materially different

```text
A. bounded relation standing is a finding

   Would make the continuation immediate. Not established:
   `01.External.D` reserves "Fidelity finding" for a bounded
   comparison of constitutional grammar, a bounded expectation,
   and an implementation witness — a different subject. Whether
   comparison results are findings generally is unrecovered.

B. the standing must be preserved as testimony before a later
   comparison may consume it

   Consistent with the consumable kinds, since testimony is
   listed. Requires a crossing from relation standing into
   preserved testimony, with its own responsible occurrence.

C. chained comparison needs grammar 05.Testimony.E does not supply

   Possible, and not excluded by anything recovered here.
```

This report does not choose among them. It records that the continuation requires one of them and currently has none.

## Finding 4 — what this changes about persistence

If B holds, the persistence answer sharpens.

`orientation_attempt_observable_evidence_recovery_001.md` established that candidate formation and Compare may remain transient at first contact, because their results are consumed immediately and no later projected recognition depends on them. That remains correct for results nothing later consumes.

But under B, a result feeding a later Compare must cross into a consumable kind. Preservation would then be **structural** — required for the result to be consumable at all — rather than **archival**, required because history wants it.

```text
archival reason      later reconstruction, provenance, accountability,
                     or cross-tick recognition depends on the occurrence

structural reason    the result's kind must change before any later
                     comparison may consume it
```

Those are different justifications and they bind differently. The archival reason is conditional on a later dependency arising. The structural reason applies at the moment the loop is attempted at all.

The proposal that "comparison results unused later may remain transient, and those used later should be preserved" reaches the right operational answer, but by the archival route. If B is the resolution, the structural route is the load-bearing one.

## Finding 5 — translator as entity, translation as act

The proposal treats competency and Translation together as external grammar imposed on Seed. That is right for one and wrong for the other.

```text
translator as entity     no constitutional object
                         "English competency", "Prometheus competency",
                         "general translator" — competency has no recovered
                         grammar as a kind

bounded translation      established. 01.External.A: a bounded translation
as act                   "may state or form only an attributed,
                         Seed-addressable translated representation, term,
                         assertion, claim proposal, or relation proposal"
```

Bounded translation is the only recovered act that forms a relation proposal. Discarding the entity is correct; discarding the act would remove the sole recovered route from presentation to candidate.

## The cup

Sufficient to hold one exact Compare occurrence, from recovered grammar only:

```text
owner              the bounded comparison boundary, local to this
                   instantiated comparison, not named universally

consumed           independently preserved testimonies or findings,
                   each preserving attribution, provenance, support basis,
                   subject, scope, authority, confidence or uncertainty,
                   Unknowns, standing, and forbidden inferences

act                Compare

occurrence         separately evidenced; comparison occurrence is not
                   recorded comparison

result             bounded relation standing inside the comparison
                   boundary — agreement, disagreement, contradiction,
                   conflict, refinement, unmet requirements, Unknown, or
                   another supported relation

limits             comparison alone does not establish truth, warrant,
                   reliance, source independence, permission, or
                   reconciliation
```

Every coordinate above is recovered. The cup is buildable now.

## The faucet

Not buildable from recovered grammar. The single missing link:

```text
what kind must a bounded comparison result become
before another bounded comparison may consume it?
```

Until that is answered, chained comparison cannot be built without inventing the bridge, and inventing it would repeat the pattern this campaign has corrected repeatedly.

## Supported findings

- Occurrence-local Compare ownership is the recovered position, already on record.
- The ownership investigation's negative results are explained by that recovery rather than by insufficient evidence.
- `05.Testimony.E` consumes testimonies or findings and produces bounded relation standing.
- Those kinds do not match across iterations, so the continuation does not follow from the clause.
- Bounded translation as an act is established; translator as an entity is not.

## Unsupported findings

- That bounded relation standing is a finding.
- That a comparison result may be consumed by a later comparison without a kind-crossing.
- That the continuation is impossible — it is unrecovered, not excluded.
- That competency has no standing — it has no recovered grammar as a kind, which is a different claim.

## Unresolved coordinates

- Which of A, B, or C resolves the continuation.
- If B, the responsible occurrence that crosses relation standing into preserved testimony, and its owner.
- Whether comparison results are findings in any general sense.
- Whether chained comparison requires its own clause distinct from `05.Testimony.E`.

## Disposition

```text
cup       buildable from recovered grammar
faucet    blocked on one unrecovered kind-crossing
```

The next recovery is narrow and stated above. No implementation slice for the faucet should be attempted before it is answered.
