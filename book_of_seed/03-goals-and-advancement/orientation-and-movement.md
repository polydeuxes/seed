# Movement Coordinates

## Constitutional subject
The role of orientation in describing where attention or concern lies without claiming advancement.

## Core question
What additional support turns orientation toward a result condition into constitutional movement?

## Bounded resolution
Orientation associates and presents result condition dimensions; movement requires a separately established transition that changes lawful position or standing.

## Addressable boundaries for movement

### 03.Movement.A — Movement is not mutation
Constitutional movement is an established transition in lawful position, standing, or advancement posture. It may be conceptual, evidentiary, temporal, responsibility-bearing, selection-bearing, authorization-bearing, or projection-bound without mutating runtime condition, external systems, the event ledger, or cluster truth. Conversely, a runtime write, recorded event, route change, projected condition update, cache change, or displayed label is not constitutional movement unless the responsible boundary preserves the support, subject, Authority, Evidence, Scope, and limits for the transition claimed.

Movement is not a rule call or Act occurrence by identity. Standing is not a durable object by identity, a constitutional subject is not a class, a named constitutional subject does not require a dedicated artifact, and changed Standing does not automatically open later movement.

## Important distinctions
- orientation != movement
- association != advancement
- candidate identity resolution != selection or movement
- current movement position label != constitutional transition
