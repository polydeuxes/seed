# Operator-Ingress Common-Grammar Prerequisite

## Constitutional subject

The goal-semantic admission required when bounded first-contact presentation is used before bounded operator goal establishment.

## Bounded resolution

Bounded operator goal establishment (**BOGE**) names the downstream operator-origin constitutional boundary addressed here. The name does not require one universal realization. Seed does not establish a self-goal or an instrumental goal in order to communicate. Preserved operator ingress cannot yet become available to BOGE through the required upstream relations. A relevant upstream relation may not presently be established; its exact act, owner, evidence, producer, and consumer remain **Unknown**. No common-grammar prerequisite is assigned to applicability, admission, or BOGE by proximity. BOGE does not interpret unresolved raw prose.

## Guarded first-contact topology

This display shows alternative local branches, possible later relations, and separately responsible occurrences. It requires no universal traversal and no compulsory order beyond an exact local dependency:

```text
preserved operator ingress whose richer grammar is not presently shared
-> developer-supplied potential-goal candidate and meaning, attributed and bounded:
   establish richer shared grammar with the operator
+ local alternative: establish no such goal and stop locally
-> a responsible occurrence forms presented alternatives representing those
   sources and presents them as a bounded closed-choice representation

alternative response branches:

one exact bounded presentation C is formed with exact token T identifying presented
alternative A, C is emitted, and the operator produces response material R after emission
-> Seed captures R and preserves its response-occurrence lineage
-> a responsible Seed comparison act compares R with C's exact response coordinates
-> a responsible Seed identification act consumes the comparison finding, exact C,
   and the established applicable T-to-A relation

1. R equals T and A is identified as corresponding to R within C
   -> represented source-candidate identity is recovered through preserved lineage
   -> a separately warranted meaning relation establishes that the candidate expresses the bounded goal proposition
   -> BOGE-local applicability examines whether that warranted relation may support
      BOGE's bounded use of the proposition
   -> a consumer-local occurrence admits the warranted relation to BOGE while
      preserving its identity, lineage, and limits
   -> the BOGE establishment occurrence may rely on the admitted relation and consume
      the proposition as expressed by the source candidate
      and establish bounded operator goal standing

2. R equals the token identifying the presented local-stop alternative, and that
   alternative is identified as corresponding to R within C
   -> the represented local-stop source is recovered through representation lineage
   -> a separately warranted meaning relation establishes that the source expresses its bounded proposition
   -> a competent stopping occurrence may rely on that bounded relation
      when establishing the bounded stop
   -> no grammar-acquisition goal is established, and Seed may return to
      quiescence

3. no exact coordinate of C matches R
   -> no presented alternative is identified
   -> meaning, intent, and requested treatment remain Unknown
   -> no potential-goal or local-stop presented alternative is identified from R
   -> zero or more bounded, separately evidenced retries may occur
   -> repeated obstruction may produce recurrence evidence
```

Possible later advancement relation:

```text
bounded grammar-acquisition goal standing
-> ordinary bounded-goal advancement responsibilities
-> any later capability applicability determination, movement consideration, movement selection,
   authority, performance, and evidence production require their own responsible
   occurrences
```

Responsibility for every later relation remains local, while exact translation ownership and interpretation implementation remain **Unknown**:

```text
preserved ingress
-> translated or otherwise bounded source material
-> interpretation candidate
-> candidate-local warrant standing
-> selected interpretation
-> purpose-local applicability
-> consumer-local admission
-> BOGE establishment

each relation requires its own responsible occurrence and evidence
```

This is a distinction display, not a compulsory universal sequence. The Book assigns neither exact translation nor candidate-production ownership here. A candidate's existence does not warrant it; warrant does not select it; selection does not establish applicability; applicability does not establish admission; and admission does not establish a bounded goal. The topology does not require every relation to produce a dedicated artifact. Retry has no constitutionally fixed count and remains optional, bounded, and separately evidenced.

## Closed-choice representation and first-contact use

A closed-choice representation is a communication representation for exposing an exact bounded set of presented alternatives. It may remain useful whenever exact bounded selection is preferable to free-form testimony; availability of prose competency does not retire it. Closed-choice grammar and free-form prose grammar are distinct, and closed-choice representation is not limited to any universal initial-use grammar. A bounded potential-goal candidate may be eligible for one presentation purpose, after which a responsible representation occurrence forms a presented alternative that refers to or represents it. The presented alternative, not the source candidate by identity, participates in the exact presented set. That participation establishes neither the source candidate's or alternative's truth or warrant nor selection, applicability, admission, authority, goal standing, movement, or performance. The representation occurrence preserves, where applicable, source candidate identity and meaning, presented content, representation purpose, scope, provenance, known loss, **Unknowns**, and conflicts. It neither invents the sources nor strengthens their standing. Tokens, bindings, literal encodings, numeric positions, and rendered labels remain distinct local representation coordinates: a compressed rendered label is not full candidate meaning, and presentation compression does not replace that meaning.

Multiplicity does not establish a bounded set, and establishment of a bounded set does not establish that any presented alternative participates in it. Source-candidate eligibility and a presented alternative's participation in an exact presented set are separately warranted relations for their declared purposes.

A bounded presentation may expose presented alternatives representing sources under different local roles, including a potential-goal candidate, presentation-navigation treatment, local-stop treatment, or clarification treatment. The role and source lineage under which an alternative participates remain explicit where relevant; these examples do not form a closed taxonomy. A presented alternative is not universally a potential goal. For example, `more options` or `show details` identifies a presentation-navigation alternative, not a goal. A selected local-stop alternative requires recovery of its preserved meaning and a separate competent stopping occurrence.

The operator-ingress case is one ordinary use of bounded potential-goal presentation when richer free-form translation is not presently available. For this exact use, the alternatives whose local participation is established may express the attributed potential-goal meaning “establish richer shared grammar with the operator” and the local alternative “establish no such goal and stop locally.” The local-stop alternative is not itself a goal. That two-alternative set is not a universal choice set, and closed-choice sets need not concern grammar acquisition or contain affirmative and negative alternatives.

In a first-contact occurrence, a presented closed-choice set together with exact token binding may provide the first shared grammar presently available between Seed and the operator. This possibility establishes no universal first event: not every Seed begins with an enum, and the first shared grammar need not be binary or numeric. Exact token binding is not free-form interpretation. It neither selects English as constitutional common grammar nor establishes permanent common grammar. Common-grammar standing remains relative to the consumer, material, act, purpose, participants, and scope.

For the current case, the closed choice exposes a presented alternative representing the bounded potential-goal candidate and a presented alternative representing the local-stop treatment. After the presentation is emitted and an operator response is captured, responsible Seed comparison and identification acts may identify one exact presented alternative as corresponding to that response within the exact presentation. The representation does not interpret the original ingress, prove shared grammar beyond the bounded comparison and identification, select English, establish a goal, authorize acquisition, or perform stopping.

A responsible Seed comparison act compares captured operator-response material R with the exact response coordinates of emitted presentation C and establishes either an exact match to token T or that no coordinate matches. A distinct responsible Seed identification act consumes the comparison finding, exact presentation identity C, and the established T-to-A relation only where that relation is applicable to the exact identification act, purpose, scope, and occurrence. Its result is that presented alternative A corresponds to R within C, or that no presented alternative is identified. One responsible Seed occurrence may perform comparison and identification, but shared occurrence does not collapse their evidence, claims, or results. Where R equals T but the T-to-A relation is unsupported, conflicting, inapplicable, absent from C, or **Unknown**, A is not lawfully identified from R.

Identification does not select the source candidate by identity automatically, establish that every presented alternative is a potential goal, make its rendered label its full meaning, warrant a meaning relation, adopt every upstream assertion, admit a meaning relation to BOGE, or establish a bounded goal. A separately responsible represented-source recovery act may consume identified A and preserved representation lineage from A to its represented source to recover bounded source-candidate identity, relation path, and attribution source. That recovery does not itself warrant that the source expresses the proposition. A separate responsible occurrence must warrant the meaning relation that candidate G expresses bounded goal proposition M. BOGE-local applicability then examines whether that warranted relation may support BOGE's bounded use of M; it does not make M applicable by proposition identity. A consumer-local occurrence must admit the warranted relation, not detach and admit bare M. Where applicable, that admission preserves identified presented alternative A, captured response and its occurrence lineage, source candidate G, bounded proposition M, the warranted relation that G expresses M, representation lineage, source and attribution, scope and purpose, authority limits, known loss, conflicts, **Unknowns**, and consumer identity. Candidate recovery is not applicability or admission, and applicability is not admission. The admission may reach BOGE without materializing a dedicated meaning-relation or admission artifact. BOGE may then rely on the admitted relation, consume M as the proposition expressed by G, and establish bounded operator goal standing; it does not establish the relation again. The meaning relation's identity and lineage must not be erased by equal proposition text: the same proposition expressed by another candidate, source, scope, or warrant is not automatically the same admitted relation.

One external operator-response occurrence supplies captured response material and preserved response-occurrence lineage. The independently established external fact is only that the operator produced R after C was emitted. It does not establish that the operator intended R to mean T, understood the binding or A, intended A, assented to a label or represented-source meaning, authorized downstream treatment, or performed a constitutional selection act. Operator selection occurrence remains **Unknown** unless separately established; no negative operator-intent claim follows. The source-recovery consumer requires identified A plus preserved representation lineage, not operator intent, understanding, an independently established operator selection act, or stronger testimony. BOGE-local applicability, consumer-local admission, and possible BOGE establishment may preserve response and representation lineage, but they derive no standing merely from the response or identification. The warrant that the source candidate expresses M remains independently supported by bounded developer-supplied or Seed-derived meaning testimony and its applicable authority, provenance, scope, conflicts, known loss, and **Unknowns**. Identification determines which independently warranted or warrant-examinable relation becomes relevant to the road; it does not establish what the candidate means. Each crossing retains its appropriate responsibility and evidence.

The lawful local relation is:

```text
bounded potential-goal standing
-> eligibility for one presentation purpose
-> a responsible representation occurrence forms a presented alternative
   referring to or representing that source candidate
-> participation of the presented alternative in the exact presented set
-> captured operator-response material and preserved response-occurrence lineage
-> exact response comparison finding
-> presented-alternative identification establishes that A corresponds to R within C
-> represented source-candidate identity recovered through preserved representation lineage
-> separately warranted meaning relation establishes that the source candidate
   expresses bounded goal proposition M
-> BOGE-local applicability examines whether the warranted relation may support
   BOGE's bounded use of M
-> BOGE-local admission preserves the warranted relation and its limits
-> BOGE establishment occurrence relies on the admitted relation and consumes M
   as the proposition expressed by the source candidate
-> possible bounded operator goal establishment
```

An identified presented alternative and a potential-goal candidate
recovered through its representation lineage are not an established
bounded goal. BOGE relies on the consumer-local admitted warranted relation and consumes the bounded goal proposition as expressed by the exact source candidate when establishing bounded operator goal standing. Only after that establishment do any grammar acquisition, capability applicability determination, movement consideration, movement selection, authority, performance, and evidence production belong to ordinary bounded-goal advancement responsibilities.

Exact identification of presented local-stop alternative A_stop establishes only that A_stop corresponds to captured response R within exact presentation C. The same responsible Seed occurrence may perform token comparison and alternative identification without collapsing those distinct acts, and identified A_stop plus preserved representation lineage may support separately responsible recovery of represented source S_stop without a second operator response. Neither response nor identification supports the independently warranted meaning relation that S_stop expresses bounded local-stop proposition M_stop. A competent stopping occurrence may rely on that independently warranted and applicable relation when establishing the local stop. The relation being admitted or otherwise usable does not mean stopping occurred; stopping occurrence does not establish a grammar-acquisition goal. No grammar-acquisition goal is established, and Seed may return to quiescence. It does not interpret the original ingress or establish a general refusal.

A captured response token unsupported by the probe is neither affirmative nor negative, neither refusal nor authorization. Comparison against the exact response coordinates of this one presented closed-choice representation establishes a nonmatch, and identification identifies no presented alternative. The response's meaning, operator intent, operator selection occurrence, and requested treatment remain **Unknown**. A token-binding nonmatch does not establish candidate nonparticipation. One or more retries may occur only as bounded, separately evidenced interactions. Repetition supplies scoped obstruction evidence; it neither interprets the response nor automatically establishes a Demand.

## Authority and movement boundary

Selection and goal-semantic admission do not establish acquisition applicability, select an acquisition movement, or supply authority. After BOGE establishes the bounded grammar-acquisition goal, any later acquisition movement and its authority require separate warrant and responsible occurrences. Authorized movement remains limited to the established goal and its evidence. It does not authorize universal initial acquisition, general language learning, a competency implementation, shell control design, or adoption of English or any other privileged constitutional grammar.

## Later acquisition and truth boundary

A later responsible acquisition occurrence may make attributed external-grammar resources or interpretation candidates available. Availability of those resources does not interpret, warrant, apply, admit, or establish the preserved ingress by identity, and neither external grammar nor an interpretation candidate is constitutional truth.

Newly available material may support later upstream translation or interpretation concerning the same preserved ingress under the material's preserved limits. The same ingress occurrence and provenance remain recoverable; acquisition does not replace that ingress with a new operator occurrence. Translation, candidate interpretation, warrant, selection, applicability, admission, and BOGE establishment remain distinct and require their own responsible occurrences and evidence. Failure at any boundary retains its own lawful Unknown, refusal, or stopping standing. One acquisition occurrence or one closed-choice selection does not establish permanent common-grammar standing for every consumer, material, act, purpose, participant, or scope.

BOGE relies on a consumer-local admitted warranted relation, with preserved lineage and consumer-local evidence, and consumes its bounded proposition as expressed by the identified source candidate rather than consuming unresolved raw prose or proposition content detached from its standing. The admitted-interpretation road and closed-choice road are distinct possible roads sharing that narrower constitutional requirement; neither is identical to the other or universally compulsory. A visible numbered enum is one possible representation, not the constitutional primitive. Bounded potential-goal standing may lead through source eligibility, formation and exact-set participation of a presented alternative representing that candidate, emission, capture of operator-response material, exact response comparison, presented-alternative identification, represented-source identity recovery through lineage, separate warrant of the meaning relation that the candidate expresses the bounded proposition, BOGE-local applicability of that relation, BOGE-local admission of that relation, BOGE reliance on the admitted relation while consuming its proposition, and possible establishment. Potential-goal standing does not establish eligibility or a bounded goal; eligibility does not form the presented alternative; representation does not establish exact-set participation; participation does not identify; response occurrence does not establish a coordinate match; comparison does not identify without the applicable token-to-alternative relation; identification does not recover a source by identity or warrant its meaning relation; warrant does not establish applicability; applicability does not establish admission; admission does not establish the goal; establishment does not consider it for movement; consideration does not select movement; and movement selection neither authorizes nor performs movement.

BOGE remains operator-origin throughout. Internal responsibilities are expressed through prerequisite, Demand, movement, selection, authority, and stopping grammar; Seed establishes no independent Seed-origin goal to perform it.
