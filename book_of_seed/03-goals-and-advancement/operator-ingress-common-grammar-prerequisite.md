# Operator-Ingress Common-Grammar Prerequisite

## Constitutional subject

The goal-semantic admission required when bounded first-contact presentation is used before bounded operator goal establishment.

## Bounded resolution

Bounded operator goal establishment (**BOGE**) names the downstream operator-origin constitutional boundary examined here. The name does not require one universal realization. Seed does not establish a self-goal or an instrumental goal in order to communicate. Preserved operator ingress cannot yet become available to BOGE through the required upstream relations. A shared-grammar dependency may not presently be established for one exact upstream act; the first interpretation-examination responsibility is assigned below without assigning its implementation district or the downstream road. No common-grammar prerequisite is assigned to applicability, admission, or BOGE by proximity. BOGE does not examine unresolved raw prose.

## Guarded first-contact topology

This display shows alternative local branches, possible later relations, and separately responsible occurrences. It requires no universal traversal and no compulsory order beyond an exact local dependency:

```text
preserved operator ingress whose richer grammar is not presently shared
-> developer-supplied potential-goal candidate and meaning, attributed and bounded:
   establish richer shared grammar with the operator
+ local alternative: establish no such goal and stop locally
-> a responsible occurrence forms presented alternatives representing those
   sources and presents them as a bounded closed-choice representation

alternative response branches:

1. exact selection of the presented alternative representing the potential-goal candidate
   -> exact selection testimony identifying that presented alternative
   -> represented source-candidate identity is recovered through preserved lineage
   -> a separately warranted meaning relation establishes that the candidate expresses the bounded goal proposition
   -> BOGE-local applicability examines whether that warranted relation may support
      BOGE's bounded use of the proposition
   -> a consumer-local occurrence admits the warranted relation to BOGE while
      preserving its identity, lineage, and limits
   -> the BOGE establishment occurrence may rely on the admitted relation and consume
      the proposition as expressed by the source candidate
      and establish bounded operator goal standing

2. exact selection of the presented local-stop alternative
   -> the represented local-stop source is recovered through representation lineage
   -> a separately warranted meaning relation establishes that the source expresses its bounded proposition
   -> a competent stopping occurrence may rely on that bounded relation
      when establishing the bounded stop
   -> no grammar-acquisition goal is established, and Seed may return to
      quiescence

3. unsupported captured response token
   -> comparison with this representation's exact token bindings identifies
      no presented alternative
   -> meaning, intent, and requested treatment remain Unknown
   -> no potential-goal candidate and no local-stop alternative is selected
   -> zero or more bounded, separately evidenced retries may occur
   -> repeated obstruction may produce recurrence evidence
```

Possible later advancement relation:

```text
bounded grammar-acquisition goal standing
-> ordinary bounded-goal advancement responsibilities
-> any later capability examination, movement consideration, movement selection,
   authority, performance, and evidence production require their own responsible
   occurrences
```

Responsibility for every later relation remains local, while exact translation ownership and interpretation implementation remain **Unknown**:

```text
preserved ingress
-> translated or otherwise bounded source material
-> interpretation candidate
-> candidate-local warrant standing
-> selected interpretation
-> purpose-local applicability
-> consumer-local admission
-> BOGE establishment

each relation requires its own responsible occurrence and evidence
```

This is a distinction display, not a compulsory universal sequence. The Book assigns neither exact translation nor candidate-production ownership here. A candidate's existence does not warrant it; warrant does not select it; selection does not establish applicability; applicability does not establish admission; and admission does not establish a bounded goal. The topology does not require every relation to produce a dedicated artifact. Retry has no constitutionally fixed count and remains optional, bounded, and separately evidenced.

## Closed-choice representation and first-contact use

A closed-choice representation is a communication representation for exposing an exact bounded set of presented alternatives. It may remain useful whenever exact bounded selection is preferable to free-form testimony; availability of prose competency does not retire it. Closed-choice grammar and free-form prose grammar are distinct, and closed-choice representation is not limited to any universal initial-use grammar. A bounded potential-goal candidate may be eligible for one presentation purpose, after which a responsible representation occurrence forms a presented alternative that refers to or represents it. The presented alternative, not the source candidate by identity, participates in the exact presented set. That participation establishes neither the source candidate's or alternative's truth or warrant nor selection, applicability, admission, authority, goal standing, movement, or performance. The representation occurrence preserves, where applicable, source candidate identity and meaning, presented content, representation purpose, scope, provenance, known loss, **Unknowns**, and conflicts. It neither invents the sources nor strengthens their standing. Tokens, bindings, literal encodings, numeric positions, and rendered labels remain distinct local representation coordinates: a compressed rendered label is not full candidate meaning, and presentation compression does not replace that meaning.

Multiplicity does not establish a bounded set, and establishment of a bounded set does not establish that any presented alternative participates in it. Source-candidate eligibility and a presented alternative's participation in an exact presented set are separately warranted relations for their declared purposes.

A bounded presentation may expose presented alternatives representing sources under different local roles, including a potential-goal candidate, presentation-navigation treatment, local-stop treatment, or clarification treatment. The role and source lineage under which an alternative participates remain explicit where relevant; these examples do not form a closed taxonomy. A presented alternative is not universally a potential goal. For example, `more options` or `show details` identifies a presentation-navigation alternative, not a goal. A selected local-stop alternative requires recovery of its preserved meaning and a separate competent stopping occurrence.

The operator-ingress case is one ordinary use of bounded potential-goal presentation when richer free-form translation is not presently available. For this exact use, the alternatives whose local participation is established may express the attributed potential-goal meaning “establish richer shared grammar with the operator” and the local alternative “establish no such goal and stop locally.” The local-stop alternative is not itself a goal. That two-alternative set is not a universal choice set, and closed-choice sets need not concern grammar acquisition or contain affirmative and negative alternatives.

In a first-contact occurrence, a presented closed-choice set together with exact token binding may provide the first shared grammar presently available between Seed and the operator. This possibility establishes no universal first event: not every Seed begins with an enum, and the first shared grammar need not be binary or numeric. Exact token binding is not free-form interpretation. It neither selects English as constitutional common grammar nor establishes permanent common grammar. Common-grammar standing remains relative to the consumer, material, act, purpose, participants, and scope.

For the current case, the closed choice exposes a presented alternative representing the bounded potential-goal candidate and a presented alternative representing the local-stop treatment; a responsible selection occurrence may identify one exact presented alternative. The representation does not interpret the original ingress, prove shared grammar beyond the bounded selection, select English, establish a goal, authorize acquisition, or perform stopping.

Exact selection testimony identifies which presented alternative the operator selected. A responsible binding occurrence may both compare the captured token against the exact presented set and identify the corresponding alternative; the Book does not universally require separate occurrences for those two acts. Binding does not select the source candidate by identity automatically, establish that every presented alternative is a potential goal, make its rendered label its full meaning, warrant a meaning relation, adopt every upstream assertion, admit a meaning relation to BOGE, or establish a bounded goal. Preserved representation lineage may support recovery of the represented source-candidate identity, relation path, and attribution source, but does not by itself warrant that the source expresses the proposition. A separate responsible occurrence must warrant the meaning relation that candidate G expresses bounded goal proposition M. BOGE-local applicability then examines whether that warranted relation may support BOGE's bounded use of M; it does not make M applicable by proposition identity. A consumer-local occurrence must admit the warranted relation, not detach and admit bare M. Where applicable, that admission preserves selected presented alternative A, source candidate G, bounded proposition M, the warranted relation that G expresses M, operator-selection lineage, representation lineage, source and attribution, scope and purpose, authority limits, known loss, conflicts, **Unknowns**, and consumer identity. Candidate recovery is not applicability or admission, and applicability is not admission. The admission may reach BOGE without materializing a dedicated meaning-relation or admission artifact. BOGE may then rely on the admitted relation, consume M as the proposition expressed by G, and establish bounded operator goal standing; it does not establish the relation again. The meaning relation's identity and lineage must not be erased by equal proposition text: the same proposition expressed by another candidate, source, scope, or warrant is not automatically the same admitted relation.

One external operator-response occurrence may supply selection testimony consumed by exact token binding, identification of the selected presented alternative, and source-identity recovery through representation lineage. BOGE-local applicability, consumer-local admission, and a possible BOGE establishment occurrence may consume standing derived from that selection while preserving the response and lineage; they need not each consume the raw response directly. The warrant that the source candidate expresses M remains independently supported by bounded developer-supplied or Seed-derived meaning testimony and its applicable authority or constitutive convention, provenance, scope, conflicts, known loss, and **Unknowns**. Selection identifies which independently warranted or warrant-examinable relation becomes relevant to the selected road; it does not establish what the candidate means. The warrant may be produced before or after selection, and production after selection does not mean that selection supplied the warrant. Separate constitutional acts do not require repeated operator selections. Each crossing retains its appropriate responsibility and evidence, and none asks the operator to recreate the same selection merely because the internal responsibilities are decomposed. The one selection does not automatically establish any downstream standing.

The lawful local relation is:

```text
bounded potential-goal standing
-> eligibility for one presentation purpose
-> a responsible representation occurrence forms a presented alternative
   referring to or representing that source candidate
-> participation of the presented alternative in the exact presented set
-> exact operator selection testimony identifying one presented alternative
-> represented source-candidate identity recovered through preserved representation lineage
-> separately warranted meaning relation establishes that the source candidate
   expresses bounded goal proposition M
-> BOGE-local applicability examines whether the warranted relation may support
   BOGE's bounded use of M
-> BOGE-local admission preserves the warranted relation and its limits
-> BOGE establishment occurrence relies on the admitted relation and consumes M
   as the proposition expressed by the source candidate
-> possible bounded operator goal establishment
```

Selected potential-goal meaning is not an established bounded goal. BOGE relies on the consumer-local admitted warranted relation and consumes the bounded goal proposition as expressed by the exact source candidate when establishing bounded operator goal standing. Only after that establishment do any grammar acquisition, capability examination, movement consideration, movement selection, authority, performance, and evidence production belong to ordinary bounded-goal advancement responsibilities.

An exact local-stop selection identifies presented local-stop alternative A_stop only. The same operator response may support token comparison, alternative identification, selection testimony, and recovery of represented source S_stop through preserved representation lineage without a second confirmation. It does not support the independently warranted meaning relation that S_stop expresses bounded local-stop proposition M_stop. A competent stopping occurrence may rely on that independently warranted and applicable relation when establishing the local stop. The relation being admitted or otherwise usable does not mean stopping occurred; stopping occurrence does not establish a grammar-acquisition goal. No grammar-acquisition goal is established, and Seed may return to quiescence. It does not interpret the original ingress or establish a general refusal.

A captured response token unsupported by the probe is neither affirmative nor negative, neither refusal nor authorization. Comparison against the exact token bindings of this one presented closed-choice representation identifies no presented alternative. The response's meaning, operator intent, and requested treatment remain **Unknown**; no potential-goal candidate or local-stop alternative is selected. A token-binding nonmatch does not establish candidate nonparticipation. One or more retries may occur only as bounded, separately evidenced interactions. Repetition supplies scoped obstruction evidence; it neither interprets the response nor automatically establishes a Demand.

## Bounded common-grammar interpretation examination

Seed assigns the first communicative interpretation-examination responsibility relationally to the exact Seed consumer whose declared communicative examination requires common-grammar standing for its exact material, act, purpose, participants, and scope. Seed owns this constitutional act generally; no universal subsystem, service, parser, translator, competency, registry, module, or implementation district thereby owns every interpretation. The subject is one exact preserved operator-ingress material occurrence together with the exact attributed grammar or grammar-relation hypothesis proposed for that bounded examination. The material retains its identity, source and attribution, provenance, scope, known loss, authority limits, **Unknowns**, and conflicts.

That consumer performs one bounded communicative examination of whether the exact preserved operator material may be interpreted under the exact applicable attributed grammar or grammar-relation hypothesis for the consumer's declared use. Its strictly local purpose is to determine whether the material can lawfully participate in that exact examination under the required bounded common-grammar relation. Addressability does not establish applicability, and a caller-supplied grammar hypothesis is testimony only: it establishes neither applicability, selection, warrant, nor common-grammar standing.

The examiner may rely only on exact inputs whose applicability to this examination has been established and for which responsible occurrences have established or preserved whatever standing, warrant, admission, authority, scope, provenance, or other relation this exact proposed use requires. No coordinate is universally required merely because the subject is an input. Depending on the exact case, those inputs may include the preserved material, attributed candidate-grammar or grammar-relation testimony, bounded translation standing, prior measurement findings, prior interpretation testimony, or closed-choice representation lineage; none is universally required. Available, addressable, applicable, admitted, and consumed or relied upon remain distinct. A measurement result is evidence only where separately applicable and is not a prerequisite by identity. Bounded comparison may occur inside the examination, but mechanical equality or recurrence measurement is not communicative interpretation examination; a comparison finding is not an interpretation relation; and an interpretation relation is not automatically a common-grammar relation. No distinct comparison occurrence or measurement-before-interpretation order is required.

Here operator grammar remains attributed external grammar, and candidate comparison grammar remains attributed external or realization grammar. Common-grammar standing is a bounded warranted relation for one exact consumer, material, act, purpose, participant set, and scope. What becomes Seed-native is the warranted constitutional distinction or relation, not either source grammar. This creates no third grammar kind, global shared-language state, permanent language adoption, or constitutional grammar by recurrence.

According to the evidence, the occurrence may produce only the already declared family of supported or unsupported interpretation relation, conflict, **Unknown**, refusal, or lawful bounded stop. Mechanical evidence supports only its mechanical finding and may not be promoted into meaning. An interpretation supported under one exact relation preserves that bounded relation and its limits, not permanent common grammar. Every failure, refusal, or stop preserves its exact reason. A token nonmatch, decoder or parse failure, unrecognized material, unsupported candidate, or failed examination does not establish absent common grammar.

Where the exact examination cannot rely on a required common-grammar relation because that relation lacks the standing required for participation, the result preserves separately the exact required relation; the exact missing or unusable standing boundary; whether the relation is unsupported, conflicting, **Unknown**, inapplicable, unadmitted, or otherwise unavailable for reliance; the exact consumer, material, act, purpose, participants, and scope; the resulting bounded non-performance, refusal, or stop; and the evidence and negative authority. That preservation creates no new universal result kind or global grammar claim. No such result establishes by identity a Demand or Demand family, prerequisite invocation, closed-choice presentation applicability, grammar-acquisition goal, movement, authority, acquisition, or permanent grammar absence.

Result testimony is available to a later, separately responsible, bounded recurrence-measurement responsibility, but recurrence is not mandatory and its owner, threshold, implementation, and recording mechanism remain unassigned here. Any later comparison of separately evidenced occurrences requires exact sameness or relation rules preserving, where applicable, consumer, material, act, purpose, required common-grammar standing, failure or stopping reason, scope, currentness, **Unknowns**, and conflicts. One attempt and loop repetition are not recurrence; same text is not the same constitutional subject; and recurrence is not meaning, common grammar, or Demand.

This responsibility does not by itself adopt external grammar as constitutional grammar; privilege English or another language; establish permanent competency; form or establish a Demand or choose its family; open movement; authorize or perform grammar acquisition; establish a bounded operator goal; form a constitutional Question; perform bounded translation without separate responsibility; automatically admit material downstream; write unrelated constitutional standing; or mutate repository or world state. The exact Demand family remains **Unknown**; Unknown family is not missing family assignment. Exact Demand examiner and establisher, the consumer of an established common-grammar-related Demand, translation owner, translation-applicability location, bounded standing-establishment owner, exact Question-forming implementation district, question-local examination consumer, and whether downstream claims share one occurrence all remain **Unknown**.

This responsibility concerns unresolved operator material and applicable attributed grammar relations; it does not replace the closed-choice selection and BOGE road. In that distinct local case, formed closed-choice presentation plus exact token bindings plus a captured matching response and a responsible bounded binding occurrence may provide minimal shared-grammar standing for that exact selection act and scope. Binding remains conditional rather than automatic: a matching token establishes neither free-form interpretation nor candidate meaning, permanent common grammar, or shared grammar beyond the warranted bounded selection.

## Authority and movement boundary

Selection and goal-semantic admission do not establish acquisition applicability, select an acquisition movement, or supply authority. After BOGE establishes the bounded grammar-acquisition goal, any later acquisition movement and its authority require separate warrant and responsible occurrences. Authorized movement remains limited to the established goal and its evidence. It does not authorize universal initial acquisition, general language learning, a competency implementation, shell control design, or adoption of English or any other privileged constitutional grammar.

## Re-examination and truth boundary

A later responsible acquisition occurrence may make attributed external-grammar resources or interpretation candidates available. Availability of those resources does not interpret, warrant, apply, admit, or establish the preserved ingress by identity, and neither external grammar nor an interpretation candidate is constitutional truth.

Newly available material may support later upstream translation or interpretation concerning the same preserved ingress under the material's preserved limits. The same ingress occurrence and provenance remain recoverable; acquisition does not replace that ingress with a new operator occurrence. Translation, candidate interpretation, warrant, selection, applicability, admission, and BOGE establishment remain distinct and require their own responsible occurrences and evidence. Failure at any boundary retains its own lawful Unknown, refusal, or stopping standing. One acquisition occurrence or one closed-choice selection does not establish permanent common-grammar standing for every consumer, material, act, purpose, participant, or scope.

BOGE relies on a consumer-local admitted warranted relation, with preserved lineage and consumer-local evidence, and consumes its bounded proposition as expressed by the selected candidate rather than consuming unresolved raw prose or proposition content detached from its standing. The admitted-interpretation road and closed-choice road are distinct possible roads sharing that narrower constitutional requirement; neither is identical to the other or universally compulsory. A visible numbered enum is one possible representation, not the constitutional primitive. Bounded potential-goal standing may lead through source eligibility, formation and exact-set participation of a presented alternative representing that candidate, exact operator selection testimony identifying the presented alternative, represented-source identity recovery through lineage, separate warrant of the meaning relation that the candidate expresses the bounded proposition, BOGE-local applicability of that relation, BOGE-local admission of that relation, BOGE reliance on the admitted relation while consuming its proposition, and possible establishment. Potential-goal standing does not establish eligibility or a bounded goal; eligibility does not form the presented alternative; representation does not establish exact-set participation; participation does not select; selection does not recover a source by identity or warrant its meaning relation; warrant does not establish applicability; applicability does not establish admission; admission does not establish the goal; establishment does not consider it for movement; consideration does not select movement; and movement selection neither authorizes nor performs movement.

BOGE remains operator-origin throughout. Necessary internal work is expressed through prerequisite, Demand, movement, selection, authority, and stopping grammar; Seed establishes no independent Seed-origin goal to perform it.
