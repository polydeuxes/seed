# Construction and Establishment

## Constitutional subject
The transition from a constructed goal-shaped representation to an established bounded operator goal.

## Core question
Which ingress evidence and invariants establish a goal's constitutional standing?

## Bounded resolution
Construction produces a representation; establishment binds admitted operator meaning, scope, provenance, and boundedness into a goal with standing.

BOGE relies on a consumer-local admitted meaning relation, consumes the bounded goal proposition as the proposition expressed by the exact source candidate under that standing, and establishes bounded-goal standing. A selected presented alternative and the potential-goal candidate recovered through its lineage are not an established bounded goal. On a closed-choice road, bounded potential-goal standing, eligibility for one presentation purpose, formation of a presented alternative that represents the source candidate, participation of that alternative in the exact presented set, a responsible binding occurrence that may both compare the captured token with the exact presented set and identify its alternative, recovery of represented source-candidate identity through preserved lineage, a separately warranted meaning relation establishing that the candidate expresses the bounded goal proposition, BOGE-local applicability of that relation, BOGE-local admission preserving that relation and its limits, BOGE reliance on the admitted relation while consuming its proposition, and possible BOGE establishment remain distinct. The candidate is not itself the presented alternative; candidate meaning is not rendered content; and selecting the presented alternative does not select the source candidate by identity or adopt every upstream assertion. On an admitted-interpretation road, interpretation candidate, candidate-local warrant, selection, purpose-local applicability, consumer-local admission, and BOGE establishment remain distinct. These are different possible roads sharing a narrower consumer-local admission requirement, not identical or universally compulsory roads.

Separation of these constitutional responsibilities does not require repeated operator selections. One operator response occurrence may supply selection testimony consumed by responsible Seed-side binding and source identification through lineage; later applicability, admission, and BOGE establishment acts may consume standing derived from that selection while preserving the response and lineage. The meaning-relation warrant remains independently supported by bounded developer-supplied or Seed-derived meaning testimony, applicable authority, provenance, scope, conflicts, known loss, and **Unknowns**. Selection identifies the relevant candidate and therefore which independently warranted or warrant-examinable relation becomes relevant to the selected road; it does not establish what that candidate means. A meaning-relation warrant may be produced before or after selection, but production after selection does not make the selection its evidence. Exact token comparison and identification of the bound alternative need not universally be separate constitutional occurrences. Every crossing requires its appropriate evidence, but no crossing asks the operator to recreate the same selection merely because Seed decomposes its internal acts. One selection does not automatically establish any later standing. Neither applicability nor admission requires a dedicated relation or admission artifact.

## Important distinctions
- establishment assertion != artifact field named established
- establishment lineage != proof that each upstream producer ran
- construction != establishment
- interpreted expression != established goal
- valid fields != admitted meaning
- potential-goal standing != bounded goal established
- source candidate != presented alternative
- candidate meaning != rendered content
- presented alternative != token binding
- token binding != operator selection occurrence
- binding result != meaning relation warranted
- binding result != goal-semantic admission
- proposition != warranted relation that the source candidate expresses that proposition
- meaning relation warranted != proposition applicable to BOGE by identity
- BOGE-local applicability != BOGE-local admission
- admission of the warranted meaning relation != bounded goal established
- BOGE consumes the proposition != BOGE consumes it without candidate and relation standing
- selected candidate identity != proposition identity
- same proposition expressed by another candidate != same admitted meaning relation automatically
- source candidate recovered through lineage != meaning relation admitted to BOGE
- separate constitutional acts != repeated operator selections
- selection != goal-semantic admission
- goal-semantic admission != BOGE establishment
- BOGE establishment != movement consideration

## Counterexamples or failure modes
- Treating a legacy `Goal` model instance as proof of bounded establishment.
- Establishing a goal directly from unadmitted text.
