# Construction and Establishment

## Constitutional subject
The transition from a constructed goal-shaped representation to an established bounded operator goal.

## Core question
Which ingress evidence and invariants establish a goal's constitutional standing?

## Bounded resolution
Construction produces a representation; establishment binds admitted operator meaning, scope, provenance, and boundedness into a goal with standing.

BOGE relies on a consumer-local admitted meaning relation, consumes the bounded goal proposition as the proposition expressed by the exact source candidate under that standing, and establishes bounded-goal standing. An identified presented alternative and the potential-goal candidate recovered through its lineage are not an established bounded goal. On a closed-choice road, bounded potential-goal standing, eligibility for one presentation purpose, formation of an exact presentation C containing a presented alternative A that represents the source candidate, participation of A in C, emission of C, capture of operator-response material R and its occurrence lineage, a responsible comparison act that may establish an exact match between R and presentation token T, a responsible identification act that consumes that comparison finding together with the established applicable T-to-A relation and exact presentation identity C, recovery of represented source-candidate identity from identified A through preserved representation lineage, a separately warranted meaning relation establishing that the candidate expresses the bounded goal proposition, BOGE-local applicability of that relation, BOGE-local admission preserving that relation and its limits, BOGE reliance on the admitted relation while consuming its proposition, and possible BOGE establishment remain distinct. The identification result is only that A corresponds to R within C. The candidate is not itself the presented alternative; candidate meaning is not rendered content; and identifying the presented alternative does not select the source candidate by identity or adopt every upstream assertion. On an admitted-interpretation road, interpretation candidate, candidate-local warrant, selection, purpose-local applicability, consumer-local admission, and BOGE establishment remain distinct. These are different possible roads sharing a narrower consumer-local admission requirement, not identical or universally compulsory roads.

One operator-response occurrence supplies captured response material and preserved occurrence lineage. A responsible comparison act may compare that material with C's exact response coordinates and establish an exact coordinate match or nonmatch. A responsible identification act may then establish which presented alternative corresponds to that response within C, but only by consuming the comparison finding, exact presentation identity, and an established T-to-A relation applicable to the exact identification act, purpose, scope, and occurrence. A match to T does not identify A when that relation is unsupported, conflicting, inapplicable, absent from C, or **Unknown**. Comparison and identification are distinct acts; one responsible Seed occurrence may perform both without collapsing their evidence, claims, or results or requiring dedicated occurrences for either act.

Represented-source recovery may then consume identified A and preserved representation lineage from A to its represented source. Later applicability, admission, and BOGE establishment acts may preserve the response and representation lineage, but derive no standing merely from the response. The meaning-relation warrant remains independently supported by bounded developer-supplied or Seed-derived meaning testimony, applicable authority, provenance, scope, conflicts, known loss, and **Unknowns**. Identification determines only which independently warranted or warrant-examinable relation becomes relevant to this road; it does not establish what the candidate means. A meaning-relation warrant may be produced before or after identification, but production after identification does not make the response or identification its evidence. The independently established external fact is only that the operator produced R after C was emitted. Operator intent, understanding, and operator selection occurrence remain **Unknown** unless separately established. Every crossing requires its appropriate evidence. Neither applicability nor admission requires a dedicated relation or admission artifact.

## Important distinctions
- establishment assertion != artifact field named established
- establishment lineage != proof that each upstream producer ran
- construction != establishment
- interpreted expression != established goal
- valid fields != admitted meaning
- potential-goal standing != bounded goal established
- source candidate != presented alternative
- candidate meaning != rendered content
- presented alternative != token binding
- operator response occurrence != operator selection occurrence
- response material occurred != response matched a presentation coordinate
- response matched token T != T identifies alternative A
- comparison != identification
- A corresponds to response R != operator intended A
- A corresponds to response R != represented source identity recovered
- represented source identity recovered != source meaning warranted
- source meaning warranted != applicability != admission != BOGE establishment
- token binding != operator selection occurrence
- binding result != meaning relation warranted
- binding result != goal-semantic admission
- proposition != warranted relation that the source candidate expresses that proposition
- meaning relation warranted != proposition applicable to BOGE by identity
- BOGE-local applicability != BOGE-local admission
- admission of the warranted meaning relation != bounded goal established
- BOGE consumes the proposition != BOGE consumes it without candidate and relation standing
- selected candidate identity != proposition identity
- same proposition expressed by another candidate != same admitted meaning relation automatically
- source candidate recovered through lineage != meaning relation admitted to BOGE
- separate constitutional acts != repeated operator selections
- selection != goal-semantic admission
- goal-semantic admission != BOGE establishment

## Counterexamples or failure modes
- Treating a legacy `Goal` model instance as proof of bounded establishment.
- Establishing a goal directly from unadmitted text.
