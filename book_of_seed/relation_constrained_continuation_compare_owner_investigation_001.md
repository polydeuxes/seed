### 3.2 Relation-constrained addressing



### 3.3 Row and coordinate work

For each addressed projection pair, Python then requires:


These constraints are the observer's exact rule. They are generic in the
sense that they contain no arithmetic or English answer. They are nevertheless
authored Python, not an active Seed rule.

### 3.4 Observer result

```text

```

That is a plausible Compare result shape: exact subjects are considered under
