conflicts. It expressly withholds comparison, interpretation, applicability,

therefore one supported proposal-formation road, not the Compare owner and not

The Responsibility tree requires an exact owner, subject or material, Act,
Authority, Evidence, purpose, Scope, and locality. Where a result is produced,
Producer, production occurrence, production-occurrence Evidence, and result
are separate coordinates. Where Standing is claimed, the

Standing are separate again. Neither adjacency nor a filled coordinate assigns
the unfilled ones.

Active Applicability law gives the ordinary rule: unless Book law explicitly
assigns otherwise, the owner of an exact Act is responsible for ensuring that
each proposed input is applicable to that Act. That rule presupposes the Act
owner; it does not discover or create the Shape B owner. The owner may perform
the determination locally or validate one explicitly assigned for the exact
input-to-Act relation. Applicable material is not thereby admitted or consumed,
and no Act occurrence follows.

Active Uptake law likewise starts with a responsible Consumer that already has
Standing to perform a bounded use. A Consumer-local occurrence may use upstream
material only under its exact purpose, Scope, Authority, Applicability,
Admission requirements, conflicts, Unknowns, and limits, and produces only its
own bounded assertion or Standing. Availability does not name that Consumer,
produce its occurrence, or revise it.

The active free-form material supplies less. The operator-ingress
common-grammar chapter distinguishes closed-choice grammar from free-form prose

Consequently, **the active free-form interaction supplies neither the exact

Standing is not established automatically. A claimed Standing requires its own
responsible establishment boundary, occurrence, Evidence, subject, Authority,
