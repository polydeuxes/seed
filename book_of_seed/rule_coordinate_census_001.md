## DNA/RNA comparison

Biological discrimination can depend on independently real machinery:
molecular structure, geometry, charge, sequence, cofactors, and current
conformation. Those are addressable material and relations, not a prose rule
stored beside an interaction.

Seed's closest current analogue is its inherited family-local physiology: the
exact operation and relations constrain what can occur. The embedded rule
string is closer to an observer description of that machinery than to another
participant in the Act.

This does not establish that rules can never be material. A supplied program,
relation table, executable structure, or other exact result could later govern
otherwise identical subjects differently. Such material would survive this
census because its identity, source, provenance, and consequences would be
independently addressable.
