## Question

Does the ordinary `!pytest` operator invocation expose increasingly strong
associations from the existing sixteen-book corpus when the current runtime is
allowed to proceed without a curated input or a new recursive procedure?

This observation changes no Book or runtime material.

```text
!pytest operator occurrence

↓
```

The first stop is the immediate blocker for this experiment. The second stop
is the lawful boundary for the material returned by the failed invocation.

## Stale local artifact

The pre-existing untracked `seed-16-books.sqlite` was not used as current
testimony. It contains retired `material.ingest.*` occurrences and the removed
second operator Ingest road. Its 87-event history predates the active material
acquisition decomposition and cannot answer this experiment.

## Finding

The ordinary `!pytest` experiment is currently blocked before corpus
acquisition by a machine-grammar integration mismatch:

```text
implementation measurement expects clause_coordinates
!=
active witness grammar exposes book_coordinates
```

No special corpus road should be added. The ordinary `!pytest` path must first
be able to collect and execute its existing tests. Only then can the same
observation be rerun to inspect whether successive lawful results grow into
stronger associations.
