## 5. Why this is a different floor

The measurements now have three exact layers:

```text
occurrence surface
-> what one record carries

closed frame
-> what several record surfaces share

frame-bounded walk
-> how assignment, Acts, Yield, and results proceed

walk-of-walks
-> how the complete source operation composes those arcs
```

Frame 52 alone exposed a Responsibility-assignment-shaped construction. The
frame-bounded walk exposes the full local arc. The walk-of-walks exposes one
bounded whole whose varying middle is determined by the acquired material.

In the operator's ordinary analogy:

```text

one assignment frame is not the story
one frame-bounded walk is one local story
the walk-of-walks is the first measured grand story of this source operation
```

This does not establish that the walk-of-walks is the final or universal floor.
It establishes that moving up one exact composition layer reveals lawful source
structure that is invisible when records are inspected separately.
