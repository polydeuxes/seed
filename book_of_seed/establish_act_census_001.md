# Establish Act census 001

## Question

Does `establish` name an exact Act that can vary independently from the Act,
its subjects, and its result relation?

## Active Locality controls

The active Locality grammar used `establish` in three different coordinate
shapes:

```text
exact Locality relation
recorded-boundary Locality relation
operator-destination Locality relation
```

The word did not identify one shared Act among them.

`06.Locality.A` requires an exact Act and separately requires its exact Act
occurrence and relation occurrence. Removing `establish` does not collapse
those occurrences.

`06.Locality.C` has an exact preservation Act. Its recorded result occurrence
is the Locality relation occurrence. The preservation Act, Act occurrence,
and relation occurrence remain separately addressed without an Establish Act.

`06.Locality.D` has an exact Locality Act. Its operator material occurrence,
source Locality, destination Locality, Act occurrence, and relation occurrence
remain separately addressed without an Establish Act.

No active control permits two otherwise equal Acts to differ only by
`establish`.

## Authored runtime material

Two authored runtime phrases carried the word.

The operator-destination phrase narrated its exact subjects and relation. Its
Act coordinate is `Locality`; the exact material occurrence and source and
destination Localities supply the remaining coordinates.

The byte-pair result boundary narrated an operation. Its surviving coordinate
is the exact byte-pair occurrence count in source order within exact bounded
source material. It is not an Establish Act.

The recorded-boundary runtime Act is named `Preservation`, matching its active
Book coordinate rather than narrating its subject and destination.

## Falsifier

Removing `establish` must preserve all of these distinctions:

```text
exact Act
exact Act occurrence
exact relation occurrence
preservation Act
Locality Act
source and destination Localities
recorded boundary reference
byte-pair occurrence-count result boundary
```

Changing an exact Act, subject reference, Locality, result boundary, Act
occurrence, or relation occurrence must still invalidate the corresponding
reader.

## Disposition

```text
established   no independent coordinate
establish     no independent Act

Act           exact Act coordinate
Act occurrence
               exact occurrence of that Act
relation occurrence
               separate exact result relation occurrence
```

`establish` leaves active Book admission. The exact Acts and the resulting
relations remain independently addressed.
