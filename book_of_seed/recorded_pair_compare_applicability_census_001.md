## Active inward order

For each new operator material result after the first, the inward operation
records one byte-pair Measurement and addresses the immediately prior carried
byte-pair Measurement. The active Compare road then records:

```text

        ↓

        ↓

        ↓

```

This is an active inward producer, not an authored test-only constructor.

## The question is answered before Applicability

Before the Compare binding is appended, `_comparison_inputs...` validates:

```text
two distinct pair Measurement results
same exact Locality
earlier result before later result
both results current
later exact source occurrence sequence
    = earlier exact source occurrence sequence
    + one exact material result occurrence
exact operator source coordinates for the added occurrence
```

If any coordinate fails, no Compare binding and no Applicability occurrence
is recorded. Witness-source substitution, a source occurrence before the
earlier premise, a changed sequence, another Locality, and unavailable current
coordinates are all refused at this earlier boundary.

## Binding-production boundary

The forced verdict does not settle why the Compare binding occurrence exists.
The operator loop keeps the immediately prior pair Measurement as a premise
and joins it with the newly produced pair Measurement. No prior Ledger
occurrence independently records that exact pair before the binding event.

Therefore two questions remain separate:

```text
Does recorded-pair Applicability vary?          no
What establishes this exact Compare binding?   not found
```

Removing forced Applicability would not establish the binding producer. It
would leave that earlier boundary visible and unchanged.

## Controls

its authored result can be `applicable` or `inapplicable`, and absence remains
distinct from a negative result.

The active byte-pair Measurement road is the subtraction control: its exact
source result position existed before a forced-positive Applicability stage,
and that stage failed subtraction without deleting callable variable
