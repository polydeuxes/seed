# Inquiry: constitutional status recovery 001

## 1. Executive

**Inquiry names something.** This is the fourth cat test in the sequence and
the first to return a clear affirmative. `proposed input` named nothing.
`relation grammar` named nothing and mapped onto a dimension list. `candidate
equivalence` named a proposal in unadopted vocabulary. Inquiry is not like any
of them: it holds a named act, a named standing, five clauses, a Demand family,
stop grounds, and cited runtime.

**But the shape being carried is still wrong, and in a way the noun test would
have missed.** The shape was:

```text
Operator Inquiry  →  Bounded Constitutional Question
```

Active law states the normal relationship for exactly this, at `04.Question.E`
(`04-inquiry/questions-and-inquiry.md`), and **Inquiry is not a node in it**:

```text
external or operator material
↓
attributed testimony, pressure, goals, constraints, corrections, or response
↓
Seed-side interpretation and bounded standing establishment
↓
Seed-owned question formation
↓
Seed-owned applicability, selection, and composition of lawful internal means
↓
bounded Seed question
↓
operator response as further testimony
```

The node in that position is **question formation**. So the defect was never
the noun — it was the **arrow and the position**. Inquiry is real and was
placed where active law puts something else.

This means the curator-proposed replacement — `operator occurrence → responsible
Question formation → Question` — is very close to what `04.Question.E` already
states, with the stated version carrying three more steps and naming the
ownership at each.

## 2. Why Inquiry survives the test

Eleven distinct supports, none of which the previous three phrases had.

**A home chapter with five clauses.** Chapter 04 is Inquiry, and
`questions-and-inquiry.md` carries `04.Question.A` through `04.Question.E`. One
is titled **"Inquiry origination requires bounded translation."**

**A named act with stated preconditions.** `04.Question.B`: an operator ask,
prompt phrase, uncertainty statement, reformulation, or presentation label "may
create attributed testimony or inquiry pressure only. Seed may initiate
internal inquiry after a bounded translation preserves identity, source, scope,
evidence demand, authority limit, uncertainty, and lawful stop." Seven
preserved coordinates gate the act.

**A named standing.** `04.Question:54` — `question surface != inquiry
standing`.

**Non-equivalences that presuppose it is real.** `04.Question:53` — `operator
testimony != inquiry origination`. `:56` — `uncertainty statement != inquiry
origination`. A clause distinguishing X from Y presupposes Y.

**Stated Seed ownership, distinct from question formation.** `04.Question:47`:
"Missing realization does not transfer **inquiry origination or question
formation** to the operator." Two things, both Seed-owned, named side by side.

**A Demand family.** `03.Demands:17`: "The canonical goal-advancement Demand
families are clarification, **inquiry**, authority, and operational
realization."

**A production claim.** `05.Provenance:56` — `answer not recoverable !=
inquiry produced nothing`. A clause about what inquiry produces.

**Stop grounds.** `08.Stopping:4` — "The grounds for stopping inquiry or
movement."

**Spread.** Inquiry appears in eleven active-law files (plus the chapter
README) across chapters 01, 02, 03, 04, 05, and 08.

**Old provenance, unlike the #2244 terms.** `inquiry standing` traces to
#1710/#1711, `inquiry origination` to #1731 — all before the Book skeleton at
#1742. This is not vocabulary that entered through a correction round.

**It survived a cut that its chapter-mate did not.** The decisive one. Chapter
04 was `04-inquiry-and-examination` until **#2210, "Remove Examination and
Work"**, which renamed it to `04-inquiry`. Examination was removed as a
constitutional kind; Inquiry was retained. Capitalized `Examination` now
appears zero times in active law. The cat test has already been run on this
exact chapter, on the other word, and the two words came apart.

## 3. What is *not* established

**`inquiry standing` has no defining clause.** It is named once, inside a
non-equivalence at `04.Question:54`. Searching active law for a clause stating
what inquiry standing *is* returns nothing. The term is used; it is not
defined. Recorded as a gap, not resolved here.

**The relationship between inquiry origination and question formation is not
stated.** `:47` names them together as Seed-owned. It does not order them, make
one part of the other, or say how they relate. Nothing here should be read as
establishing that inquiry origination precedes, contains, or produces question
formation.

**Cited runtime is not verified against the clauses.** Active law cites
`seed_runtime/inquiry_orientation.py` and
`seed_runtime/inquiry_artifacts.py::InquiryArtifactVisibility`, and both files
exist. That is all this report checked. Whether they implement what the clauses
state is a separate question and a known failure mode in this corpus — a cited
implementation is not a corroborated one.

## 4. The clause that limits every chain, including §1's

`04.Question.C` is titled **"Question relations are local and
non-collapsing"**, and `:21` states:

> A relation involving inquiry pressure, an inquiry demand, a bounded question,
> a finding, an answer, completion, communication, or later re-entry exists
> only where the exact source standing, target standing, crossing evidence or
> warrant, authority boundary, and surviving limits are preserved for that
> local relation. **Naming several such standings in one report, graph,
> implementation surface, or sequence does not establish a canonical node
> inventory, universal pipeline, constitutional ordering, or missing edge.**

This governs the shape that was being carried, the replacement proposed for it,
and the `04.Question.E` chain quoted in §1 alike. All three are sequences of
named standings.

The two clauses are compatible rather than in conflict: `E` states the
**normal** relationship and its subject is ownership — its title is "Normal
internal questioning is Seed-owned," and its closing sentence is about
ownership not transferring. `C` denies that such a listing becomes
**canonical** or **universal**. Normal is not universal.

So §1's chain should be read as active law's account of where ownership sits,
not as a recovered pipeline. It is enough to show that Inquiry is not the node
in the position it was given, and not enough to establish an ordering.

## 5. Bearing on the next question

The act word is the next target — which of `examine`, `compare`, `consider`,
`warrant` names a Responsibility. Part of that is already answered by the
history found here, and is recorded rather than developed.

After #2210 removed Examination, `examine` and `examination` survive in active
law four times:

```text
02.Selection:37        `seed_runtime/examination_work_selection.py`
                       (a runtime filename, vestigial)
03.Prerequisite:35     BOGE-local applicability examines whether that
                       warranted relation may support ...
03.Prerequisite:120    BOGE-local applicability examines whether the
                       warranted relation may support ...
03.Prerequisite:102    (identification clause)
```

Two are the same sentence pattern with **applicability** as the subject doing
the examining, and one is a filename. No clause names an Examination
Responsibility, act, or standing.

This is evidence that `examine` is an ordinary verb in active law rather than a
Responsibility, and it was already decided at #2210. It does not settle
`compare`, `consider`, or `warrant`, and this report does not extend to them.

## 7. Correction record

No defect to record in this report's own reasoning. One method note, carried
forward from #2341's correction.

The three overclaims corrected at #2341 all had the shape of substituting a
nearby established thing for the unrecovered one, and all passed a citation
check. The guard applied here was to write each clause and each conclusion as
separate lines and ask what carries one to the other. It caught two candidate
statements before they were written:

```text
clause          :47 names inquiry origination and question formation
                together as Seed-owned
not written     therefore inquiry origination precedes question formation
                — :47 orders nothing

clause          :54 says question surface != inquiry standing
not written     therefore inquiry standing is defined — it is named
                inside a non-equivalence and defined nowhere
```

Both are recorded as gaps in §3 instead.
