## 1. `Movement` currently names several distinct pressures

The hypothesis initially compresses at least four things beneath `movement`:

```text
A. one new operator material boundary occurrence

B. acquisition by one Assertion of a new Locality relation

C. one operator invocation Locality relation

D. movement in Standing for one exact subject
```

They are not interchangeable.

### 1.1 Operator material boundary occurrence

`01.Source.G` assigns preservation of one exact material result supplied at one
operator boundary. The result carries exact assignment, Act, Act occurrence,
Yield, result, Locality, Scope, Authority, limits, Unknown, and exact material
coordinates where recorded.

It establishes no represented relation, Applicability, Participation,
recurrence, completion, or Authority for another occurrence.

This is the ordinary ingress disturbance:

```text
operator boundary supplies exact material
↓
exact acquisition result occurrence
↓
bounded Locality read contains one additional exact occurrence
```

No constitutional Movement occurrence is recorded merely because the material
arrived.

### 1.2 Assertion Locality movement

`03.Movement.A` and `06.Locality.A` describe a responsible movement occurrence
that preserves one exact Assertion while establishing one new Locality
relation.

That movement is deliberately narrow:

```text
preserves original Assertion identity
preserves prior locality relations
preserves Scope, Evidence, Standing, Unknown, limits
establishes one new locality relation
```

It explicitly does not:

```text
copy or strengthen the Assertion
revise its Standing
establish Applicability
require another Act
```

Therefore constitutional Assertion movement cannot be the whole spring release
by identity. Its active boundary refuses exactly that strengthening.

### 1.3 Operator invocation Locality relation

`06.Locality.D` records one direct relation from an operator Locality to one new
invocation Locality. It carries no operator Standing into the destination and
establishes no supplied-material Applicability or Participation.

Current runtime reaches that family only behind an external byte predicate:

```python

```

The separate prefix-classification investigation already found that this host
predicate is not Seed Standing that the material begins an invocation.

Ordinary material such as:

```text

```

does not acquire an invocation Locality relation by its occurrence alone.

### 1.4 Movement in Standing

`01.Standing.D.2` names a responsible occurrence establishing movement in
Standing for one exact subject. It requires preservation of earlier established
coordinates, Unknown, and the exact subject coordinate where no new Standing
is established for them.

No generic runtime occurrence inspected here instantiates:

```text

↓
movement in Standing for every subject
```

The phrase therefore remains distinct from operator ingress, Assertion
Locality movement, and operator invocation Locality movement.
