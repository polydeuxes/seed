## Independent control

The Witness material-source road independently carried the same family-local
result identity through its binding and result. Its exact result occurrence
also has a Ledger occurrence identity.

Removing the family-local result identity preserves:

```text
exact supplied bytes
exact source boundary
source occurrence references

exact Act identity

one result per Act occurrence
separate equal-material result occurrences

```

The exact result occurrence identity now supplies the material-result subject
reference in bounded current coordinates. Later Measurements address that
occurrence without a copied result identity.

source references, bytes, or Locality remains refused.

```text

```

The operator and Witness material-source roads now agree independently on this
coordinate shape.
