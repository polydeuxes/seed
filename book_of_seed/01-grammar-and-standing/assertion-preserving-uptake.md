# Assertion-Preserving Uptake

## Constitutional subject
The difference between material being available to an exact Act and an exact Act possessing a warranted transition between constitutional subjects.

## Core question
What makes a relationship an assertion-preserving Uptake relation rather than repository adjacency or informative visibility?

## Bounded resolution
Uptake names an act-local relation family, not a universal act or ordered position. When a responsible exact Act has prior standing to perform a bounded use of upstream material, and when the upstream material and its preserved evidence, warrant, identity, scope, authority, confidence, and Unknown limits meet the exact requirements of that exact Act's declared act and purpose, the exact Act may perform a responsible local use occurrence. Any resulting assertion or standing is produced through that act-local occurrence and remains bounded to its exact subject, exact Act, purpose, and preserved limits. The Uptake vocabulary is dimensional rather than a list of sibling kinds: assertion adoption or narrowing describes the upstream-material relation; coherence checks are exact Act constraints; projection, request formation, testimony preservation, explanation construction, and establishment describe exact Acts; typed request formation describes bounded representation formation; emission describes a separately evidenced presentation occurrence; and transport structure describes possible carriage, routing, addressing, or compatibility structure without proving emission or arrival. An assertion-preserving Uptake relation is the subfamily of Uptake in which the exact Act accepts the responsible occurrence's assertion for a declared purpose and validates the identities and invariants needed at that boundary. A View may participate in Uptake, including an assertion-preserving Uptake relation, but neither call adjacency, repeated co-occurrence, mechanical compatibility, nor the noun ``Uptake`` establishes one. Representation selection that only chooses among already-formed representations for rendering or composition is not selection of a subject; a selection with subject standing is different and requires evidence that a subject, not merely a representation, was selected for a bounded downstream purpose. Standing for an assertion-preserving Uptake relation is exact Act- and purpose-relative: upstream Warrant, artifact preservation, and act-local validation are related but not interchangeable.

Material applicable to an exact Act, material admitted to an exact Act, and material consumed by an exact Act are distinct constitutional standings. Available upstream material may be evaluated by an act-local applicability boundary and remain applicable, inapplicable, Unknown, or conflicting; applicable material does not thereby become admitted. A separate act-local admission boundary may consume applicability and admission evidence to produce admitted, unadmitted, Unknown, or conflicting admission standing; that admission-boundary consumption does not mean the final addressed subject exact Act has consumed the admitted material. Responsible exact Act use of admitted material may then produce a new act-local assertion or standing without re-establishing upstream standing, proving upstream production occurrence, or expanding authority beyond the declared purpose. Material may be applicable but lawfully unadmitted, admitted but never consumed, and an exact Act may establish act-local non-reliance, exclude material from its exact use, or preserve Unknown at any crossing.

## Addressable boundaries for act-local change

### 01.Uptake.A — New availability does not revise an exact Act
Evidence becoming available does not by itself change any exact Act assertion, standing, confidence, reliance, or current result. A revision requires a responsible act-local occurrence that lawfully uses the material under the relevant purpose, scope, authority, applicability, admission constraints, conflicts, Unknowns, and temporal limits. Availability, applicability, admission, consumption, Uptake, reliance, and standing change remain distinct, but the Book requires neither a universal order among them nor a separately produced admission occurrence when only responsible consumption is evidenced. An upstream responsible occurrence owns its attributed production and availability testimony, not universal emission or any exact Act's applicability, admission, Uptake, reliance, or downstream revision. One exact Act's Uptake, assertion, or reliance does not establish another's or transfer automatically to another purpose or boundary.

## Important distinctions
- availability != assertion-preserving Uptake relation
- repository adjacency != constitutional sequence
- constructibility != source-to-exact Act warrant
- visibility != transition warrant
- production validation != exact Act validation automatically
- exact Act != exact Act constraint
- compressed transfer wording != constitutional relationship
- typed values transported != Uptake automatically
- Uptake != universal stage
- artifact fields read != assertion adopted
- act-local warrant may coexist with weaker upstream standing
- newly available evidence != act-local Uptake or revision
- one exact Act's Uptake or reliance != another exact Act's Uptake or reliance
