## 2. Corrections to PR 2223

### Correction 1 — structural HEAD does not imply hierarchy

PR 2223's disposition reasoning (§2.2, §6) used the independent-ownership rule at `constitutional-kinds-and-artifact-standing.md:67` ("Acts, constraints, and relations are structurally distinct from dimensions... Their presence in dimensional characterization does not make them additional dimensions, a mandatory intermediate layer, or one exhaustive taxonomy") to reject Responsibility as a structural HEAD outright. That clause forbids *ownership reassignment* — treating Constraint, Act, or Relation as though Responsibility constitutionally absorbs them. It does not address whether Responsibility may serve as a *presentation and traversal root* that references those independently owned subjects without owning them.

This is a real conflation, and PR 2223's own topology artifact (§4.1) already avoided it in practice: it marked "consumer and Uptake" and "constraints and lawful Stopping" as "independently owned neighbor," not as owned children, while its disposition prose (§2.2, §6) then used that same independence to reject HEAD status generally. The artifact was more precise than the disposition conclusion drawn from it.

**Retained from 2223:** the independent-ownership finding itself (`:67`) is correct and remains controlling — no correction here treats Authority, Standing, Constraint, Relation, Consumer, Uptake, or Stopping as owned by Responsibility.

**Corrected:** structural HEAD and constitutional superkind are not the same claim, and PR 2223's evidence supports the former without supporting (or needing) the latter.

### Correction 2 — Authority is not Authorization

PR 2223 marked the authority branch conditional, reasoning that "many responsibilities, e.g. lenses, applicability determinations, and selection acts, consume no authorization at all" (§4.1). This conflates two distinct things:

- **Authority coordinate**: the warrant under which the exact responsibility and act may be borne or performed at all, including a bounded negative-authority finding, an authority limit, or an exact coordinate-local Unknown.
- **Authorization standing**: a separately established bounded standing, produced from exact approval or authority testimony, that a particular downstream act is granted (`authority-scope.md:44`, Authorization boundary correction 001: "authority subject, granting source, recipient or responsible boundary, authorized act or movement, scope, purpose, temporal standing, constraints, evidence, occurrence, negative authority, and Unknowns").

The active Book treats the *coordinate* as general, not conditional. `constitutional-kinds-and-artifact-standing.md:37` (01.Standing.E.1) lists "authority" directly among the coordinates for which applicability is determined for *every proposed input to every exact act*: "purpose, scope and locality, authority, participants and roles, consumer context." The same clause repeats it at `:47` among coordinates every independently recoverable claim retains: "each retaining its own subject, responsible act, purpose, evidence basis, result, consumer, scope, **authority**, provenance, Unknowns, conflicts, negative authority, and failure or absence-of-occurrence reason." Neither passage scopes "authority" to acts that consume a separate Authorization artifact.

A lens's own chapter confirms the coordinate is present even where no Authorization is consumed: `lenses-views-and-roads.md:10` — a lens's "assertion is about what the supplied sources expose under its declared method, **warranted by source identity and faithful transformation**." That is an authority/warrant answer of "ordinary, source-fidelity-bounded" rather than "absent." Per PR 2223's own §2.8 discipline (which this correction retains), a coordinate whose answer is "not applicable" or "ordinary" is still a required coordinate that was addressed — it is not the same as the branch being uninstantiated.

**Corrected rule:**
```text
authority coordinate: required for every responsibility (value may be "ordinary," "not applicable," bounded negative authority, or Unknown)
separate Authorization standing: conditional, required only where the exact road establishes one
```

Audited against PR 2223's five original examples: the frontier-establishment and BOGE-establishment examples already implicitly carried an authority answer ("not instantiated" and "BOGE-local," respectively) that PR 2223 folded into "not instantiated" rather than recording as an answered coordinate; the corrected re-projections in §4 make this explicit.

### Correction 3 — occurrence is not preservation testimony

PR 2223 marked "occurrence and preservation" as one conditional branch, reasoning that "a lens or applicability determination may never reach recorded occurrence." This conflates three distinct things:

- **Act occurrence**: the responsible constitutional occurrence at which the act happens and its exact result is produced, preserved, established, or found absent.
- **Occurrence evidence**: the evidence supporting the bounded occurrence claim.
- **Preservation testimony**: a separately retained artifact or testimony reporting or preserving that occurrence for later recovery.

`acts-and-act-artifacts.md:10` defines occurrence as intrinsic to an act happening at all — "An act is a bounded responsibility at which something constitutionally occurs" — and explicitly denies that read-only or unrecorded form negates it: "Read-only and non-mutating boundaries prove side-effect limits; they do not by themselves prove that no constitutional occurrence happened. A read-only selector can still perform a bounded selection act, while its artifact does not warrant the asserted occurrence unless produced through the responsible validation boundary." The occurrence is not conditional on being recorded; what is conditional is whether a separate artifact *warrants* that the occurrence happened. PR 2223's own §3 chapter map already recorded the matching distinction for a different chapter without applying it to its own topology — `05.Testimony.B` (quoted in that map): "A measurement occurrence may exist transiently without ever becoming a recorded measurement; that does not make the measurement unreal... Measurement occurrence != recorded measurement, and recording measurement testimony != producing measurement testimony." `recording-and-knowledge-extraction.md`'s own Important Distinctions list carries the same pattern nine further times (act occurrence != recording occurrence; record exists != recorded standing lawfully established; and others).

**Corrected rule:**
```text
act occurrence: required wherever a result or result standing is established (may be a negative/absence-of-occurrence finding)
occurrence evidence: required only where an occurrence claim is actually made
preservation testimony: conditional, independent of both
```

### Correction 4 — owner is not subject

PR 2223's root topology (§4.1) listed a single combined branch, "responsible subject / owner," despite its own §2.3 prose stating the distinction correctly ("Responsibility != the responsible subject") and its own §3 whole-Book map consistently using two separate table rows — `responsible subject:` and `material addressed:` — across all 23 chapters without exception. The summary artifact regressed behind the report's own detailed analysis.

```text
owner / responsible boundary: who bears and answers for the responsibility
subject or material addressed: what the responsibility concerns, consumes, evaluates, forms, preserves, establishes, or stops
```

The same entity may occupy both locally (e.g., a single lens producer both owns and is the boundary that exposes its own supplied sources), but identity between them must be established per instance, not assumed by the branch structure. This correction retains PR 2223's per-chapter map data (§3 of the original), which already kept the two separate, and corrects only the summary tree.

### Correction 5 — production was left implicit between act and result

PR 2223's root topology moved directly from "exact responsible act" to "result," with "occurrence and preservation" appended afterward as one further, conditional branch. This exposed the consumer/Uptake side of a responsibility explicitly (as its own branch, correctly marked independently owned) while leaving the producing side — the exact occurrence that turns an act into an established result — implicit.

`constitutional-kinds-and-artifact-standing.md:37-39` (01.Standing.E.1) is direct textual evidence that owner and producer are not automatically the same responsible party: "The owner may determine applicability within the same bounded occurrence, **or may validate and consume applicability standing established by an explicitly assigned responsible occurrence** for that exact downstream act... An explicit Book assignment therefore overrides the ordinary owner; delegation does not create a universal applicability service." PR 2223's own §3 map independently demonstrates the same pattern at scale without naming it structurally: `construction-and-establishment.md` chains nine separately named responsible occurrences (formation, comparison, identification, source recovery, meaning-relation warrant, applicability, admission, establishment — with BOGE establishment as the final producer of the claimed result), and `operator-ingress-common-grammar-prerequisite.md` chains twelve. In both, the *material addressed* by a downstream responsibility is very often exactly the *result* of a distinct upstream responsibility, and PR 2223 never named that chaining as a structural coordinate.

`testimony-and-established-fact.md:14,18` supplies the clearest textual ground for splitting "result" from "result standing," a further place where production was collapsed: "A Fact artifact does not prove its own Fact standing; constructor availability, schema validity, replay, view visibility, or ledger presence may preserve or reconstruct an assertion but do not by themselves show that the establishing boundary occurred." A result (the Fact artifact) and its standing (the Fact being constitutionally established) require separate producing/establishing occurrences.

**Corrected rule:** the road must expose both sides —
```text
input source or prior producer → material addressed → owner → act → producing occurrence → result → result-standing establishment (where claimed) → preservation testimony (where applicable) → consumer → consumer-local applicability/admission/Uptake/reliance (where applicable)
```
with the explicit non-collapse rules the correcting prompt supplied, all independently verified above: owner != producer automatically; producer != source; act != production occurrence automatically; produced result != established result standing; producer of a result != standing-establishment boundary automatically; production occurrence != preserved testimony of production; result existence != consumer Uptake; consumer != producer.

### Correction 6 (additional, beyond the five supplied) — authority and warrant remain collapsed in the corrected proposal itself

The corrected topology supplied by the correcting prompt still lists one combined branch, "authority / warrant," which repeats the exact conflation pattern Correction 4 fixes for owner/subject. `constitutional-kinds-and-artifact-standing.md:63` (01.Standing.F) states directly: "authority and warrant are neighboring but not equivalent." The Book uses "authority" for the permission/standing-source coordinate (who may act, under what grant, per `authority-scope.md`) and "warrant" as a broader synthesis term for sufficiency of support (`concordance.md`'s own entry: "warrant | Evidence and Knowledge | ... | bounded support, mechanism-fitness warrant"), closely associated with but not identical to the "evidence basis" branch already listed separately in the corrected topology. Rather than adding a third overlapping branch, the cleanest correction is to keep "authority" as its own required branch (per Correction 2) and drop "warrant" as a separate named branch, since the corrected topology already has "evidence basis" doing that work; "warrant," where it needs to be discussed at all, is the emergent conclusion that authority, evidence, and scope taken together are sufficient — not a fourth independent coordinate. This correction is applied in §3 below.

## 5. Reassessed disposition

**Disposition A — Responsibility is warranted as the active Book structural HEAD for presentation and traversal, while neighboring constitutional subjects retain independent ownership.**

This is not chosen merely because Responsibility fails to qualify as a constitutional superkind (which would default to B by elimination); it is chosen because the corrected evidence affirmatively supports it: every one of the 23 active chapters' Bounded resolutions already instantiates the same six required branches without exception (owner, subject, act, evidence, scope/purpose, result), the production side is now explicit rather than implicit (Correction 5), and two independent, already-merged audits (`examine_uptake_and_production_residue_audit_001.md`, `authorization_to_act_responsibility_audit_001.md`) both used exactly this decomposition, by name, as their controlling method with clean, actionable, repeatedly-verified results — not as a hypothetical convenience but as demonstrated, working practice across two separate investigations. Once "structural HEAD" is correctly bounded to presentation/traversal (Correction 1) rather than ownership, nothing in the active Book contradicts using it as the root, and the corrected topology in §3 shows it can hold eighteen branches, six required, without absorbing any independently owned neighbor's constitutional standing.

Disposition B is rejected as too weak given the corrected evidence: an "optional reading lens" undersells a pattern that is not optional in practice — every chapter already exhibits it, and no chapter contradicts it. Disposition C is rejected: no candidate root was found in this Book, or in PR 2223's whole-Book map, that is more general than Responsibility and could serve the same traversal purpose (Constitutional subject is a single label, not a branching structure; Act is explicitly a species of Responsibility per `acts-and-act-artifacts.md:10`, not its superior). Disposition D is rejected: the corrected topology in §3 and the five re-projections in §4 demonstrate the structure is recoverable, non-vacuous, and differentiates real chapters from one another (compare example 1's near-empty authority/occurrence branches against example 3's fully reflexive ones) rather than forcing every chapter into an identical shape.

## 7. Structural test standard

A first structural test must check the heading/branch structure itself, not merely that selected words appear in prose (the standard PR 2223's own proposed test fell short of). Once Amendment (§6) lands, the following become mechanically checkable against the Markdown structure of `constitutional-kinds-and-artifact-standing.md` alone, without prose parsing:

- **Responsibility structural root exists** — the new addressable-boundary heading (e.g. `01.Standing.G`) is present.
- **Required direct structural branches are present** — the six required branch labels (owner, subject, act, evidence basis, scope/purpose, result) appear as distinguishable sub-items under that heading.
