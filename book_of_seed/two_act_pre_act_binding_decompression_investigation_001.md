# Two-Act pre-Act binding decompression investigation 001

## Question

Current runtime records often place two exact Act positions in one
assignment-shaped occurrence:

```text
Applicability Act position
+
governed Act position
```

Does that material establish:

```text
one pre-Act binding carrying two Acts
```

or:

```text
two pre-Act bindings
joined by an exact subject-to-Act relation?
```

This investigation changes no Book, machine grammar, runtime, or test.
`Responsibility` remains disputed vocabulary. `Assignment` names the current
runtime construction only; it is not admitted here as replacement Book
grammar.

## Direct finding

Active grammar requires **two exact pre-Act bindings**.

```text
binding A
    subject = exact subject-to-governed-Act position
    Act = Applicability

binding B
    subject = the exact family-local work subjects
    Act = Measurement / Compare / determination
    rule = the exact family-local rule
```

The exact connection is already stated without a new generic relation:

```text
binding A's result
    = Applicability for exact subject-to-governed-Act position

that exact applicable position
    is required by Participation in binding B's Act occurrence
```

Current runtime does not record those two bindings separately. Five active
roads place both Act identities under one assignment identity and cite that
same recorded occurrence from both Acts. A sixth road places both Act
positions directly in the Applicability Act occurrence and records no earlier
assignment occurrence.

Therefore:

```text
two exact Act positions                         established
two distinct Act occurrences and results       established
one assignment-shaped storage occurrence       current runtime
one constitutional binding carrying two Acts   contradicted by active grammar
two separately addressable pre-Act bindings     not recorded by current runtime
```

The current assignment-shaped occurrence is a serialization compression. It
does not establish one lawful two-Act binding.

## 4. Subjects

The two Acts do not have the same subject.

### Applicability subject

Applicability concerns an exact position such as:

```text
source Assertion
    applicable_to
exact governed Measurement Act
```

or:

```text
ordered relation path input
    applicable_to
exact Compare Act
```

The Applicability result records the exact source/input reference, role, and
addressed governed Act identity.

### Governed-Act subject

The governed branch concerns the family work itself:

```text
all exact pair-position Assertion references carrying one addressed coordinate
```

or:

```text
two exact recorded Measurement results under one Compare rule
```

or:

```text
one exact ordered path and one exact recorded pair-finding result
```

The generated `assignment_subject_identity` does not express either subject's
material. It is an opaque identity beside the actual source references and
relations.

Thus one assignment subject identity cannot establish that Applicability and
the governed Act share one exact subject.

## 5. Rules

Each governed family carries its exact Measurement, determination, or Compare
rule. The runtime copies that family rule into its Applicability Act and
Applicability result.

This copying has a narrow reading:

```text
Applicability is concerning a subject position
for this exact governed Act under this exact family rule
```

It does not make Applicability and the governed Act the same Act.

The active Applicability grammar does not declare a separate generic rule
coordinate. The family-local code computes the exact status. Therefore this
investigation finds:

```text
governed family rule                      exact and carried
Applicability concerning that exact rule  carried by current runtime
second generic Applicability rule object  not established
```

No new rule is needed to distinguish the bindings. Their exact Acts and
subjects already differ.

## 6. Scope, Locality, and boundaries

The five assignment-record roads copy one Scope and Locality across the
Applicability and governed branches. Shared Scope is compatible with two
bindings concerning the same local work. It is not identity of those bindings.

The branch result identities remain distinct:

```text
Applicability result identity
!=
governed result identity
```

The standing boundary is also distinct from both results. It says where the
pre-Act material was current. It does not say that one Act absorbs another.

Therefore:

```text
same Scope / Locality / prior Standing boundary
!=
same pre-Act binding
```

It does not prove:

different result identities

The missing lineage is not Act lineage. It is **separate pre-Act binding

## 8. Applicability result and Participation are the exact connection

The exact relation between the branches is not inferred from append order.

The current material establishes it through coordinates:

```text
Applicability result
├── exact subject/input reference
├── exact role or subject-to-Act relation
├── exact governed Act identity
├── exact status
└── Scope / Locality where carried

governed Act occurrence
├── cites that exact Applicability result
└── records Participation for applicable inputs where implemented
```

The governed Act reader refuses an absent, changed, inapplicable, or
cross-assignment Applicability result.

The later append order is real runtime chronology:

```text
Applicability occurrence
-> Applicability result
-> governed Act occurrence
```

But the pre-Act binding distinction does not depend on calling the bindings
earlier and later. It depends on:

```text
Applicability is about the exact subject-to-governed-Act position
```

and:

```text
Participation in governed occurrence requires that exact applicable position
```

No generic assignment-to-assignment relation is established or needed by the
active grammar.

Participation is not uniformly recorded across these roads. The shared-position
and Compare roads carry exact Participation relation identities into the
governed Act. The addressed-determination and byte-position-pair roads do not
record an explicit Participation relation in their module-local Act material.
That absence prevents treating current runtime Participation as the uniform
identifier of the missing Applicability binding.

## 9. One concrete shared-position discrepancy

The shared-position Applicability Act currently carries two incompatible
top-level readings:

```text

    = Applicability Act identity

each input relation's addressed_act_identity
    = Measurement Act identity
```

Its Applicability result then sets top-level `addressed_act_identity` to the
Measurement Act identity.

The readers enforce these generated surfaces exactly, but exact enforcement
does not reconcile the two subjects. The input relations and result agree that
the Applicability concerns the Measurement Act position. The Applicability Act
surface's top-level coordinate disagrees.

This is an existing runtime discrepancy. It is not repaired here because this
commit is findings only.
