## 1. Standing reads an assignment already established

The active Standing source requires:

```text
Standing
whose subject is one exact Responsibility assignment
↓
this Seed reads that exact Responsibility
```

The assignment retains its own:

```text
subject
responsible boundary
exact Responsibility
occurrence
Evidence
Authority
Scope
limits
Standing
```

This is a downstream read.  It cannot be inverted into prospective discovery:

```text
Standing B exists
↓
every unrecorded Responsibility assignment at B is readable
```

An unrecorded assignment is not the subject of assignment Standing.  Reading
recorded assignment occurrences therefore cannot establish the first
assignment occurrence at B.

The active source also refuses:

```text
Standing whose subject is an assignment
!= Responsibility

Standing
!= occurrence that performs the Responsibility
```

Thus Standing supplies neither a hidden declaration iterator nor an Act that
instantiates every Responsibility named elsewhere in the Book.


choose, create, enumerate, or assign the Responsibility that owns that Act.
