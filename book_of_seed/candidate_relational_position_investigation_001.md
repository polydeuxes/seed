# Candidate relational position investigation 001

## Boundary

This investigation begins after `6bdfc781`, which removed the empty
`candidate_result_occurrences` current-coordinate slot. It changes no Book
clause, Witness Grammar coordinate, runtime occurrence, or test.

The question is whether the Candidate physiology declared by `01.Source.E`,
`01.Source.E.1`, and `04.Compare.C` carries any distinction beyond:

```text
exact existing subject
+ exact rule
+ exact Scope and Locality
+ exact subject-to-Act position
+ Applicability, Admission where required, and Participation
```

The test does not assume that Candidate is absent because no live producer
exists. It subtracts the Candidate Act, Yield, result, identity, and role from
the declared coordinates and asks what exact distinction becomes
inexpressible.

## DNA/RNA adversarial control

An observer may call each tRNA sampling a ribosomal position a candidate tRNA.
The local discrimination does not require a separately produced Candidate
certificate. Its exact subjects are the tRNA, codon, and ribosomal position;
their exact relations make the interaction addressable.

Biology also supplies the opposing control. An encounter or binding Act may
produce an independently real intermediate complex. If a later Act addresses
that new complex rather than the earlier subjects and relations, the
intermediate result is irreducible even when an observer calls it a candidate.

The discriminator is therefore not the word `Candidate`:

```text
existing subject in an exact relation to a later Act
!=
an Act occurrence that produces a new exact subject or condition
```

DNA/RNA is external adversarial testimony only. The repository must establish
which side of that distinction Seed carries.
