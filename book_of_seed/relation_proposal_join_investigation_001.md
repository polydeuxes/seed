# `relation proposal`, `candidate relation`, `relation claim`: investigation 001

Findings only. No runtime or Book amendment.

## Executive

**All three are hapax or near-hapax terms.** `relation proposal` and
`candidate relation` occur once each, in different chapters. `relation
testimony` occurs once. Nothing relates any of them to any other.

**The ladder at `01.Kinds:28` is a distinctness statement, not a road.** Its
sentence ends "**remain distinct**". Reading its four items as stages is the
same error as reading `02.Acts:13`'s non-equivalence chain as a pipeline, and
this session has now made that error twice.

**There is more missing than one join.** `01.Kinds:28`'s requirement attaches
to "a relation claim **or standing**". Two of the four rungs are standing by
name. **`candidate relation` and `relation testimony` are related to nothing** —
not to each other, not to `relation claim`, not to `relation standing`.

**Only one relation-producing transition is established with an owner**, and it
is not on the assumed road. `05.Testimony:27`: a bounded **comparison** may
produce bounded relation standing inside the comparison boundary. Its inputs are
"independently preserved testimonies or findings" — not proposals, not
candidates.

**`#2381`'s bridge does assume an unwarranted identity**, and curator's
reservation is also correct: resolving that identity would still not settle
occupancy. Both joins are real and ordered.

## 6. Reconciling `#2381` and `#2382`

**[inference] `#2381`'s bridge does assume an unwarranted identity.** It would
let "a responsible relation-proposal occurrence" place content into "the
relation assertion". That coordinate is required by `01.Kinds:28`, which governs
relation claims and standings. A relation proposal is not established to be
either. So the bridge presumes §5's missing edge.

**[inference] Curator's reservation is also correct, and the two are
independent.** Establishing that a relation proposal is a candidate relation
claim would establish that its dimensions must be *preserved or explicitly
unresolved*. It would not establish *what may satisfy* the relation-assertion
dimension. "Must have a lawful answer or an Unknown" and "an exact
source-derived Representation is a lawful answer" are different propositions.

```text
JOIN 1  relation proposal → relation claim / candidate relation
        prerequisite; §5's missing edge

JOIN 2  exact source-derived Representation → may occupy relation assertion
        downstream; #2381's bridge; unaffected by Join 1
```

**[inference]** `#2382` was right that Join 1 precedes. `#2381` was right that
Join 2 exists. Neither established the other, and Join 1 does not imply Join 2.

**Occupancy is not decided here**, because resolving the three terms does not
settle it.

## 9. Correction: `translation` is Book wording, not a recovered act

**[operator correction]** The sections above originally named "a bounded
translation" as the owner of a transition. That treats a word the Book contains
as the name of an established Act, responsibility, or producer. It is not one.

**[active law]** `translat*` occurs 34 times across six chapters and is **never
defined**. No clause states what a translation is, names a translation
occurrence, or names a translation boundary. All five capitalised occurrences
are sentence-initial, so the capital is orthographic.

**[inference]** The word drags an ordinary-language bundle behind it — source
language, target language, semantic equivalence, meaning preservation, a
translator — none of which any clause supplies. Importing that bundle because
the Book contains the word is the same error as reading `learn` in operator
material as establishing acquisition machinery.

**Discipline.** Use `translation` only to quote or locate existing Book
wording. Do not use it as the name of an act, responsibility, producer, or
transition until it is recovered. The safe form of §4's first row is:

```text
01.External:15 states that a relation proposal may be formed at a
boundary it words as "a bounded translation". What responsible
distinction that wording names remains unrecovered.
```

This does not weaken the report's findings. The transition is still the only
established production of a relation proposal; what is withdrawn is the claim
to know whose act it is.

## What this does not establish

direction. Absence is not distinctness.

**That the ladder is not a road.** §3 establishes that active law does not state
it is one. A road may exist and be unstated.

**That `#2381`'s bridge is wrong.** §6 finds it presumes a prerequisite, which
is a different finding.

**That Compare is the intended producer.** §4 records it as the one established
production of relation standing. Nothing says it is the only one, or the one
this road wants.

**That the chronology explains intent.** §7 establishes when the terms entered
and under what stated subjects. Why they were never related is not recoverable.

**That `translation` names nothing.** §9 establishes that active law does not
define it and that this report should not have used it as an owner. Whether it
names a real responsibility is unrecovered, not denied.

**Independence.** `#2382` was written by this session; §6's reading of it is
self-review. `#2381` is not.
