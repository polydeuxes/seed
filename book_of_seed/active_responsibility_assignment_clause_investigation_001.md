# Active Responsibility-assignment clause investigation 001

## Question

At one exact responsible boundary `B`, what does each active Book clause
actually require before a Responsibility assignment to this Seed may be
recorded?

This investigation surveys the active clauses.  It does not construct one
Book-wide Responsibility population, a traversal Responsibility, or a
scheduler.  It does not use Applicability to discover Responsibility:

```text
Responsibility assignment
!= Applicability
```

Conditional connective language is expanded locally below.  `if`, `when`,
`where`, `unless`, `before`, and `after` do not name one constitutional
relation.

## Finding shared by every implemented assignment road

The runtime records many exact Responsibility-assignment occurrences.  No
active runtime road records the complete positive Standing physiology for one
of those assignments:

```text
assignment occurrence                         recorded on many roads

assignment Standing responsible boundary      not recorded
assignment Standing occurrence                not recorded
assignment-Standing occurrence Evidence       not recorded
exact positive assignment Standing            not recorded
```

A scalar coordinate named `standing`, a bounded replay carrying an assignment
event, and the assignment event itself do not fill these positions.

Therefore the classifications below deliberately distinguish:

```text
exact assignment occurrence recorded
!=
positive Standing established for that assignment
```

This is load-bearing because current Standing carrying an exact assignment is
required before this Seed may constitutionally read that Responsibility for
the later Act.

## Conditional connective disposition

The assignment clauses use ordinary connectives for different jobs:

```text
where / when     locate required coordinates at B
before / after   distinguish occurrence order
unless           state a default Responsibility assignment and its revision
if               preserve ordinary unresolved explanatory language
only             limit a local claim
```

These readings do not establish one conditional relation, causation,
Applicability, Authority, or sufficient trigger.  In particular:

```text
required coordinate present
!= complete assignment established

required coordinate absent
→ the exact assignment is not established under that clause
```

The active admission whitelist retains no claim that these words are one
Seed-native relation.  Their active Book occurrences remain cleanup debt for a
later decomposition.

## Result

The inventory does not expose one missing Book-wide traversal road.  It exposes
several local states:

```text
complete local subject exhaustion through B
    01.Source.D position Measurement
    01.Source.D exact-byte Measurement
    01.Source.E.1 Candidate source populations
    04.Compare.B subject read
    06.Locality.C exact-one reference

one explicitly addressed subject only
    01.Source.D.2
    01.Source.E
    01.Source.G
    04.Compare.A
    05.Recording.D
    06.Locality.B
    06.Locality.D

assignment clause or occurrence absent
    Witness material acquisition
    Representation emission
    boundary-failure preservation
```

Across every implemented assignment road, positive assignment Standing remains
absent.  Across several roads, exact Authority, Seed-native subject
establishment, clause-local subject exhaustion, or a clause coordinate is also
absent.

Therefore the current exact frontier is not:

```text
discover all Responsibilities
```

It is:

```text
for each exact active assignment clause:
preserve its own subject and required coordinates at B
record its own assignment occurrence where established
establish positive Standing for that assignment
refuse at its first missing coordinate
```

Durable recording order remains distinct from constitutional dependency.
Complete exhaustion of one clause's local subjects remains distinct from a
Book-wide collective.
