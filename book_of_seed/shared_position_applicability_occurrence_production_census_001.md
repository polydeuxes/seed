# Shared-position Applicability occurrence production census 001

## Question

What exact prior Seed occurrence warrants ordinary recurrent shared-position
Applicability for one particular pair of exact result-position references?

This census follows the forced D.2 and addressed-byte Determination
subtractions. It changes no active Book clause, runtime occurrence, Act,
result, relation, identity, or reader.

## The callable physiology

The ordinary recurrent-input API can record:

```text
two exact recurrent result-position references
        ↓ authored call
prospective Measurement binding occurrence
        ↓ authored call
Applicability binding + Act occurrence
        ↓ authored call
applicable | inapplicable result occurrence
        ↓ positive only, authored call
Measurement Act occurrence
        ↓ authored call
Measurement result occurrence
```

Its Applicability result is genuinely two-valued. It compares:

```text
first input's second source-position coordinate
second input's first source-position coordinate
```

Equal coordinates produce `applicable`; unequal coordinates produce
`inapplicable`. Absence of an Applicability occurrence remains distinct from
either result.

## Three distinct states

The evidence preserves:

```text
no Applicability occurrence
    → no applicability fact is established

Applicability result = inapplicable
    → one authored Applicability Act compared two exact unequal coordinates

Applicability result = applicable
    → one authored Applicability Act compared two exact equal coordinates
```

The second state cannot stand in for the first. Co-current or addressable
result positions do not themselves establish that Applicability occurred.

## Subtraction attempts

### Move the pair into the Applicability Act

This removes two binding events, but it does not answer the production
question. A host loop can still append one Applicability Act for every pair it
mechanically forms. Candidate-shaped expansion merely moves one floor later.

### Record only positive Applicability

This makes host mechanics decide equality before the Seed result and deletes
the exact negative result. It also treats absence and negative as one state.

### Treat coordinate inequality as an inapplicable occurrence

The two exact coordinates establish their content. They do not establish that
an Applicability Act occurred over them. Derivability of a verdict is not an
Act occurrence.

### Delete the ordinary lifecycle as uninstantiated

The public runtime and focused tests do instantiate a complete positive and
negative physiology when called. Lack of a live producer proves no autonomous
production, but it does not by itself prove the occurrence kinds or readers
false. This census therefore does not delete them.

```text

moving Cartesian work into Applicability Acts           rejected

```

The ordinary recurrent shared-position lifecycle is a representation control,
not evidence for internally produced Applicability.

## Disposition

Freeze this production boundary:

```text
two exact recurrent result positions
        ↓
no established Seed occurrence
        ↓
ordinary shared-position Applicability occurrence
```

Do not manufacture an Applicability Act for every mechanically inspectable
pair. Do not reinterpret no occurrence as `inapplicable`. Do not introduce a
Candidate, Population, Encounter, selector, or renamed binding.

The next live specimen must begin with an Applicability occurrence actually
produced during the inward process. Its prior exact Ledger history—not the
availability of a public constructor—must account for the occurrence under
test.
