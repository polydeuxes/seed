## Method

The investigation tests:

Current Book material is tested first. Witness grammar, runtime, tests, and

## 1. Assertion recursion without Assertion monism

The Book root carries this sequence:

```text
Standing
-> Responsibility
-> exact Act
-> Act occurrence
-> result
-> Standing
```

It also states that the later Standing has the exact result or relation under
Yield Evidence as its subject.

The sequence is recursively Assertion-shaped at its Standing boundaries:

- current Standing may have a Responsibility assignment Assertion as its
  subject;
- a support relation is another Assertion;
- a result may be an Assertion;
- a relation result may be an Assertion;
- later Standing may have that exact result or relation as its subject.

This supports:

```text
Assertion
-> supported through an exact support-relation Assertion
-> participates in one exact Act occurrence
-> result Assertion
-> later Standing whose subject is that result Assertion
```

It does not support:

```text
everything in the physiology is an Assertion
```

The following remain distinct:

- Responsibility assignment Assertion;
- exact Act;
- Act occurrence;
- Evidence;
- Authority;
- Scope;
- Locality relation;
- exact reference;
- result Assertion;
- support relation Assertion;
- Standing whose subject is an Assertion.

Therefore `Assertions all the way up` is a useful statement about recursive
Standing subjects. It cannot collapse every coordinate beneath those subjects
into one Assertion kind.

Disposition: **recursive Assertion Standing established; universal Assertion
monism not established**.

## 2. A stronger Assertion may carry several lower subjects


carry exact references to several lower Assertions, its comparison rule,

result is one bounded distinction Assertion.

The exact-byte Measurement supplies another relevant topology. One result in a
recording Locality may preserve exact references to source occurrences from
named source Localities. The references do not move their sources into the
result Locality.

The higher coherent subject can therefore be one Assertion:

```text

        |

        v

        | exact reference

```

This topology establishes none of:

- A local to L;
- B local to L;
- A applicable to the responsible Act;
- B applicable to the responsible Act;
- A related to B;
- equality;
- represented meaning.

Disposition: **lawful topology under an exact responsible occurrence; current
occurrence not yet identified**.

## Result

The constitutional stack is recursively Assertion-shaped upward at its
Standing boundaries. One higher Assertion may have a bounded subject carrying
exact references to lower Assertions. That Assertion can occupy one Locality
without moving its referenced sources.

Therefore the coherent calculator subject need not be a special population,
container, Locality union, or all-Assertion Measurement result. It may be one
ordinary bounded Assertion Q, followed by later result Assertions under their
own responsible occurrences.

The current calculator witness does not establish Q.

The claim branch and calculator branch have exact independent source and
provenance paths. Neither addresses the other. Their first co-consideration is
developer test construction. No one ledger occurrence carries both exact
references as its bounded subject, and no exact translation, question-forming
Act, support relation, Authority, or Standing fills that gap.

The revised critical vacancy is:

```text
What responsible occurrence establishes
one exact Assertion whose bounded subject
addresses both calculator branches?
```

Until that occurrence is recovered:

- A and B are not one Seed-side inquiry subject;
- no movement is required merely to carry references;
- no later Responsibility becomes applicable;
- no Compare or represented relation is established;
- equality and arithmetic meaning remain Unknown.
