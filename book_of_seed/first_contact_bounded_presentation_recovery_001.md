# First-contact bounded presentation recovery 001

## Scope

This report records what `03-goals-and-advancement/operator-ingress-common-grammar-prerequisite.md` testifies about the first-contact sequence, and what it does not. It authorizes no build. It amends no active Book law, runtime, or tests.

It answers two questions that a preceding thread relocated repeatedly instead of resolving, corrects two errors by this report's author, and locates one remaining gap precisely.

Throughout, the Book is treated as **Evidence**: currently the single witness, testimonial in standing. "The witness testifies to X" is not "X is established." No claim here is corroborated by an implementation or an occurrence.

## Finding 1 — a third comparison, whose actor the witness names

A preceding thread distinguished two comparisons:

```text
bounded testimony comparison
  — multiple independently preserved testimonies or findings
    compared with one another

candidate equivalence compared with applicable relation grammar
  — one proposed relation tested against grammar Seed already has
```

The witness testifies to a third, at `:100`:

> A **responsible Seed comparison act** compares captured operator-response material R with the exact response coordinates of emitted presentation C and establishes either an exact match to token T or that no coordinate matches. A **distinct responsible Seed identification act** consumes the comparison finding, exact presentation identity C, and the established T-to-A relation only where that relation is applicable to the exact identification act, purpose, scope, and occurrence.

For this comparison the witness names more than for either other:

```text
actor kind    Seed
input 1       captured operator-response material R
input 2       the exact response coordinates of emitted presentation C
result        exact match to token T, or no coordinate matches
```

It also separates the following act rather than absorbing it. `:167` states `comparison != identification`, and `:100` adds that one occurrence may perform both while "shared occurrence does not collapse their evidence, claims, or results."

```text
comparison says        R matches coordinate T

identification says    T identifies alternative A,
                       therefore A corresponds to R within C
```

Neither establishes what the operator intended. `:168` — `A corresponds to response R within C != operator intended A`.

**What this does not supply.** `Seed` names the actor kind, not the exact responsible boundary. This is materially stronger testimony than the candidate comparison receives, where `candidate_versus_grammar_comparison_recovery_001.md:127` records the owner as Unresolved and enumerates five things it is not. It is not owner recovery.

`:17`, inside the guarded first-contact topology:

That reframes the relocation pattern. It was not only a discipline failure. Each name was placed at a position where this sequence testifies the candidate came from elsewhere.

## Finding 3 — the unbidden ingress is not the response material and is not compared in this sequence

The sequence at `:16-27` runs:

```text
:16   preserved operator ingress whose richer grammar is not presently shared
:17   -> developer-supplied potential-goal candidate and meaning
:20   -> a responsible occurrence forms presented alternatives ... and presents
         them as a bounded closed-choice representation
:25   one exact bounded presentation C is formed with exact token T ...
:26   C is emitted, and the operator produces response material R after emission
:27   -> Seed captures R and preserves its response-occurrence lineage
```

The ingress arrives **first** and occasions the presentation. Within this guarded first-contact exchange it is not an input to the comparison at `:100`: there is no presentation preceding it against whose coordinates it could be compared, and only R — material produced after an emission — enters that comparison.

This is sequence-local. The witness does not testify that no later Responsibility ever compares the preserved ingress, and `:142-144` leaves later interpretation open. Absence within this exchange is not universal nonoccurrence.

The ingress is not discarded, and it is not interpreted. `:142-144`:

> Newly available material may support later upstream translation or interpretation concerning the same preserved ingress under the material's preserved limits. The same ingress occurrence and provenance remain recoverable; acquisition does not replace that ingress with a new operator occurrence.

So it is preserved, uninterpreted, and available to later interpretation that Seed cannot presently perform.

```text
preserved ingress    != response material R
occasions a presentation
                     != compared against one
preserved            != interpreted
uninterpreted        != discarded

not compared in this first-contact exchange
                     != never compared by any later Responsibility
```

A design that treats every operator message as a response to a presentation erases this asymmetry.

## Finding 4 — closed choice follows from bounded grammar orientation

This report's author wrote that Seed uses closed-choice representation *because* free-form translation is unavailable at first contact — that it is a bootstrap substitute prose competency would retire. The witness contradicts that at four points:

```text
:88    "It may remain useful whenever exact bounded selection is preferable
        to free-form testimony; availability of prose competency does not
        retire it ... closed-choice representation is not limited to any
        universal initial-use grammar."

:195   prose competency available != enum obsolete

:154   closed-choice grammar != free-form prose grammar
:155   closed-choice representation != limited to one initial-use purpose

:92    presented alternatives carry roles including presentation-navigation
       treatment — "`more options` or `show details` identifies a
       presentation-navigation alternative, not a goal"
```

The correct relation is the reverse of what was written:

```text
bounded grammar orientation
-> closed-choice representation, at first contact and after

prose rendering        != unbounded grammar
richer shared grammar  != release from boundedness
```

`:94` says the operator-ingress case is "**one ordinary use** of bounded potential-goal presentation when richer free-form translation is not presently available." The scoping clause was read as the mechanism's cause; the sentence names one use of a general thing.

This bears on `:9` — "BOGE does not interpret unresolved raw prose" — which is therefore not a first-contact-only restriction. `:72-82`'s alternative route requires "translated **or otherwise bounded** source material": the requirement is that the material be bounded. Translation is one way to reach that. It does not release a later comparison from boundedness once prose competency exists.

Consequently a design in which Seed compares accumulated raw operator messages with one another, to build relation standing before meaning is warranted, is not a permitted variant of either route.

## Finding 5 — four distinct Authority and Warrant relations

The exchange carries four relations that this report's ingredients imply but did not name. Collapsing any two of them is what produces the jump from an operator-origin message straight to an operator goal.

```text
Source Authority
  establishes that the operator produced this ingress or response
  material, at the preserved occurrence and in the preserved
  relation to C

response-comparison Authority
  bounds R against C's exact response coordinates and permits only
  match to T or no coordinate match

meaning Warrant
  supports what the identified represented source expresses

Operator Authority
  may give the warranted proposition operator-origin force —
  goal, Selection, or Authorization standing — where separately
  established
```

`:104` withholds each of the later ones by name from the earlier:

> The independently established external fact is only that the operator produced R after C was emitted. It does not establish that the operator intended R to mean T, understood the binding or A, intended A, assented to a label or represented-source meaning, authorized downstream treatment, or performed a constitutional selection act. Operator selection occurrence remains **Unknown** unless separately established; no negative operator-intent claim follows.

```text
operator is the source        != Source Authority establishes intent
operator produced R           != operator selected A
R corresponds to A within C   != operator intended A          (:168)
A expresses proposition P     != P carries Operator Authority
                                 automatically
```

**On the response comparison's Authority.** It does not borrow Authority from bounded testimony comparison, and it does not depend on a universal comparison-Authority object. Its exact Authority is instantiated by the completed exchange:

```text
C contains exact alternatives and response coordinates
C is emitted
R is produced after emission and captured with lineage
comparison is restricted to R against C's coordinates
the permitted result is match to T or no coordinate match
```

```text
comparison Authority not globally named
!= the response comparison lacks Authority

exact response-comparison Authority
= the bounds carried by this instantiated exchange
```

### A withdrawn fill — the response comparison's owner

An earlier version of this report proposed that because the comparison consumes C's exact response coordinates, the boundary holding C's preserved representation is the only structurally available owner candidate. That is withdrawn.

The premise holds; the conclusion does not follow:

```text
the occurrence requires C's exact coordinates as input   holds

availability of C != ownership of the comparison
```

A boundary may form C, emit C, preserve C, recover C, consume C, or expose C. None of those relations makes it the comparison's owner. The proposal derived a **necessary condition** on the performer and presented it as **identifying** the performer.

Its stated falsification test was also circular. It asked for a boundary that cannot recover C's preserved representation yet holds C's exact response coordinates — but holding the coordinates is itself a form of access, so the test can never fail. It examined input availability, not ownership.

This is the seventh instance in the surrounding thread of relocating an unresolved owner rather than closing it, and the first authored by this report. The previous six named a new act; this one named an existing boundary by a structural relation. The pattern is the same.

The honest standing:

```text
actor kind                Seed
required input            C's exact response coordinates
exact responsible boundary  unresolved
```

Removing this fill does not weaken Finding 5. Exact Authority may be instantiated by the bounded exchange while exact ownership remains unresolved:

```text
Authority resolved locally != owner identity resolved
```

**What distinguishes a legitimate fill.** The continuation fill above is retained and this one is not, on a difference worth stating: the continuation fill records a testified link (bounded comparison is a named input to Learning establishment) together with two named open questions, and can fail if either is answered against it. This one offered no condition under which it could fail.

## Supported findings

- The witness testifies to a comparison of captured operator-response material against an emitted presentation's exact response coordinates, naming Seed as actor kind, both inputs, and the result form, and separating the following identification act.
- For first contact, the potential-goal candidate and its meaning are testified as developer-supplied, attributed, and bounded. No Seed candidate-forming act is required at that position.
- The unbidden operator ingress occasions the first presentation, is not the response material R, and is not compared within this first-contact exchange. It remains preserved, uninterpreted, and available to later interpretation.
- Closed-choice representation follows from bounded grammar orientation and is not retired by prose competency.
- Bounded comparison is a testified input to Learning establishment, which produces revised retained understanding.
- Source Authority, response-comparison Authority, meaning Warrant, and Operator Authority are four distinct relations. The witness withholds intent, understanding, assent, selection, and authorization from Source Authority by name.
- The response comparison's exact Authority is instantiated by the completed bounded exchange rather than borrowed or held universally.

## Unsupported findings

- That the exact responsible boundary of the response comparison is recovered. Only the actor kind is named, and access to C's coordinates does not identify it.
- That locally instantiated Authority resolves owner identity.
- That universal candidate-production or translation ownership is resolved. `:69` records them Unknown; `:84` assigns neither.
- That any of this is corroborated by implementation or occurrence Evidence.
- That the candidate-versus-relation-grammar comparison's owner is resolved by anything here.
- That Learning establishment's "bounded comparison" reaches the response comparison.
- That a design comparing accumulated raw operator messages with one another is testified by either route. Its conflict with this witness is not a prohibition on construction supported by other Evidence.
