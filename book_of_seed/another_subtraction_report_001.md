# `another` subtraction report 001

## Question

Does active `another` establish an exact nonidentity coordinate, or does it
point backward to coordinates whose nonidentity is established elsewhere?

## Active surface

The active Book uses were in `01.Source.G`:

```text
another operator boundary occurrence
another exact subject-to-Act binding
```

The first supplied-material occurrence and binding had just been named. Both
uses pointed to a later instance of the same coordinate forms.

One Seed-authored Movement determination also said:

```text
exact addressed result content in another Locality
```

Its event material already carries exact source and destination Locality
coordinates.

## Subtraction

The Source clause now states the exact distinction directly:

```text
separate operator boundary occurrences
are exact subjects in separate exact subject-to-Act binding coordinates
```

The Movement determination names `destination Locality` rather than pointing
backward from its exact source Locality.

The durable source coordinates remain:

```text
operator boundary occurrence
subject-to-Act binding coordinates
material result occurrence
source occurrence references
Locality
```

No occurrence identity, result identity, source reference, binding coordinate,
order refusal, or Locality coordinate changes.

## Why `another` adds nothing

The later operator boundary occurrence is nonidentical because it has its own
exact occurrence identity. Its binding coordinates differ because the exact
source subject differs. `another` neither establishes that identity nor names
the coordinate domain in which nonidentity is claimed.

It also does not establish:

```text
different material content
later occurrence order
separate real-world source
preferred source
```

Those claims require their own exact coordinates.

## Finding

```text
additional exact occurrence          established by its occurrence identity
different binding coordinates        established by its exact subject
backward narrative pointer           `another`
independent coordinate               absent
```

## Disposition

Remove `another` from active Book admission.

Repeated equal material may arrive through separate exact source occurrences.
The exact occurrence and binding coordinates establish that fact without a
generic backward pointer.
