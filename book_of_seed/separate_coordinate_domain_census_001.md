# `separate` coordinate-domain census 001

## Question

Does active `separate` merely mean unequal, or does it establish non-collapse
of exact occurrence and address coordinates?

## Active domains

Active law uses `separate` for four exact distinctions:

```text
separate containing result occurrences and addressed coordinates
separate operator and Witness source coordinates and occurrences
separate Locality Act and relation occurrences
separate Emission success and failure Act occurrences and results
```

The source-coordinate clause now also uses the word for separate operator
boundary occurrences and their separate binding coordinates.

## Equal content control

`01.Current.D.1` establishes:

```text
same content
+ same result-local position
+ separate containing result occurrences
→ separate exact addressed coordinates
```

Thus `separate` does not mean different content. It says the exact parent
occurrence or resulting address has not collapsed into the other one.

## Source control

Operator-supplied and Witness-supplied material can have equal bytes. Their
source boundary, source Act, source occurrence references, and material result
occurrences remain independently addressable.

Likewise, repeated operator material can have equal bytes while separate
operator boundary occurrences produce separate binding coordinates.

## Locality control

`06.Locality.A` previously compressed its identity claim into the phrase
`separate relation occurrence`. The clause now identifies the two exact
occurrence domains:

```text
exact Act occurrence
exact relation occurrence
```

They are separate. On instantiated Locality roads, the recorded result
occurrence is the relation occurrence; this wording does not add a second
relation wrapper beside that result.

## Emission control

Active Emission law distinguishes the accepted write occurrence/result from a
failure Act occurrence with its own result. Equal material, destination, or
reported count cannot collapse those Act and result occurrences.

## Relation to `another`

```text
another       backward pointer to an additional instance
separate      explicit non-collapse of named coordinate domains
```

The first adds no coordinate and leaves active admission. The second changes
an exact identity claim and survives.

## Finding

```text
content inequality                       not implied
occurrence nonidentity                   established where named
address nonidentity                      established where named
independent source coordinates           established
Act occurrence != relation occurrence    established in Locality
success occurrence != failure occurrence established in Emission
`separate` coordinate distinction         survives
```

## Disposition

Keep `separate` in active Book admission.

The exact nouns surrounding it are load-bearing. The word never establishes
generic inequality or separation without a named occurrence, address, source,
Act, result, or binding-coordinate domain.
