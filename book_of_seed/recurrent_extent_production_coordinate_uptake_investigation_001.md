# Recurrent extent production-coordinate uptake investigation 001

## Question

Exact recurrence of a bounded internal same-content/difference result preserves
the exact occurrences producing that result. Do those production references
already address a further current Responsibility that can measure corresponding
carried coordinates across the production population?

This investigation changes no active Book chapter, witness grammar, runtime, or
test. `Extent`, `role`, `surface`, and `production` are report rendering only.
They introduce no active Seed grammar.

## Finding

The proposed third rung has an exact subject shape, but no live producer or
consumer currently records it.

Internal recurrence and cross-production recurrence are separate work:

```text
within each bounded occurrence
↓
complete same-content/difference findings
↓
exact recurrence of that complete result
```

does not establish:

```text
corresponding carried material across the producing occurrences recurs
```

The internal result deliberately omits material values. Its recurrence result
must preserve the exact occurrence/result references that produced the same
internal surface. Those references make a finite cross-production population
addressable.

For every exact coordinate role in the common bounded extent:

```text
exact recurrence result
├── producing occurrence P0 ── role r ── material M0
├── producing occurrence P1 ── role r ── material M1
├── producing occurrence P2 ── role r ── material M2
└── ...
```

The nearest operation is literal declared Measurement by exact role and exact
carried material. Pairwise Compare is not required merely to establish repeated
literal material.

```text
exact role r
+ exact producing occurrence references
+ exact carried material at r in each occurrence
+ exact completeness boundary
↓
exact material groups
↓
exact occurrence references for each group
↓
count
↓
recurrence where established
```

No developer chooses an interesting occurrence, coordinate role, or material
value. The internal recurrence result fixes the production population. The
common extent fixes the complete role population. The referenced occurrence
and role fix each material subject.

## Why internal recurrence does not discover literal material

Consider observer rendering of three bounded occurrences:

```text
2 + 2 =
3 + 3 =
4 + 4 =
```

Each occurrence has the same internal equality surface:

```text
role 0 same-content role 2
all other distinct role pairs difference
```

Recurrence of that surface establishes repetition of the exact internal
same-content/difference population. It does not establish that `+` or `=`
recurs, because neither literal value is part of that surface.

The exact production references carried by recurrence expose separate role
populations:

```text
role 0: 2, 3, 4
role 1: +, +, +
role 2: 2, 3, 4
role 3: =, =, =
```

Literal Measurement under each exact role yields:

```text
role 0: three exact material groups, count 1 each
role 1: exact material "+", count 3, recurrence
role 2: three exact material groups, count 1 each
role 3: exact material "=", count 3, recurrence
```

Thus `=` becomes a recurrent exact result without being embedded in the
internal equality rule and without the developer selecting role 3.

A control population preserves the distinction:

```text
2 + 2 =
3 - 3 #
4 x 4 ?
```

The internal same-content/difference surface still recurs. The corresponding
role material does not:

```text
role 1: +, -, x
role 3: =, #, ?
```

No literal recurrence is established at either role. Internal recurrence keeps
the branch addressable; it does not manufacture agreement in carried values.

## Why pairwise Compare is not the first consumer

Generic `04.Compare` can compare exact subjects under an exact rule. It remains
available where a later Responsibility genuinely needs a relation between two
exact production coordinates.

It is not required for literal grouping. For `m` producing occurrences at an
exact role, pairwise Compare creates:

```text
m * (m - 1) / 2
```

subjects before recording that several occurrences carry the same exact value.
Declared Measurement can instead group the `m` exact carried values directly
and preserve every exact supporting occurrence reference.

```text
repetition of exact material
!=
pairwise comparison of every occurrence carrying that material
```

Compare remains separate work. Measurement of exact material does not establish
occurrence identity, semantic equivalence, significance, translation, or a
relation between the producing occurrences.

## What automatic uptake would and would not mean

This exact producer/consumer join can eventually demonstrate a rung of the
elevator:

```text
internal recurrence result Yielded
↓
corresponding role-coordinate Measurement becomes addressable
↓
Applicability and responsible occurrence
↓
literal recurrence result Yielded
```

That does not yet establish a general automatic-uptake mechanism. The current
declared-Measurement registry is explicitly wired and source-specific. Adding
this exact declaration later could prove the small join without inventing a
central chooser or scanning possible subject constructions.

The producer need only preserve exact references. The consumer owns resolving
its exact bounded subject population. Neither side needs to know that the
result belongs to a deeper rung.

The next implementation should not begin with a general elevator. It should
first establish the smallest exact recurrent-extent producer and connect that
result to a literal role-coordinate Measurement under current `01.Source.D`
and `01.Source.D.1`. The two synthetic populations above can then prove both
recurrence and nonrecurrence without developer-selected roles or values.
