# `one` coordinate-domain census 001

## Question

Does active law use `one` for one exact cardinality distinction, or does the
same spelling also introduce ordinary singular prose?

This census changes no Book clause, admission, machine grammar, runtime
occurrence, Act, result, relation, identity, or reader.

## Surface

Active chapters contain 76 case-insensitive occurrences of `one` across nine
chapters.

No unqualified global singleton claim was found. Every use attaches `one` to
an exact coordinate domain, occurrence kind, relation role, boundary, result,
or stated alternative.

## Exact cardinality controls

Several uses have runtime falsifiers and cannot be replaced by an indefinite
article without weakening the claim.

### One result per Act

Active readers refuse a second result occurrence for the same exact Act
occurrence:

```text
one Act occurrence
→ zero results before result recording
→ exactly one result afterward
→ second result refused
```

Here `one` participates in exact cardinality.

### One result position

Result-position addressing distinguishes:

```text
one parent result occurrence
+ one exact local position
```

Equal local integers under separate parent results remain separate exact
addresses. `one` is qualified by its containing result; it does not assert a
global singleton position.

### One added occurrence

Recorded-pair Compare preserves:

```text
later ordered source references
=
earlier ordered source references
+ one added exact occurrence
```

Zero additions or two additions would establish another source relation. The
cardinality is load-bearing.

### One alternative result coordinate

Applicability result law states:

```text
one of applicable or inapplicable
```

This means exactly one coordinate from the stated alternatives. Absence is
not either answer, and the two answers cannot coexist for one exact result.

## Singular binder

Many uses introduce one arbitrary exact instance before naming its
coordinates:

```text
one exact relation
one exact subject-to-Act binding
one exact Act occurrence
one Locality relation
one exact source boundary
```

These clauses do not assert that only one such instance exists in the Ledger.
They scope the coordinate law to one exact addressed instance:

```text
for an exact instance X
→ these are X's exact coordinates
```

The singular binder and global cardinality are therefore distinct. Repeated
instances remain lawful and separately addressable.

Some binder uses may be expressible with `a`, `an`, or `the exact` without
loss. That is a prose subtraction question, not evidence against cardinality
`one`.

## Coordinate role

Several uses say that an exact thing is one coordinate or one subject of a
larger exact structure:

```text
reported count is one coordinate of failure
material is one exact subject of a binding
boundary is one coordinate of a Measurement position
```

These do not claim that the larger structure has only one coordinate or
subject. They assign one exact role to the addressed thing. Some may reduce to
`a coordinate` or `an exact subject`; they remain under individual pressure.

## Identity collapse control

Emission states that its Act occurrence, attempt, and accepted write are one
occurrence and one result. Here `one` refuses three separately occurring
wrappers. Replacing it with an indefinite article would lose the asserted
identity collapse.

## Order control

The Locality continuation clause says its carried prior boundary is one exact
earlier boundary under the same Locality occurrence order.

It does not say:

```text
the only earlier boundary
the immediately previous boundary
```

`one` binds the exact carried role. `earlier` supplies order. Neither word
supplies adjacency.

## Finding

```text
exact cardinality one                   survives
one exact alternative                   survives
one added occurrence                    survives
identity collapse into one occurrence   survives
singular binder                         distinct from global singleton
coordinate-role prose                   residual subtraction candidate
unqualified global singleton            absent
```

## Disposition

Keep `one` in active Book admission.

Do not globally replace it. Pressure only singular-binder and coordinate-role
uses against `a`, `an`, or direct exact-coordinate wording. Preserve every use
whose falsifier distinguishes zero, one, and multiple occurrences or whose
claim identifies several named surfaces as one occurrence.
