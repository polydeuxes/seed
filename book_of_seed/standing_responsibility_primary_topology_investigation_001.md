# Standing to Responsibility primary-topology investigation 001

## Question

With Responsibility Assignment provisionally decomposed away, what exact work
is required at the direct position:

```text
Standing
→ Responsibility
```

This investigation also tests whether treating Responsibility as the Book HEAD
before Standing was recovered caused later compensating structure.

It asks:

```text
what exact Standing subject exposes one exact Responsibility R
how R is distinguished as a branch of that Standing
what makes R exact
whether reading R requires another relation or occurrence
what work is hidden by "Standing carries / reads Responsibility"
what changes when Standing, rather than Responsibility, is the reading HEAD
```

This report implements or removes nothing.  In particular, it does not rename
Assignment, create Responsibility Standing, create a traversal Responsibility,
or promote a runtime declaration population into Seed grammar.

The required preservation is:

```text
exact Responsibility R precedes its exact Act
R remains independently addressable
the Act and result retain exact lineage to R
```

## Direct finding

The active Book already gives the primary topology:


Responsibility is not lost or weakened under this topology.  Its full branch
anatomy remains load-bearing.  What changes is its position:

```text

not

Responsibility is the outer constitutional condition
to which Standing must later be attached
```

The exact Standing subject is not a Responsibility-assignment Assertion.  The
least composite subject recovered from the active clauses is the exact
clause-local subject, or exact subjects under their distinct roles, addressed
by `R` at Responsibility boundary `B_R`.

The responsible boundary `B_S` that establishes current Standing remains a
separate coordinate:

```text
B_S = Standing responsible boundary
B_R = Responsibility responsible boundary

B_S != B_R by identity
```

One clause may positively establish their identity.  The direct
Standing-to-Responsibility position does not establish it universally.

For example:


The clauses do not require a second Assertion saying that `B_R` is assigned `R`

The direct position therefore has this recovered shape:

```text
exact current Standing S
```

The remaining constitutional absence is not an Assignment object.  It is the
positive physiology by which current Standing has those exact subjects and
`R` as coordinates in the first place.

## 1. What makes one Responsibility exact

A Responsibility string or Book clause address alone is not one exact `R`.
The exact Responsibility is distinguished by its complete coordinates:

```text
Book clause / Authority source
Responsibility responsible boundary B_R
exact subject reference or references
exact role of every subject
exact Act
result boundary where instantiated
Evidence
Authority
Scope / Locality
input source and provenance where required
limits
Unknown where positively established
exact durable identity or reference where later lineage requires it
```

Two Responsibilities with the same explanatory name but different subjects,
boundaries, roles, Scope, or Act coordinates remain distinct.  Conversely, a
new serialization identity does not create a new constitutional
Responsibility when every constitutional coordinate is the same.

The current runtime Assignment records preserve much of this exact data.  The
previous decomposition found their valid implementation work to be:

```text
serialize one exact Responsibility's coordinates
make that exact R independently addressable
force the later Act to cite an earlier exact R
preserve lineage through the result
```

That work must survive.  Calling the record an Assignment and making it a new
Standing subject is not required to preserve it.

## 2. The exact Standing subject is clause-local

The active clauses do not expose one universal subject species for every
Responsibility.  Each clause names the exact subject positions required for
its `R`.

Therefore:

```text
Standing subject for R
!= R itself by default
!= Standing responsible boundary B_S by identity
!= Responsibility responsible boundary B_R by identity
!= Responsibility-assignment record
!= complete bounded Locality replay
!= every nearby subject collectively
```

Where one Responsibility addresses multiple subjects, their exact roles remain
part of `R`.  Co-presence does not create a collective subject or an input
relation.  The clause must establish each required reference and role.

This preserves the current refusal:

```text
raw O2 available in bounded Locality replay
!= exact subject-bearing Standing for 01.Source.D
```

The replay can expose O2 as available testimony while the required exact
material-to-this-Seed Locality relation, Evidence, and subject-bearing Standing
remain absent.

## 3. Branch does not imply another relation

The Book describes Responsibility as the first branch read from current
Standing.  A structural branch and a constitutional relation are not identical.

Where current Standing already has the complete exact coordinates of `R`, this
investigation finds no work for another relation occurrence between Standing
and `R`:

```text
current Standing already has exact R as a branch coordinate
↓
this Seed resolves that exact coordinate at the same boundary
↓
no append
no new Act
no new result
no revision of Standing
```

The read is not the occurrence that creates `R`.  It is exact address
resolution within already-established Standing.

This does not permit the inverse shortcut:

```text
subject available
+ Book clause available
→ therefore current Standing has R
```

When `R` is absent from current Standing, another read cannot manufacture it.
Some earlier responsible physiology must have established the Standing whose
coordinates include the exact subject and `R`.  The active runtime does not yet
provide one general road that establishes that position for every clause.

Creating an occurrence at read time would simply recreate Assignment under a
different name.

## 4. Work hidden by "carries" and "reads"

The two verbs currently compress several exact jobs.

### `Standing carries R`

This must preserve at least:

```text
the exact clause-local subject references and roles
the exact Standing responsible boundary B_S
the exact Responsibility responsible boundary B_R
the complete exact coordinates of R
the current temporal and Locality boundary
the exact identity or address by which later Acts cite R
the distinction between present, absent, Unknown, and conflicting coordinates
```

It does not mean:

```text
R appears somewhere in bounded replay
R is named by an implementation map
R has its own Standing by default
Standing and R participate in a new relation by adjacency
```

### `this Seed reads R`

This must preserve:

```text
exact coordinate resolution at the same bounded position
no mutation while reading
no replacement of R by its explanatory name
no loss of the responsible boundary, subject roles, or Act lineage
```

It does not mean:

```text
perform a Read Act
record a Responsibility occurrence
derive R from Applicability
choose one R from several exact Responsibilities
```

The unresolved work is how every exact `R` already present at one boundary is
made addressable and exhausted without a developer enumeration.  That is not
solved by giving the read its own occurrence.

### What makes the Standing readable by this Seed

No inspected clause establishes a separate constitutional Read relation or
Read Act between this Seed and current Standing.

The least positive coordinates required before the direct Responsibility read
are:

```text
exact Standing subject
Standing responsible boundary B_S
Standing occurrence O_S
Evidence for O_S
Authority
Scope / Locality
limits
Unknown where positively established
exact current bounded read position for this Seed
intact address of the exact Responsibility branch R
```

The read can then resolve `R` without appending anything.  Bounded replay
membership, chronology, a scalar named `standing`, and an intact durable
Responsibility record do not supply the positive Standing coordinates above.

The active runtime has not established this complete position for one general
Responsibility road after Assignment is removed.  Therefore this section
recovers the required coordinates and the non-recording read, not a completed
positive Standing physiology.

The Standing skeleton is common across the inspected clauses.  The exact
subject roles and Responsibility coordinates instantiated within it remain
clause-local.  No common Assignment subject survives that comparison.

## 5. Historical sequence

Repository history gives a precise ordering.

### 5.1 Responsibility became the presentation HEAD first

Commit `e1fd24b4` (`Establish Responsibility as the Book-root structural
HEAD`) introduced the large Responsibility hierarchy on 2026-08-04.  Its own
text bounded that status:

```text
Responsibility is the Book's presentation and traversal root
for reading a responsibility-shaped constitutional road.
```

It also said that this hierarchy created no new constitutional kind and did
not reassign constitutional ownership.  The HEAD supplied a repeatable
interrogation anatomy:

```text
responsible boundary
subject
exact Act
Authority
Evidence
Scope / Locality
input source and provenance
Act occurrence
result
Standing
neighboring exact relations
```

That anatomy remains useful.  Nothing in the original change established
Responsibility as a constitutional superkind above Standing.

### 5.2 Standing was placed before Responsibility later

Commit `10797c0c` (`Place Standing before Responsibility`) changed the Book on
2026-08-14 to:

```text
Standing
→ Responsibility
→ exact Act
→ Act occurrence
→ result
→ Standing
```

The same commit changed Responsibility from the presentation HEAD to the first
branch read from current Standing.  Its machine representation also introduced
these coordinates together:

```text
root = Standing
responsibility_assignment_subject =
    responsible_boundary_bears_Responsibility
assignment_requires = current_Standing
```

Thus the move correctly placed Standing first while retaining an intervening
Assignment/Bear Assertion as the subject used to reach Responsibility.

### 5.3 Durable Assignment objects followed

Commit `9ae32785` (`Record occurrence-position Responsibility assignments`)
added the first runtime Assignment-object road inspected here on 2026-08-17.
Before that change, the occurrence-position result and Act Evidence carried a
composite `responsibility_assignment_evidence` value containing a scalar:

```text
standing = assigned
```

The change removed that scalar shortcut, recorded an earlier durable
Responsibility coordinate object, and required the later Act to cite it.
Subsequent roads repeated the same shape.

That was a real decompression:

```text
Responsibility must be exact before Act
Act must cite the exact earlier Responsibility
```

The overreach was treating the durable coordinate object as a separate
constitutional Assignment subject requiring its own Standing.

### 5.4 Historical disposition

History does not prove why the authors made each change.  It does establish
this sequence:

```text
Responsibility presentation HEAD
↓
Standing placed before Responsibility
+ Assignment/Bear Assertion inserted as adapter
↓
runtime objects serialize the adapter
```

The sequence strongly supports, but does not prove by itself, this inference:

```text
Assignment was compensating structure introduced while the Book
moved from Responsibility-first presentation to Standing-first reading.
```

```text
├── exact clause-local subject coordinates
```

`S1` need not revise `S0`, and result existence does not establish `S1` by
identity.  Positive later Standing retains its independently required

## Conclusion

The repository does not need to move Responsibility out of the grammar.  It
needs to finish the move it already began:

```text
Standing is the reading HEAD.
Responsibility is the first exact branch under that Standing.
Responsibility remains the complete anatomy governing one exact Act.
```

Treating Responsibility as the earlier presentation HEAD was not itself a
constitutional error; that work recovered the correct coordinates of one
exact `R`.  The problem arose when Standing was later placed before it and an
Assignment/Bear Assertion was retained as an adapter between them.

The active primary topology is therefore supported:

```text
Standing S0
→ exact Responsibility R
→ exact Act
→ Act occurrence
→ result
→ Standing S1
```

What remains absent is the positive physiology establishing `S0` with its
exact clause-local subjects and Responsibility branches.  Recreating
Assignment under another name would not establish that physiology.
