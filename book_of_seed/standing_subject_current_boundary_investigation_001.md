## Direct finding

The active grammar does not provide one semantic definition of Standing. It
does provide enough exact structural constraints to recover a smallest account:

```text
Standing for exact subject S
=
current Standing carries at least one exact Responsibility branch
whose subject is S
and whose exact responsible occurrence, Yield, Authority, Scope, limits,
and result satisfy the Standing requirements for S

the branch's exact result is one coordinate of that Standing for S
```

This is a structural account, not a new constitutional definition.

It establishes these separations:

```text
Standing for S
!= the result R
!= Standing for R as another subject
!= the EventLedger
!= append boundary B
!= one Locality-wide runtime projection
!= every preserved assertion about S
!= one universal Standing occurrence
```

The word `Standing` currently compresses at least four addressable surfaces:

```text
1. subject-relative Standing
   warranted coordinates for one exact subject through exact branches

2. current Standing
   the presently addressed topology/population of carried Responsibility
   branches at an exact boundary

3. relation Standing
   the additional exact requirements when the subject is a relation Assertion

4. operator Locality Standing
   one recomputable runtime projection over supported occurrences in one
   Locality through an exact occurrence address
```

These surfaces are related. They are not identical.

Most importantly:

```text
ordinary responsible result production
-> branch carries result as one Standing coordinate for branch subject
```

requires no generic movement-into-Standing Act in active law or runtime.

If another Responsibility instead makes that prior result its own exact
subject, that is another ordinary subject-relative branch. The current README
calls that case `Later Standing`, but history does not establish a distinct
positive physiology behind the name. `later` contributes chronology, not an
exact Standing coordinate, and `exact Standing Act` names no independently
recovered work.

The retained `Later Standing` scaffold is best classified as blacklist residue:
it originally sat between two explicit denials preventing result existence
from automatically establishing Standing for the result as a new subject.
Positive branch/result membership was recovered later, both denials were then
deleted, and the scaffold remained. Its later machine reprojection copied that
README surface; it did not recover an owner from a numbered clause.

No active generic revision, replacement, supersession, promotion, or Standing
movement relation is defined. Live Locality Standing accumulates validated
family coordinates and never removes an earlier branch/result coordinate.
History says a later responsible occurrence is required for revision, but the
active compact Book no longer states a generic revision physiology.

The append boundary result is exact:

```text
B addresses an immutable global EventLedger prefix

current Locality Standing through B
= one reader's projection of one Locality's supported exact occurrences
  inside that prefix

B != projection
```

The observer proves the distinction: appending a complete occurrence in a
second Locality changed the global append-prefix identity while the first
Locality's Standing projection and through-occurrence identity remained byte
for byte equal.

The number road should therefore no longer say merely:

```text
give Seed prior number-system Standing
```

Its exact intended premise is:

```text
at one exact current-Standing boundary,
exact Responsibility branches carry warranted origin and successor relation
coordinates for their exact subjects under the bounded experimental
Authority, Scope, limits, and Unknown
```

Which exact declaration Responsibility creates those branches remains vacant.

## 1. Subject-relative Standing

### 1.1 The active subject is explicit

`01.Standing.A` says:

```text
Standing for one exact subject
```

and requires:

```text
exact Responsibility
Act occurrence
Yield
Authority
Scope
limits
result for that subject
```

`01.Standing.A.1` then says:

```text
one exact Responsibility branch of current Standing
-> exact result is one coordinate of Standing
-> for the exact subject of that Responsibility
```

The subject of this Standing is therefore not inferred from the result's
serialization, content, type, or name. It is the exact subject already carried
by the Responsibility branch.

### 1.2 Result membership does not change the subject

Commit `100c161a` recovered the positive relation that the earlier Book lacked:

```text
the result is not promoted into Standing
the result is one coordinate of Standing already organized through its
Responsibility branch
```

Commit `6319c0bb` implemented the live witness. The Locality Standing reader
had already resolved the responsible Act evidence before admitting a yielded
result, but reduced the Responsibility ownership to a boolean/`None` value.
It now preserves beside the exact result:

```text
recorded Responsibility assignment occurrence
assignment identity
assignment subject identity
Book clause identity
result boundary identity
and any other exact coordinates the assignment reference carries
```

The result remains keyed by its own occurrence identity, but the exact subject
remains the branch's `assignment_subject_identity`.

Thus:

```text
Responsibility subject S
-> Act occurrence
-> Yield result R
-> R is a Standing coordinate concerning S

R is now carried
!=
Standing concerning R exists as another subject
```

### 1.3 Standing is not one generic relation in active grammar

Active relation grammar requires first subject, exact relation content, second
subject, and a responsible relation occurrence. The Standing clauses do not
give `Standing` that binary anatomy.

Nor do they name one universal Standing Act or Standing result. The machine
projection for `01.Standing.A` contains a subject, requirements, and result
name but no `Responsibility` or `exact_Act`. `01.Standing.A.1` only carries the
exact result.

The safest active classification is therefore:

```text
Standing for S is a subject-relative warranted condition/topology of exact
carried coordinates
```

not:

```text
Standing is one universal Act
Standing is one universal relation
Standing is one serialized object
```

`condition/topology` is report language. The Book has not admitted either as a
replacement constitutional noun.

## 2. Current Standing

### 2.1 Active topology

The active README says:

```text
current Standing
└── exact Responsibility
```

and says Responsibility and Standing are separate coordinates. Active family
clauses repeatedly place independently exact Responsibilities as branches of
current Standing.

The topology is therefore plural:

```text
current Standing
├── Responsibility R0 for subject S0 -> result X0
├── Responsibility R1 for subject S1 -> result X1
├── Responsibility R2 for subject S2 -> result X2
└── ...
```

One branch does not determine another's subject, Act, result, Applicability, or
completion.

### 2.2 Current is boundary-relative, not latest-truth selection

Nothing in the active compact Book defines `current` as:

```text
the latest assertion by chronology
one selected truth
one replacement for every earlier result
one global mutable State object
```

The live reader likewise does not select one latest result per subject. It
accumulates validated occurrences in independent family populations, plus
known loss, Unknown, and conflicts.

The runtime's currentness is narrower:

```text
which supported coordinates are carried by this Locality projection
through this exact occurrence boundary?
```

That is boundary currentness, not semantic precedence.

### 2.3 Multiple Responsibilities may concern one subject

Active law contains no uniqueness constraint of the form:

```text
one subject -> exactly one Responsibility branch
```

Each Responsibility is exact by its own boundary and coordinates. Two branches
may therefore concern the same exact subject without becoming one branch or
without either replacing the other by identity.

The current runtime indexes assignments and exact results by occurrence
identity, not by subject identity, so it contains no generic last-write-wins
collision rule. The source-addressed observer run found ten independent
branches at one source Locality and one at its Measurement Locality. That run
had distinct assignment subject identities, so it witnesses plurality but not
same-subject coexistence directly.

Same-subject conflict, agreement, revision, or replacement would require its
own exact responsible relation/result. Availability under one projection does
not choose among the branches.

## 3. Relation Standing

`RelationStanding`. It specializes the requirements when the exact subject is

```text
relation Assertion subject
├── first subject
├── exact relation content
├── second subject
├── exact relation occurrence
├── Authority
├── Scope
├── Locality
├── limits
├── conflicts
```

This is ordinary subject-relative Standing with additional coordinates the
relation subject requires.

Therefore:

```text

!= relation Standing

endpoints are present
!= relation occurrence

relation occurrence/result is a coordinate for its branch subject
!= relation result automatically becomes another Standing subject
```

The bounded successor premise needs exact relation Assertion subjects and
their exact relation occurrences. It does not need a `NumberStanding` object.

## 4. `Later Standing` does not survive as an independent physiology

### 4.1 The active surface renders one ordinary re-subjecting case

The current README says:

```text
Standing for one result requires another exact Responsibility and Act
occurrence
```

and renders:

```text
current Standing
└── exact Responsibility
    ├── subject: prior result
    ├── exact Standing Act
    └── Standing Act occurrence -> Yield -> Standing for prior result
```

The machine surface projects this as:

```text
later_Standing.subject = prior_result
```

This is not a general rule that every new result advances all Standing. Its only
positive subject shape is the ordinary case where a prior result becomes the
exact subject of another Responsibility:

```text
prior result R
-> exact subject of Responsibility RR
-> ordinary 01.Standing.A/A.1 requirements for R
```

Nothing in that shape requires `later`, a separate `later_Standing` species, or
an Act whose substantive work is merely `Standing`.

### 4.2 The surface has no numbered Act owner

The numbered `01.Standing.A` and `A.1` clauses name no exact Standing Act. The
machine's top-level `later_Standing` surface has no `book_reference`; commit
`6f1d73c1` says it was reprojected from the README diagram.

No live generic `Standing Act` or `later Standing occurrence` was found. The
runtime's `advance_operator_locality_standing()` is an implementation reader,
not that Act: it records no occurrence, Participation, Yield, or result and is
fully recomputable from the ledger.

Consequently the README surface does not appoint the owner or rule of any work
performed by a `Standing Act`. If an exact substantive Responsibility later has
R as its subject, that Responsibility and its Act own their own work. Their
ordinary result can supply a Standing coordinate for R. A second generic Act
that transforms those already-exact coordinates into Standing would duplicate
the topology rather than add a distinction.

Commit `f956b6fd` compacted an older denial-heavy Standing chapter. The new

No blacklist is needed to prevent the second fact. It requires its own exact

The focused runtime test still bears the denial-shaped name

They positively verify that the result identity is not a Responsibility

### 4.4 `Later` does not mean replacement

No active clause says that Standing for prior result R:

```text
replaces Standing for earlier subject S
revises R's producing branch
removes the earlier result coordinate
becomes a new global current Standing snapshot
```

It is another exact branch for another exact subject unless an independently
established relation says otherwise.

The phrase `later Standing` is therefore not a safe constitutional coordinate.
Where chronology genuinely matters, use the exact occurrence/boundary address.
Where a prior result becomes a subject, expand the claim to:

```text
Standing for exact subject X carried by exact Responsibility branch RX
through exact boundary B
```

That is ordinary subject-relative Standing plus an exact boundary. It does not
warrant a special Standing species or generic Standing Act.

## 5. Does anything move into Standing?

### 5.1 No active generic movement exists

No active Book clause or live event family names a generic Act whose subject is
`move result into Standing`.

Active `03.Movement.A` concerns an exact subject moving from source coordinates
to destination coordinates. Assertion Locality movement has its own assignment,
Act, occurrence, Yield, and result. Its result is then carried by the same
ordinary result-membership physiology as other exact yielded results.

Thus:

```text
Movement result enters the current projection
because it is an exact yielded result of its branch
```

not:

```text
Movement is the universal Standing promotion Act
```

### 5.2 Runtime `advance` is projection maintenance

`advance_operator_locality_standing()`:

- accepts exact occurrence identities in ledger append order;
- validates them with their family readers;
- mutates/takes over the prior projection's accumulators;
- adds family-specific occurrence references;
- advances one through-occurrence address;
- returns a dictionary;
- records no event of its own.

Its docstring says the result is fully recomputable and not itself recorded.
`advance` therefore describes efficient projection maintenance. It is not a
constitutional Standing change occurrence.

### 5.3 The generic result gate is membership, not promotion

For supported event families, `_carries_exact_result()` verifies the exact
Yield chain and exact result bytes. The reader then places the result occurrence
in `exact_result_occurrences` and preserves the branch ownership when the
responsible Act evidence carries it.

This is the live implementation of `01.Standing.A.1`:

```text
responsible yielded result
-> coordinate carried beside its exact branch/subject ownership
```

It is not a generic Standing result producer.

One runtime discrepancy remains. A result whose Act evidence carries no
Responsibility assignment reference is still admitted as:

```python
exact_result_occurrences[result_identity] = None
```

The focused test explicitly pins this absence. Active `01.Standing.A` requires
an exact Responsibility for positive Standing. Such a membership can therefore
be read safely only as an available exact yielded result with no recoverable
Standing owner, not as complete positive Standing for an exact subject.

The runtime dictionary name currently compresses those two cases:

```text
owned Standing coordinate
unowned exact-result availability
```

This report does not repair that representation.

## 6. Append boundary B and Standing boundary

### 6.1 Append boundary is an immutable prefix identity

`EventLedgerBoundary` is the exact identity of one append prefix. It is derived
cryptographically from the ordered occurrence prefix. Its contract says:

```text
same ordered prefix -> same boundary
boundary carries no append position
only EventLedger reads it
```

`append_boundary_through_occurrence(E)` resolves the already-derived prefix
ending at exact occurrence E. It does not mint a new occurrence or Standing.

### 6.2 Runtime Standing uses an occurrence address to resolve B

`read_operator_locality_standing_through()` accepts:

```text
Locality identity L
through occurrence E
```

It resolves E to the immutable global append boundary B, lists only Locality L
occurrences inside B, and projects those occurrences through the live family
rules.

The returned mapping stores:

```text
locality_identity = L
through_event_occurrence_identity = E
```

not the `EventLedgerBoundary` value itself.

Therefore the exact topology is:

```text
global append occurrence E
-> resolves immutable append prefix B

B + Locality L + supported-family projection rules
-> Locality Standing projection through E
```

### 6.3 B does not select Responsibility material by itself

B says only which ledger prefix is addressable. It does not say:

```text
which Locality is the subject
which event families carry Standing coordinates
which Responsibility consumes which coordinates
which result is current by semantic precedence
which conflicts are resolved
```

The Locality reader selects its exact Locality and validates supported event
families. A later Responsibility then validates only the exact current-Standing
coordinates it consumes rather than authenticating every sibling branch.

Thus the campaign's prior shorthand expands to:

```text
B
-> exact immutable available occurrence prefix

Standing projection through B for L
-> exact currently carried branch/result coordinates visible at L

Responsibility rule R
-> exact subset/positions from that projection that R consumes
```

### 6.4 Observer result

## 8. Revision, replacement, coexistence, and persistence

### 8.1 Active law does not define a generic revision operation

The active compact Book contains no positive physiology for:

```text
revision
replacement
supersession
current selection
abandonment
confidence movement
expiry movement
```

Consequently no generic rule can presently decide that a later result removes
an earlier Standing coordinate.

### 8.2 Live projection is cumulative

The Locality reader's carried populations are keyed by exact occurrence or
result identity. `advance_operator_locality_standing()` takes over the prior
accumulators and appends validated coordinates. It has no generic branch that
deletes or replaces an earlier exact result because another result has the
same subject or later chronology.

Known loss, Unknown, and conflicts are accumulated as distinct strings. No one
Representation is designated current merely because it is later; the code
explicitly refuses that inference.

The live behavior is therefore:

```text
preserved coordinate persists in the projection
until an exact family rule says otherwise
```

No active family rule currently removes an earlier Responsibility/result
coordinate from Locality Standing.

### 8.3 History is testimony, not current law

Before the compact Book reordering, the active Standing chapter said:

```text
an Assertion with current Standing needs no other Act merely to persist
new/conflicting material does not automatically revise it
different exact asserted content is a different Assertion
further movement requires another applicable responsible occurrence
```

It also said a Compare result does not replace or discharge the subject it
compared, and that a subject ceasing to be carried was abandoned rather than
implicitly superseded.

Those distinctions explain the current cumulative runtime and are strong
recovery testimony. Most are absent from the compact active law after
`f956b6fd` and the subsequent denial cleanup. They must not be quoted as though
the current Book still states a generic revision physiology.

