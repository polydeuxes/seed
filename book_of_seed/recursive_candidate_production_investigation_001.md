## 1. The earlier report placed three later coordinates too early

The revised vacancy in the previous report required:

```text
exact bounded surface
+ eligible Assertion species
+ common source/provenance relation
+ exact formation rule
+ Responsibility and Authority
```

That bundle is too strong in three ways.

### 1.1 A common relation between A and B is not required first

A neutral candidate may carry:

```text
candidate C
|- exact reference to A
|- exact source coordinates of A
|- exact reference to B
|- exact source coordinates of B
|- exact production occurrence and rule
`- represented relation: Unknown
```

Standing that this exact candidate was produced does not establish the
represented relation between A and B.

Requiring A and B to share a source or provenance relation before C can be
formed would require the investigation's result as an input. What remains
required is narrower:

- A carries its own exact source coordinates;
- B carries its own exact source coordinates;
- the production Act carries exact references to both;
- the production rule assigns their exact roles in that occurrence;
- no source relation between A and B is inferred from those roles.

A same-source requirement may still be exact for one family. The ordered
Admission-result comparison requires the same exact source material. That is
its family-local input boundary, not a universal prerequisite for candidate
formation.

Disposition: **withdraw common source/provenance relation as a universal
prerequisite; preserve each source lineage independently**.

### 1.2 A future Participation species need not define the neutral candidates

A candidate can be produced while what it represents remains Unknown. The
candidate need not already be known as a lawful participant in the later Act
that may test or consume it.

That later Act must determine its own input Applicability and establish its
own Participation relation. Requiring those future relations before candidate
production would again place a later finding before the material it evaluates.

Disposition: **future Participation is not a prerequisite to neutral
candidate production**.

### 1.3 Candidate Standing is not relation Standing

Active relation grammar already separates:

```text
candidate relation material
source-labelled relation material
established relation Standing
```

Therefore a responsible production occurrence could establish only:

```text
this exact candidate Assertion was produced
under this exact rule and boundary
from these exact references
```

while carrying:

```text
what the represented relation establishes: Unknown
```

Disposition: **Candidate existence can be a bounded Standing subject without
Standing for its represented relation**.

## 2. Applicability does not enumerate an Act's source surface

`01.Standing.E.1` makes Applicability an exact input-to-Act determination. It
does not discover every subject that might be useful, generate proposed
inputs, or assign an Act.

The source surface must therefore already be bounded by the exact Act's
subject, Scope, rule, roles, source references, and completeness boundary.
Applicability then determines whether each proposed source may participate in
that exact Act.

This supports the operator's correction:

```text

!=

```

But it does not allow an Act to bypass Applicability. The production
onslaught is itself an exact Act. Its source Assertions are inputs to that Act
where they occupy input roles.

The exact decomposition therefore has two different Applicability boundaries:

```text
SOURCE SIDE OF PRODUCTION ACT F

bounded source surface S
        |
        v

        |
        v
Participation of applicable/admitted sources in F
        |
        v

        |

        |
        v

        |
        v

```

The second boundary must not be moved backward and used to define which
candidates F produces. The first boundary must not be omitted merely because
F is exhaustive.

Disposition: **Applicability does not enumerate either surface; every exact
Act still owes its own input Applicability before Participation**.

## 3. Admission has no one universal position beside Applicability

Active law keeps Admission, Applicability, and Participation distinct. It does
not impose one universal Admission/Applicability order across all Act

## 5. Shrinking must not mean erasure

The operator's `shrinking` description is accurate only for the exact boundary
of what may proceed into one later Act.

An inapplicable, conflicting, or Unknown proposed input may not participate in
that Act. Its prior Assertion and Standing are not erased. Its exclusion is
itself an exact finding bounded to that input-to-Act relation.

Likewise, material Admission retains all exact source material while refining
its coordinates. It does not shrink the source material count.

The safe distinction is:

```text

candidate allowed to participate in exact Act H
may be narrowed by H's exact Applicability and Admission
```

Therefore the recursive road must preserve both:

- every produced candidate Assertion and its lineage;
- every later applicability, admission, exclusion, or participation finding.

It must not rewrite the earlier candidate result as though excluded
candidates never existed.

Disposition: **later gates narrow participation for one exact Act, not the
recorded candidate Standing**.

## 6. Complete formation and Applicability cannot silently redefine each other

Suppose exact production rule G requires every ordered role formation over an
enumerated source surface S.

If one proposed source is inapplicable to production Act F, the implementation
cannot silently remove that source from S and still claim completion over the
original S. One of the following must be exact in G:

1. G defines its formation surface over the applicable source results;
2. G requires every enumerated source, so one inapplicable source prevents the
   complete result;
3. G permits an exact partial or exclusion result carrying what was not
   produced and why.

A resource limit has the same boundary. If G owes N candidate formations and
only M are recorded, M is not complete merely because the process stopped.

This is the Fidelity boundary of the onslaught:

```text
the rule defines what may not be omitted
Applicability cannot silently edit that debt
resource limits cannot silently edit that debt
```

Disposition: **completeness is measured against the exact rule and its exact
input boundary, not against whatever the implementation managed to produce**.

## Result

The stage-order correction is sustained with one necessary decomposition.

Applicability does not create the universe it evaluates. A neutral candidate
can be lawfully produced with exact source references and independent lineage
while its represented relation remains Unknown. A common relation between its
sources and its future Participation species are not prerequisites to that
production.

Candidate production is nevertheless an exact Act. Its own proposed sources
must be enumerated independently and then become Applicable, admitted where
required, and participating under that production Act's exact roles. The
newly produced candidates then face a separate later Applicability and
Participation boundary. Conflating those two levels either lets production
bypass Act physiology or makes future findings prerequisites to production.

`Survivor` is accurate testimony only when its exact pressure is named. In
material Admission, every source member survives as material while the
complete ordered evaluation refines which members remain undistinguished.
In Act-local Applicability, a candidate may survive as an Assertion while
being excluded from one Act. Neither use presently establishes one universal
Candidate kind.

The remaining calculator vacancy is therefore not why A+B deserves attention
and not a missing common provenance relation. It is the exact bounded source
surface, formation grammar, Responsibility, Authority, source participation
physiology, and completeness boundary under which this Seed would be unable
to omit any required neutral candidate—including A+B if and only if the rule
actually places A and B in those roles.
