## DNA/RNA adversarial control

A molecule's exact role in one reaction occurrence is real. Biology does not
require an additional Participation certificate beside the reaction and its
exact participants. Removing such a certificate would not remove the molecule,
its reaction-local role, or the reaction occurrence.

That is the relevant pressure here: preserve exact occurrence-local subjects
and roles; require a separate Participation relation only if a later occurrence
can address a distinction not already fixed by the binding and Act occurrence.
