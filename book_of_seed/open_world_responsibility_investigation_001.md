## 1. Two experiments had been combined in recollection

The nine English words and the nine blind frames came from different scripts,
different rules, and different commits.

### 1.1 The nine English words

Commit `cce08487` added
`scripts/observe_relation_words_from_usage.py`.

That script reads six developer-selected English books. It defines a
developer-written determiner list, matches a lower-case word immediately before
one of those determiners with another word before it, and retains words whose
count is greater than four in every selected book.

Its own prose correctly says that both narrowing rules are the script author's:

```text
developer supplies:
    English-only file selection
    determiner list
    three-word regular expression
    which regular-expression coordinate is read
    count greater than four
    occurrence in every selected book

source supplies:
    the exact matching material and counts
```

The resulting nine words were:

```text
of  in  to  by  for  with  on  from  under
```

They were not selected by a source-determined aperture. The source supplied the
occupants of an authored English position.

The later `compare_relation_words_against_physiology.py` step was weaker still
as discovery authority. It hard-codes dictionary senses and eighteen authored
readings against two earlier relation signatures. That script explicitly says
the readings are its author's judgments.

Therefore the nine words do not establish:

```text
external material chose connective grammar
external material chose a relation
external material chose a Responsibility
```

They remain ordinary-language testimony gathered through a declared heuristic.

### 1.2 The nine blind frames

Commit `05927819` added the genuinely open-world aperture experiment.

For each exact 300-line source window it:

```text
divides the window into four exact 75-line scopes
finds every byte value recurring in all four
uses each such value to delimit exact spans
groups middle spans by exact preceding and following spans
retains frames carrying more than one exact middle material
```

The developer still supplied the source windows, four-scope division,
byte-resolution observation, and recurrence threshold. The source supplied the
separator values, exact spans, occupants, and occurrence addresses.

The first nine sources produced:

```text

-> 9 recurrent substitution frames
```

Those nine findings were frames, not words. They included line-layout changes,
inserted annotations, and `the _ of`. Scalar remeasurement then showed that
some byte findings were properties of UTF-8 bytes rather than decoded scalars.

This is the valid anti-selection precedent:

```text

+ exact source material
-> source-selected exact subjects and findings
```

It does not establish that the source selected the observation rule or Act.

## 6. Disposition

The hypothesis separates into two claims.

### Established

```text
Once an exact Responsibility and rule are fixed,
open-world source pressure can establish all exact local subjects
without a developer choosing the occupants.
```

`01.Source.D.2` and the open-world observers both demonstrate this, at different
levels of constitutional completeness.

### Not established

```text
Recurring external structure can itself establish
which new Act or Responsibility Seed must bear.
```

The nine-word road cannot support that claim because its position and cutoff
were authored. The blind road cannot support it because its deepest controlled
finding remained source geometry and never established a Responsibility
condition.

The smallest exact next question is therefore not another outward material
observer. It is:

```text
Can active clause-local antecedents be expressed as exact machine coordinates
such that current Standing alone establishes which of them are satisfied?
```

Current machine grammar cannot answer that question target-blind. No Book or
runtime build is warranted by this report. Adding machine coordinates would
first require tracing one active clause end to end and proving that its
antecedent is exact without turning English prose into executable authority.

Until then:

```text
scalar road remains parked
W remains idle
no Responsibility selector is added
no emergent English word is promoted into Seed grammar
```
