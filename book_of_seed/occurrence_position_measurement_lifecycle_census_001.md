# Occurrence-position Measurement lifecycle census 001

## Question

After the Compare-Distinction Measurement lifecycle reached its smaller exact
shape, which remaining active Measurement family is the narrowest independent
specimen for lifecycle and copied-coordinate pressure?

This census compares occurrence-position Measurement with byte Measurement.
It does not generalize the prior subtraction result to either family.

## Active families

Both families occur on the inward operator road.

Byte Measurement is not one isolated lifecycle. Its current implementation
also contains byte-pair Measurement, result-position Locality Movement, two
pair-Measurement roads, old authored Applicability controls, and downstream
result-position readers. Pressure on that module can move several independent
subject and result domains together.

Occurrence-position Measurement is one smaller active family:

```text
exact occurrences in source Locality L
+ exact completeness boundary B
        ↓
occurrence-position Measurement binding E
        ↓
Measurement Act A
        ↓
Measurement result R
```

The finding is exact before `E`: it contains the ordered pairs of exact
occurrence identity and zero-based position for every occurrence in `L`
through `B`.

## Prospective identities

The binding mints all three family-local lifecycle identities before either
later occurrence exists:

```text
exact_act_identity
act_occurrence_identity
measurement_result_identity
```

`measurement_result_identity` is then copied into `R` as `result_identity`.
No prior independent occurrence addresses that value. The result's actual
Ledger occurrence identity is already the exact result address used by current
coordinates and later readers.

`act_occurrence_identity` is copied through `E`, `A`, and `R` while the actual
Act occurrence has `A.identity`, and `R` already addresses `A.identity`
directly.

`exact_act_identity` is copied through the binding reference and the later
payloads. Its current validation compares values authored by the same
lifecycle. No independent Act object supplies the expected opaque identity.

These are the same suspicious shapes seen in earlier family-specific
lifecycles, but analogy is not the falsifier.

## Binding occurrence

The binding occurrence remains independently stoppable in the current API,
but every durable fact it contains is either:

```text
an exact Measurement subject coordinate
a source Locality or completeness boundary
an exact Act coordinate
a prospective lifecycle identity
a copied Book clause label
```

No prior Act or result occurrence produces `E` as a distinct result. Its event
identity becomes load-bearing because `A` and `R` copy a reference to it.

The binding occurrence therefore remains under subtraction pressure, but only
after its prospective identities are independently tested. Removing the
wrapper first would move the identity questions at the same time.

## Coordinates that must not be collapsed

Occurrence-position Measurement has two distinctions that make it unlike the
recent Compare-Distinction specimen.

First, the source Locality may differ from the Locality in which the
Measurement Act occurs. The authored control measures Locality `a` and records
the Act in Locality `measurement`. `Event.locality_identity` on `A` therefore
cannot automatically replace the source Locality coordinate.

Second, the finding's completeness boundary is not universally identical to a
current-coordinate through-occurrence identity. One is the exact boundary of
the measured source population. The other is the recording Locality's current
cut and may be absent on an authored road.

Thus this census refuses:

```text
source Locality = Act Locality
completeness boundary = current recording cut
```

without separate falsifiers.

The ordered `result_positions` are also the actual Measurement findings. They
do not disappear merely because the reader can reconstruct an expected
finding from source occurrences. Durable result coordinates and copied
lifecycle metadata must be pressured separately.

## Byte Measurement control

One active byte Measurement currently has the same broad outer pattern:

```text
binding event with future identities
→ Act with copied binding coordinates
→ result with copied lifecycle and source coordinates
```

Its result additionally carries byte dimensions, occurrence-preservation
coordinates, byte-count result positions, and exact source material-result
references. The same module immediately feeds byte-pair Measurement and
result-position Movement.

This makes byte Measurement a valid later specimen but a worse first control.
Choosing occurrence-position Measurement is a boundedness decision, not a
claim that the byte lifecycle survives or fails.

## Pressure order

The independent falsifiers should proceed in this order:

```text
1. prospective Measurement-result identity
2. prospective family Act-occurrence identity
3. opaque exact Act identity
4. separate binding occurrence
5. copied Act/result coordinates, one at a time
6. narrated Act wording, independently from the Measurement Act
7. read breadth for exact cardinality and occurrence-order questions
```

Every pass must retain:

```text
exact source Locality
exact completeness boundary
every bounded occurrence and its ordered position
Measurement Act occurrence
Measurement result occurrence
Act-before-result order
one result per Act
current-coordinate replay
restart
changed source, boundary, position, and Locality refusal
separate occurrence identity under equal material
```

## First falsifier

The narrowest first target is `measurement_result_identity`.

Before `R` occurs, no physiological distinction requires its future opaque
identity. When `R` occurs, `R.identity` supplies an exact durable address.
Removing the prospective identity must preserve every result finding,
Act/result ordering, one-result cardinality, restart, replay, and later
occurrence reference.

The falsifier must not treat successful deletion in another Measurement family
as evidence. It must remove this identity only from occurrence-position
Measurement and make the current family pass independently.

## Disposition

```text
occurrence-position Measurement   next independent specimen
byte Measurement                  retained for later pressure
global Measurement rewrite        refused
first target                       prospective result identity
```

No runtime road, Book clause, occurrence, Act, result, relation, coordinate,
or admitted word changes in this census.
