## 1. Active Acquisition anatomy

Active `01.Source.H` says one material-acquisition Responsibility carries one
source boundary and one acquisition Act. The Act occurrence Yields one exact
material result carrying:

```text
source role
source boundary
provenance

Locality

```

Every one of those is already an admitted coordinate with its own job. The
clause names no Acquisition relation, finding, subject, or result beyond:

```text
responsible Act occurrence at a source boundary
-> Yield
-> exact material result
```

Active `01.Source.A` already states the same positive source-preservation job:
one responsible Act occurrence preserves supplied material and its exact
source coordinates at its boundary.

Thus the Book currently has:

```text
Source preservation
-> responsible Act occurrence
-> supplied material and source coordinates

Material acquisition
-> acquisition Act occurrence
-> exact material result and the same source coordinates
```

No separately stated distinction separates the second physiology from the
first plus ordinary Act, Yield, and result grammar.

## 2. Provenance of the active clauses

The operator-specific `01.Source.G` entered in `59bc4e4e` together with its

Current Book presence is therefore active law, but the chronology supplies no

## 3. The boundary-crossing physiology is real

Retiring the noun must not retire the occurrence.

The operator road records:

```text
exact Responsibility before touching the operator boundary
-> exact Act occurrence
-> boundary supplies exact material
-> exact Yield
-> exact material result
```

The Witness road likewise preserves its exact Act occurrence, Yield, material,
source boundary, provenance, Locality relation, and result identity.

The two roads are not equally complete. The operator road has a standalone
Responsibility assignment before its Act. The Witness road carries a
Responsibility string on its Act but records no preceding Responsibility
assignment occurrence. That is a separate active-law/runtime discrepancy; a
string cannot substitute for the exact Responsibility required by
`01.Standing.A` and `01.Source.H`. This investigation does not repair it while
the status of Acquisition itself is under audit.

Without that physiology, exact material would appear in current Standing with
no responsible source occurrence. The real distinction is the responsible
Act at an exact source boundary and its exact result, not a freestanding thing
called Acquisition.

Commit `6172c663` added a private exact-material reference so equal bytes can


Then it performs one declared exact-byte Measurement over current Standing.


The private pointer proves material equality for storage. It establishes no

## 5. One implementation overreach

The live console collects private exact-material references from earlier byte
Measurements and skips later Measurement work when a new source result has an
equal private reference.

That gate is implementation control flow. It records no responsible relation
from the later source occurrence to the earlier Measurement result. The
declared Measurement physiology itself gives the contrary exact reading: when
both source occurrences are inside its completeness boundary, both are exact
subjects and both affect the findings.

This investigation does not remove the gate because the larger question of
what makes that declared Measurement owed remains unresolved. It does establish
that the private pointer cannot be used as constitutional warrant for skipping
or merging a later source occurrence.

## 6. Runtime cat test

Current uses of Acquisition decompose into these jobs:

```text
acquire Act identity
-> exact Act identity

acquisition result identity
-> exact material result occurrence identity

source material acquisition occurrence reference
-> exact source material result occurrence reference
```

The identities and validation are real. The Acquisition label adds no
coordinate after those exact parts are carried.

The generic reader currently dispatches between operator and Witness roads by
durable event labels. That is an implementation aperture, not positive proof
of an Acquisition subject. Removing the word without first preserving each
source-specific exact read would destroy real physiology.

## 7. Disposition

The cat test finds:

```text
Acquisition as a separately constituted subject/relation/result   not recovered
responsible source-boundary Act occurrence                             required
exact material result                                                  required
distinct occurrence provenance                                        required
exact material equality                                                required
material continuity                                                    Unknown
```

`Acquisition` is therefore a compression over the source-boundary Act and its
ordinary exact coordinates. Its noun does not need to remain constitutional.
The physiology beneath it must remain.

The smallest warranted cleanup is Book-first:

```text
retain Source preservation
retain exact source-boundary Responsibilities and Act occurrences
retain exact Yield and material results
retain source role, boundary, provenance, Scope, Locality, loss, conflicts,
and Unknown

remove Acquisition only where it adds a second name for that physiology
```

Runtime deletion or renaming should follow the corrected active grammar, not
precede it. No continuity relation, global material object, source grouping,
or automatic result uptake is warranted here.
