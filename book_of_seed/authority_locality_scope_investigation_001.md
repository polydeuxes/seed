## 7. Locality is the valid part of the operator's original intuition

If the intended question was:

```text

and across which exact boundary?
```

then active Locality carries that distinction.

If the intended question was:

```text
what exact subjects, relations, Acts, occurrences,
results, Localities, and limits are addressed here?
```

then active Scope carries that distinction.

The remaining coordinates are already separate:


Locality should not be renamed Authority or made to carry all of these. That
would compress the same distinctions into another word.

The cleaner finding is:

```text

the exact bounded-address job belongs to Scope
Authority presently adds no separately enforced job
```
