## Candidate coordinates

### Compare binding

The binding owns the exact subjects and governed Act identity, but it exists
before Applicability. The same binding can receive an `applicable` or
`inapplicable` result. It cannot account for why one governed occurrence exists
and another is refused.

### Applicability result

It therefore constrains the occurrence but does not account for recording it.

### Current coordinates

exact boundary. They are a bounded reading, not another occurrence. Re-reading

### Operator material result

result. Those coordinates establish its lawfulness after it exists; they do not
establish a different prior occurrence that made it occur.
