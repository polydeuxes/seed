# Source Act name census 001

## Question

Does `Source` independently name the exact Act on the operator and Witness
material-source roads?

## Independent coordinates

Before the proposed name, both roads already had:

```text
exact source boundary subject
exact Act identity
exact Act occurrence identity
exact Act occurrence
exact result identity
exact material result occurrence
Locality
source occurrence references where supplied
```

Operator and Witness roads remain separate through their exact boundary,
material, occurrence, reference, and Locality coordinates.

## Falsifier

Changing the newly authored `Source` literal made the new readers fail only
because those readers copied and required that same literal. No earlier Book
coordinate, recorded occurrence, result, or later consumer testified that the
Act name was `Source`.

The implementation family names cannot supply that testimony. `source`
continues to name source boundaries, source occurrences, source references,
and source coordinate domains; none of those coordinates identifies the Act
word by itself.

Removing the `act = Source` and `exact_act = Source` payload copies preserves
the exact Act identity, Act occurrence identity, Act occurrence, subject
binding, result, and all source coordinates. Readers continue to refuse
changed Act identities and occurrence identities.

## Disposition

```text
source coordinates   exact coordinate family                 survive
exact Act identity   exact unnamed binding/occurrence coordinate survives
Source Act           no independent testimony                withdrawn
```

The exact Act coordinate remains unnamed until an independent distinction
supplies its Act word.
