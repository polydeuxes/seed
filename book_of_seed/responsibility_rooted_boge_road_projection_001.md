# Responsibility-Rooted BOGE Road Projection 001

## 1. Scope, method, and answer discipline

This report projects bounded operator goal establishment (BOGE) through the active Responsibility root. It is a constitutional-road recovery, not a Markdown-fidelity inquiry. The repository starting point was `d636ea9`, including PR 2228. This report changes no active Book clause.

The authority order is the active Book first, repository realization and its tests second as implementation testimony, prior reports third as attributed testimony, and history last as provenance testimony. The findings below come from direct reading of the active Book. In particular, the Responsibility root says that its headings expose coordinates without reassigning ownership, that conditional branches remain uninstantiated when the exact road does not establish them, and that an instantiated but unresolved coordinate is not **Unknown** without a responsible occurrence that establishes **Unknown** (`README.md:20-27`).

Classification vocabulary is limited to the six requested classes:

- **positively instantiated and resolved**
- **positively instantiated but conflicting**
- **positively instantiated and responsibly Unknown**
- **positively instantiated but unresolved**
- **not instantiated on this exact road**
- **owned by a neighboring responsibility**

The active Book establishes two alternative approaches upstream of the common BOGE seam. A closed-choice road and an admitted-interpretation road are distinct possible roads, not one compulsory sequence (`03-goals-and-advancement/construction-and-establishment.md:12`; `03-goals-and-advancement/operator-ingress-common-grammar-prerequisite.md:145-147`). Accordingly, this projection first states the common exact seam, then preserves the two upstream branches separately.

## 2. Constitutional subject and exact BOGE responsibility

The constitutional subject is the transition from a constructed goal-shaped representation to an established bounded operator goal (`03-goals-and-advancement/construction-and-establishment.md:3-7`). BOGE is the downstream operator-origin constitutional boundary, without a required universal realization (`03-goals-and-advancement/operator-ingress-common-grammar-prerequisite.md:3-9`). It remains operator-origin; the Book establishes no independent Seed-origin goal for it (`03-goals-and-advancement/operator-ingress-common-grammar-prerequisite.md:147-149`).

The exact responsibility recovered is:

```text
BOGE establishment boundary
  addresses the consumer-local admitted warranted meaning relation
  that exact source candidate G expresses bounded goal proposition M,
  relies on that admitted relation,
  consumes M as the proposition expressed by G under that standing,
  and establishes bounded operator goal standing.
```

This is established directly by `construction-and-establishment.md:10-12` and `operator-ingress-common-grammar-prerequisite.md:101-105,119-131`. BOGE does not establish the meaning relation again (`operator-ingress-common-grammar-prerequisite.md:103`).

## 4. Exact upstream responsibilities

### 4.1 Common seam immediately upstream of BOGE

The exact common responsibilities recovered are:

1. **meaning-relation warrant responsibility** — separately warrants that G expresses M;
2. **BOGE-local applicability responsibility** — examines whether that exact warranted relation may support BOGE's bounded use of M;
3. **consumer-local admission responsibility** — admits that exact relation to BOGE while preserving its identity, lineage, and limits;
4. **BOGE establishment responsibility** — relies on the admitted relation, consumes M as expressed by G, and establishes bounded operator goal standing.

These are separate because warrant does not establish applicability, applicability does not establish admission, admission does not establish the goal, and no one of them establishes act occurrence by identity (`01-grammar-and-standing/constitutional-kinds-and-artifact-standing.md:34,36-47`; `operator-ingress-common-grammar-prerequisite.md:147`).
