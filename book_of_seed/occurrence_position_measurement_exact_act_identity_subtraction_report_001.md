occurrence addresses. The binding reference addresses `E` and its exact

coordinates distinguish this physiology from other Acts.

Current-coordinate reconstruction required a family-specific coordinate
shape for this still-separate binding. Other binding families continue to
require their opaque identity until independently pressured.
