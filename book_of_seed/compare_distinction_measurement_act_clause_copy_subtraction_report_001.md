# Compare-Distinction Measurement Act clause-copy subtraction report 001

## Question

Must a Compare-Distinction Measurement Act occurrence repeat its Book clause
inside its material?

## Prior shape

The runtime carried Book ownership in event-kind declarations and repeated a
chapter label inside the Act material:

```text
Act event-kind declaration → 02.Acts.A
result event-kind declaration → 01.Source.D
Act material.book_clause_identity = 01.Source.D
```

The material label did not address another occurrence or vary independently
from the event family. It narrated which chapter described coordinates already
validated by the reader.

## Falsifier

Remove the material label while preserving:

```text
event-kind-to-Book declarations
exact Measurement Act
exact subject and through-occurrence boundary
Act occurrence
result occurrence
current-coordinate replay
restart
```

## Result

The active event declarations retain constitutional ownership for the Act and
result kinds. The copied payload label contributes no additional coordinate.

## Disposition

```text
Act event-kind declaration       retained
result event-kind declaration    retained
copied Act-material clause       withdrawn
```

No Book clause, Act, result, binding occurrence, Applicability, Yield, or
relation is added.
