# Relation-binder `one` subtraction report 001

## Question

Does `one` add cardinality when a clause scopes exact subjects through an Act,
relation, boundary, Locality, result position, binding, or Measurement?

## Result

Exact `a` and `an` binders preserve those containing coordinates:

```text
an exact Act
a Yield relation
an exact occurrence boundary
a Locality
an exact recorded result position
an exact subject-to-Act binding
a declared Measurement
```

The clauses remain per addressed instance. They do not assert a global count
of Acts, relations, boundaries, Localities, positions, bindings, or
Measurements.

## Preserved cardinality

The pass does not alter:

```text
one Act occurrence + one result occurrence negative Yield control
one Applicability Act per exact binding
one exact Act in counted binding-coordinate lists
one added source occurrence
one exact result from an Act
one of applicable or inapplicable
Emission identity collapse into one occurrence and one result
```

## Finding

Relation-binder `one` is ordinary singular scoping in this slice. Exact
cardinality and identity-collapse `one` remain active and independently
falsifiable.
