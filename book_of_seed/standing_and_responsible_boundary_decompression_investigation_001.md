## 3. The standing boundary is independently exact

`Standing` and `standing boundary` do not have the same disposition.

The live boundary is:

```text

```

for the exact empty prefix, or:

```text

```

for a nonempty prefix.

The ledger resolves that occurrence to its append boundary. The reader then
includes only Locality occurrences through that boundary. A missing,
corrupted, or differently localized occurrence is refused. Supplying an
earlier or later intact occurrence changes which exact coordinates are
current.

Pre-Act records use this boundary to prove that their subjects were already
carried before the new occurrence. Call-local carrying checks also require the
prior returned boundary and the new ledger append order to agree exactly.

Therefore:

```text

exact through-occurrence boundary     independently exact and load-bearing
```

If the Standing noun is later removed from Seed grammar, the boundary cannot
be removed with it. Its smallest current expression is the exact occurrence
through which the current coordinate projection is read.
