Book rename. Current Assignment records sometimes bind two exact Act

exact Act. That compression must be resolved before deciding whether the exact

## 9. Word pressure

### Responsibility

Gets right:

```text
one exact pre-Act branch belongs to one responsible boundary
```

Adds unsupported pressure toward:

```text
duty
obligation
owed work
inevitable occurrence
agent choice
```

The repeated historical question `why is this work owed?` is a demonstrated
cost of the word, not a coordinate found in active grammar.

### Assignment

Gets right:

```text
exact subjects, Act positions, rule, Scope, and clause are bound together
the binding has exact identity and lineage
the binding precedes Applicability and occurrence
```

Current machine language already uses Assignment identities and references for
that work.

Still unresolved:

```text
whether Assignment means the completed binding or an assigning Act
whether one Assignment may bind two exact Acts
whether assignment subject identity is an exact subject or storage scaffolding
```

No assigner, Assignment Act, Assignment relation, or Assignment result is
currently required. If the word is retained, it can only denote the exact
binding, not an unrecorded assigning event.

### Disposition

Adds a tendency to manifest under conditions. Active Seed carries no tendency,
rate, probability, or latent property. Reject as stronger than the finding.

### Configuration

Accurately describes neutral co-carriage, but does not express the exact clause
and later lineage. Mere co-presence is repeatedly insufficient in active Seed.

### Case

Accurately avoids duty but contributes no exact distinction beyond saying that
one local instance exists.

### Exact Act

An exact Act already exists before its occurrence and could potentially carry
its subject, rule, Scope, Locality, required relations, and responsible
boundary.

But the active assignment-shaped records often carry an Applicability Act and
a governed Act together. Until that compression is resolved, collapsing the
whole construction into one exact Act would erase a current distinction.

## 10. DNA/RNA pressure at the right strength

The existing biological-language report established:

```text
material present
!= active continuation

local molecular compatibility
!= reaction occurrence

exact relation chain
!= intrinsic meaning
```

A promoter, ribosome, tRNA, or synthetase bears no demonstrated duty to make a
later reaction occur. Exact molecular relations and current conditions make
local interactions possible; an interaction occurrence remains another
position.

Therefore DNA/RNA physiology strongly refuses the normative reading:

```text

-> work is owed
```

It does not choose `Assignment` for Seed. Biological systems use many exact
local constructions rather than one noun matching this entire Seed position.

The useful vocabulary test is only:

```text
Does the proposed word remain accurate when there is
no agent, intention, duty, semantic interpretation, or guaranteed occurrence?
```

`Responsibility` fails that pressure in ordinary reading. `Assignment` can
pass only when it names the completed exact binding rather than an assigning
agent or Act.
