# Source-exhausted coordinate Responsibility implementation 001

## Boundary

This change implements only the first build authorized by
`compound_subject_addressability_investigation_001.md`.

It does not produce a word, numeral, bounded-literal contraction, domain
subject, automatic result dispatcher, or new Standing operation.

The pre-existing proving road begins with the complete population of adjacent
source-position coordinates, records the complete internal Compare findings,
measures recurrence of those findings, and may add the next source position to
each exact recurrent production.  Before this change it had two defects:

1. its Acts and results had no preceding exact Responsibility;
2. the caller supplied `extension_count`, choosing how many later coordinate
   populations the road was allowed to reach.

## Implemented topology

Every live Act in this road now has this exact recorded topology:

```text
prior Locality occurrence boundary B
+ exact subject coordinates
+ Book clause
+ Authority / Scope / Locality / limits / conflicts / Unknown
↓
exact Responsibility
↓ exact Responsibility reference
Act occurrence
↓ Yield
result
```

The Responsibility carries the exact Act identity, Act-occurrence identity,
result boundary, and subject.  The Act carries an exact reference back to that
Responsibility.  Current Standing carries both the Responsibility occurrence
and the resulting Applicability, Compare, or Measurement coordinate.  The
reference on the Act is the exact joint; the result is not misclassified as a
raw `exact_material` result.

This is existing `01.Standing.A` / `01.Standing.A.1`, `01.Source.D`, and
`04.Compare` physiology.  It is not a new generic Responsibility and not a
Standing Act.

## Source-selected continuation

The public `extension_count` parameter was removed rather than deprecated.
There is no compatibility route retaining the caller-selected limit.

The live stopping rule is now:

```text
complete adjacent source-coordinate population
↓
complete Compare findings
↓
recurrence Measurement
↓
for every recurrent exact production:
    address its next source position, if one exists
↓
record only the newly introduced Compare pairs
↓
repeat

no recurrent production has an addressable next source position
↓
stop
```

Coordinate count increases strictly on every continuation.  The exact source
population is finite.  The road therefore terminates without a developer
choosing length 3, 4, 5, or any other final size.

`exhausted=True` in the returned mechanics reading reports that the loop
reached this source-derived stopping boundary.  It is not a new constitutional
completion object.

## Vocabulary restraint

`extent` remains in the historical Python module/API name.  In this road it
means only one bounded set of adjacent source positions preserved in source
occurrence order.  It does not mean word, phrase, pattern, or a kind of
subject.

`consecutive` is used only as an English implementation gloss.  The durable
evidence is the exact source-position population and its positions, not a new
`consecutive` grammar coordinate.

The active Book still uses `ordered` for source-occurrence order, ordered
Candidate pairs, and ordered relation paths.  This implementation establishes
no additional ordering distinction.  Runtime event `kind` remains storage
discrimination and is not recorded as constitutional physiology.

## Focused witnesses

The focused positive source is:

```text

```

No caller supplies the two `a+a` positions.  The source-derived road reaches
their complete three-coordinate Compare surface, measures its recurrence, and
the existing corresponding-coordinate consumer finds recurrent `+` material
at the middle role without a caller naming the role or value.

The control:

```text
a+aa-a
```

preserves the same recurrent internal `p-q-p` surface while the middle
material varies.  Corresponding-coordinate Measurement preserves `+` and `-`
as separate exact findings and establishes no recurrence for that role.

The positive witness also proves:

- the length-two Compare result is reused by its length-three descendant;
- only the two comparisons introduced by the third coordinate are recorded;
- every produced result resolves through its Act to one preceding exact
  Responsibility carried by current Standing;
- the last recurrence population contains no recurrent group and the producer
  stops without a caller limit.

An acquisition-boundary control compares `aba` with `xabay`.  The interior
three-position result has the same exact material and complete Compare surface
in both acquisitions.  Unrelated surrounding material may lawfully create
other source-local results; isolation is not a prerequisite for the interior
result.

Mutation refusal and SQLite restart coverage remain active.  Restart rereads
the exact results and Responsibility references from durable storage.

## Cost and remaining vacancy

The source population is bounded, but the durable physiology is still
expensive.  In the current constrained environment the complete six-position
positive test took about 41 seconds.  A combined SQLite restart and
surrounding-material control took about 121 seconds.  This is serialization and
nested validation cost, not unbounded coordinate generation.

No caller limit was restored to hide that cost.

The first intentionally unimplemented rung remains:

```text

```

No current Responsibility owns that contraction.  This change stops before
inventing it.
