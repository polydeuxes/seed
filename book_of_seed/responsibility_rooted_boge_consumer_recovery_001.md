# Responsibility-rooted BOGE consumer recovery 001

## 1. Question, scope, and authority

This recovery asks whether the active Book establishes one or more exact
responsibilities that consume either established bounded operator goal standing
or the bounded operator goal under that standing. Those are two different
possible subjects. This recovery presumes neither a consumer, singularity,
firstness, nor consumption of standing by identity.

The authority order applied here is:

1. the active Book;
2. runtime and tests as implementation testimony;
3. reports as attributed testimony;
4. git history as provenance testimony.

The active Book alone can establish the constitutional relation sought. Lower
authority can locate a candidate, but cannot fill an absent owner, subject, act,
or subject-to-act relation. This report adds no active law and changes no active
Book clause.

## 3. Controlling active-law findings

### 3.1 What BOGE establishes

`construction-and-establishment.md:9-16` assigns BOGE a precise upstream use:
BOGE relies on a consumer-local admitted meaning relation, consumes the bounded
goal proposition as expressed by the exact source candidate under that
standing, and establishes bounded-goal standing. This identifies what **BOGE
itself** consumes while establishing its result. It does not identify a later
consumer of either the result or its standing. The chapter expressly separates
BOGE establishment from movement consideration at lines 35-36.

`operator-ingress-common-grammar-prerequisite.md:25-41,99-105,107-131`
repeats the same bounded proposition use and preserves its candidate, meaning
relation, applicability, admission, lineage, and limits. Its result is bounded
operator goal standing. A result and its standing remain distinct under
`README.md:153-186`; neither establishes a downstream consumer by existence.

### 3.2 The only active downstream naming is not a consumption clause

The decisive neighboring clause is
`operator-ingress-common-grammar-prerequisite.md:60-68`. It displays a
**possible later advancement relation**:

```text
bounded grammar-acquisition goal standing
-> ordinary bounded-goal advancement responsibilities
-> any later movement consideration, movement selection,
   authority, later act occurrence, and evidence production require
   their own responsible occurrences
```

Lines 129-139 say only that those later matters belong after establishment and
require separate warrant, authority, and responsible occurrences. The clause
does not:

- decompose the named district into exact responsibilities;
- assign an owner to an exact consumer act;
- say that standing is consumed;
- say that the bounded goal under standing is consumed;
- identify another exact consumed subject;
- establish applicability, admission, Warrant, Authority, or Constraint for a
  consumer use; or
- establish an Uptake result.

The word “after” gives a boundary against premature advancement. It does not
give a consumer edge, uniqueness, or constitutional firstness. The same chapter
expressly says that establishment does not consider the goal for movement,
consideration does not select movement, and movement selection neither
authorizes nor performs movement
(`operator-ingress-common-grammar-prerequisite.md:147`).

### 3.3 Uptake grammar prevents completion by implication

`lenses-views-and-roads.md:9-20` requires a responsible consumer with prior
standing, exact upstream material, a declared act and purpose, satisfied local
requirements, and a responsible local use occurrence. It also separates
availability, applicability, admission, consumption, uptake, reliance, and
standing change. In particular, admitted material may never be consumed, and an
admission boundary's consumption does not prove consumption by the final target
consumer. Thus BOGE result existence, visibility, applicability, or admission
cannot supply the absent downstream relation.

`constitutional-kinds-and-artifact-standing.md:9-25` and
`acts-and-act-artifacts.md:9-22` separately prevent an artifact or act-shaped
record from proving constitutional kind, standing, or occurrence. Runtime
shape therefore cannot repair the active-law absence.

### 3.4 Conditions do not become consumed material

`constraints-and-preconditions.md:9-20` makes a Constraint local to an exact
proposed act and separates precondition satisfaction from authorization and act
occurrence. `authority-scope.md:9-18` makes Authority source-, subject-, act-,
scope-, and time-bounded. Neither chapter states that established bounded goal
standing is the applicability, admission, Warrant, Authority, Constraint, or
other condition of an exact later consumer. Even if such a requirement were
stated, requiring standing would not by itself mean consuming that standing.

`evidence-provenance-and-explanation.md:9-23` preserves Evidence, Provenance,
Explanation, confidence, warrant, truth, and current applicability as separate.
No recurrence of a goal identity or reference is an exact consumer use by
identity.

## 6. Concrete relation-type audits

only positive relation relevant here.

### 6.3 Movement consideration

The active Book names later movement consideration but does not positively
establish an exact movement-consideration responsibility, owner, consumed

BOGE is insufficient. **Classification: C.**

set plus selection evidence or policy and produces selection standing for a

or evidence. The BOGE chapter names later movement selection and explicitly
separates consideration, selection, authorization, and movement. No exact BOGE
consumer edge is supplied for either selection candidate. **Each is C.**

### 6.5 Ordinary bounded-goal advancement responsibilities
