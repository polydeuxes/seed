# Declared Responsibility force surface investigation 001

## Question

The spring-loaded Measurement investigation found one exact narrow loop:

```text
new exact material occurrence
↓
changed bounded Locality replay
↓
declared Measurement discovery
↓
family-local assignment / Act / Yield / result records
↓
changed bounded replay
↓
repeat until the declared Measurement surface has no remaining subject
```

That loop is currently driven by a host tuple containing two Measurement
declarations. This report tests the proposed replacement:

```text

↓
read exact Responsibility assignments this Seed bears from current Standing
↓
each family determines Applicability
↓

↓

↓

```

The exact question is:

```text
Can current Standing expose the next Responsibility assignment
before the exact assignment occurrence has been recorded?
```

`Force surface`, `spring potential`, `cocked spring`, `release`, and `scheduler`
are orientation language in this report. They establish no Book kind,
Responsibility, Act, occurrence, result, or Standing.

This report changes no Book clause, Witness Grammar coordinate, or runtime
event. The accompanying test records the exact before/after assignment surface
for one operator-material phrase.

subject-specific assignment occurrences alone:

```text

    exact material subject present
```

The missing crossing is earlier:

```text

```

The two-item tuple currently compresses the first arrow and external flow steps
over the second. Expanding that tuple across every family would enlarge the
compression rather than recover it.

The spring mechanism is therefore not missing wholesale. Its family-local
release shape exists. The unresolved part is how Book-declared potential
becomes one exact Standing-bearing assignment without the host selecting the
relation between current material, Responsibility family, and recorder.
