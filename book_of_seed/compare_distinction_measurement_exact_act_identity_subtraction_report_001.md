# Compare-Distinction Measurement exact-Act identity subtraction report 001

## Question

Does the exact Compare-Distinction Measurement Act require a separately minted
opaque identity?

## Prior shape

The Act occurrence carried both:

```text
act = Measurement of exact Compare Distinctions
addressed_act_identity = minted identity M*
```

The result copied both coordinates. No prior occurrence addressed `M*`, and no
independent reader supplied the identity that this lifecycle should have used.

## Falsifier

Remove `M*` while preserving:

```text
exact Compare-result subject
exact Measurement Act
exact Act occurrence
exact result occurrence
Act-to-result occurrence reference
subject and Act mutation refusal
complete Distinction findings
current-coordinate replay
restart
```

## Result

The exact Act remains a coordinate of the Act occurrence. Changing that Act to
`Compare` makes the Measurement occurrence refuse. The exact subject, Locality,
and actual Act occurrence remain independently addressed.

The opaque identity supplied no additional discriminator. Its accepted value
came only from the value minted and copied by this lifecycle.

## Disposition

```text
opaque exact-Act identity        withdrawn
copied addressed-Act identity    withdrawn
exact Measurement Act            retained
exact subject                     retained
actual Act occurrence identity   retained
actual result occurrence identity retained
```

No Act word is inferred from the implementation family. No binding occurrence,
Applicability, Yield, result, or relation is added.
