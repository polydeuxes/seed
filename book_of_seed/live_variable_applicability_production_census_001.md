## Verdict computed before Applicability

The combined-input reader evaluates each `(S, C)` pair while the host is
forming the Cartesian product. It computes:

```text

    S.source material result occurrence
    ==
    C.added material result occurrence

    both ordered pair subjects from S
    have addressed finding references in C
```

binding, Applicability Act, or Applicability result is appended. The later Seed
Applicability result repeats the computed positive or negative content.

This establishes implemented calculation. It does not establish a Seed
occurrence that warrants asking the question.
