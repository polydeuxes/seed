## Local constitutional context

The chapter subject is preservation of meaning, evidence, limits, provenance, Unknowns, and authority boundaries when Seed forms and may emit a bounded representation toward a consumer. The same subject separates receipt, interpretation, Uptake, reliance, responsibility transition, authority transition, and external realization.

The core resolution establishes these distinctions:

```text
representation formation != emission
emission != delivery
delivery != receipt
receipt != interpretation
interpretation != uptake
uptake != reliance
reliance != responsibility transition
responsibility transition != authority transition
authority transition != external realization
```

The same chapter later states that representation formation alone does not establish operator-facing occurrence, external presentation, delivery, receipt, interpretation, uptake, reliance, responsibility transition, authority transition, or external realization. It also states that emission testimony does not establish delivery, receipt, interpretation, uptake, reliance, responsibility transition, authority transition, or external realization.

Active evidence law separately states that missing or unavailable provenance does not establish a negative claim about material, source, producer occurrence, claim, or relation, and does not prove that a producer occurrence did not occur. Active external-grammar law also states that a dimension may be unresolved and that unresolved dimensions must keep Unknown or unresolved standing explicit.

## Unknown versus nonoccurrence versus inapplicability audit

The final active phrase `unevidenced coordinates remain Unknown` is too broad if read as a universal identity rule. Active Unknown discipline supports a narrower reading:

```text
Unknown applies only where a positively instantiated coordinate remains unresolved by responsible finding.

A coordinate not required or not applicable
is not Unknown by identity.
```

Audit findings:

| Situation | Correct classification |
|---|---|
| Compressed wording names a coordinate. | Naming does not positively instantiate that coordinate, establish its Applicability, require it, or make it Unknown by identity. |
| A coordinate is positively instantiated, but its exact value or occurrence remains unresolved by responsible finding. | Unknown or unresolved standing is preserved for that coordinate. |
| A responsible finding establishes that a coordinate is inapplicable to the exact subject, Act, purpose, Scope, occurrence, or Consumer. | The established inapplicability is preserved; it is not Unknown. |
| No Evidence establishes an Act occurrence. | Absence of evidenced Act occurrence may be preserved; nonoccurrence is not established by identity. |
| A responsible negative finding establishes that a coordinate did not occur within a bounded evidence boundary. | Established negative occurrence or nonoccurrence, preserving its evidence boundary and limits. |
| A coordinate is unsupported, missing, or not selected under a lawful local rule. | Unsupported, missing, or lawful non-selection, as established by that rule; not Unknown by identity unless the coordinate itself is positively instantiated and unresolved. |
| Missing provenance for material or source. | Provenance Unknown or unavailable provenance; not falsehood, invalidity, nonexistent source, or nonexistent producer occurrence. |

The relation protected by the clause is therefore a combination: absence of evidence is protected from being turned into proof; nonoccurrence is protected from being inferred without a responsible negative finding; inapplicability is protected from being mislabeled Unknown; and unresolved positively instantiated coordinates remain Unknown or unresolved rather than supplied.

## Equivalence finding

`bounded road` has no exact constitutional equivalent in this occurrence. It does not name one subject, one relation, one act, one responsibility, one owner, or one ordered set. It is ordinary compressed external wording for a set of possible communication-boundary coordinates named in the same paragraph.

`instantiates every coordinate` correctly describes the rejected overclaim only if `instantiates` means that the exact subject, occurrence, result, or relation has been positively established for that coordinate under its own evidence, authority, Scope, limits, and Unknown preservation. It is not correct if read to mean that every coordinate must exist, apply, or become Unknown for every compressed wording or emitted representation.
