# Positional coordinate census 001

## Question

Which admitted position-shaped words establish exact coordinates, and which
compress different physiology under one ordinary-English surface?

This census follows the Assertion result established in
`assertion_coordinate_census_001.md`:

```text
result-local position alone                         not an exact address
parent result occurrence + result-local position   exact address
```

The report uses organizational terms only to compare evidence. It does not
propose `Frame`, `Order`, `Orientation`, or another constitutional category.

## Admission surface

Book admission currently includes:

```text
position  Locality  boundary
source    destination
earlier   later     before   after
first     second
prior     through   current  added
at        within    beyond
same      another   separate
```

It does not include:

```text
start       end
next        previous
advance     middle     between
adjacent    consecutive
earliest    latest
```

The absent words are not required by any finding below.

## Exact local position

Live byte, pair, position-coordinate, recurrent-position, and ordered-path
roads use integer positions inside an exact recorded result or exact source
material occurrence. A bare integer is insufficient. The independently
required coordinates include its containing result or source occurrence and,
where established, Locality and a bounded source extent.

The recent plain-byte and movement subtractions provide the positive control:

```text
same result-local position in result R1
!=
same result-local position in result R2
```

`position` therefore survives as a local coordinate. It does not establish
its own coordinate domain.

## Source and destination

Movement establishes exact source coordinates and exact destination
coordinates before its Act occurrence. Locality roads likewise distinguish a
source Locality or through-occurrence reference from a destination Locality.

These endpoints are directional. They are not occurrence-order claims:

```text
source / destination   direction of one established physiology
earlier / later        relative occurrence order under an exact basis
first / second         slots in one structured subject or relation
```

No inspected road permits one pair to replace another.

## Earlier and later

Recorded-pair Compare uses `earlier_measurement_reference` and
`later_measurement_reference`. The distinction is not established by those
labels alone. Its reader requires exact ordered source occurrence references,
the one occurrence present only in the later source sequence, one Locality,
and Ledger append order for the required occurrences.

The exact order basis is therefore recoverable on that road. The Book's naked
`earlier` and `later` wording does not itself name that basis.

The census does not authorize replacing `earlier` or `later`. It establishes
that every use must answer:

```text
earlier or later under which exact occurrence order?
```

Chronology, result-local position, source occurrence order, and Ledger append
order are not interchangeable.

## Before and after

`before` and `after` are used as ordering statements around an Act, Yield, or
result occurrence. Live readers establish those statements with exact Ledger
append order or an exact through-occurrence cut.

This differs grammatically from naming the earlier and later endpoints, but no
independent `before` or `after` coordinate was found. They currently express
an established order relation rather than another durable coordinate.

The census leaves both admitted while requiring the same exact order basis as
their endpoint wording.

## First and second

Three distinct uses were found:

```text
first subject / second subject       oriented endpoints of one relation
first position / second position     ordinal byte positions in one pair
This Seed first carries ...          initial-time prose
```

The first two are not equivalent. A first relation subject need not occur
earlier and need not occupy numeric position zero. Pair-position readers, by
contrast, prove `second_position == first_position + 1` inside exact source
material.

`01.Current.G` uses `first` for neither distinction. It means initially:

```text
This Seed first carries no current coordinates.
```

That use is an established lexical overload. It should be pressure-tested
before `first` is used as a general ordinal or time word.

## Prior

Live `prior_*` names have at least three meanings:

```text
prior_coordinates
    exact current coordinates supplied from one earlier through-occurrence cut

prior_through_event_occurrence_identity
    the exact cut from which a complete new interval is advanced

prior_act / prior_result / prior_yield
    any already-recorded occurrence scanned for duplicate or refusal
```

`prior_supplied_occurrence_references` names an ordered population, not one
immediate predecessor. None of these meanings establishes adjacency.

`prior` is therefore overloaded and cannot lawfully imply `previous` or
`immediately before`. Its exact following coordinate currently supplies the
real distinction.

## Through and current

`through_event_occurrence_identity` is a strong live coordinate. It names an
inclusive Locality cut. The current-coordinate reader resolves the event,
derives the Ledger boundary through it, reads the complete Locality prefix,
and refuses an omitted intervening occurrence.

`current` is not another position. It names the exact coordinates established
in one Locality through that cut. The empty reading before any occurrence and
the reading through an exact occurrence are distinct and reproducible.

`through` and `prior` are not a pair. A prior coordinate set may be the input
to an advance, while `through` names the inclusive result boundary.

## At

The Book uses `at` for at least two distinct statements:

```text
result occurrence at exact Locality
    established by the event's exact Locality coordinate

boundary at or after the result occurrence
    established by occurrence order
```

It also says coordinates are established `at` a source or recording boundary.
That use names a boundary-relative extent, not event Locality.

No single live `at` relation or occurrence spans these meanings. `at` is
ordinary connective grammar currently compressing location, order
coincidence, and boundary-relative establishment.

## Boundary is not one coordinate

At least five noninterchangeable boundary shapes are live or admitted:

```text
through-occurrence boundary
    inclusive Locality/Ledger cut addressed by an exact occurrence

completeness boundary
    exact extent over which Measurement or Compare claims complete findings

source boundary
    exact observed material interface supplied to a source Act

destination boundary
    exact outward interface addressed by Emission grammar

result_boundary_identity
    generated identity of a future lifecycle result
```

The first four use boundary as an extent or endpoint on different axes. Their
qualifiers are load-bearing.

`result_boundary_identity` is different. Across Locality, Measurement,
Applicability, Compare, material-source, checkpoint, and determination roads,
it is assigned the exact result identity and later compared with that result
identity. It does not establish a read cut, completeness extent, source
interface, destination interface, or position.

The census therefore finds:

```text
future exact result identity   established
independent result boundary    not established
```

This is the highest-pressure subtraction candidate exposed by the census.

## Added

Recorded-pair Compare uses `added` for one exact source occurrence present at
the end of the later ordered source references and absent from the earlier
ordered source references. The reader validates both complete ordered source
populations, the exact occurrence, its Locality, and its source physiology.

`added` therefore names a derived population difference on this road. It is
not a general occurrence-order coordinate and does not authorize a generic
delta object.

## Same, another, and separate

These words do not locate anything:

```text
same       equality under named exact coordinates
another    nonidentity from one addressed coordinate
separate   independently occurring or independently addressed physiology
```

The word alone never establishes the comparison basis. `another_position` is
lawful only because two exact parent-result-local positions are compared. It
does not create a position family or an ordering.

## External Witness interval words

`start_position` and `end_position` occur in supplied compiled-Witness
material. They address byte-range endpoints and are checked for continuity.
They are not Book-admitted and do not currently establish internal Seed
grammar. This census records the testimony without promoting it.

## Current finding

The repository does not contain one positional vocabulary family. It contains
several independently constrained distinctions:

```text
local result/source position
directed source and destination endpoints
occurrence order under an exact basis
structured first and second slots
inclusive through-occurrence extent
boundary-qualified extents and interfaces
identity or nonidentity comparisons
```

The exact coordinate is consistently larger than the positional word. It is
the addressed occurrence or bounded population plus the local position,
endpoint, order basis, or extent required by that road.

## Required pressure order

The smallest next tests are:

```text
1. subtract result_boundary_identity where it equals the exact result identity
2. split `prior` uses by their exact following coordinate
3. remove initial-time `first` from 01.Current.G
4. replace ambiguous `at` only where exact Locality/order/boundary wording exists
5. require an explicit order basis for earlier/later Compare coordinates
6. retain each real boundary qualifier; never normalize them to generic boundary
```

Do not introduce a Position object, Frame object, Order object, Boundary
object, adjacency relation, successor relation, or replacement vocabulary
family.
