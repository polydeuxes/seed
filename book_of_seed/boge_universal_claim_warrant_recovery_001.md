# BOGE universal claim warrant recovery 001

## Scope

This report traces the warrant of the clause introduced at PR #2026:

```text
BOGE is Seed's only goal-establishment apparatus
```

It amends no active law, runtime, test, projection, or prior report. It proposes
no replacement goal architecture and constructs no goal-establishment
Responsibility. Verified at `6a4c6db`.

## 1. The finding that reorders the question

**The universal claim is not in active law. It was withdrawn at PR #2053, and
an existing report had already identified it as a contamination pressure.**

```text
#2026  2c2c59e   introduced:
                 "BOGE is Seed's only goal-establishment apparatus and
                  remains operator-origin."

#2053  ad68553   replaced with:
                 "BOGE names the operator-origin constitutional boundary
                  examined here. The name does not require one universal
                  realization."

current  :9      "names the downstream operator-origin constitutional
                 boundary addressed here. The name does not require one
                 universal realization."
```

The narrowing is exact and it is the correct one: `only apparatus` became `names
the boundary examined here`, and `does not require one universal realization`
was added as an explicit denial of the withdrawn reading.

**This corrects `pre_boge_boundary_and_examination_composite_recovery_001.md`**,
which recorded the clause as standing in active law and classified it **D**,
un-adjudicated. That was this author's error: the clause was quoted from
`#2026`'s original text via `git show 2c2c59e:...` and not checked against the
current chapter. It has been absent from active law since #2053.

### 3.4 Was only operator-origin establishment in scope?

Yes, on the surrounding evidence. The chapter's Constitutional subject is *"the
goal-semantic admission required when bounded first-contact presentation is used
before bounded operator goal establishment."* Its sibling chapter
`construction-and-establishment.md` scopes itself to *"the transition from a
constructed goal-shaped representation to an established bounded **operator**

Both chapters address operator-origin establishment. Neither surveys goal
establishment generally. A universal claim about Seed's goal apparatus was made
inside a chapter whose declared subject is one origin.

## 4. Inheritance

### 4.1 Active law

```text
no surviving clause depends on uniqueness
```

Three chapters currently reference BOGE — the prerequisite chapter,
`construction-and-establishment.md`, and
`representation-emission-and-consumer-boundaries.md`. Every reference is to
BOGE's own boundary, its consumption of an admitted meaning relation, or its
operator-origin character. None asserts or requires exclusivity. `:148`'s *"BOGE
remains operator-origin throughout"* is an origin claim, not a uniqueness claim.

### 4.3 The dependent upstream sentence

The pre-#2062 sentence — *"preserved operator ingress cannot yet become
available to BOGE through the required upstream translation, interpretation,
applicability, and admission relations"* — was written at #2026, in the same
commit as the universal claim, and inherited its BOGE dependency from it.

It does not, however, depend on **uniqueness**. Its content is that certain
upstream relations were unestablished. That claim stands or falls on whether
those relations existed, independent of whether BOGE is the only apparatus. This
report therefore does not classify that sentence as warranted merely because it
predates #2062, and its recorded dependency on #2026 does not by itself defeat
it.

Likewise the pre-#2062 closed-choice road is not treated here as constitutional
by age.

## 5. Dispositions

### The universal claim

```text
BOGE is Seed's only goal-establishment apparatus

B — worked-road assumption promoted into active law
```

Introduced without antecedent, without recorded Evidence or Warrant, inside a
chapter scoped to one origin, and withdrawn 27 PRs later by a commit that
replaced it with an explicit denial of universal realization. Its removal is
itself part of the record: active law did not merely stop asserting it, it added
`the name does not require one universal realization`.

### The narrower claim

```text
BOGE is a bounded operator-origin goal-establishment Responsibility

C — mixed, requiring decomposition
```

Recovered in part: the *boundary* is named in three chapters, its consumption of
an admitted meaning relation is stated, and its operator-origin character is
asserted repeatedly. Not recovered: its exact owning Responsibility, Producer,
Consumer, occurrence, or Evidence — none of which this trace located, and none of
which the withdrawal at #2053 supplied. `BOGE names a boundary` is what active
law currently holds; `BOGE is a Responsibility` is one step further than the text
goes.

## 6. What this does not establish

that no other goal-establishment apparatus exists

Absence of a second recovered apparatus is expressly not treated as evidence for
or against uniqueness, per the request's exclusion list.

## 7. Smallest next constitutional boundary

The trace closes the question it was given and exposes one narrower one.

`BOGE` is currently a **named boundary without a recovered Responsibility**. Three
chapters describe what it consumes, what it does not establish, and that it
remains operator-origin. None names its owner, Producer, Consumer, occurrence, or
Evidence. The withdrawal at #2053 removed a false universal and did not supply
those coordinates.

So the smallest next boundary is not about uniqueness at all:

```text
what exact Responsibility, if any, does the name BOGE denote,
and what are its owner, Evidence, and occurrence?
```

That is the same shape as the question `candidate-versus-relation-grammar
Compare` turned out to be — a name carried through many clauses whose owning
Responsibility was never recovered. This report does not open it.
