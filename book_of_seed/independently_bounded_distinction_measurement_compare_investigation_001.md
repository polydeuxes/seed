# Independently Bounded Distinction Measurement Compare Investigation 001

## Question

Can existing physiology establish one internal Compare whose exact subjects
are Distinction Measurement results from two independently bounded material
populations?

The proposed shape is:

```text
independently bounded material population
→ existing physiology
→ exact Distinction Measurement result

independently bounded material population
→ existing physiology
→ exact Distinction Measurement result

two exact results
→ one Compare subject-to-Act binding
→ Applicability
→ Compare Act occurrence
→ Compare result
```

This investigation does not add that road. It asks whether the existing
runtime already establishes every coordinate and occurrence in the shape.

## Existing H/X boundary

`exact_material_h_x_result_content_report_001.md` uses two separate
`EventLedger` histories. Each history establishes exact material,
Measurement, Applicability, Compare, and Distinction Measurement results.

The H/X relation is external testimony. No occurrence in either Ledger
addresses the other Ledger, and no exact identity crosses between them. The
source-relative reading compares exact content without claiming a cross-Ledger
identity or relation.

Consequently, the existing H/X experiment supplies neither subject to an
internal Compare. Reusing its `first` and `second` experiment positions as
subjects would make the authored experiment the selector.

## One Ledger, two Localities

A focused control placed two small independently bounded populations in one
`EventLedger`:

```text
Locality 1: ab\n ac\n
Locality 2: ab\n ax\n
```

The Ledger contains both exact Distinction Measurement results:

```text
evt_000139  first-locality
evt_000280  second-locality
```

Each Locality's exact current-coordinate reading contains only its own result:

```text
first-locality  measurement occurrences: evt_000139
second-locality measurement occurrences: evt_000280
```

`compare_distinction_measurement_references_from_current_coordinates()` returns
no joint in either Locality. It reads one Locality's current coordinates and
does not combine Localities.

The results therefore coexist in one Ledger but do not coexist as current
subjects in one Locality.

## One Ledger, one Locality

A second control supplied the same two small populations successively in one
Locality. This makes their result occurrences current together, but destroys
the proposed independent population boundary.

The exact byte Measurement source populations are:

```text
Measurement 1: (evt_000003)
Measurement 2: (evt_000003, evt_000063)
Measurement 3: (evt_000003, evt_000063, evt_000144)
Measurement 4: (evt_000003, evt_000063, evt_000144, evt_000231)
```

The second invocation continues the first Locality's ordered source
population. It does not begin another independently bounded source population.
The pair premise also continues across that invocation boundary.

Using Measurements 2 and 4 as if they represented two independent populations
would compare an earlier prefix with a later cumulative population. That is a
different experiment.

## Current subject census

`COMPARE_DISTINCTION_MEASUREMENT_RESULT_KIND` is currently consumed by:

- its exact result reader;
- current-coordinate reconstruction;
- declared-Measurement discovery; and
- the read-only function that finds two results whose producing pair Compares
  meet through one exact Measurement occurrence.

No live runtime function records a Compare subject-to-Act binding with two
Distinction Measurement results as its subjects.

The read-only joint function establishes only exact joint addressability. It
appends no binding, Applicability lifecycle, Compare occurrence, or result. Its
exact shared Measurement coordinate comes from overlapping producing pair
Compares inside one continuous Locality history. It does not establish an
independent population boundary and does not choose an Act.

## Compare census

Active `04.Compare` states the coordinates of a Compare once one exact
subject-to-Act binding exists. The instantiated roads are narrower:

```text
04.Compare.A
earlier byte-pair Measurement result
+ later byte-pair Measurement result
under exact same-Locality result order

04.Compare.B
ordered relation-path result position
+ recorded pair-finding Compare result
current in one Locality
```

Neither road admits a Distinction Measurement result in either subject
position. There is no generic runtime Compare dispatcher that turns any two
current exact subjects into a binding.

Creating a new binding merely because two results are current would make
availability select Compare before Applicability. Creating it because the H/X
experiment names two populations would make the external experiment select
Compare.

## Locality and Movement census

Active current coordinates are bounded by one exact Locality. The two-Locality
control therefore has no current-coordinate reading that contains both exact
Distinction Measurement results.

The instantiated Movement road addresses exact result-position content. Its
supported live subjects are byte result positions, recorded pair-position
content, and path-Compare finding result positions. No current Movement road
addresses one complete Distinction Measurement result.

Generic Movement grammar does not instantiate that missing road by itself.
Choosing Movement solely to make the proposed Compare possible would add an
Act before its Applicability or occurrence physiology had been recovered.

## Historical falsifier

Commits `823db38d`, `8d446de3`, and `e49c632c` previously constructed and made
live a Compare of successive Distinction Measurement results.

That road used equality of their producing shared Measurement reference to:

1. select the two results;
2. establish positive Applicability; and
3. choose Compare as the governed Act.

It also scanned the current Measurement population for pairs and had no prior
occurrence owning the new Compare Act. Commit `4a99dabd` withdrew the entire
1,071-line runtime road and its live console wiring as an unestablished
measured-result Compare.

The surviving read-only shared-Measurement function is useful testimony for
exact joint addressability. It is not a dormant authorization to restore the
deleted Act road.

The new H/X case supplies less internal joint physiology than that historical
case: independently bounded results in separate Localities do not even share
the exact producing Measurement occurrence used by the old road.

## Earliest missing coordinate

The first missing coordinate is:

```text
one exact current Locality coordinate
in which both independently produced
Distinction Measurement results are exact subjects
```

No existing source, Locality, current-coordinate, or Movement occurrence
establishes that coordinate.

This statement does not propose a new shared-Locality object or relation. A
future exact occurrence could establish the needed subject position through
Movement or through physiology not yet recovered. The present evidence does
not choose between those possibilities.

Even if that coordinate later exists, a second vacancy remains:

```text
two exact current subjects
→ which exact Act is bound?
```

Current generic Compare law describes the binding after it exists. It does not
produce the binding or select Compare. The already-frozen positive
Applicability-result to governed-Act-occurrence vacancy would remain after any
positive Applicability result.

## Falsifiers

The investigation tried each currently available shape:

| Shape | Exact result |
| --- | --- |
| separate Ledgers | exact results exist; no cross-Ledger coordinate or relation |
| one Ledger, separate Localities | exact results coexist; no one current-coordinate reading contains both |
| one Ledger, one Locality | results become cumulative history, not independently bounded populations |
| shared producing Measurement reference | exact joint addressability only; not available across independent populations |
| current result-position Movement | no live subject shape for a complete Distinction Measurement result |
| generic Compare clause | describes an exact binding; does not instantiate or select one |
| historical measured-result Compare | previously falsified and withdrawn at `4a99dabd` |

Content equality is not a substitute. The H/X experiment already found all 12
Distinction Measurement reference shapes equal under experiment-relative
coordinates while the final addressed findings resolve to different exact
pair subjects. Equality of the reference shapes establishes neither identity
nor a Compare relation.

## Disposition

The proposed internal Compare is not currently established.

```text
independently bounded Distinction Measurement results  ✅ exist
both results in one Ledger                             ✅ possible
both results current in one exact Locality             ❌ not established
existing Movement of the complete results              ❌ not found
existing Compare binding for those subjects            ❌ not found
existing Applicability lifecycle for that binding       ❌ not found
existing governed Compare Act occurrence                ❌ not found
```

The vacancy freezes before implementation. This investigation introduces no
Experiment, Population, Version, Difference, Change, selector, cross-Ledger
identity, Movement road, Compare road, or new constitutional grammar.

The next material experiment need not wait for this vacancy. Existing
physiology can receive exact arithmetic material without claiming arithmetic
meaning, while this internal cross-population Compare remains honestly absent.
