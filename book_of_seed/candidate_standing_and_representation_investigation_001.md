# Candidate Standing and Representation

## Status

Investigation only.

This report changes no active Book material, machine grammar, Rosetta
material, runtime, admission, or test.

Amended after review: the first version correctly separated the producing
Responsibilities and Acts, then incorrectly used that distinction to exclude
the Candidate result from Representation. Distinct producers do not establish
distinct result kinds. This report now treats both Candidate membership in
Representation and Candidate exclusion from Representation as Unknown.

The operator and curator supplied this orientation:

```text
C
= one completed Candidate-production result boundary

what exists through C
may be this Seed's bounded Representation of prior Standing

Standing
    -> Representation
    -> Standing
    -> Representation
    -> Standing
```

They call one recurrence a `heartbeat`. `C` and `heartbeat` are explanatory
testimony in this report. Neither is treated as a constitutional kind.

The narrow questions are:

1. Does Candidate Standing have the same Responsibility physiology as a
   Representation result?
2. Do both preserve the same coordinates?
3. Do both have the same result-to-Standing relation?
4. What exact distinction, if any, becomes impossible if Candidate Standing
   is treated as one Representation?

## Method

This investigation used:

- active clauses `01.Source.A`, `01.Source.E.1`, `02.Acts.A`, and
  `05.Source.A`;
- the active representation material in chapter 14;
- current machine grammar only as a represented witness;
- `operator_representation.py`,
  `candidate_standing_from_exact_result_assertions.py`, and Locality Standing
  replay as implementation testimony;
- chronology of the two roads;
- an elimination test over their exact Acts, source boundaries, results, and
  preserved coordinates.

Book material, machine grammar, runtime, tests, and history are independent
testimony. Agreement among them does not make two Act families one Act.

## 1. C is a boundary, not a Representation

The Candidate reader exposes two distinct append boundaries:

```text
B = frozen source-ledger boundary
C = later Candidate-result append boundary
```

`C` identifies where the Candidate result occurrence was appended. It does
not identify:

- a constitutional kind;
- a source population;
- the Candidate formation Act;
- the Candidate result by itself;
- a Representation;
- global Standing.

The ledger prefix through C also contains the source history through B and
the Candidate assignment, Applicability, Act Evidence, Yield Evidence, and
result occurrences recorded after B. That entire prefix is not one result
merely because it is recoverable through one append boundary.

Disposition: **C is only the exact later result boundary**.

## 2. The shared event sequence is generic physiology

Both roads have this bounded shape:

```text
exact source coordinates
        |
        v
responsible Act occurrence
        |
        v
Evidence of Yield relation
        |
        v
bounded result
        |
        v
later Locality Standing
```

That sequence is not specific to Representation. Measurement, Compare,
Candidate production, movement, recording, and other responsible Act
families also use the Act-to-result physiology.

Therefore:

```text
same Act / Yield / result skeleton
!=
same exact Act
```

`Standing -> Act -> result -> Standing` can support `heartbeat` as Rosetta
orientation. It cannot make every beat a Representation beat.

Disposition: **the common skeleton survives, but it does not collapse the
Act families**.

## 3. Representation preserves one addressed source boundary

Active `01.Source.A` assigns source-coordinate preservation to a responsible
Representation Act occurrence. Chapter 14 says a Representation preserves
where established:

- subject and content;
- source and provenance;
- Standing and Evidence;
- Authority and Scope;
- Locality;
- conflicts, Unknown, and limits.

For exact material, the current Representation rule preserves the exact
material and source-result occurrence. It establishes neither what the
material represents nor a represented relation.

The runtime road is correspondingly narrow:

```text
one Locality Standing boundary
+ optionally one exact source occurrence in that Locality
        |
        v
one bounded Representation result
```

It does not enumerate every Assertion through the boundary. It does not form
one result per source Assertion or per ordered pair. It does not claim that
every source required by an exhaustive formation Act was included.

The Representation can even be recorded with no exact source occurrence. If
one source occurrence is addressed, the current runtime requires it in the
same Locality as the Representation occurrence.

Disposition: **Representation is an addressed preservation road, not the
current exhaustive Candidate-formation road**.

## 4. Candidate production owes higher Assertion subjects

Active `01.Source.E.1` assigns two exact Responsibilities to this Seed:

1. record one Candidate for every exact source Assertion through B;
2. record one Candidate for every distinct ordered source-Assertion pair
   through B.

Each exact Act owes:

- source Applicability;
- source Participation in the Act-local input role;
- the exact source Assertion references and source coordinates;
- every Candidate required by that Act;
- deterministic order;
- one result that refuses recovery when a required Candidate is missing,
  extra, repeated, changed, reordered, unsupported, or partial.

Each Candidate is itself an Assertion with a new bounded subject:

```text
unary Candidate
    subject = one exact lower Assertion reference

ordered-pair Candidate
    subject = first exact lower Assertion reference
            + second exact lower Assertion reference
```

What either Candidate represents remains Unknown. The Candidate's existence
does not establish its represented relation.

This is more than preserving one addressed source boundary. The exact Act
forms higher Assertion subjects and owes all of them through its boundary.

Disposition: **complete formation of higher Candidate Assertions is a real
remaining distinction**.

## 5. Active material separates the Acts, not necessarily their result kinds

`05.Source.A` currently states:

```text
An exact Representation Act may represent asserted content
and source coordinates in another exact Representation.

It cannot create a higher subject.
```

Candidate production creates higher subjects. In particular, an ordered-pair
Candidate carries two lower Assertion references under two exact roles as its
own Assertion subject.

Therefore the current active distinction between the producing Acts is
direct:

```text
Representation Act
    cannot create a higher subject

Candidate-production Act
    creates Candidate Assertions with higher bounded subjects
```

This proves that the Candidate-production Act is not the exact Representation
Act described by `05.Source.A`. It does not prove that the result of the
Candidate-production Act cannot itself bear Representation Standing.

The Book separately states:

```text
representation != Act that established it
```

and says that a recording boundary may create an exact representation of
Events. Recording is not thereby the Representation Act. This supplies a
counterexample to the first report's inference:

```text
Act A != Representation Act
does not establish
result of Act A != Representation
```

Collapsing the two Acts would still either:

- erase the higher-subject formation that the Candidate Act actually owes;
  or
- silently strengthen Representation so that it can create higher subjects.

Neither follows from the shared preservation shape. But recognizing a result
of the Candidate Act as a Representation would not necessarily collapse the
Acts. It could instead establish Representation Standing for that exact
result under its own support relation.

Disposition: **Candidate-production Act != existing Representation Act is
established; Candidate Standing = Representation and Candidate Standing !=
Representation are both Unknown**.

## 6. The preserved coordinate sets overlap but are not identical

The Representation road carries or preserves:

```text
source occurrence reference
Locality Standing boundary
source provenance
Scope / Locality
Authority
known loss
conflicts
Unknown
exact material and its rule where present
Representation Act and occurrence
Yield Evidence
Representation Locality Evidence
```

The Candidate road carries:

```text
B
ordered source Assertion references
source Locality
source Standing boundary
source Evidence
source Authority
source Scope
source limits
source Unknown
Candidate Responsibility assignment
source Applicability
source Participation
Candidate Act and occurrence
Yield Evidence
ordered Candidate Assertion references
completeness coordinates
```

Both conserve source distinctions and both refuse represented-relation
Standing. But neither coordinate set is simply the other under a different
name.

Candidate source references permit recovery of the lower source material;
that recoverability does not turn the Candidate result into a Representation
occurrence. Conversely, a Representation's source reference does not create
the Candidate Act's source surface, formation debt, or higher subjects.

Disposition: **shared preservation law; distinct exact coordinate debt**.

## 7. Runtime carries separate result coordinates without proving exclusion

Locality Standing replay carries the roads separately:

```text
Representation results
    -> representations

Candidate Standing results
    -> candidate_result_occurrences
```

The Representation map preserves the Representation identity, occurrence,
source occurrence, represented Standing boundary, and later emission
coordinates. No Representation is designated current merely because it is
present.

The Candidate result is recovered only after replaying B, its exact source
Assertions, its two exact Act families, and every owed Candidate. Candidate
result Assertions and the Candidate Assertions they carry may then enter a
later Candidate source surface.

Active material also keeps this negative boundary:

```text
Representation
!=
Standing carried by its material
```

Thus neither a Representation nor a Candidate result becomes the Standing of
everything it references.

But separate runtime maps are implementation addressability. They establish
that the current readers recover different exact result coordinates. They do
not establish that one result cannot also receive Standing under a broader
constitutional kind.

The same caution applies in the other direction. A representation-shaped
result does not acquire Representation Standing because its Python material
resembles another represented result.

Disposition: **different current recovery obligations are established;
constitutional exclusion from Representation is not**.

## 8. The Representation road has an unresolved assignment boundary

The Candidate road records an exact Responsibility assignment and carries
its exact reference through Applicability, Participation, Act Evidence,
Yield Evidence, and result.

The current Representation road does not record a Representation
Responsibility assignment. It directly records Representation Act Evidence
with:

```text
responsible boundary: this Seed
Authority: unestablished
```

`01.Source.A` describes what a responsible Representation Act occurrence
preserves. Its machine coordinate names a source-coordinate-preservation
Responsibility shape. Neither presently supplies the exact assignment and
Authority physiology that the Candidate road records.

This does not prove that Representation is unlawful. It establishes that the
current Representation road is not a safe constitutional parent for the
newer Candidate road merely because both preserve sources.

Disposition: **Representation Responsibility assignment and Authority remain
an independent vacancy**.

## 9. Producer identity does not determine Representation Standing

The original report proposed a later wrapper:

```text
Candidate Standing result
        |
        v
later exact Representation Act
        |
        v
Representation of that exact Candidate result
```

That relation would preserve the distinction:

```text
Representation of Candidate Standing
!=
Candidate Standing
```

That remains one possible later road, but it assumes the Candidate result and
Representation are already distinct kinds. The current evidence does not
warrant that assumption.

The active Book gives two important counterpressures.

First:

```text
A recording boundary may create an exact representation of Events.
```

Therefore Representation need not be the exclusive result of the current
Representation Act family.

Second:

```text
representation shape != constitutional kind
representation != Act that established it
```

Therefore preservation shape and producer identity do not independently
establish Representation Standing.

The sharper question is not whether another Representation Act can wrap the
Candidate result. It is:

```text
What exact Standing makes one result a Representation?
```

Active constitutional Standing requires the exact support relation from the
result's Evidence, Authority, Scope, and preserved limits to the Assertion
whose Standing is claimed. A name, result shape, behavior, producer kind,
Locality, or record address cannot supply that relation.

For the Candidate result, the required Assertion would be bounded to:

```text
this exact Candidate result
    is one Representation
```

with its exact responsible occurrence, Evidence, Authority, Scope, limits,
Unknown, and Standing. Current Candidate material establishes its exact
production, source debt, higher Assertions, completeness, and negative
boundaries. It does not carry an Assertion or support relation establishing
that the result is a Representation.

Absence of that Assertion establishes no contrary result:

```text
no Standing that Candidate result is Representation
!=
Standing that Candidate result is not Representation
```

Current runtime also provides no such classification. Its Representation
source reader accepts selected exact-material, Measurement, and Compare result
families, while Candidate results are carried separately. That is testimony
about current readers, not a constitutional negative relation.

Disposition: **the exact relation between Candidate Standing and
Representation is Unknown in both directions**.

## 11. Answer

The narrow answer is:

```text
C
= the Candidate result append boundary

Candidate-production Responsibility
!= existing Representation Responsibility

Candidate-production Act
!= existing Representation Act

Candidate Standing = Representation
Unknown

Candidate Standing != Representation
Unknown
```

Candidate Standing is **representation-shaped** in ordinary explanatory
language because it preserves exact lower references and coordinates without
establishing their represented relation. That resemblance recovers a common
conservation law. It neither grants nor refuses Representation Standing.

The distinction that survives is between the exact producing Acts:

```text
existing Representation Act
    preserves an addressed source and its coordinates

Candidate production
    forms every higher Candidate Assertion owed by one exact Act through B
    and proves none was omitted
```

The recursive physiology is therefore currently:

```text
Standing
    -> exact responsible Act family
    -> result
    -> Standing
    -> exact responsible Act family
    -> result
    -> Standing
```

`heartbeat` is good Rosetta testimony for that recurrence. A stronger
hypothesis remains open:

```text
Standing_n
    -> several exact Responsibilities
    -> several exact Acts
    -> exact results
    -> Representation_n+1 where independently established
    -> Standing_n+1
```

This topology preserves distinct Acts while allowing several result families
to bear Representation Standing. Current material does not yet establish
which results do.

## 12. Smallest next question

Do not rename Candidate Standing as Representation and do not establish its
exclusion from Representation.

The next bounded question exposed by this investigation is:

```text
What exact Standing makes one exact result
a Representation independently of the Act family that produced it?
```

That investigation must recover independently:

- the exact result subject;
- the Assertion that the result is a Representation;
- the responsible occurrence supporting that Assertion;
- Evidence, Authority, Scope, limits, Unknown, and Standing;
- which Representation coordinates are necessary for that exact result;
- whether Candidate Standing already carries those coordinates;
- what independent coordinate, if any, Candidate Standing lacks;
- what this establishes neither about the producing Act nor about what the
  result represents.

Until those coordinates are established:

```text
Candidate Standing is a Representation
```

and

```text
Candidate Standing is not a Representation
```

both remain bounded hypotheses, not current Standing.
