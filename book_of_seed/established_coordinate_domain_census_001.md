# `established` coordinate-domain census 001

## Question

Does `established` have an exact truth condition distinct from Book admission,
recording, exact validation, and currentness through a selected boundary?

## Four candidate domains

The active uses compressed four different stages:

```text
Book clause names a possible coordinate
Ledger contains a recorded occurrence
exact reader validates occurrence and coordinates
exact coordinate is current through selected boundary B
```

These stages are independently variable.

## Book declaration is not an occurrence

Active grammar can name a coordinate family while an empty Ledger contains no
occurrence of that family. Admission of the Book word or clause creates no
runtime coordinate.

The no-current-coordinate clause therefore needs only the exact condition:

```text
with no exact coordinate for this Seed
→ no current coordinates for this Seed
```

## Recording is not exactness

The Ledger can return a recorded event whose integrity or referenced
coordinates are corrupted. Exact readers refuse that event. Its stored row
does not make its coordinate exact.

```text
recorded occurrence    yes
exact coordinate       no
```

`established` adds no discriminator between those states; the exact reader
does.

## Exactness is not currentness

The current-boundary falsifier supplies one durable exact result `R`:

```text
complete Ledger reads R exactly                  yes
R current through an earlier selected boundary  no
R current through R or a later boundary          yes
```

The exact occurrence and its provenance do not change. `current` names the
boundary-relative distinction.

## Active-use subtraction

Every active `established` use resolves to its named coordinate directly:

```text
exact established coordinates        exact coordinates
coordinates established by Act       exact Act occurrence coordinates
coordinates established in boundary  exact coordinates within boundary
distinctions established by Compare  exact Compare Act distinctions
earlier/later established by order    coordinates of exact occurrence order
Yield established                     exact Yield relation occurrence
```

No separate establishment occurrence, identity, relation, result, or reader
survives.

## `establish`

This subtraction does not decide the Act word `establish`. Active Locality law
still names an exact Act to establish a Locality relation, and runtime/witness
coordinates name corresponding Acts. That verb remains under independent
pressure rather than borrowing survival from the removed predicate.

## Finding

```text
Book declaration                    possible coordinate grammar
recorded                            occurrence stored
exact                               occurrence and coordinates validate
current                             exact coordinate through selected boundary
established                         no additional exact coordinate
```

## Disposition

Remove `established` from active Book admission and state the exact coordinate,
Act occurrence, relation occurrence, order, or boundary condition directly.

Keep `establish` pending its own Act census.
