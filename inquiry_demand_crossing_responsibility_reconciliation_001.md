## Controlling constitutional recovery

### Demand is required-result standing, not the later acts

The Demand chapter defines the subject as the exact bounded result, relation,
clarification, inquiry, authority, transformation, competency, or condition
required under an exact responsibility and scope. A responsible examination may
decide whether such a requirement exists. A separately responsible establishment
occurrence establishes the bounded required-result standing. The chapter expressly
denies that establishment opens, selects, authorizes, executes, or completes
movement.

The word `inquiry` in a family label adds no stronger standing. Therefore, if an
exact inquiry-family Demand is established, its assertion can be only that a
bounded inquiry or clarification is required under the established responsibility
and scope, unless a local establishment occurrence separately warrants more. No
current Book clause makes the same Demand-establishment act establish which exact
material is applicable, admit it, supply authority, translate it, establish
question-local standing, or form the question.

The bounded common-grammar counterexample remains especially important. Its
possible subject is the common-grammar standing required by an exact consumer to
examine exact preserved ingress for its declared act and purpose. Recurrence is
measurement evidence only. No Demand examination or establishment is implemented,
no consumer of an established Demand was found, and the exact family remains
**Unknown**. Later inquiry cannot reclassify that possible Demand as inquiry-family.

### There is no universal crossing act

The Book does use a local crossing requirement: a relation among inquiry pressure,
Demand, question, examination, finding, answer, or later re-entry exists only when
the exact source and target standing, crossing evidence or warrant, authority
boundary, and surviving limits are preserved. This is a requirement on a local
relation, not a universal constitutional `Crossing` kind.

For operator asks, prompt phrases, uncertainty statements, reformulations, and
presentation labels, the Book calls for bounded translation before Seed initiates
internal inquiry. For normal question formation it permits attributed testimony,
pressure, goals, constraints, corrections, responses, repository uncertainty, and
prior findings as source material. These clauses establish conditional source
eligibility, not automatic applicability. Each exact material still needs its
local warrant, scope, limits, and source-to-target relation.

Thus an inquiry-family Demand may be one source of durable inquiry responsibility;
it is not shown to be the universal precursor. Conversely, a goal or uncertainty
may warrant inquiry without first becoming a Demand where its local responsible
act and consumer are established. Neither possibility automatically performs
translation or forms a question.

### Translation, standing establishment, and question formation do not collapse

Bounded translation has the source material as subject and preserves question-local
identity, source, scope, evidence demand, authority limit, uncertainty, and lawful
stop for the bounded purpose of allowing a Seed-side inquiry boundary to evaluate
or use the translated relation. It does not make external wording knowledge,
establish an answer, assign universal inquiry ownership, retire neighboring
questions, or authorize investigation beyond the boundary.

The Book also names `Seed-side interpretation and bounded standing establishment`
before Seed-owned question formation. Translation and sufficient standing are
therefore distinct constitutional claims. Current authority does not decide whether
one responsible occurrence may establish both in every district, nor does it assign
one universal artifact or producer. Shared occurrence would be lawful only if it
separately establishes both subjects, acts, purposes, warrants, output standings,
negative authority, and consumer relations.

Seed-owned question formation then owns a different assertion: a bounded internal
question now exists with identity, provenance, scope, evidence demand, authority
limits, uncertainty, and lawful stop. Its purpose is to bound an inquiry posture
for the later Seed-owned applicability, selection, and composition of lawful
internal examination means. It does not establish the answer, select the outcome,
force a lens, supply demanded evidence, or authorize unbounded investigation.
Which exact internal responsibility realizes this act in every district remains a
Book-preserved Unknown.
