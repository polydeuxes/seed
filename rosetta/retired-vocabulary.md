# Retired and external vocabulary

Translation testimony. **No entry here carries constitutional Authority**, and
no mapping establishes equivalence — see [README](README.md).

Each entry records what the word was reaching for and what current grammar
covers that ground. Where no single current term covers it, the entry says so:
that is the more informative answer, because it records that the old word
compressed several distinctions rather than simply being renamed.

## Status vocabulary used below

```text
retired         removed from active law

ordinary        still present in active law as ordinary lowercase English,
                carrying no constitutional subject
```

---

## State

```text
status          retired in #2354 (Book VI renamed to Standing and
                Projection); this entry is accurate once that merges
closest grammar Standing · Representation · View · Projection
                no single current equivalent
```

`State` was an attempt at a single universal derived representation of
everything Seed knows. Two things went wrong with it, and only the first is
usually noticed.

It erased boundaries. Current grammar derives a bounded representation from
*relevant* evidence for an *exact* consumer purpose; `State` derived one
representation from all evidence for no particular consumer. Rebuildability in
active law is explicitly "bounded by the retained evidence, projection rules,
and declared purpose."

And it treated availability as a revision trigger. New evidence arriving caused
a rebuild — but active law holds that "evidence becoming available does not by
itself change any consumer assertion, standing, confidence, reliance, or
current result. A revision requires a responsible consumer-local occurrence."
Even a perfectly bounded representation rebuilt on every new event would be
wrong for this reason.

Active law now mentions `State` only to deny it: "current standing is not a
constitutional State object."

The runtime still contains `StateProjector` and `ProjectionStore`. Those are
implementation testimony about a design that was tried, not grammar. The useful
testimony they carry: full replay becomes pathological at scale, and
incremental derivation can preserve replay equivalence. Neither observation
warrants the noun.

## Examination

```text
status          retired  (removed from active law, 2026-08-03)
closest grammar no single equivalent
```

The chapter now called Inquiry was `inquiry-and-examination` until Examination
was removed. It compressed several responsibilities into one word — comparison,
applicability determination, and relation establishment among them — so no
single current term replaces it.

`examine` survives in active law only as an ordinary verb, with *applicability*
as the thing doing the examining.

## Consideration

```text
status          retired  (removed from active law, 2026-08-03)
closest grammar Selection
```

`consideration selection`, `selected-for-consideration standing`, `movement
consideration`, and `candidate consideration` were all removed. The surviving
grammar is Selection: a responsible Selection act choosing from a bounded
candidate set for an exact declared purpose.

The word `consideration` survives twice as ordinary English, in a clause that
contrasts it *against* the real acts — membership creates visibility "for
consideration, not selection, authorization, or resolution."

`consider` is an ordinary verb and names no Responsibility.

## Lens

```text
status          ordinary  (never capitalised in active law)
closest grammar View
```

Often assumed to be a retired proper term. It is not — `Lens` capitalised does
not appear in active law at all; only lowercase `lens` does. It was an
explanatory metaphor that helped make the behaviour intuitive: same evidence,
different lens, different bounded result.

`View` is the better word for what Seed actually does, because a lens sounds
like an apparatus that acts on reality, while a View is a bounded
representation available to a particular consumer under particular coordinates.

Supporting observation: active law never contrasts `lens` with `view`. Every
lens non-equivalence contrasts it with something else — Uptake, evidence,
answer, question, non-mutating act. Two distinct kinds would be distinguished
from each other somewhere.

## Answer

```text
status          ordinary
closest grammar no constitutional subject; a threshold word
```

Every substantive use of `answer` in active law is a limit on what something
does *not* amount to. There is no Answer act, formation, producer, occurrence,
responsibility, or standing definition.

It works like `truth`, which appears throughout active law with explicit
`!= truth` non-equivalences and no Truth kind. Both mark a threshold an
occurrence may not claim to have reached, without naming a thing.

So `lens output != answer` does not establish a second subject. It says forming
a representation is not establishing that the representation satisfies the
exact consumer purpose.

## Composition

```text
status          ordinary
closest grammar representation formation
```

`composition` describes *how* a particular representation was formed, not a
separate occurrence. Active law places it in a list of verbs — a lens
"exposes, selects, projects, or composes a bounded representation" — and no one
would recover an Exposure Responsibility from its neighbours.

Active law also warns against the reification directly, listing as an error
"treating the pipeline's projection-selection-composition call order as a
universal constitutional sequence."

## remembering

```text
status          ordinary  (capitalised only at sentence starts)
closest grammar preserved evidence remaining available to a later consumer
```

Frequently read as a proper term because active law contains the sentence
"Remembering is preservation of sufficient testimony or standing for later
lawful recovery." Every capitalised occurrence is sentence-initial; every
mid-sentence occurrence is lowercase, and the section heading is lowercase. The
capital is orthographic.

The underlying invariant is real and holds regardless: preserved material
stays available for later lawful recovery, and a later occurrence consuming
earlier material produces new occurrences rather than altering the old ones.

## proposed input

```text
status          ordinary  (the phrase remains in active law)
closest grammar input
```

`proposed` is ordinary grammatical qualification meaning *under
consideration*. There is no Proposal responsibility, act, occurrence, or
standing, and none is required.

Neighbouring modifiers all correspond to recovered acts — selected/Selection,
warranted/Warrant, admitted/Admission, attributed/attribution. `proposed`
corresponds to nothing, and the same construction recurs unrelated elsewhere
(`proposed act`, `proposed use`) with the same ordinary sense.

## relation grammar

```text
status          never adopted
closest grammar the constitutional dimensions applicable to the exact relation
```

Absent from active law entirely. It is the ordinary `<X> grammar` construction,
which throughout active law means *the established grammar concerning X* — a
medium through which responsibilities are expressed, never a subject that is
produced or owned. Active law uses that construction about twenty ways and
gives none of them an owner.

It entered a report during a correction round whose stated subject was
something else, and was cited as established vocabulary thereafter.

## candidate equivalence

```text
status          never adopted
closest grammar a candidate claim concerning the presented thing
```

Absent from active law. Introduced by the same correction round as `relation
grammar`, as attributed PESC orientation vocabulary — a bounded proposed
equivalence concerning a Presentation, explicitly not thereby true or
established. PESC itself appears in no active-law chapter.

## Artifact

```text
status          ordinary  (real grammar; not a Responsibility)
closest grammar preserved representation or record carrying another
                responsibility's assertion
```

Included because it is easy to misfile in both directions. `artifact` is
genuine established vocabulary with a definition and a chapter, so it is not
retired. But it owns no Responsibility, no Act, and no independent Standing —
every substantive clause about it is a limit, and its own definition says its
shape "does not supply the warrant for those assertions."

A different Artifact — a pipeline node between answer composition and
presentation, said to own "consumer-specific organization" — was never
recovered and is gone. Sharing a word transfers neither the defect nor the
standing.
