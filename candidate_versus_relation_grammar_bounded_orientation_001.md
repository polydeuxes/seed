### 3.2 What `CandidateExternalGrammarSet` exactly is

`CandidateExternalGrammarSet` is an immutable, read-only diagnostic artifact
assembled from caller-supplied input. Each candidate preserves:

```text
candidate_id
structural_claim
claim_scope
provenance strings
supporting-testimony strings
contradicting-testimony strings
unresolved alternatives
explicit Unknowns
```

The set also preserves `representation_scope`, set-level Unknowns, fixed boundary
notes, `read_only=true`, `writes_event_ledger=false`, and
`mutates_cluster=false`.

The direct Producer-shaped function is
`assemble_candidate_external_grammar_set`. It checks candidate-id presence and
uniqueness and copies the supplied material. The caller supplies the substantive
claims and testimony. No constitutional production occurrence is recorded.

Its carried Authority is negative and boundary-setting only: the boundary notes
say the candidates are caller-supplied structural hypotheses, their supporting
and contradicting testimony relations are preserved rather than evaluated, no
candidate is selected/verified/promoted/treated as semantic truth, and missing
support or contradiction cannot be inverted. Its testimony fields are strings;
they do not establish Seed Evidence standing.

Current consumers are:

* its JSON and human formatters through `seed --candidate-external-grammar`;
* two campaigns that assemble and render/serialize the artifact;
* structural/testimony-projection helpers that begin from its caller material;
* tests of validation, preservation, rendering, and diagnostic visibility.

No found consumer admits it to any exact Compare Responsibility. Searches over
all imports and calls found no event-ledger occurrence, applicability finding,
admission finding, or comparison result involving a
`CandidateExternalGrammarSet`. No code or test uses it as a comparison operand.

Therefore:

```text
candidate material exists != candidate material is admitted
candidate material admitted != comparison occurred
candidate structural claim != established relation grammar
caller-supplied testimony != Seed-native Standing
```
