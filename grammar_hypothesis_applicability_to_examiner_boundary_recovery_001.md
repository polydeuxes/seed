# Grammar-hypothesis applicability-to-examiner boundary recovery 001

## 1. Executive disposition

**D. the Book leaves one exact ownership assignment Unknown.**

The Book assigns a bounded communicative interpretation examination to the exact
Seed consumer whose declared examination requires common-grammar standing, and
it requires the hypothesis consumed there to be applicable for that use. It does
not assign who establishes that purpose-local applicability: the examiner, an
upstream responsibility, or a locally composed admission/applicability boundary.
That single missing assignment is not generic uncertainty about the road.

Accordingly, PR 2168 established only this conditional responsibility:

```text
attributed grammar/relation hypothesis
+ sufficient consumer-local standing
+ established applicability for this examination
-?-> consumption by the assigned interpretation examiner
```

It established neither a present examiner occurrence nor automatic consumption,
`CandidateExternalGrammarSet` applicability, candidate-production demand, or a
universal producer. Throughout this report: hypothesis testimony is not an
applicable hypothesis; applicability is not admission; admission is not selection;
an assigned consumer responsibility is not a consumer occurrence; a possible
consumer is not a current consumer; conditional consumability is not present
producer demand; and warranted applicability does not demand candidate formation.

Likewise, caller supply is not constitutional applicability; addressability is not
attribution; attribution is not a warranted relation; failure is not Demand;
recurrence evidence is not Demand establishment; Demand established is not
movement opened; and distinct claims need not be distinct modules, artifacts,
stages, producers, consumers, or occurrences.

## 4. Applicability coordinates

For one hypothesis to participate in one examination, the determination must
resolve or explicitly preserve Unknown for these local coordinates:

| Coordinate | Required resolution |
| --- | --- |
| identity / subject | Exact preserved ingress occurrence, exact material/span, and exact hypothesis identity; text equality is not identity |
| content / assertion | The proposed grammar or relation assertion, distinct from the carrier and from warranted meaning |
| standing | Attributed testimony standing and the claimed applicability result; no promotion to admission, selection, or truth |
| source / provenance | Source role or Unknown, producer/formation occurrence or Unknown, lineage, translation history, known loss, and evidence references |
| responsibility | Exact responsible applicability owner and act; this is the presently missing assignment |
| authority / warrant | Claim-appropriate basis for applying the hypothesis, authority limits, confidence/uncertainty, negative authority, and no relocation to source/carrier |
| scope / locality | Exact consumer, material, act, participants, temporal/currentness boundary, and examination scope |
| occurrence / preservation | Identity of the responsible determination occurrence, whether transient or preserved, and separation from mere construction/serialization |
| purpose | The examiner's declared communicative use for this exact examination; purpose is required locally, not declared a ninth universal dimension |
| consumer | The relationally assigned interpretation examiner occurrence, not a possible class, caller, test, or diagnostic |

Conflicts and Unknowns must remain typed to the coordinate they obstruct. Stops
must retain the exact reason. Negative authority must deny automatic grammar
adoption, meaning or relation warrant, admission, selection, common-grammar
standing, Demand, movement, goal establishment, execution, recording, or cluster
mutation. Applicability is not inferred from accessibility, structural coherence,
recurrence, a matching class, a caller assertion, or the examiner's mere existence.

## 7. Minimum vertical witness

A bounded implementation PR is **not yet constitutionally warranted**, because
the exact applicability owner/act composition is unassigned. It would be circular
to require a preexisting downstream runtime consumer before implementing adjacent
Book-established responsibilities, but it would be invention to use that allowance
to choose the missing owner. Once the Book assigns it, the minimum witness—not a
framework—must bind one actual recorded ingress occurrence, one exact examiner
purpose, one responsible applicability determination, one assigned examiner
occurrence, and one supported/unsupported relation, conflict, Unknown, refusal, or
lawful stop. A caller may supply attributed testimony but may not supply
applicability, admission, selection, examiner conclusion, constitutional Question
text, or Demand standing. Adjacent claims may be co-established in the same
bounded occurrence if the assignment permits it.
