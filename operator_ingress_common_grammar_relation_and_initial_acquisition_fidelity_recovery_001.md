## 1. General common-grammar relation

```text
participants + exact consumer + exact material + exact act + exact purpose + scope
→ evidence examined by the locally responsible examiner
→ common-grammar standing required for that bounded use
```

**Topology.** The participants are the parties whose shared representation is at issue (in the pinned first-contact case, Seed and the operator), but the relation is required by an **exact consumer**, not by Seed globally. Material is the exact bounded material consumed—e.g. one preserved ingress or one closed-choice token set—not “the language.” The consumer attempts a declared semantic examination act for a declared purpose. Scope binds the relevant occurrence, material, participants, use, and any temporal or other limits. Applicability and consumer admission remain distinct where the first-contact topology requires them.

**Evidence and ownership.** No universal evidence shape or examiner is assigned. Positive standing requires a responsible occurrence with evidence adequate to the exact relation. Absence, insufficiency, conflict, or **Unknown** likewise needs evidence from the responsibility competent to examine that relation; missing state, decoder failure, or unexplained non-use is not such a finding. The occurrence establishing the result is that responsible, bounded examination/establishment occurrence—not successful byte decoding, one exchange, projection, or competency existence. Who owns that examination is local to the consumer/responsibility and otherwise **Unknown**; the first-contact chapter expressly leaves the upstream exact act and owner Unknown.

**Classification:** **mixed locality**, specifically consumer-local + material-local + act-local + purpose-local + participant-local + scope-local. It is not global, session-wide, operator-wide, language-wide, or competency-wide. Session/operator may be implementation coordinates inside a particular scope, not constitutional promotion axes.

Preserved distinctions:

```text
decoder compatibility != common-grammar standing
one successful exchange != permanent common grammar
closed-choice grammar for one token set != free-form prose grammar
common grammar for one consumer != common grammar for every consumer
competency exists != competency applicable to this material and act
```

## 2. Possible pre-goal Demand road

```text
preserved exact operator ingress
+ exact responsible consumer
+ declared examination act and purpose
+ required consumer-local common-grammar relation not established
→ that consumer fails or stops locally
→ each obstruction occurrence is separately preserved
→ recurrence may be measured over bounded comparable occurrences
→ evidence may become applicable to a separately responsible Demand examination
→ possible bounded relational Demand establishment by that separate occurrence
```

This topology applies only once there is an **exact responsible consumer attempting the examination**. The counterexample says that consumer may repeatedly fail or stop while examining the **same preserved operator ingress**. Accordingly, recurrence here requires comparable obstruction occurrences for that same consumer/material/act/purpose scope; merely counting decoder errors, sessions, unsupported tokens, or different consumers does not satisfy the counterexample.

Recurrence establishes only bounded measurement evidence that obstruction repeated. It does not establish ingress meaning, the reason for every failure, common-grammar insufficiency by itself, a Demand, family, candidate, Capability, retry, goal, movement, authority, or execution. The obstruction is material only if an exact responsibility/purpose makes the blocked examination and required relation material; repetition does not manufacture materiality.

A separately responsible Demand examiner may examine the evidence. Its exact identity is not universally assigned and is absent from the current road. If established, the required result is:

```text
common-grammar standing required by the exact consumer
to examine the exact preserved ingress for its declared act and purpose
```

The Demand must preserve exact responsibility and scope, source evidence, materiality, applicability, Unknowns, and conflicts. A separately established competency is only one possible satisfaction candidate because it is neither the required relation nor proof of applicability, Capability, authority, or use.

## 3. Initial acquisition goal-establishment road (stops at BOGE)

```text
developer-supplied, attributed bounded potential-goal candidate G
  expressing M = “establish richer shared grammar with the operator”
+ separately represented local-stop source
→ responsible bounded closed-choice representation and exact-set participation
→ operator response occurrence
→ exact binding / selected presented-alternative identification
→ represented source G recovered through preserved representation lineage
→ separately warranted relation: G expresses M
→ BOGE-local applicability examination
→ consumer-local admission of that warranted relation, identity, lineage, limits
→ BOGE establishment occurrence relies on the admission and consumes M as expressed by G
→ bounded operator goal standing established
[STOP: first-contact special case ends]
```

The minimal shared representation is the bounded communication mechanism, not grammar acquisition. Operator response is external testimony; binding identifies the selected presented alternative; lineage recovers its represented source. None warrants meaning. The meaning relation must independently warrant that exact candidate G expresses proposition M, with applicable authority/convention, provenance, scope, known loss, conflicts, and Unknowns. BOGE consumes the consumer-locally admitted warranted relation and M-as-expressed-by-G, not raw prose or detached proposition text.

The goal proposition is exactly **“establish richer shared grammar with the operator.”** It is operator-origin at establishment. Evidence of origin/selection is exact operator selection testimony identifying the presented alternative representing the attributed potential-goal candidate, followed by lineage recovery; this does not itself warrant the candidate meaning. BOGE establishes bounded operator goal standing only. It does not establish the meaning relation again, grammar acquisition, movement selection, authority, competency, Capability, or performance. The special case ends at the BOGE establishment occurrence. The current implementation contains none of the representation, response, binding, source-recovery, meaning-warrant, applicability, admission, or BOGE producers removed before/at PR 2127; therefore it does not establish this goal.
