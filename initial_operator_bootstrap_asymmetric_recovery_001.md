# Initial operator bootstrap asymmetric recovery 001

## 1. Boundary and finding

This is one bounded, report-only recovery at commit `da047b8`, immediately
after PR 2023. It changes no canonical Book, production code, tests, root
documentation, `docs/`, prior report, diagnostic, CLI, ledger, projected State,
or runtime wiring. Examples are treated as inquiry specimens, not sources of
enum meaning.

**Finding 2:** an existing operator-specific owner is recoverable, but exact
option meanings require a narrow product/constitutional decision.

The recoverable owner is **Seed-owned bounded question formation for the local
interpretation/communication obstruction**, composed with responsible bounded
representation and emission. Its input is not arbitrary global Null. Its input
is one preserved, source-attributed operator occurrence whose representation,
grammar, or meaning remains Unknown or unsupported for the exact intended
consumer. It may form a bounded clarification or interpretation-treatment
question only when it preserves that occurrence's identity, attribution,
scope, evidence demand, uncertainty, authority limits, provenance limits, and
lawful stop conditions. Egress may faithfully present the resulting question
or stopping result, but may not strengthen it.

This responsibility is enough to own production of a future operator-specific
closed choice set. It is not enough to determine the members. The Books do not
say which answer forms Seed should offer, which local tokens should denote
them, or what selecting each form establishes. Those are still a narrow
product/constitutional decision. No enum may be populated from examples,
tests, presentation labels, reports, or the shapes accepted by current
consumers.

PR 2023 was right that no universal initial enum is warranted and right that
binding is not meaning. It overreached when it preferred a fixed,
application-wide bootstrap vocabulary and treated every arbitrary
uninterpreted first line as having one terminal result. That conclusion arose
from compressing materially different local beginnings into a single
“uninterpreted input off null” problem. The repository instead supports
asymmetric, independently advancing roads. Failure on the operator
interpretation road does not erase standing already established on occurrence,
addressability, storage, temporal, emission, or other local dimensions.

## 3. Exact local standing at the operator obstruction

Assume a responsible ingress boundary has preserved an operator occurrence and
the evidence available to the exact consumer supports no common grammar for
interpreting it. The strongest standing is local and layered:

| Coordinate | Standing that may exist | Standing that does not follow |
| --- | --- | --- |
| occurrence | one bounded operator-material occurrence was preserved, if the responsible boundary and occurrence evidence exist | the represented operator act occurred outside the boundary exactly as imagined; complete prehistory |
| material | exact bounded material/reference is addressable with source attribution or Unknown source role | interpretation, testimony content truth, command, question, goal, or common grammar |
| provenance | known ingress/source facts and explicit provenance Unknowns | verified authorship, complete route, producer authority |
| representation/grammar | the applicable representation or common grammar is Unknown, or a bounded comparison found no supporting grammar for this consumer and scope | no grammar exists globally; the operator cannot communicate; Seed is incapable |
| interpretation | no admitted meaning is available for this occurrence and purpose | the material has no meaning; every possible interpretation is false |
| communication | preservation may evidence material intake; a separate emission may evidence Seed presented a response toward the operator | delivery, receipt, mutual understanding, uptake, reliance |
| inquiry pressure | the unresolved local relation may support Seed forming a bounded question about representation, grammar, intended treatment, or relevant meaning | an answer, an operator-authored internal inquiry, permission for unbounded investigation |
| goal | Unknown unless a separate goal-establishment road has exact admitted goal evidence | a goal inferred from prose, clarification, token binding, or non-understanding |
| stopping | the local road may refuse or stop when preservation, warrant, authority, or answer-domain requirements fail | global failure, empty Seed, or null standing on every other road |

“No common grammar supports interpretation” must itself be scoped. If it means
only that the repository currently has no warrant, the standing is **grammar
support unavailable/Unknown for this occurrence, consumer, and purpose**. If a
competent bounded comparison actually tested an identified grammar set, it may
establish only its declared negative result. Neither version warrants the
global proposition that Seed and the operator share no grammar.

Material preservation is therefore not communication uptake. An occurrence is
not interpretation. Yet lack of interpretation does not undo the occurrence's
bounded addressability or prevent Seed-owned question formation about the
preserved Unknowns.

## 4. The bounded response owner

The owner is not ingress, the binder, BOGE, or egress alone. It is this local
composition:

```text
preserved attributed operator occurrence
  + exact consumer/purpose
  + representation/grammar/meaning Unknown or bounded insufficiency evidence
  + bounded translation preserving identity, source, scope, evidence demand,
    uncertainty, authority limits, negative authority, and stop conditions
  -> Seed-owned question formation
  -> operator-specific response meaning production (decision still missing)
  -> faithful bounded representation and responsible emission
  -> later operator material as a distinct occurrence/testimony
  -> exact selection binding, if and only if a real choice was presented
  -> option-specific consumer, not automatic BOGE
```

### Prerequisites

The responsibility may act only when all of the following are present:

1. a distinct preserved operator occurrence, not merely a caller-authored
   string or replayed token;
2. source attribution or explicit Unknown source role and available provenance;
3. the exact consumer and interpretation/communication purpose being blocked;
4. bounded evidence for the Unknown or insufficiency, without upgrading lack of
   warrant into universal absence;
5. a Seed-owned translation that fixes question identity, source, scope,
   evidence demand, uncertainty, authority limits, and lawful stop;
6. a product/constitutional specification of every offered result, its stable
   identity, token, selection effect, eligible consumer, and refusal behavior;
7. a presentation occurrence tying the immutable set to the later response;
   and
8. consumer-local validation after exact token binding.

### Limits

The owner is local to the exact operator occurrence and response purpose. It
may not interpret arbitrary prose silently, claim common grammar, turn its
question taxonomy into constitutional ontology, create global bootstrap state,
reuse token meanings outside the presented set, or route all selections into
BOGE. It may stop without offering an enum when the answer domain lacks warrant.

Question formation is the semantic responsibility; representation/emission is
only its operator-facing boundary. Calling this an “enum producer” describes a
possible realization shape, not a new constitutional primitive. An enum remains
a representation of already specified bounded results, not common grammar by
identity.

## 5. What a selection could establish

The repository warrants distinct **result kinds**, but not the missing initial
members or their tokens. A future decision must state exactly which result each
option produces; labels cannot do this work.

| Possible result kind | Maximum effect of a warranted selection | Required next consumer | BOGE treatment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| additional operator testimony | a new attributed operator statement/material occurrence linked as a response to the bounded question | preservation, then interpretation/warrant examination | **bypass** |
| requested interpretation treatment | the operator selected one offered treatment for this material (for example, a specified examination or representation treatment only if constitutionally adopted) | the exact treatment owner, with separate applicability and authority checks | **bypass** unless the specified effect itself is an admitted goal |
| clarification | the operator selected a specified clarification relation or supplied material within a specified answer domain | question/interpretation owner | **bypass** |
| stopping | local operator-requested or policy-defined non-continuation for this road | stopping/emission boundary | **bypass** |
| bounded operator goal | the exact selected meaning is an operator goal, but only if its producer, evidence, scope, authority, and consumer contract say selection establishes that goal | BOGE with goal-specific admission | **may enter BOGE** |
| interpretation-candidate selection | selection evidence for one exact, already-produced candidate, without proving applicability or admission | contextual interpretation selection, applicability, then admission | **bypass initially**; only a later admitted goal meaning may enter BOGE |
| non-selection / unsupported token | no offered candidate was selected, or selection remains Unknown/conflicted | local response owner for refusal, a separately warranted retry, or stop | **bypass** |

This table classifies constitutional effects; it does not propose enum members.
Even names such as “clarification” and “stopping” denote families of results,
not operator-visible choices. A choice must define whether it requests new
testimony, selects a treatment, answers a question, terminates one road, or
establishes an exact goal. Token binding proves only membership in the exact
presented set. It cannot supply that semantic effect.

All results bypass BOGE by default. Only an option whose independently
warranted contract says that operator selection establishes an exact bounded
operator goal may cross into BOGE. Clarification is not necessarily goal
establishment; an answer can merely narrow an Unknown or add testimony.

## 6. Product/constitutional decision still required

No exact option meaning is currently warranted. The Books warrant the owner,
the bounded question-forming discipline, distinct response standings, lawful
non-selection, refusal, and stopping. They do not warrant:

* a mandatory enum rather than another bounded answer form or immediate stop;
* a closed list of operator response meanings;
* any token, label, order, default, retry policy, or global reuse;
* whether selection supplies testimony, requests treatment, clarifies, stops,
  or establishes a goal;
* an answer domain for an arbitrary preserved occurrence; or
* a rule that absence of admitted meaning should always produce the same
  terminal text.

The smallest remaining decision is not “define Seed's universal bootstrap
vocabulary.” It is: **for the exact initial operator-communication
responsibility, under the prerequisites above, which bounded response results
may Seed offer, what does selection of each establish, and which consumer may
rely on it?** The decision must also authorize an empty/no-question result and
lawful stop when none applies.

Configuration may carry a decision already made; it cannot make the decision.
Tests may exercise arbitrary members; they cannot authorize them. Consumer
shape may reject incompatible results; it cannot originate meanings. Repeated
labels and reports remain presentation or historical testimony. Consequently
PR 2023's proposed fixed vocabulary is neither preferred nor dispreferred by
repository authority: its *application-wide* scope is unwarranted, while a
narrow operator-specific vocabulary remains a product/constitutional choice.

## 7. Asymmetric topology of independent initial occurrences

There is no privileged first constitutional event and no global transition
“off null.” Independent occurrences may establish different local standings in
different orders.

| Independent initial occurrence | Local responsibility/road | What it can establish locally | What it cannot establish |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| exact external material such as `.` with source/provenance limits | addressability and bounded examination/question formation | literal identity, bounded presence/addressability, attributed source or Unknown, candidate examination pressure, representation/meaning Unknowns | punctuation/path/current-directory meaning, operator communication, goal, Fact, universal first movement |
| operator-attributed material | operator ingress preservation, then Seed-owned interpretation/question formation | exact operator occurrence and attribution; inquiry pressure; possibly a bounded Seed question after translation | uptake, admitted meaning, internal question by identity, goal, shared grammar, enum meanings |
| environment reports expected database absent | storage discovery responsibility | bounded result of a declared discovery at its scope/time, if competently evidenced | constitutional Null, global nonexistence, goal, interpretation of operator material, Seed birth |
| database creation occurrence | storage operation responsibility | bounded operation occurrence/effect testimony, if evidenced; storage availability within scope | first constitutional event, event-ledger history, boot completion, cluster truth, communication standing |
| timestamp or clock reading | temporal evidence responsibility | the declared clock/source reading and its provenance/uncertainty | event time, causal order, currentness for every subject, meaning of adjacent material |
| first lawful ledger append | the competent event-producing/recording owner | one event artifact asserting its bounded occurrence/claim | truth of every assertion, database creation as biography, global initialization |
| responsible Seed response emission | representation/emission owner | formation and separately evidenced emission of a bounded representation toward a candidate operator | delivery, receipt, interpretation, uptake, reliance, operator agreement |
| exact response to a presented local set | occurrence owner and closed-choice binder | response occurrence and exact local token binding/non-binding | semantic effect, goal, authority, shared grammar, applicability to another set |

These roads may coexist without convergence. Storage can exist while operator
meaning remains Unknown. An operator occurrence can be addressable while no
event has been recorded. A timestamp can have source-relative standing while
the event it appears beside remains unverified. External material can open
bounded examination without being operator communication. Successful storage
discovery does not advance interpretation; failed interpretation does not
revoke storage or temporal standing. No single occurrence initializes Seed
globally.

## 8. Correction of PR 2023

PR 2023 correctly rejected invented options and correctly found the current
closed-choice-to-BOGE seam unsafe for non-goal results. Four conclusions need
narrowing:

1. **“arbitrary uninterpreted first input” was too broad a subject.** It merged
   operator communication, unattributed external material, environmental
   discovery, storage creation, and temporal evidence under one bootstrap
   condition.
2. **question formation was made too dependent on semantic interpretation.**
   Normal inquiry includes Seed-side interpretation and bounded standing, but
   the Books also allow bounded question formation from attributed material and
   preserved uncertainty. The translation must bound the question; it need not
   answer the material first.
3. **one terminal refusal was made universal.** Lawful refusal remains possible,
   but repository authority does not require identical treatment of every
   non-EOF occurrence or erase local standing already gained.
4. **a fixed application-wide vocabulary was called the preferred smallest
   decision.** The recoverable scope is narrower: one operator-specific
   interpretation/communication response responsibility. Its exact meanings
   remain undecided.

The corrected topology is therefore not:

```text
global null -> arbitrary material -> universal enum or terminal refusal
```

It is:

```text
independent occurrence
  -> its own responsible preservation/production boundary
  -> local standing and local Unknowns
  -> optional local question, response, refusal, or stop
  -> only separately warranted downstream establishment
```
