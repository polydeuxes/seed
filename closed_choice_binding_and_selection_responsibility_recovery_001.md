# Closed-choice binding and selection responsibility recovery 001

## Mandate, method, and governing answer

This report asks what exact responsibility, if any, consumes one captured
operator-response occurrence together with one exact bounded presentation and its
coordinates, and what bounded claim it may produce about the presented alternative
the operator selected. It changes no active law or implementation. The labels used
for the ten inspections and the topology positions are **report-local analytical
labels**. `Unknown` is active constitutional vocabulary. Unmarked connective prose
is ordinary descriptive language, not a proposed constitutional kind.

**Principal disposition: D — operator act and Seed responsibility are collapsed.**

The active district independently warrants (1) an external operator selection, (2)
Seed comparison of captured material with already-formed presentation coordinates,
and (3) identification of a corresponding presented alternative. It does not
faithfully assign the producer of the stronger testimony that the operator selected
that alternative. `responsible selection occurrence` assigns identification to a
phrase which can name the operator act, while `responsible binding occurrence`
assigns comparison and identification even though the token-to-alternative relation
already belongs to representation formation. Finally, an external occurrence is
said to “supply” `selection testimony` while the same testimony is treated as input
to binding and identification. Owner, act, evidence, result, and first consumer are
therefore cyclically compressed. This is not repaired by inventing a replacement
name.

The strongest bounded recovery is: a responsible Seed occurrence may compare the
captured response material with the exact coordinates of the emitted closed-choice
presentation and, under that exact relation, identify the corresponding presented
alternative or identify none. Whether the same occurrence also establishes the
stronger claim “the operator selected alternative A,” the exact producer of that
claim, and the exact result kind remain **Unknown**. The external operator selection
must not be assigned to Seed.

The active representation chapter repeats this grammar outside the BOGE chapter,
so the words are not lexically confined to that file. It supplies no second
independent current consumer, however: its closed-choice paragraph restates the same
BOGE/local-stop road and examples. One current consumer does not warrant a universal
responsibility for future prose, clarification, action, navigation, treatment,
evidence, explanation, or stopping uses. Those possible uses are **operator design
testimony**, not current constitutional demand.

## Required decomposition

### 1. Presented-alternative formation

The source is bounded potential-goal standing or the local-stop source/treatment,
not arbitrary prose. Eligibility for one presentation purpose is a separate
warranted relation. A responsible representation occurrence forms an alternative
which “refers to or represents” that source and preserves source identity/meaning,
presented content, purpose, scope, provenance, loss, Unknowns, and conflicts. The
formation result is the presented alternative and preserved representation lineage.
Formation does **not** itself establish exact-set participation: the active Book
expressly separates eligibility, formation, and participation. The owner of the
participation-establishing relation is **Unknown**.

### 2. Exact-set participation

The Book requires an exact presented set and separately warranted participation of
each presented alternative, but assigns no exact owner for that establishment. Set
identity and alternative identity must be recoverable; exact presentation identity
is implied by the local scope but its constitutive identifier is **Unknown**.
Position, label, token, literal encoding, and binding are distinct representation
coordinates; none is universally constitutive. Later comparison/identification
requires the participation relation. Source existence, eligibility, formation, and
participation remain four positions.

### 3. Token or presentation-coordinate relation

`exact token binding` presently most strongly names a **relation between an exact
token coordinate and one participating presented alternative**, carried in the
bounded representation. It may also be compound historical shorthand. The active
chapters say tokens/bindings/labels are representation coordinates, compare a later
response “against” bindings, and distinguish token binding from operator selection.
Therefore the relation already exists before emission and response. The exact act
and owner that establish it are **Unknown**; the later response-consuming occurrence
does not create it merely by comparing against it.

### 4. Operator selection

The operator is the subject of the external occurrence: within one exact emitted
presented set, the operator may select one presented alternative by emitting
material intended as the corresponding coordinate. The occurrence evidence is the
external response plus its presentation context; Seed captures only material and
lineage. The act exists before Seed establishes testimony about it, but its exact
constitutional occurrence name beyond the Book's quoted `operator selection
occurrence` wording is not independently recovered. `operator selected A` is not
equivalent to `Seed has evidence that operator selected A`.

### 5. Response capture

Current runtime assigns `competent-raw-material-capture` in
`_capture_representation`. `CapturedOperatorMaterial` supplies exact bytes, EOF,
delimiter, capture boundary, byte-material origin, encoding testimony, and known
loss. A separate representation examination may strictly decode those bytes; then
`operator.ingress.ingress_occurred` preserves decoded text and lineage. Current
runtime captures only the initial ingress, not a later closed-choice response. The
active Book calls the local input a `captured response token` but does not assign its
capture responsibility or prove that decoded material is constitutionally a token.
Capture establishes no meaning, equality, relation, identity, or selection.

### 6. Comparison

The active sentence independently requires a Seed-side responsible occurrence to
compare captured response material against the exact presented set/bindings. Its
rule is exact coordinate equality within that presentation, not free-form
interpretation. Inputs must preserve response occurrence, presentation, set,
alternative participation, and token relation. A positive comparison finding or
bounded nonmatch is the strongest direct result. Scope is one presentation and one
response occurrence. First positive consumer is presented-alternative
identification; negative consumption preserves no identified alternative and
Unknown meaning/intent/treatment. The exact responsibility name and artifact form
are **Unknown**. No current implementation performs it.

### 7. Presented-alternative identification

Identification is a distinct relation-producing act because equality of response R
and token T plus the pre-existing relation from T to A must be used to claim A. The
Book permits comparison and identification in one responsible occurrence; that does
not make their subjects or results identical. The bounded identification claim is:
within exact presentation C, captured material R equals coordinate T, and the
preserved T-to-A relation therefore identifies participating presented alternative
A as corresponding to R. On nonmatch, no alternative is identified. Whether this
claim alone supports the stronger operator-selected-A testimony is **Unknown**.

### 8. Selection testimony

Active wording intends testimony about which alternative the operator selected. It
is not testimony *by* the operator in the preserved response alone, because raw
material does not identify A without Seed comparison and representation lineage. It
is not lawfully produced by the external occurrence alone. Evidence would require
the exact external response occurrence, capture lineage, emitted presentation,
set/participation standing, token relation, comparison evidence, and identification
relation. The producer and exact result kind are **Unknown**. Source recovery is the
first stated positive consumer. `exact token binding` and identification are
illogically also called consumers of testimony which they help establish. It cannot
establish source identity, meaning, warrant, applicability, admission, BOGE,
authority, movement, performance, or stopping.

### 9. Represented-source recovery

After A is identified, an independently responsible lineage use may recover the
source S represented by A. Active wording says representation lineage “may support”
recovery but does not name its responsible owner. Recovery produces bounded source
identity, relation path, and attribution source; it does not select S by identity,
establish S means M, warrant the meaning relation, establish applicability or
admission, establish BOGE, or stop. Its first consumers are separate meaning-relation
warrant and the relevant local road.

### 10. Downstream consumption

BOGE does **not** directly consume raw response or selection testimony. The positive
road is identification -> source recovery -> separate meaning-relation warrant ->
BOGE-local applicability -> consumer-local admission -> BOGE reliance on the
admitted relation and consumption of M -> possible establishment. The stop road is
identification -> local-stop source recovery -> separate meaning relation ->
competent stopping reliance -> bounded stop. Presentation navigation is only an
example, with no current consumer assignment.

## One occurrence versus several acts

1. Comparison and alternative identification are distinct acts: comparison produces
   coordinate equality/non-equality; identification uses that finding and the
   pre-existing coordinate relation to identify A.
2. One responsible occurrence may lawfully perform both because the active Book says
   separate occurrences are not universally required.
3. Evidence must preserve both predicates, their inputs, rule, lineage, result,
   scope, and responsible occurrence; a bare returned A does not prove both.
4. Shared occurrence does not collapse results.
5. A Python function or method boundary is implementation structure, not
   constitutional occurrence evidence.
6. One external response occurrence is not one Seed occurrence.
7. One preserved response can support several later Seed acts without recapture.
8. The Book states the multiplicity distinction but does not faithfully preserve it
   in `responsible selection occurrence`, `binding occurrence`, and the testimony
   supply/consumption cycle.
