# Constitutional Kinds and Artifact Standing Audit 001

## Audit boundary and method

The sole audit subject is
`book_of_seed/01-grammar-and-standing/constitutional-kinds-and-artifact-standing.md`
as read in full at the audited occurrence. Active Book text is constitutional
authority. Runtime and tests are implementation testimony only; historical
reports are attributed testimony only; Git history is provenance testimony
only. Recurrence, adjacency, capitalization, headings, grammar, plausibility,
implementation names, and stylistic similarity are not authority.

The following identifiers are **report-local only; not active Book vocabulary;
not a proposed constitutional subject**: `P01` through `P20` (paragraph
segments), `C01` through `C39` (materially distinct claims), and `L01` through
`L76` (ledger entries). They permit exact cross-reference without renaming any
Book subject, act, result, or relation.

The audit applies the required vocabulary gate to each load-bearing phrase:
whether it is defined or otherwise established, is the subject of an assigned
act, has a producer or establishment boundary, has bounded participants or
coordinates, or is only ordinary language or shorthand. A phrase is not
warranted by the clause in which it is under examination; the warrant cited
below is a defining, assigning, bounding, or negative-authority clause elsewhere
in the file or the surrounding grammar explicitly supplied by the same section.
