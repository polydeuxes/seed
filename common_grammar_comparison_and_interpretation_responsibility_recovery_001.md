# Common-grammar comparison and interpretation responsibility recovery 001

## Result

This is one bounded, report-only recovery on current merged `main`. Current Book
text is authority; production is implementation evidence; tests and the five
required prior reports are testimony. No conceptual diagram is treated as an
established producer–output–consumer road.

**Governing answer: D.** The Book permits locally different positions. Exact
closed-choice binding can establish a tiny bounded shared-grammar relation without
recurrence. A communicative interpretation examination can examine exact material
under an attributed grammar-relation hypothesis. Mechanical measurement may occur
before it, and recurrence measurement over separately evidenced outcomes is later,
but neither ordering is universal. `01.External.E` constrains a measurement if one
is declared; it does not assign its producer or make it a prerequisite.

**Disposition (exactly one):**

> **comparison and interpretation responsibilities are locally variable and no
> universal ordering is established**

This does not overturn PR 2166. Its first missing production occurrence remains an
identified responsible consumer performing a communicative,
common-grammar-dependent examination of exact preserved operator material for a
declared act and purpose. “Bounded cross-grammar comparison” is too broad to be a
single replacement responsibility: it can name measurement, an act inside that
interpretation examination, or later comparison of interpretation outcomes. The
Book preserves those claims separately even when one occurrence could produce more
than one.

## Controlling authority

`01.External.A–C` keeps provider, operator, realization, and other source grammar
attributed and external across addressability, translation, and consumption.
Translation can create bounded evidence or examination standing, but cannot adopt
the source grammar as constitutional law. `01.External.F/G` likewise separates a
candidate's carriage and production from selection, applicability, and a warranted
Seed-native meaning relation.

`01.External.E` says a **declared measurement may** produce exact equality, count,
recurrence, prefix, predicate, or adjacency findings. It requires the examined
representation/projection, sameness rule, and bounded scope for recurrence. Its
negative authority is decisive: measurement does not establish structural,
grammatical, or semantic meaning or standing beyond the measurement assertion.
The permissive clause supplies an output boundary, not an owner, demand, input
applicability rule, consumer, or mandatory occurrence.

`01.Standing.E` makes every relation—including meaning—its own bounded claim. A
warranted meaning relation remains distinct from proposition truth, applicability,
admission, and consumer reliance. What becomes Seed-native is the warranted
constitutional **distinction or relation**, not either attributed source grammar.

The operator-ingress prerequisite supplies the decisive local counterexamples.
Exact token binding in one closed-choice representation may provide the first
shared grammar for that exact consumer, material, act, purpose, participants, and
scope. It is not free-form interpretation, adoption of English, or permanent
competency. Conversely, repeated unsupported bindings supply scoped obstruction
evidence only. The chapter also displays later translation, interpretation,
warrant, selection, applicability, and admission as distinct relations while
leaving the exact upstream translation and interpretation owner Unknown.

Evidence, authority, refusal, and stopping law prevent all stronger promotions:
availability is not applicability; a reference is not resolved support; adjacency
and repetition are not semantic support; reliance cannot expand standing; and
unsupported, conflict, Unknown, refusal, or bounded stop remain lawful local
outcomes.

## Candidate responsibility tests

### 1 — measurement-only comparison

The Book constrains this responsibility but does not require or assign it.
Applicable, addressable representations plus a declared equality/equivalence or
predicate rule may be measured inside a bounded scope. Its sole positive output is
a measurement finding: equality, count, recurrence, prefix occurrence, adjacency,
declared-predicate result, or bounded Unknown/insufficiency where responsibly
examined. It produces no structural hypothesis, grammar applicability, selection,
translation, meaning, or common-grammar standing. Purpose governs later reliance
but is not required for every exact count. The exact owner and consumer are local
and presently Unknown.

### 2 — interpretation examination

This is the missing responsibility recovered in PR 2166. Its subject is whether
exact preserved operator material can be interpreted under one attributed
candidate grammar or relation hypothesis for an exact consumer, act, purpose,
participants, and scope. It requires applicable/admitted inputs and claim-relative
warrant, not merely addressable material or caller assertions. It may produce a
supported interpretation/meaning relation, unsupported, conflict, Unknown,
failure, refusal, or lawful stop. Cross-grammar comparison can describe its
bounded analytical act, but a measurement finding is not its semantic output.

### 3 — recurrence across interpretation outcomes

Where it consumes multiple separately evidenced interpretation attempts or
results, stable coordinates, and an exact sameness rule, it is necessarily
downstream of those attempts. It may produce recurrence, correspondence,
contradiction, or variation evidence only. The Book generically permits such a
measurement but does not establish the proposed positive operator/candidate-
grammar recurrence road or assign its producer.

### 4 — common-grammar relation examination

The claim “this bounded relation is warranted” is distinct from both mechanical
measurement and “this material can be interpreted under hypothesis G.” It needs
relation-appropriate evidence or constitutive convention, source/provenance,
consumer-local purpose, scope, authority, loss, conflicts, and Unknowns. The Book
requires a responsible warrant boundary for a meaning/common-grammar relation but
does not assign a universally separate module or occurrence. One interpretation
occurrence may examine and warrant both claims if it exposes each output and its
separate support; recurrence is not universally required.

### 5 — direct exact binding

The prerequisite chapter establishes this local case. A formed closed-choice
presentation, exact token bindings, and a captured matching response permit a
responsible binding occurrence to identify the matching presented alternative.
That is minimal shared-grammar standing without recurrence. It does not interpret
original prose, warrant the represented source candidate's meaning, admit that
meaning to BOGE, or establish a goal.

## Recurrence taxonomy

### Negative recurrence

The measured subject is repeated obstruction across separately evidenced attempts.
Sameness requires the same exact consumer, preserved material, examination act,
purpose, required grammar standing, relevant failure/stop reason, and bounded
scope/currentness. Text equality, loop iteration, same function, or repeated token
nonmatch does not silently establish those coordinates. The output is scoped
obstruction evidence potentially usable by a later claim-relative consumer. It
does not interpret the response, establish meaning, establish common grammar,
establish a Demand, choose a Demand family, prove global incapability, or mandate a
retry count.

### Positive correspondence recurrence

The proposed pairing of O1/G1/outcome and O2/G2/outcome is constitutionally
plausible as a declared measurement over separately evidenced outcomes. Current
Book text establishes only the generic measurement and recurrence constraints. It
does not establish that exact evidentiary form, its sameness rule, producer,
threshold, consumer, or road to common grammar. Therefore:

```text
separately evidenced interpretation outcomes
-?-> declared positive-correspondence recurrence measurement
-?-> bounded correspondence evidence
-X-> grammatical meaning or common grammar automatically
```

## Candidate topologies

### A — same responsibility

Conditionally possible for C2 only. One responsible interpretation occurrence may
compare exact material under G and expose supported, contradictory, or Unknown
interpretation and relation findings. It cannot silently turn mechanical
correspondence into meaning. Thus comparison-as-C2-act and C2 may share an
occurrence, while their claims stay explicit.

### B — measurement before interpretation

Permitted, not established as a road:

```text
operator material + attributed candidate grammar
-?-> declared measurement-only comparison
-?-> applicable measurement finding
-?-> interpretation examination
```

No Book clause assigns the first producer or requires its output.

### C — interpretation before recurrence

Conditionally valid whenever recurrence actually consumes interpretation outcomes:

```text
separately evidenced interpretation results
-?-> outcome recurrence comparison
-?-> recurrence/correspondence/variation evidence
-?-> common-grammar relation examination
```

The first dependency is definitional for this candidate; the later consumer road
remains unresolved.

### D — direct exact binding

Book-established as a local relation:

```text
formed closed-choice presentation
+ exact token bindings
+ captured matching response
-> responsible exact binding and selected-alternative identification
-> minimal bounded shared-grammar standing for that selection
```

It does not establish the meaning of the represented source or free-form ingress.

### E — local variability

This is the strongest universal conclusion. Exact binding may establish a tiny
relation immediately; richer free-form interpretation may use comparison evidence
but has no fixed recurrence requirement; negative recurrence establishes
obstruction only. “Locally variable” is not permission to omit local warrant—it
means the Book assigns no universal sequence.

## Amendment effect and recommendation

PR 2166's recommended amendment should **remain unchanged** in constitutional
target. The Book still needs the exact communicative interpretation consumer,
material, act, purpose, authority, outputs, and stops assigned. A later amendment
may describe comparison as part of that act only if it carefully distinguishes
measurement findings from interpretation and common-grammar relation findings.
It should not add a mandatory upstream measurement producer, a recurrence
prerequisite, or a universal producer–consumer road on current authority.

**Recommendation (exactly one next operation):** **draft the narrow Book amendment
recommended by PR 2166, using the refined responsibility contract**. “Refined”
means explicitly preserving the separate possible outputs and local comparison
act; it does not change the target or draft amendment wording in this report.
